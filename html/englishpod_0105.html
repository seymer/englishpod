A:  Yeah, well, that was some good advice. You made some really good points.是啊，那确实是些很好的建议。你提出了一些非常有力的观点。
B:  Well, I just wanted to say well done. Really you did a great job.好吧，我只是想表扬一下。你真的做得很棒。
