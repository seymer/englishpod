Daily Life - Going To The Bakery (C0329)

A:  Welcome to Al’s Bakery. What can I get you?欢迎光临 Al 的面包店，请问您需要点什么？
B:  Hi! Let me get a dozen croissants, four blueberry muffins and a loaf of sourdough bread.嗨！我要一打牛角面包、四个蓝莓玛芬和一条酸种面包。
A::  Sure. Would you like to have the loaf sliced?好的，您想要我把这条面包切片吗？
B:  No, that's OK. Do you have any whole wheat bread?不用了，谢谢。你们有全麦面包吗？
A:  We are out at the moment. May I suggest some rye bread?我们现在没有了。要不要试试黑麦面包？
B:  Sure that sounds good. Do you have any cakes?听起来不错。你们有蛋糕吗？
A:  We have various birthday cakes and also ice cream cakes.我们有各种生日蛋糕，还有冰淇淋蛋糕。
B:  I’ll just take a cheesecake.那我就来一块芝士蛋糕吧。
A:  Will that be all?就这些了吗？
B:  Yes.是的。
A:  Your total is forty three dollars and twenty cents.您的总额是 43 美元 20 美分。
