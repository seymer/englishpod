Intermediate - You Are Fired!(0072)

A:  Hi Isabel! You wanted to see me?嗨，伊莎贝尔！你想见我？
B:  Yes Anthony, come on in. Have a seat.是的，安东尼，请进。坐吧。
A:  Is everything okay? You seem a bit preoccupied.一切还好吗？你看起来心事重重的。
B:  Well, Anthony, this is not going to be an easy conversation. The company has made some decisions which will have a direct impact on your employment.好吧，安东尼，这可不是什么轻松的谈话。公司已经做出了一些决定，将直接影响你的工作。
A:  Okay...好吧……
B:  I don't want to beat around the bush so I'm just gonna come out with it. We are going to have to let you go.我不想拐弯抹角，所以我就直说了。我们不得不让你走人。
A:  What? Why? I mean... just like that? I'm fired?什么？为什么？我的意思是……就这样？我被解雇了？
B:  I'm sorry but, to be honest, you are a terrible employee!对不起，但老实说，你是个糟糕的员工！
A:  What! I resent that!什么！我抗议！
B:  Anthony, you were caught making international calls from the office phone, you called in sick in eight times this month and you smell like alcohol!安东尼，你被发现在办公室用座机打国际电话，这个月你请了八次病假，而且你身上有酒味！
