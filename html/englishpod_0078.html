The Weekend - International Workers Day(0078)

A:  Alright everyone settle down. Let's get started. As you know, an important aspect of becoming a good citizen is understanding the genesis of our legal system. It is not enough to simply memorize our laws, it is necessary that we comprehend why and how they were formed. This brings me to our topic for today. Does anyone know what we celebrate on May first?大家安静，我们开始吧。众所周知，成为合格公民的一个重要方面是了解法律体系的起源。仅仅死记硬背法律是不够的，我们必须理解它们为何及如何形成。这引出了我们今天的话题：谁知道五月一日我们在庆祝什么？
B:  Cinco de mayo?五月五日？
A:  No, that's May fifth in Spanish, James, no wonder you are failing my Spanish class. No, May first is International Workers' Day.不，那是西班牙语的五月五号，詹姆斯，难怪你西班牙语不及格。不，五月一日是国际劳动节。
B:  Do we get a day off from school then?那我们能放假吗？
A:  No! It is not considered to be a national holiday here in the US, but in other countries it is.不行！在美国这里它不算国家法定假日，但在其他国家却是。
B:  Aww, man!哎呀，真可惜！
A:  In the nineteenth century, working conditions were appalling, with workers being forced to work ten, twelve, and fourteen hours a day. Support for the eight-hour work day movement was growing rapidly, despite the indifference and hostility of many union leaders, and by April 1886, 250,000 workers were involved in the May Day movement. Previous legislative attempts to improve working conditions had failed, so labor organizers took drastic measures. They passed a resolution stating that eight hours would constitute a legal day's work. And, on May First 1886, the resolution took effect.十九世纪的工作条件极其恶劣，工人们被迫每天工作十、十二甚至十四个小时。尽管许多工会领袖漠不关心甚至充满敌意，但支持八小时工作制运动的呼声却迅速增长。到 1886 年 4 月，已有 25 万名工人参与了五一运动。此前改善工作条件的立法尝试均告失败，因此劳工组织者采取了果断措施。他们通过决议规定八小时为法定工作日。1886 年 5 月 1 日，该决议正式生效。
B:  Cool! Is that why we only work eight hours now?太棒了！这就是我们现在只工作八小时的原因吗？
A:  Yes! But the happy ending came at a high price. On May third, 1886, police fired into a crowd of strikers at the McCormick Reaper Works Factory, killing four and wounding many. A mass meeting was called for the next day to protest the brutality.是的！但那个幸福的结局代价高昂。1886 年 5 月 3 日，警察向麦考密克收割机工厂的罢工人群开枪，造成四人死亡、多人受伤。第二天，人们集会抗议这种暴行。
B:  And then what happened?那之后又发生了什么？
A:  Well, as we say, the rest is history...嗯，正如我们所说，剩下的都是历史……
