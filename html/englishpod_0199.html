Global View - Learning About First Aid (C0199)

A:  Hey Joe! Where have you been these past few days? 嘿乔！你这几天去哪儿了？
B:  I've been busy with a first aid course that I started about a week ago at the Red Cross. 我上了一门急救课程，大概一周前在红十字会开始的。
A:  Cool! I've always wanted to do something like that! Have you learned anything useful? 太酷了！我一直想学点这类东西！你学到了什么有用的吗？
B:  For sure! I mean we've learned how to apply pressure to stop bleeding, how to check for a pulse, and even how to apply CPR! 当然！我们学了如何施压止血、如何检查脉搏，甚至心肺复苏术！
A:  Have you treated any real emergencies? 你处理过真正的紧急情况吗？
B:  Well, they took us along with some paramedics. There was this guy who fell off his motorcycle and suffered a concussion as well as a couple of compound fractures. His wounds were pretty serious so they had to rush him to the hospital. It was intense! 嗯，他们带我们跟着一些急救人员一起行动。有个家伙从摩托车上摔下来，受了脑震荡还有几处开放性骨折。他的伤势相当严重，所以得赶紧送他去医院。那场面太紧张了！
A:  I can imagine! I tend to faint when I see blood, so I think I won't be taking up a course like that anytime soon! 我能想象！我一见血就容易晕，所以我估计短期内不会去上那种课程了！

Supplementary Vocabulary

A strip of material such as gauze used to protect, immobilize, compress, or support a wound or injured body part.
a hard shell usually made of gauze and plaster, used to immobilize an injured body part
A rigid device used to prevent motion of a joint or of the ends of a fractured bone.
