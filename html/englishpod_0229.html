Daily Life - Buying Men's Shoes (C0229)

Mom:  Hi! I am looking for a pair of shoes for my son. 嗨！我想给我儿子买双鞋。
Salesgirl:  Sure thing! Here we are! If you're looking for dress shoes, we have several different styles of Oxfords for boys. We also carry athletic shoes, hiking boots. 没问题！就在那边！如果您在找正装鞋，我们有多种适合男孩的牛津鞋款式。我们也有运动鞋和徒步靴。
Mom:  Oh Jacob, how about these sneakers? 哦，雅各布，那双运动鞋怎么样？
Jacob:  Mom? They've got Velcro. 妈妈？它们有魔术贴。
Mom:  Well, then how about these? What is this style called? 那这双呢？这种鞋叫什么？
Salesgirl:  They're tennis shoes. They're very popular with teens and young adults. 它们是网球鞋。青少年和年轻人非常流行。
Jacob:  Oooo, Mom, can I get these? 哇，妈妈，我能买这双吗？
Mom:  What are those? 那些是什么？
Jacob:  They're Chuck Taylor's! Everyone has them! Can I, please? 它们是康威士！人人都有的！我可以买吗，拜托了？
Mom:  I don't know. Would they go with your clothes? The backs are really high. and the way the tongue just sticks up. They're almost like a boot. And the sole doesn't look like it would have a very good grip. 我不知道。它们能搭配你的衣服吗？后跟特别高，鞋舌也竖着翘起来。它们几乎像靴子一样。而且鞋底看起来抓地力也不怎么样。
Jacob:  They're only forty-five dollars! And they've got cool fluorescent orange shoelaces! Mom? 只要四十五美元！还有超酷的荧光橙色鞋带！妈妈？
Mom:  Ok, try them on. 好，试穿一下。
Salesgirl:  What size are your feet? 你脚多大码？
Mom:  He is a size nine. 他是九码。
Salesgirl:  We'll try a size forty-three on you first and see how that fits. 我们先给你试试四十三码的欧洲码，看看合不合脚。
Mom:  A what? 什么？
Salesgirl:  They come in European sizes. He should be a size forty-three. I'll be right back. 它们有欧洲尺码。他应该是四十三码。我马上回来。
