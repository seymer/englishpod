Daily Life - At The Pharmacy(0084)

A:  Hello sir, how can I help you?您好，有什么可以帮您？
B:  Yes, I need this prescription please.是的，请给我这个处方。
A:  Let's see. Okay, so 50 mg of Prozac, would you prefer this in capsule or tablet?让我看看。好的，50 毫克的百忧解，您希望是胶囊还是片剂？
B:  Capsules are fine.胶囊没问题。
A:  Okay, you should take 1 capsule 3 times a day. Be sure not to take it on an empty stomach, and also, don't ever mix it with alcohol!好的，您每天应服用三次，每次一粒。切记不要空腹服用，也千万不要和酒精混用！
B:  Yes, I know. It's not the first time I'm taking this! Don't worry, I won't overdose!是的，我知道。这不是我第一次吃这个了！别担心，我不会过量服用的！
A:  Okay, anything else I can get you?好的，还需要我帮您拿点什么吗？
B:  Oh, yes, I almost forgot! Can I also get some eye drops and um, some condoms?哦，对，我差点忘了！还能顺便给我一些眼药水，还有……安全套吗？
A:  Sure. Darn condoms aren't registered in our system.当然可以。该死，安全套在我们的系统里没有登记。
B:  Oh, well that's okay, I'll get some later, thanks... Really it's no problem.哦，那没关系，我稍后再买就好，谢谢……其实真的没问题。
A:  Just hang on there a sec. Can I get a price check on " Fun Times Ribbed Condoms" please!稍等一下。请问能帮我查一下“Fun Times 螺纹避孕套”的价格吗？
