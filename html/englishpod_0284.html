Daily Life - Tune Up (C0284)

A:  Welcome to Al’s Garage. What seems to be the problem? 欢迎光临 Al 的修车厂。请问您的车出了什么问题？
B:  No problem at all! I am taking a long road trip and I want to make sure my car is in good mechanical condition. 完全没问题！我正准备进行一场长途旅行，想确保我的车况良好。
A:  Very wise decision. When was the last time you had a tune up? 您真明智。上次做保养是什么时候？
B:  Not that long ago, I think it was four months ago. 没太久，我想大概是四个月前吧。
A:  We usually recommend that you bring your car in every five thousand kilometers. 我们通常建议每行驶五千公里就进厂检查一次。
B:  Why? I mean, what exactly do you do to a car that you need to check it so often? 为什么？我是说，你们到底会对车子做什么检查，需要这么频繁？
A:  First of all, we change the motor oil and oil filter. If you don’t do this, it can cause your engine to wear faster and that means you would probably have to change the pistons and intake valves. 首先，我们会更换机油和机滤。如果不这样做，会导致发动机磨损加剧，您可能就得更换活塞和进气阀了。
B:  I see. What else? 我明白了。还有别的吗？
A:  We also check your spark plugs, fuel filter, and other oil levels such as hydraulic fluid. We also check the clutch and brakes to determine when you will need new ones. 我们还会检查火花塞、燃油滤清器以及其他油液液位，比如液压油。同时也会检查离合器和刹车片，以确定何时需要更换新件。
B:  Ok, well, when you put it that way, it doesn’t seem like a waste of time and money. 好吧，既然您这么一说，倒也不像是浪费时间或金钱。
A:  Trust me, regular tune ups will keep your car running smoothly and avoid break downs. 相信我，定期保养能让你的车运行顺畅，避免抛锚。
