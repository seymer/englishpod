The Weekend - Talking About Skincare (C0348)

A:  You want to go get a facial with me today?今天你想跟我一起去做个面部护理吗？
B:  Dude, what are you talking about? Only girls do that.哥们儿，你在说什么呢？只有女生才做那种事。
A:  Not at all, guys also get facials, manicures and pedicures. There is nothing wrong with looking after your skin and looking good.完全不是这样，男生也会做面部护理、美甲和足疗。好好保养皮肤、让自己看起来精神点没什么不对的。
B:  True. So what do they do to you at your beauty spa?确实。那你们在美容院会做什么？
A:  Well, first they exfoliate my face, getting rid of all the dead skin. Then I get a face mask with nutrients that keep my skin healthy and young. Afterwards, they apply some moisturizer and you leave feeling like a million bucks.嗯，首先他们会给我的脸去角质，去掉所有死皮。然后敷上富含营养的面膜，让肌肤保持健康年轻。之后涂上保湿霜，你会觉得自己价值连城。
B:  That doesn’t really sound like something I would be interested in. In any case, I just wash my face every night and use sunscreen during the day.这听起来真不像我会感兴趣的事。反正我每晚只洗脸，白天涂防晒霜就够了。
A:  Well you should come with me one day, I’m sure you’ll love it.那你哪天还是跟我一起去吧，我相信你会喜欢的。
B:  Uh... no.呃……不了。
