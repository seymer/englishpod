Daily Life - Shopping Online (C0207)

A:  What are you doing? 你在干嘛？
B:  I'm just looking for a nice pillow on Ebay. 我刚刚在 eBay 上找了一个不错的枕头。
A:  You are shopping for a pillow online? That's absurd! 你是在网上买枕头吗？这太荒谬了！
B:  Why? I don't have to leave the house or browse a dozen stores to find what I'm looking for. This way, I just search for it online quick and easy. 为什么？我不需要离开家或者逛十几家店就能找到我要的东西。这样我只需在网上快速轻松地搜索一下。
A:  I see, but how do you pay for it? How do you know you aren't going to be ripped off by the seller? 我明白了，但你怎么付款呢？你怎么知道不会被卖家骗？
B:  Well, the website handles a point system where if the seller does something wrong, people comment negatively and then you know that he or she may not be trustworthy. 嗯，网站有一个积分系统，如果卖家做错了什么，人们就会留下负面评论，这样你就知道他们可能不可信了。
A:  Wow, that sounds pretty safe. So how do you pay? Do you need a credit card? 哇，听起来还挺安全的。那你怎么付款？需要信用卡吗？
B:  You can use a credit card or your debit card. They also let you use the PayPal system which is really safe and fast. I have never had any problems with someone hacking my information or anything. 你可以用信用卡或者借记卡。他们还提供 PayPal 系统，既安全又快捷。我从来没遇到过有人盗取我的信息之类的麻烦事。
A:  Do you think I can find a sweater for my dog online? 你觉得我能在网上给我家狗找到一件毛衣吗？
B:  You can find anything! Are you sure you want to start shopping online though? Once you step into this world, there is no turning back! 你能买到任何东西！不过你确定要开始网购吗？一旦踏入这个世界，就再也回不去了！
A:  Let's do it! 我们开始吧！
