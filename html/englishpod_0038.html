Daily Life - Buying a Car(0038)

A:  Hi there, can I help you folks?您好，有什么可以帮您的吗？
B:  I'm just browsing; seeing what's on the lot. My daughter wants a car for her birthday, you know how it is.我只是随便看看，了解一下车场里有哪些车。我女儿想要一辆车作为生日礼物，您懂的。
C:  Dad! I'm sixteen already and I'm, like, the only one at school who doesn't have a car!爸爸！我都十六岁了，学校里就我一个人没有车！
A:  She is right, you know. Kids these days all have cars. Let me show you something we just got in: a 1996 sedan. Excellent gas mileage, it has dual airbags and anti lock brakes; a perfect vehicle for a young driver.她说得对，你知道的。现在的孩子都有车。让我给您看看我们刚到的货：1996 款轿车。油耗极低，配备双安全气囊和防抱死刹车系统；非常适合年轻驾驶员的完美座驾。
C:  Dad, I love it! It's awesome! Can we get this one please?爸爸，我太喜欢了！太棒了！我们能买下这辆车吗？
B:  I see... What can you tell me about this one?我懂了……能跟我说说这辆车的详情吗？
A:  Oh, that's just an old World War Two tank that we use for TV commercials. Now about this sedan...哦，那只是我们用来拍电视广告的一辆二战坦克。现在再说说这辆轿车……
B:  Whoa, whoa wait a minute. Tell me more about this tank.等等等等，先别走。多跟我讲讲这辆坦克。
A:  Well, Sir, if you are looking for quality and safety then look no further! Three inches of reinforced steel protect your daughter from short range missile attacks.先生，如果您追求的是品质与安全，那就看错地方了！三英寸的强化钢装甲能保护您的女儿免受短程导弹袭击。
B:  Does the sedan protect her from missile attacks?那轿车能保护她免受导弹袭击吗？
A:  It does not.不是的。
B:  Well, I don't know. Let me sleep on it.嗯，我不知道。让我再考虑一下。
A:  Did I mention the tank is a tank?我说过油箱是铁桶做的吗？
B:  I'll take it!我要了！
C:  Dad!爸爸！
