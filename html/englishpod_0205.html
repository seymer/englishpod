The Office - Closing The Deal (C0205)

Mr. Smith:  I’m glad you could find time to meet with me, Mr. Johnson. I can’t think of a nicer environment for our meeting today, the ambiance here is lovely! 很高兴您能抽出时间与我见面，约翰逊先生。我觉得今天没有比这里更合适的会面环境了，这里的氛围真不错！
Mr. Johnson:  No problem, if possible I always combine business with pleasure. Now, let’s hear more about these chocolates you’re offering. 没问题，如果可能的话，我总是将商务与休闲结合起来。那么，先听听关于您提供的这些巧克力的详情吧。
Mr. Smith:  Well, as you know, I have recently become the sole distributor for Grangers Gourmet Bon-bons here in the United States. They’re a new manufacturer and are looking to break into the luxury market. Naturally, your restaurant sprang into my mind immediately. I think your brand exemplifies many ofthe same traits as Grangers and serving these chocolates would really add to your reputation for providing elegant, luxurious, first class dining. 正如您所知道的，我最近已成为格兰杰美食糖果在美国的独家分销商。他们是一家新制造商，正试图打入高端市场。自然而然地，您的餐厅立刻浮现在我脑海中。我认为贵品牌体现了许多与格兰杰相同的特质，销售这些巧克力将真正提升您提供优雅、奢华、一流餐饮服务的声誉。
Mr. Johnson:  Hmmm, sounds interesting…. gourmet chocolates , where are they produced? Belgium? 嗯……听起来很有趣……高级巧克力，它们产自哪里？比利时？
Mr. Smith:  Actually, the factory is located in Scotland. 实际上，工厂位于苏格兰。
Mr. Johnson:  Really? I didn’t think they were known for their luxury chocolate production… 真的吗？我以为他们并不以生产高端巧克力而闻名……
Mr. Smith:  That’s what makes this such a fantastic opportunity! The government is one hundred percent supportive of creating new export markets and has guaranteed a low tariff for all wholesale orders of over one thousand units. They’ve also reduced the red tape involved at customs as well. Here, I brought these especially for you, try one! 这正是如此绝佳的机会所在！政府百分之百支持开拓新的出口市场，并为所有超过一千单位的批发订单保证低关税。他们还简化了海关手续。这些是我特意为您带来的，请尝一个！
Mr. Johnson:  Oh, thanks. Mmm, hmm, creamy texture, smooth…. 哦，谢谢。嗯，口感绵密，顺滑……
Mr. Smith:  Unique aren’t they? I bet you’ve never tasted anything like it! Quality is assured as I personally visit the factory to make sure no one’s cutting corners with the ingredients. Only the crème de la crème make it through inspection. 它们很独特吧？我敢打赌你从未尝过这样的味道！品质绝对有保障，因为我亲自去工厂检查，确保没人会在原料上偷工减料。只有最顶尖的才能通过检验。
Mr. Johnson:  Yes, very interesting flavors……. Slightly spicy, very unique, that’s for sure. Exactly what ARE the ingredients? 是的，风味非常独特……微辣，确实独一无二。这绝对没错。到底有哪些配料？
Mr. Smith:  I have it on highest authority that this traditional secret recipe has been handed down inthe Granger family for generations. I’m sure you can keep a secret. Buttermilk, cacao beans, sugar and Haggis. 我有最高权威保证，这个传统秘方已在格兰杰家族传承数代。我相信你能守口如瓶。黄油牛奶、可可豆、糖和哈吉斯。
Mr. Johnson:  Haggis? What’s Haggis? 哈吉斯？那是什么？
Mr. Smith:  It’s a traditional Scottish delicacy; you take sheep’s liver, heart and lung and stuff it inside ofthe sheep’s stomach. 这是一种传统的苏格兰美食；把羊肝、心脏和肺塞进羊胃里制成。
Mr. Johnson:  Ah, get back to you. 啊，你搞错了。
Mr. Smith:  Mr. Johnson? Mr. Johnson? 约翰逊先生？约翰逊先生？

Supplementary Vocabulary

Angel Investors天使投资人Individuals who back emerging entrepreneurial ventures, usually as a bridge to get from the self-funded stage to the level of business that would both need and attract venture capital.支持新兴创业企业的个人，通常作为从自筹资金阶段进入需要并吸引风险资本的业务水平的桥梁。
Joint Venture合资企业A legal entity created by two or more businesses joining together to conduct a specific business enterprise with both parties sharing profits and losses.由两个或两个以上企业联合成立的法人实体，共同经营特定的商业企业，双方共享利润和亏损。
Venture Capital风险投资A form of financing for a company in which the business gives up partial ownership and control of the business in exchange for capital over a limited time frame, usually 3-5 years.公司的一种融资形式，企业放弃部分所有权和控制权，以换取有限期限（通常为 3-5 年）的资本。
Merger合并A joining together of two previously separate corporations.两家以前独立的公司合并在一起。
Audit审计an evaluation of a person, organization, system, process, enterprise, project or product.对个人、组织、系统、过程、企业、项目或产品的评估。
