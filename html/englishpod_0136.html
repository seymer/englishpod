Global View - Gambling(0136)

A:  Did you hear? The state is thinking of legalizing gambling in our city! Soon we are gonna have amazing hotels and casinos here which will be good for our business!你听说了吗？州政府正在考虑在我们城市合法化赌博！很快我们这里就会有惊人的酒店和赌场，这对我们的生意大有裨益！
B:  Are you serious? Gambling is a vice industry built on deception and fed by the intentional exploitation of human weakness for the sole purpose of monetary gain! It disgusts me.你是认真的吗？赌博是一个建立在欺骗之上的罪恶产业，它利用人性的弱点谋取金钱利益！这让我感到恶心。
A:  What are you talking about? How does it exploit people?你在说什么？它是怎么剥削人的？
B:  Well, to begin with, Gambling is addictive, ruins marriages, destroys families and bankrupts communities. Once you are addicted it is very difficult to stop. People have lost their houses, cars and been left out on the street after becoming addicted. Secondly, it exploits because men become addicted to gambling most often because of the action and risk. Women gamble to escape, and senior citizens will start gambling for the social interaction. Underage gamblers often start gambling on sports with friends and then illegal bookies.首先，赌博会上瘾，毁掉婚姻，摧毁家庭，让社区破产。一旦上瘾就很难戒掉。人们因为上瘾而失去了房子、车子，最后流落街头。其次，它之所以能剥削人，是因为男性最常因行动和冒险而上瘾；女性赌博是为了逃避现实，老年人则是为了社交互动。未成年赌徒往往先和朋友一起参与体育博彩，然后转向非法庄家。
A:  Geez! Now that I think about it, maybe legalizing gambling isn't such a good idea! Although, I have been to Las Vegas, and I didn't become addicted or anything like that.天哪！这么一想，也许合法化赌博并不是什么好主意！虽然我去过拉斯维加斯，但我并没有上瘾或类似的问题。
B:  You cannot predict who will become addicted to gambling. Now excuse me, I have a protest rally to organize!你无法预测谁会染上赌博瘾。现在请原谅我，我得去组织一场抗议集会了！
