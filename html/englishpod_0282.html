Global View - Physics (C0282)

Prof. Brown:  Good morning, everybody. Welcome to Physics 101. My name is Ed Brown, and I will be your professor for this semester. Since today is our first class, I wanted to give you an overview of what this course will look like, how you will be graded, and what we will cover this semester. 大家早上好，欢迎来到物理 101。我是 Ed Brown，本学期你们的教授。因为今天是我们的第一节课，我想给大家介绍一下这门课的大致内容、评分方式以及本学期的学习安排。
Matt:  Will we be focusing more on theoretical physics or experimental physics, Professor? 我们会更侧重理论物理还是实验物理呢，教授？
Prof. Brown:  This is an introductory course, and my aim is to give you a broad overview of the field of physics. The term “ physics” encompasses many different areas of research and study, and I hope this course will provide you with conceptual understanding of physics, which will prove useful whether or not you choose to further your study in this field. 这是一门入门课程，我的目标是让大家对物理学领域有个全面的概览。“物理学”这个词涵盖了许多不同的研究和学科方向，我希望这门课能帮助大家建立起对物理学的概念性理解，无论你们之后是否选择继续深造这个领域，这些知识都会很有用。
Prof. Brown:  We will begin the course by looking at the fundamental concepts of physics, then by the middle of the semester we will begin exploring the more theoretical side of physics. It is essential that you first have a firm grasp of the fundamentals, so that you can better understand the theoretical concepts when we get to them. 课程将从物理学的基本概念开始，到学期中段时，我们将开始探索物理学中更具理论性的部分。首先扎实掌握基础至关重要，这样当学到那些理论概念时，你们才能更好地理解。
Matt:  Will we learn about black holes, wormholes, and string theory? 我们会学习黑洞、虫洞和弦论吗？
Prof. Brown:  We will learn about the general theory of relativity, including black holes. We will also explore developing theories in quantum mechanics, such as string theory. We will discuss some hypothetical features of space-time, like wormholes. 我们将学习广义相对论，包括黑洞。我们还将探索量子力学中正在发展的理论，比如弦理论。我们会讨论时空的一些假设性特征，比如虫洞。
Prof. Brown:  We will also explore some of the more influential developments in the fields of thermodynamics, electromagnetism, and nuclear physics, all of which have had significant impacts on modern life. Now, I am going to have the TAs pass out the syllabus for this class, so you can see how this course will be graded. 我们还将探讨热力学、电磁学和核物理学领域中一些更具影响力的发展成果，这些领域都对现代生活产生了重大影响。现在，我要请助教分发这门课的 syllabus（课程大纲），这样你们就能了解这门课是如何评分的。
Matt:  Oh man, looks like this isn’t gonna be the easy A I thought it’d be! 天哪，看来这可不是我以为的那种轻松拿 A 的课程！
