The Office - Interview Skills 3 - Education Background (C0241)

Mr. Parsons:  Now, if I look here I see that you completed a BA in English? 现在，如果我看这里，我看到你完成了一个英语学士学位？
Rebecca:  Yes, that's right. After graduating from high school in New York I attended York University in the UK. My major was English, and my minor was business studies . I completed my BA in 2004. 是的，没错。高中毕业后我在纽约读完书，然后去了英国的约克大学。我的主修是英语，副修是商业研究。我于 2004 年完成了本科学位。
Mr. Parsons:  Yes, I'm pleased to see that you also got a distinction. 是的，很高兴看到你获得了优异等级。
Rebecca:  Yes that's right. I've always enjoyed studying. My friends say I'm a bit of a bookworm, but my father always pushed us to succeed academically. 是的，没错。我一直很喜欢学习。朋友们说我有点书呆子气，但我父亲一直督促我们要在学业上取得成功。
Mr. Parsons:  Well, it looks like his encouragement paid off Rebecca. So how about extra-curricular activities at University嗯，看来他的鼓励确实奏效了，丽贝卡。那你在大学期间参加过哪些课外活动呢？
Rebecca:  Well I've always been keen on on writing, so I became the editor for the University student magazine, which I really loved. Also I volunteered for a group called Shelter, to help the homeless in York. 嗯，我一直对写作很感兴趣，所以我就成了校刊的编辑，这真的让我很开心。此外，我还为一个名为“庇护所”的组织做志愿者，帮助约克市的无家可归者。
Mr. Parsons:  What did that involve? 具体是做什么的呢？
Rebecca:  Providing warm meals and shelter, especially in the winter months . I found it really fulfilling to be part of that group . 提供热食和住所，尤其是在冬季月份。成为那个组织的一员让我觉得非常有意义。
Mr. Parsons:  I'm sure. Okay, now let's move on to your work experience, shall we? 我敢肯定。好的，现在我们接着谈谈你的工作经历，好吗？
Rebecca:  Yes, okay. 好的，没问题。
