The Office - Interview Skills 9 - Asking For Information About The Position (C0259)

Rebecca:  Could you tell me a little about the organization? For example, how big is your workforce here? 能请您介绍一下贵组织的情况吗？比如，你们这里的员工规模有多大？
Mr. Parsons:  Well in total we have around 150 employees based in Hong Kong with another 400 in our head office in Beijing. The newsdesk staff in Hong Kong comprises around 80 staff. 嗯，我们总共有大约 150 名员工驻守香港，另外在北京总部还有 400 人。香港的新闻部团队由约 80 名员工组成。
Rebecca:  I see. And how about the working hours? 明白了。那工作时间呢？
Mr. Parsons:  Well, as you know Rebecca, in journalism work hours are not exactly 9-5. You could be on call at any time. We do have to work very unsociable hours at times.嗯，如您所知，Rebecca，在新闻行业里，工作时间并不完全是朝九晚五。您可能随时需要待命。有时我们确实得在非正常时间工作。
Rebecca:  Well I am used to that Mr. Parsons, so that's not really a shock for me.没关系，Mr. Parsons，我早就习惯了，这对我来说没什么冲击。
Mr. Parsons:  Good, as long as you understand that. 很好，只要您理解这一点就行。
Rebecca:  And when do you need to fill the vacancy, Mr. Parsons? 那什么时候需要填补这个空缺，Mr. Parsons？
Mr. Parsons:  Yesterday! But no, we are hoping to start from the beginning of next month.昨天！不过不，我们希望从下个月一开始就着手。
Rebecca:  That sounds ideal. 听起来很理想。
