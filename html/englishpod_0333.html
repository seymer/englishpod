B:  The brain!大脑！
A:  That’s right the brain! It serves as a control center for the body, handling the processes of the central nervous system as well as cognition. Then what major organ is in our chest?没错，就是大脑！它作为身体的控制中心，负责处理中枢神经系统的各种功能以及认知活动。那么位于我们胸部的另一个主要器官是什么？
B:  The heart!心脏！
A:  Very good! It pumps blood throughout the body, using the circulatory system such as blood vessels and veins. Now let’s not forget that our lungs provide oxygen to our heart and body to keep us alive! Now what about the organs that help us digest food?非常好！它将血液泵送到全身，借助循环系统如血管和静脉来完成这一过程。别忘了我们的肺部为心脏和身体提供氧气，让我们得以存活！那还有哪些器官帮助我们消化食物？
B:  The stomach and intestines!胃和肠道！
A:  Very good! Let’s not forget that the stomach is the one that breaks down our food and our intestines process that food and then expel the waste. Are we forgetting anything?很好！别忘了胃负责分解食物，而肠道则处理这些食物并最终排出废物。我们是不是漏掉了什么？
B:  Yeah! Our kidneys, liver and bladder!有！肾脏、肝脏和膀胱！
A:  Oh yes, you are right. Very important organs indeed.哦，没错，你说得对。这些都是非常重要的器官。
