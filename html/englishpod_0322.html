The Office - Small Talk 6 - Talking About Yourself (C0322)

Michelle:  Excuse me, is this seat taken?不好意思，请问这个座位有人吗？
Stranger:  No, please feel free.没人，请随意。
Michelle:  Thanks a lot.太感谢了。
Stranger:  Do you work in Shanghai?你在上海工作吗？
Michelle:  Yes I do. How about you?是的，我在。你呢？
Stranger:  No, I'm a tourist. This place is amazing! It's much bigger than I imagined, and much more exciting! There's so much to see here.不是，我是个游客。这里太棒了！比我想象中大得多，也刺激得多！这里有很多可看的东西。
Michelle:  You can say that again! It's much more modern than people imagine. Where are you from?你这么说没错！这里比人们想象的要现代得多。你是哪里人？
Stranger:  Um, well let's see.....I'm from Kansas originally. A much quieter and more peaceful place than here, that's for sure!嗯，让我想想……我原本来自堪萨斯州。那里肯定比这里安静、平和得多！
Michelle:  Uh huh....哦……
Stranger:  But I'm living in Paris right now.不过我现在住在巴黎。
Michelle:  Oh Paris! Wonderful, I'd love to visit some time!哦巴黎！太棒了，我真想有空去逛逛！
