The Weekend - At the Deli (C0215)

A:  Honey, we are all out of wine and cheese. Do you mind running to the deli and picking up a few things? 亲爱的，家里的酒和奶酪都买光了。你介意去熟食店买点东西吗？
B:  Can't it wait? I'm watching the game right now! 等不了吗？我正在看比赛呢！
A:  Your friends and family are coming over tonight and we still need to get a lot of things. 今晚你的朋友和家人要来，我们还得买很多东西。
B:  Fine! What do you need? 行吧！你需要什么？
A:  Ok, pick up some cured meats to go with the wine. Maybe a pound of polish sausages, ham, liverwurst, salami and any other cold cuts that are on sale. I think I saw a promotion for pastrami. Also get some cole slaw and a jar of olives. 好，买点腌肉配着酒。大概要一磅波兰香肠、火腿、肝酱、萨拉米肠，还有任何打折的冷切肉。我记得看到熏牛肉有促销活动。再买点凉拌卷心菜和一瓶橄榄。
B:  Whoa wait a minute! Isn't that a bit too much? I mean, how much is all of this going to cost! 哇等等！这会不会太多了？我是说，这一大堆得花多少钱啊！
A:  Never mind that. Get some dips as well. Get a jar of spinach and blue cheese dip and also some Tzatziki. If they have bean dip get that as well. Last but not least, get some pickles. 别管价格了。还要买些蘸酱。要一罐菠菜蓝纹奶酪蘸酱，还有塔希基酱。如果有豆泥也买一份。最后别忘了买点酸黄瓜。
B:  Is that all, your majesty? 就这些吗，陛下？
A:  Very funny! Get a move on! People will be here any minute. 真逗！快点儿吧！客人随时都会到。
