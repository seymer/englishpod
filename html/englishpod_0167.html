Daily Life - Learning How To Drive (C0167)

A:  Ok! I'm ready for my driving lesson! Should I start the car? 好！我准备好上驾驶课了！我该发动汽车了吗？
B:  Hold on there Fittipaldi, first let's go over things one more time. Now before you even think of starting the car, make sure your seat is at a comfortable position and you can grip the steering wheel firmly. Next check your rear view mirrors to make sure you can see properly. 慢着，菲蒂帕尔迪，我们先再复习一遍。在你甚至想到要发动汽车之前，确保你的座椅调整到舒适的位置，并且能稳稳握住方向盘。接下来检查后视镜，确保视野清晰。
A:  We have been through this a million times! Let's get going already! I'm ready! 我们说过无数遍了！赶紧出发吧！我准备好了！
B:  Fine start the car. Now gently step on the clutch and shift to 1st gear. Good, now accelerate gently and let go of the clutch as you do it. There we go, good! 好，发动汽车。现在轻轻踩下离合器并挂入一挡。很好，接着轻踩油门，在加速的同时松开离合器。就是这样，不错！
A:  I'm doing it! I'm driving! This is awesome! Le'ts turn some music on! 我会了！我在开车了！太棒了！放点音乐听听吧！
B:  Keep your eyes on the road! No music! We are coming up to a red light, step on the brakes. What are you doing? I said the brakes! Look out for those people! Get off the sidewalk! 眼睛盯着路面！不许听音乐！我们要到红灯路口了，踩刹车。你在干什么？我说的是刹车！小心那些人！别走人行道！
A:  Get out of my way! This is just like playing video games! 让开！这跟玩电子游戏一样！
B:  It's the police! Pull over! 是警察！靠边停车！
A:  They'll never take me alive! 他们绝不会让我活着离开！

Key Vocabulary

grip紧握hold, reach抓住，到达
clutch离合器the pedal that you press before you change gear换档前按下的踏板
sidewalk人行道the part of the road where people walk人们行走的道路部分
steering wheel方向盘the wheel which the driver holds in a car驾驶员在汽车中持有的车轮
pull over靠边停车to move a car to the side of the road and stop将汽车移到路边并停下来

Supplementary Vocabulary

seatbelt安全带a belt in a car fastened around the midsection to keep the person safely secured against the seat汽车中系在中间部分的安全带，可将人安全地固定在座椅上
speed limit限速the maximum speed allowed on the road道路上允许的最高速度
intersection十字路口a place where two or more major roads meet两条或更多条主要道路交汇的地方
traffic light交通信号灯a set of electrically operated signal lights used to direct or control traffic at intersections一组电动信号灯，用于指挥或控制十字路口的交通
yield sign让行标志a road sign in the shape of an upside-down triangle, meaning to slow down and prepare to stop if necessary倒三角形的路标，意思是减速并准备在必要时停车
