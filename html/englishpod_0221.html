Daily Life - Car Trouble (C0221)

A:  Car trouble center. How may I help you? 汽车故障服务中心。请问有什么可以帮您？
B:  My car won't start! Stupid old car! 我的车发动不了！这破车！
A:  Hold on, before you kick your car let's go through some possible problems. 稍等，在您踢车之前，我们先排查一下可能的问题。
B:  Fine. 行吧。
A:  Ok, first of all, can you turn the key in the ignition? 好的，首先，您能转动点火钥匙吗？
B:  Yeah! I am here with my friend and he thinks it may be the spark plug or the starter motor. 能！我和朋友在一起，他认为是火花塞或启动马达出了问题。
A:  Those are possible problems, but tell me, when you turn the key, do you hear the starter motor crank? 这些确实是可能的问题，但请告诉我，当您转动钥匙时，能听到启动马达运转的声音吗？
B:  Yeah, it sounds like it usually does when I start the car, but nothing else happens. The engine won't start. Should I maybe press the accelerator? 能，听起来和平时发动汽车时一样，但除此之外什么都没发生。发动机就是无法启动。我应该踩一下油门吗？
A:  No. If you step on the accelerator pedal you can flood the carburetor and your car will never start. 不。如果您踩下油门踏板，会导致化油器淹缸，车子就再也发动不了了。
B:  So what do you think it is? 那您觉得问题出在哪里呢？
A:  I know this may seem like a silly question, but does your car have gasoline? 我知道这听起来像个傻问题，但你的车里有汽油吗？
B:  Umm. yeah! Right! I got the car started! Thanks for your help! I told you to fill the tank! 嗯。有！对！我把车发动起来了！谢谢你的帮助！我早就让你加满油了！
