Daily Life - Turn left here!(0016)

A:  Hurry up, get in!快点，上车！
B:  I'm in , let's go!我上来了，走吧！
A:  OK, make a left here...no wait, I meant make a right. Come on speed up!好，在这里左转……不对，我是说右转。加快速度！
B:  Geeze! What's the rush?天哪！这么着急干嘛？
A:  Don't worry about it, just drive. Oh, no, the light is about to change... step on it!别担心，只管开车。哦不，灯快变了……踩油门！
B:  Are you nuts? I'm not going to run a red light!你疯了吗？我可不会闯红灯！
A:  Whatever. Just turn right here. The freeway will be packed at this hour.... let's take a side street. Go on! Get out of our way! Move, move!随便你。就在这里右转吧。这会儿高速公路肯定堵死了……我们走小路吧。快点！让开！闪开！
B:  What's your problem? Geez. Having a fit is not going to help!你什么意思啊？天哪。发火可帮不上忙！
A:  Here, I know a short cut... just go down here and we'll cut through Ashburn Heights. Let's go, let's go! Watch out for that lady!这儿我知道一条近路……直接往下走，穿过阿什伯恩高地。走吧，走吧！小心那位女士！
B:  I'm going as fast as I can!我已经尽力了！
A:  Yes! We made it. 5:58, just before the library closes.是的！我们成功了。五点五十八分，就在图书馆关门前一刻。
B:  You're such a geek!你真是个书呆子！
