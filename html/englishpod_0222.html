Global View - Carbon Footprint (C0222)

A:  So what's your guys' take on all this global warming hysteria in the media? 那你们怎么看媒体里关于全球变暖的那些炒作？
B:  It's pretty serious, man. There have been tons of scientific studies and the scientific community says that the earth is heating up. We need to make some drastic changes to our lifestyle if we want to preserve our planet. 挺严重的，伙计。已经有很多科学研究了，科学界都说地球正在升温。如果我们想保住这个星球，就得对生活方式做出巨大改变。
A:  I don't know. It sounds like a bunch of mumbo jumbo if you ask me. "Save the earth!" The earth will save itself. It's survived worst disasters in the past. I mean, honestly, we live in the boonies. There's no way anyone here is ever going to walk or bike to work, especially in the winter. And we have no bus system. My house is forty years old and it would take a lot of money to get it refitted to be "green" and "energy-efficient". 我不知道。要是我说的话，这听起来就是一堆胡扯。“拯救地球！”地球会自己救自己的。它以前经历过更糟糕的灾难。老实说，我们住在偏远的地方。这里没人能走路或骑车上班，尤其是冬天。而且我们根本没有公交系统。我家房子四十岁了，要想把它改造成“环保”和“节能”的，得花一大笔钱。
C:  Well I don't really know if I believe in global warming either, or whether or not it was our doing or a natural change the earth is going through, but you have to admit that we're living pretty irresponsibly here in the west. 嗯，说实话我也不确定自己是否相信全球变暖，也不确定这是我们的错还是地球正在经历的自然变化，但你必须承认，我们在西方确实活得太不负责任了。
A:  I guess... 我想……
C:  I think the issue at hand is sustainability. We've only got this one earth we can live on, and our resources are quickly disappearing because of our own carelessness and our inability to think of anyone but ourselves and anything but the present. 我认为当务之急是可持续性。我们只有一个赖以生存的地球，而由于我们自己的疏忽和只想着自己、只关注眼前的短视行为，我们的资源正迅速消失。
B:  So, like I was saying, we need to change the way we live. We need to reduce our carbon footprint. 所以，就像我说的，我们需要改变我们的生活方式。我们需要减少碳足迹。
C:  But it doesn't have to be that drastic. Hybrid vehicles and solar panels are too expensive to be feasible right now. And we don't have to be hippies living off the land and buying everything organic either, though it helps. 但事情不必那么激烈。混合动力汽车和太阳能电池板太昂贵，目前不可行。我们也不必成为靠土地为生的嬉皮士，也不必购买有机食品，尽管这有帮助。
B:  I car pool to work everyday with some buddies of mine. I have a rain barrel outside my house I use to water my plants and my lawn in the summer, and I make sure I always bring reusable bags with me when I get my groceries. And we just started using bio-degradable plastic made from corn oil for take-out orders at my family's restaurant. Remember the three R's? Reduce. Reuse. Recycle. 我每天和我的一些朋友拼车去上班。我的房子外面有一个雨水桶，夏天我用它来浇灌我的植物和草坪，而且我确保在买杂货时总是随身携带可重复使用的袋子。我们刚刚开始在我家的餐厅使用由玉米油制成的可生物降解塑料来制作外卖订单。还记得三个R吗？减少。重复使用。回收。
C:  Exactly, it's just small simple changes, like buying energy-saving light bulbs, starting a compost bin, recycling bottles and papers, using reusable water bottles, stop using disposable cups and cutlery. 确切地说，这只是一些简单的小改变，比如购买节能灯泡、启动堆肥箱、回收瓶子和纸张、使用可重复使用的水瓶、停止使用一次性杯子和餐具。
A:  Like the ones we're drinking out of? 就像我们喝的那些一样？
B:  Yeah. 是的。
