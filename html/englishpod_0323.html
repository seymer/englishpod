Daily Life - Cancelled Flight (C0323)

A:  Good afternoon Sir, may I please see your passport and reservation? 下午好，先生，请问可以看一下您的护照和预订单吗？
B:  Here you go. 给你。
A:  I’m sorry sir, this flight has been cancelled due to some mechanical problems. 抱歉先生，由于一些机械故障，这趟航班已取消。
B:  Cancelled! So what am I supposed to do now? 取消了！那我该怎么办？
A:  We apologize for any inconveniences that may be caused by this. If your flight is urgent, I can put you on a waiting list for another flight this evening, but it’s on a first come first served basis, so there is no guarantee that you will be able to take that flight. 对于由此可能造成的不便我们深表歉意。如果您的行程紧急，我可以将您列入今晚另一航班的候补名单，但这是先到先得的原则，因此无法保证您能赶上那班飞机。
B:  What’s my other option? 我还有什么其他选择？
A:  If you can wait until tomorrow, we will put youup in a hotel for today and you can take scheduled flight for tomorrow morning. 如果您愿意等到明天，我们可以为您安排今天的酒店住宿，并让您乘坐明早的航班。
B:  That’s fine. I’ll do that then. 没问题，那我就这么办。
A:  Thank you for your understanding sir. I will book your flight now. 感谢您的理解先生，我现在就为您预订航班。
