Global View - All Saints Day (C0158)

C:  The Day of the Dead has arrived All Soul's Dayand All Saint’s Day!亡灵节到了，万灵节和诸圣日也来了！
A:  Your neighbor is crazy. Why is he screaming that?你邻居疯了吧？他在喊什么？
B:  Because today is the first of November the Day of the Dead因为今天是 11 月 1 日，亡灵节。
A:  Oh, that's right.哦，对啊。
B:  This is a very special day among many cultures around the world especially in Latin America 这是全球许多文化中非常特别的一天，尤其在拉丁美洲更是如此。
A:  Seriously? I thought it was just like any other day, except for the fact that people visit the cemetery and remember their loved ones.真的吗？我以为这跟其他日子没什么两样，除了人们会去墓地缅怀亲人。
B:  Well, that's just part of it People across the world celebrate in different waysIn Mexcio for example it's common to see people building private altars honoring the deceasedusing sugar skulls, preparing the favorite foods and beverages of the departed and visiting graves with these as gifts. In the Philippines , the tombs are cleaned or repainted, candles are lit and flowers are offered Entire families camp out in cemeteries .and sometimes spend a night or two near their relatives' tombs!嗯，那只是其中一部分。世界各地的人们以不同方式庆祝。比如在墨西哥，常见人们搭建私人祭坛悼念逝者，用糖骷髅、准备逝者生前喜爱的食物和饮品，并带着这些礼物扫墓。在菲律宾，人们会清理或重新粉刷坟墓，点燃蜡烛，献上鲜花。整个家庭会在墓地露营，有时甚至会在亲人墓旁住上一两晚！
A:  Whoa! That'sscary! I don't know if I could do that!哇！好可怕！我不知道我能不能做到！
B:  Why? We should fear the living, not the dead .为什么？我们应该敬畏生者，而非死者。
