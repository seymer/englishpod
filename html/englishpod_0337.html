Global View - The Night Before Christmas (C0337)

Twas the night before Christmas, when all through the house那是圣诞前夜，整个房子里
Not a creature was stirring, not even a mouse; 连只老鼠都没在动；
The stockings were hung bythe chimney with care, 长袜被小心翼翼地挂在烟囱旁，
In hopes that St. Nicholas soon would be there; 希望圣尼古拉斯能很快到来；
The children were nestled all snug in their beds, 孩子们安稳地蜷缩在被窝里，
And mama in her 'kerchief, and I in my cap, 妈妈系着头巾，我戴着睡帽，
Had just settled down for a long winter's nap, 刚要躺下享受漫长的冬日小憩，
When out onthe lawn there arose such a clatter, 忽然草坪上传来一阵喧闹声，
I sprang from the bed to see what wasthe matter. 我从床上跳起来查看出了什么事。
Away tothe window I flew like a flash, 我像一道闪电般冲向窗户，
Tore open the shutters and threw up the sash. 推开百叶窗，掀起玻璃窗。
The moon onthe breast ofthe new-fallen snow新雪覆盖的胸膛上，明月高悬，
Gave the lustre of mid-day to objects below, 将正午的光辉洒向下方万物，
When, what to my wondering eyes should appear, 正当我惊愕凝视之际，
But a miniature sleigh, and eight tiny reindeer, 竟见一辆迷你雪橇，伴着八只小驯鹿，
With a little old driver, so lively and quick, 一位老车夫驾坐其上，活泼迅捷，
I knew in a moment it must be St. Nick. 刹那间我便认出，那定是圣诞圣尼古拉斯。
More rapid than eagles his coursers they came, 他的坐骑如鹰般疾驰而来，
And he whistled, and shouted, and called them by name; 他吹口哨、呼喊、逐个呼唤它们的名字：
" Now, Dasher! now, Dancer! now, Prancer and Vixen! “现在，达舍！现在，丹瑟！现在，普兰瑟和维克森！”
On, Comet! on Cupid! on, Donder and Blitzen! 快，彗星！快，丘比特！快，雷电与闪电！
Tothe top ofthe porch! to the top ofthe wall! 冲到门廊顶端！冲到墙头！
Now dash away! dash away! dash away all! 现在全速飞奔！全速飞奔！全部冲出去！
As dry leaves that before the wild hurricane fly, 就像狂风中飞舞的枯叶，
When they meet with an obstacle, mount tothe sky, 一旦遇到阻碍便直冲云霄，
So up to the house-top the coursers they flew, 于是那群骏马腾空而起，飞向屋顶，
With the sleigh full of toys, and St. Nicholas too. 雪橇里装满了玩具，还有圣尼古拉斯也在其中。
And then, in a twinkling, I heard onthe roof转眼间，我听见屋顶上响起阵阵马蹄声，
The prancing and pawing of each little hoof. 那是每只小蹄子轻快跳跃与刨地的声音。
As I drew in my head, and was turning around, 当我缩回脑袋，转身之际，
Down the chimney St. Nicholas came with a bound. 圣诞老人顺着烟囱蹦蹦跳跳地下来了。
He was dressed all in fur, from his head to his foot, 他从头到脚一身皮袄，
And his clothes were all tarnished with ashes and soot; 衣服上沾满了灰烬和烟灰；
A bundle of toys he had flung on his back, 背上捆着一堆玩具，
And he looked like a peddler just opening his pack. 看起来就像个刚打开货箱的小贩。
His eyes -- how they twinkled! his dimples how merry! 他的眼睛多么闪亮！酒窝多么欢快！
His cheeks were like roses, his nose like a cherry! 脸颊像玫瑰，鼻子像樱桃！
His droll little mouth was drawn up like a bow, 他那滑稽的小嘴咧得像张弓，
Andthe beard of his chin was as white asthe snow; 下巴上的胡须白如雪花；
The stump of a pipe he held tight in his teeth, 嘴里紧紧叼着半截烟斗，
Andthe smoke it encircled his head like a wreath; 烟雾缭绕着他，像花环般环绕他的头；
He had a broad face and a little round belly, 他脸庞宽阔，肚子圆滚滚的，
That shook, when he laughed like a bowlful of jelly. 一笑起来，那肚子晃啊晃，就像装满果冻的碗。
He was chubby and plump, a right jolly old elf, 他又胖又壮，是个十足快乐的老精灵，
And I laughed when I saw him, in spite of myself; 我一见到他就忍不住笑，尽管我自己也控制不住；
A wink of his eye and a twist of his head, 他眨眨眼，扭扭头，
Soon gave me to know I had nothing to dread; 很快我就明白没什么好怕的；
He spoke not a word, but went straight to his work, 他没说一句废话，径直开始干活，
And filled allthe thestockings; then turned with a jerk, 把满屋子的袜子都塞得满满当当；然后猛地一转身，
And laying his finger aside of his nose, 把手指从鼻子旁移开——
And giving a nod, upthe chimney he rose; 他点点头，顺着烟囱向上爬；
He sprang to his sleigh, to his team gave a whistle, 他跳上雪橇，向马群吹了一声口哨，
And away they all flew like the down of a thistle. 他们便像蓟花的绒毛般飞走了。
But I heard him exclaim, ere he drove out of sight, 但在他驶出视线前，我听见他喊道：
" Christmas to all, and to all a good-night. “祝大家圣诞快乐，也祝大家晚安。”
