The Weekend - Bachelor Party (C0155)

A:  Hi honey! You'll never guess what! My friends Julie and Alex are getting married! 嗨亲爱的！你绝对猜不到什么！我的朋友们朱莉和亚历克斯要结婚了！
B:  Wow that's great news! They're a great couple! 哇，这真是个好消息！他们是一对绝配！
A:  I know! Anyways I just talked to Alex's best man and he is organizing the bachelor party It's gonna be gonna be so much fun! All the groomsmen are thinking up all the wacky and crazythings we are going to do that night. 没错！话说回来，我刚跟亚历克斯的伴郎聊过，他正在筹备单身派对。那一定会超级有趣！所有伴郎都在绞尽脑汁想些疯狂又搞怪的活动，就为了那天晚上能玩得尽兴。
B:  You aren't going to a strip club are you? I don't want you getting a lap dance from some stripper with the excuse that it's your friends party. 你不会是要去脱衣舞俱乐部吧？我可不想你以“这是朋友聚会”为借口，让某个脱衣女郎给你跳肚皮舞。
A:  Aw come on! It's just some innocent fun! You know how these things are! We are gonna play drinking games, get him some gag gifts and just have a good time.Nothing too over the top . 拜托啦！那只是些无害的娱乐活动嘛！你知道这类聚会的规矩！我们会玩喝酒游戏、送些搞怪礼物，大家痛痛快快地玩一场。绝对不过分。
B:  Well, I don't know.嗯……我还是不太确定。
A:  Come on! If one of your friends was getting married I wouldn't mind you going to her bachelorette party!拜托！要是你有个朋友要结婚，我也不会介意你去参加她的单身派对啊！
B:  Good,because my friend Wendy is getting married and I'm organizing her party!太好了，因为我有个朋友温迪要结婚，我正在筹备她的派对呢！
A:  What!什么？
