Daily Life - Returning A Product (C0299)

A:  Hi I would like to return this TV.嗨，我想退这台电视。
B:  Sure, do you have the receipt?好的，你有收据吗？
A:  Yeah here you go. Actually I also want to return this keyboard.有的，给你。其实我还想退这个键盘。
B:  Ok, may I ask what is the reason for returning these products?行，请问您退货的原因是什么？
A::  The TV flickers a lot when I am watching a movie and at times the image is not very clear.这台电视看大片时闪烁得很厉害，有时候画面也不清晰。
B:  I see, and what about the keyboard?我明白了，那键盘呢？
A:  I spilled some coffee on it and now it won’t work.我把它弄洒了咖啡，现在没法用了。
B:  I am sorry sir, but we can only exchange or refund defective products, we cannot take responsibility for misuse or damages.抱歉先生，我们只能退换有缺陷的产品，对于人为损坏或误用我们无法负责。
A:  Fine! I don’t know why they make these things so delicate anyways.好吧！我真不知道他们为什么要把这些东西做得这么脆弱。
