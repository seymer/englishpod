Daily Life - Dinnerware (C0248)

A:  Honey can you set the table? 亲爱的，能摆一下餐桌吗？
B:  Um, sure. What are we having for dinner? Do I need to put out anything in particular? 嗯，好的。我们今晚吃什么？需要我特意摆放什么吗？
A:  Well, make sure to put out the pepper and salt shakers. I don't know if your brother is coming tonight so set an extra place mat just in case. 嗯，记得把胡椒瓶和盐瓶摆出来。我不知道你哥哥今晚会不会来，所以先多摆一个餐垫以防万一。
B:  Ok, should I use the fancy silverware? 好的，我该用那套精致的餐具吗？
A:  Yeah go ahead, forks, spoons and knives. I roasted some meat so be sure to put out some steak knives as well. 对，用吧，叉子、勺子和刀子。我烤了些肉，所以别忘了也摆上牛排刀。
B:  I'll also set some cups and saucers for some coffee after dinner. 晚饭后我还得摆好杯子和茶托，准备喝点咖啡。
A:  Honey? Have you seen our soup bowls? 亲爱的？你看见我们的汤碗了吗？
B:  They are in the cupboard where you keep the gravy boat and serving dishes. Just be careful because the wine glasses are also there. 它们在橱柜里，就放着你装酱汁船和盛菜盘的地方。小心点，因为酒杯也在那儿。
A:  Oops!哎呀！
