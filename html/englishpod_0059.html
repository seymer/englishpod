Advanced - Job Interview I(0059)

A:  Okay, so let's go over everything one more time. I really want you to get this job!好的，我们再把所有细节过一遍。我真心希望你能拿下这份工作！
B:  I know! It's an amazing growth opportunity! They're true industry leaders, and it would be so interesting to be part of an organization that is the undisputed leader in business process platform development.太棒了！这可是个绝佳的成长机会！他们是真正的行业领头羊，能加入一家在业务流程平台开发领域毫无争议的领先企业，想想都令人兴奋。
A:  So, let's see, you did your research on the company, right?那么，你对公司做过调研了吧？
B:  Well, I visited their website and read up on what they do. They're an IT service company that offers comprehensive business solutions for large corporations. They provide services such as CRM development, and they also offer custom-designed applications.是的，我访问了他们的官网，了解了他们的主营业务。他们是一家 IT 服务公司，为大型企业提供全面的商业解决方案。比如 CRM 系统开发，还有各类定制应用。
A:  So what would your role in the company?那你在公司里具体负责什么工作呢？
B:  Well, the position is for an account manager. That basically means that I would be the link between our and our development team.这个职位是客户经理。简单来说，我就是连接我们团队与开发团队的桥梁。
A:  Sounds good, and so, why do you want to work with them?听起来不错。那你为什么想加入他们呢？
B:  Well, as I said they're the industry leaders, they have a really great growth strategy, amazing development opportunities for employees, and it seems like they have strong corporate governance. They're all about helping companies grow and unleashing potential. I guess their core values and mission really resonated with me. Oh, and they offer six weeks' vacation, stock options and bonuses... I'm totally going to cash in on that.正如我所说，他们是行业翘楚，拥有出色的增长战略，为员工提供了绝佳的职业发展机会，而且公司治理结构也很健全。他们的核心理念和使命让我很有共鸣——致力于帮助企业成长、释放潜能。对了，他们还提供六周带薪年假、股票期权和奖金……我肯定会好好利用这些福利的！
A:  You idiot! Don't say that! Do you want this job, or not?你这个白痴！别那么说！你想要这份工作，还是不要？
