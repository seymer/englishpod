The Weekend - Cooking A Thanksgiving Dinner (C0168)

A:  Hello everyone and welcome back to our show. Today we are going to learn how to cook the perfect thanksgiving turkey! I will share a secret that I have kept for over 30 years on how to cook the perfect, most mouth watering, juiciest turkey in the world! Let's get started!大家好，欢迎回到我们的节目。今天我们将学习如何烹饪完美的感恩节火鸡！我将分享一个我保守了超过 30 年的秘密，教大家如何做出世界上最完美、最诱人、汁水最丰富的火鸡！让我们开始吧！
A:  First of all, we must bring out or key ingredient; whiskey. You see, the grains used to make whiskey along with the alcohol, brings out the flavor of the turkey meat. Let's pour 1 cup of whiskey on the turkey, and serve yourself a cup as well! Cheers! Ahhh! That's good 12 year whiskey right there. Ok, back to our turkey. Preheat your oven to 475 degrees Fahrenheit. Now have another cup of whiskey.首先，我们必须拿出我们的关键配料：威士忌。你们看，制作威士忌所用的谷物连同酒精本身，能激发出火鸡肉的风味。让我们在火鸡上倒一杯威士忌，顺便给自己也倒一杯！干杯！啊哈！那可是 12 年的好酒啊。好了，回到我们的火鸡身上。先把烤箱预热到华氏 475 度。现在再拿一杯威士忌。
A:  Now that the oven is hot, we place our bird on a rack inside a roasting pan. Let's add some whiskey to give some extra flavor and let's have another cup. That hit the spot. There we go, we can leave it in there for the next 45 minutes. In the meantime, lets have another sip of whiskey and start with our delicious pumpkin pie.既然烤箱已经热了，我们把这只鸟放在烤盘里的架子上。加点威士忌增添风味，再来喝一杯吧。这真是正中下怀。好了，就这样放着，接下来 45 分钟都不用管它。趁这段时间，我们再抿一口威士忌，然后开始制作美味的南瓜派。
A:  Lightly whisk together 2 eggs and the extra yolk in a large bowl. Once that's ready we can put sugar, spices such as cloves, grated nutmeg and cinnamon with some cream and dark rum into a saucepan. Slowly bring up to simmering point stirring occasionally. Now you may notice that we are using some dark rum in this pie. This is because the richness of the pumpkin is beautifully enhanced by the dark rum, so to celebrate this great occasion, yourself a glass! Cheers!在大碗里轻轻搅打 2 个鸡蛋和额外的蛋黄。准备好后，我们把糖、香料（如丁香、磨碎的肉豆蔻和肉桂）以及一些奶油和黑朗姆酒倒入锅中。慢慢加热至微沸，期间不时搅拌。你可能会注意到这个派里用了一些黑朗姆酒。这是因为南瓜的浓郁风味会被黑朗姆酒完美提升，所以为了庆祝这个盛大的节日，大家来一杯吧！干杯！
A:  Now that we have finished with the filling, let's Bake the pie for 45 to 50 minutes until the filling has puffed up around the edges and the center is almost set. ALl done! A cup of rum to celebrate the completion of this great thanksgiving dinner!馅料做好后，我们把派放进烤箱烤 45 到 50 分钟，直到边缘鼓起、中心几乎凝固。大功告成！再来一杯朗姆酒庆祝这顿完美的感恩节大餐！
A:  After waiting for an hour, and having a couple of drinks, it's time to check on our turkey! Take the turkey out of the oven, pick the turkey off of the ground. Don't worry, it will add flavor to this recipe. Now also remove the pie from the oven and serve while still hot! Cheers and happy thanksgiving!等待了一个小时，又喝了几杯酒后，是时候检查我们的火鸡了！把火鸡从烤箱里拿出来，把它从地上捡起来。别担心，这样能增加这道菜的风味。现在也把派从烤箱取出，趁热端上桌吧！干杯，感恩节快乐！
