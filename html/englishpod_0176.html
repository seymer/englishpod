Daily Life - Heating (C0176)

A:  It's freezing in here! Can I turn up the heat? 这里太冷了！我能把暖气调高吗？
B:  Don't touch that thermostat! You don't paythe bills around here! 别碰那个温控器！这儿的账单可不是你付的！
A:  Dad! Are you serious? What's the point of having central heating if we can't use it! Look, I can see my breath! 老爸！你是认真的吗？如果我们不能用中央供暖，那装它有什么用！你看，我都能看见呼出的气啦！
B:  Put on a sweater! I'm not gonna let you run up my heating bill just because it's a bit chilly. 穿件毛衣！我才不会让你因为有点冷就把取暖费给涨上去。
A:  Dad! I'm gonna catch a cold! 老爸！我要感冒了！
B:  When I was your age, my parents didn't have central heating like you do! We had a furnace inthe center ofthe living room and that was it. We used it to cook, heat the house and even dry our clothes! We never caught a cold. You should be grateful! 我像你这么大的时候，我家可没有你们这种中央供暖！我们客厅中间有个炉子，就那个。我们用它来做饭、取暖，甚至烘干衣服！我们从来没生过病。你应该心存感激！
