Elementary - Calling In Sick(0002)

A:  Hello, Daniel speaking, how may I help you?你好，这里是丹尼尔，请问有什么可以帮您的？
B:  Hi, Daniel, Julie here.嗨，丹尼尔，我是朱莉。
A:  Hi, Julie, how are you?嗨，朱莉，你还好吗？
B:  Actually, I'm feeling quite ill today.其实我今天感觉不太舒服。
A:  I'm sorry to hear that. What's wrong?听到这个消息我很遗憾，怎么了？
B:  I think I'm coming down with the flu. I have a headache, a sore throat a runny nose and I'm feeling slightly feverish.我想我可能得了流感。我头疼、喉咙痛、流鼻涕，还有点发烧。
A:  I see... so you're calling in sick?我明白了……所以你是要请病假？
B:  Yes, I was hoping to take the day off to recover.是的，我希望请假一天好好休息恢复一下。
A:  OK, then. Try and get some rest.好的，那就这样。尽量多休息吧。
