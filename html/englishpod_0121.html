Global View - Presidential Speech(0121)

A:  Good evening, my fellow Americans. Three days from now, after a half-century of service of our country, I shall lay down the responsibilities of office as, in a traditional and solemn ceremony, the authority of the Presidency is vested in my successor. This evening I come to you with a message of leave-taking and farewell, and to share a few final thoughts with you, my countrymen.大家晚上好，我的同胞们。三天后，在我为国家服务半个世纪之后，我将卸下总统职责，并在传统而庄重的仪式上，将总统职权移交给继任者。今晚，我带着告别与致意的信息来到你们面前，并与各位同胞分享最后的一些想法。
A:  Like every other citizen, I wish the new President, and all who will labor with him, Godspeed. I pray that the coming years will be blessed with peace and prosperity for all.和每一位公民一样，我祝愿新总统以及所有与他共事的人一路顺风。我祈祷未来的岁月能为所有人带来和平与繁荣。
A:  Our people expect their President and the Congress to find essential agreement on questions of great importance, the wise resolution of which will better shape the future of our great nation. My own relations with Congress began on a remote and tenuous basis when, long ago, a member of the Senate appointed me to West Point. I then had the pleasure of building more intimate relationship with Congress during the war and immediate post-war period. Finally, we have progressed to the mutually interdependent relationship we've had during these past eight years.我们的人民期望总统和国会能在重大问题上达成关键共识，而明智地解决这些问题将更好地塑造我们伟大国家的未来。我与国会的关系始于很久以前，当时一位参议员任命我前往西点军校，那时我们的联系遥远而脆弱。随后在战争及战后初期，我有幸与国会建立了更紧密的关系。最终，我们在过去八年里发展成了相互依存的关系。
