Daily Life - New Guy In Town III(0050)

A:  Please make yourselves at home. Let me take your coats. Dinner is almost ready; I hope you brought your appetite请随意，我来帮你们拿外套。晚餐快好了，希望你们胃口好。
B:  Your house is lovely, Armand! Very interesting decor...very...Gothic.阿曼德，你家真漂亮！装饰很有特色……很哥特式。
C:  I think it's amazing! You have such good taste, Armand. I'm thinking of re-decorating my house; maybe you could give me a few pointers?我觉得太棒了！你的品味真好，阿曼德。我正打算重新装修房子；也许你能给我一些建议？
A:  It would be my pleasure. Please have a seat. Can I offer you a glass of wine?我很乐意帮忙。请坐。要不要来杯葡萄酒？
C:  We would love some!我们很想喝！
A:  Here you are. A very special merlot brought directly from my home country. It has a unique ingredient which gives it a pleasant aroma and superior flavor.给你。这是我家乡带来的特别梅洛红酒。它含有一种独特成分，散发出宜人的香气和更佳的口感。
C:  Mmm... it's delicious!嗯……真美味！
B:  It's a bit bitter for my taste... almost tastes like... like...对我来说有点苦……味道有点像……像……
C:  Ellen! Ellen! Are you okay?艾伦！艾伦！你没事吧？
A:  Did she pass out?她晕过去了？
C:  Yeah...是啊……
A:  I hope that you didn't poison her drink too much! You'll ruin our meal!希望你没把她喝的饮料下太多毒！你会毁了我们的晚餐的！
