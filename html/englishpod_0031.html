The Office - Canceling an Appointment(0031)

A:  Hello, Samantha speaking.你好，我是 Samantha。
B:  Hi Samantha. This is Angela calling.嗨，Samantha，这里是 Angela 打来的电话。
A:  Oh, hi Angela, what's up?哦，嗨 Angela，怎么了？
B:  I'm just calling about our meeting today. I wonder, is it possible to reschedule our appointment in the afternoon? I have a bit of an emergency that I need to take care of.我打电话是想问问今天的会面，不知能否改到下午？我有点急事需要处理。
A:  Let me see, it shouldn't be too much of a problem...让我看看，应该不是什么大问题……
B:  I'm really sorry, I hope it doesn't inconvenience you too much, it's just this thing came up, and ...真的很抱歉，希望不会给你带来太多麻烦，就是突然出了点状况，然后……
A:  Angela, you know what, I can't make it to our meeting, either. Why don't we postpone it to tomorrow afternoon at the same time?Angela，你猜怎么着，我也没法参加我们的会议了。要不我们推迟到明天同一时间下午？
B:  Sounds great. See you tomorrow.听起来不错。明天见。
C:  Angela... Angela, look up! See that lady over there who is trying on a red leather jacket? Isn't that Samantha?Angela……Angela，抬头看！那边那个正在试穿红色皮夹克的女士是谁？那不是 Samantha 吗？
B:  What? No wonder she told me she couldn't make it to the meeting, oh, no, I think she saw me...什么？难怪她说她来不了会议，哦不，我想她看见我了……
