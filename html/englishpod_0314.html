Daily Life - Making A Collect Call (C0314)

A:  This isthe operator, how may I help you? 这里是接线员，有什么可以帮您？
B:  Yes, I would like to make a collect call. 是的，我想打个付费电话。
A:  Ok sir, please dial the number. Now please state your name. 好的先生，请拨号。现在请报上您的姓名。
B:  Tommy.汤米。
A:  Please wait a moment. Hello, you have a collect call from Tommy. Would you like to accept the charges? 请稍等。您好，您有一个来自汤米的付费来电，愿意承担费用吗？
C:  Yes of course. 当然愿意。
A:  Dad? 爸爸？
C:  Yeah Tommy, what happened are you ok? 嗯，汤米，出什么事了？你还好吗？
A:  Yeah dad everything is ok. I’m calling you because I want to know if it’s ok for me to go to my friend’s house today after school. 没事的，爸爸，一切正常。我打电话是想问问今天放学后去朋友家是否方便。
C:  Yeah sure no problem. You scared me to death! I’ve told you to make a collect call in case of an emergency only! Why didn’t you call me from your mobile phone? 没问题，当然可以。你吓死我了！我说过只有在紧急情况下才打付费电话！你怎么不用手机给我打电话呢？
B:  I ran out of credit and I also didn’t have twenty five cents N forthe payphone. Sorry dad. 我信用额度用完了，而且我也没带够 25 美分来打公用电话。对不起，爸爸。

Key Vocabulary

operator接线员a person who operates a telephone switchboard操作电话交换机的人
charge指控amount of money to be paid应付款额
credit学分the positive balance in a person's mobile phone account个人移动电话账户的正结余
payphone公用电话a public telephone operated by coins or a phonecard用硬币或电话卡操作的公共电话
collect call对方付费电话a telephone call that the receiving party is asked to pay for要求接收方支付的电话费
