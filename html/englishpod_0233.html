Global View - Forex (C0233)

A:  Hey John! I haven't seen you in ages! What's new? What have you been up to? 嘿约翰！好久不见啦！最近怎么样？都在忙些什么呢？
B:  Pete! Nice to see you Well, on top the norm, you know, wife and kids and work, I've actually gotten into doing some trading. 皮特！见到你真高兴。除了那些常规 stuff，你知道的，老婆孩子和工作，我其实开始做交易了。
A:  Trading? You, big guy? What are you trading? 交易？你，大块头？你在交易什么？
B:  Currencies. 货币。
A:  Currencies? As in Euros, Dollars, Pounds and Rupees? 货币？比如欧元、美元、英镑和卢比？
B:  It's called Forex. Foreign Exchange. The great thing about it is that I don't have to invest a huge amount. I put in a margin deposit and then I can buy and sell up to 100 times that much! 这叫外汇。Foreign Exchange。它的好处是我不用投入巨额资金。我只需存入保证金，就能买卖高达 100 倍的金额！
A:  I don't understand. You're buying and selling money? 我不太懂。你在买卖钱？
B:  You got it! Just last night I made USD 150! 没错！昨晚我就赚了 150 美元！
A:  Last night? 昨晚？
B:  Yeah! It's a 24 hour market! I had bought some RMB earlier at a low asking price but last night it appreciated drastically so I made a split second decision and sold all my RMB at an amazing bid! I've also done some trading with CHF and AUD and HKD. I've made some good profits but I've also suffered some losses. It depends on a lot of factors just like any other market. In total I've made about USD 500 in the past few months. 对！这是个 24 小时不停歇的市场！我之前在低价时买入了一些人民币，但昨晚汇率大幅上涨，所以我立刻决定全部卖出，以惊人的买价成交了！我还交易过瑞士法郎、澳元和港币。赚了不少利润，但也亏过几次。这和其他市场一样，受很多因素影响。过去几个月总共赚了大概 500 美元。
A:  You're kidding! I'm on! Where do I sign up? 你在开玩笑吧！我报名！在哪里注册？
