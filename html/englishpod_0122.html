Daily Life - Supermarket Cashier(0122)

A:  Excuse me sir, this is the express check-out lane for people that have fifteen items or fewer. It looks like you have more than fifteen items there.抱歉先生，这是快速结账通道，限 15 件商品或以下。看起来您这儿超过 15 件了。
B:  Oh, come on! I have sixteen items! Cut me some slack, will ya?哎哟！我只有 16 件！行行好，通融一下呗？
A:  Fine! Please place your items on the belt and push your shopping cart through. Do you prefer paper or plastic?行吧！请把商品放到传送带上，然后把购物车推过去。您要纸袋还是塑料袋？
B:  Plastic. I also have a couple of coupons.塑料袋。我还带了几个优惠券。
A:  No problem, I'll take those. Sir, these coupons expired yesterday.没问题，我收下了。先生，这些优惠券昨天就过期了。
B:  Darn! Oh, well. I guess it's just not my day. Thanks anyway.哎呀！哦，算了。看来今天真是倒霉透了。谢谢啊。
A:  Do you have a club card or will it be cash?您有会员卡吗？还是直接付现金？
B:  Yeah I got a club card. Here you go.有的我有会员卡。给您。
A:  Will this be debit or credit?是借记卡还是信用卡？
B:  Debit please. Also, could I get cash back? Fifty dollars would be great.用借记卡。另外能取点现金吗？50 美元就太好了。
A:  Yeah, sure. Your total is seventy-eight dollars and thirty-three cents. Here is your receipt. Have a nice day.好的，您的总额是 78 美元 33 美分。这是您的收据。祝您今天愉快。
