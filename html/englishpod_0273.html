The Weekend - The Zodiac and Horoscopes (C0273)

Angela:  Hey Lydia, what are you reading? 嘿 Lydia，你在看什么呢？
Lydia:  I’m looking at my horoscope for this month! My outlook is very positive. It says that I should take a vacation to someplace exotic, and that I will have a passionate summer fling! 我在看这个月的星盘运势！我的前景非常乐观。上面说我该去某个 exotic 的地方度假，还会有一段热烈的夏日恋情！
Angela:  What are you talking about? Let me see that… What are horoscopes? 你在说什么呢？让我看看……什么是星盘运势？
Lydia:  It’s a prediction of your month, based on your zodiac sign . You have a different sign for the month and date you were born in. I was born on April 15th, so I’m an Aries. When were you born? 这是根据你的星座预测你每个月的运势。每个人根据出生月份和日期都有不同的星座。我是 4 月 15 日出生的，所以我是白羊座。你是什么时候出生的？
Angela:  January 5th. 1 月 5 日。
Lydia:  Let’s see… you're a Capricorn. It says that you will be feeling stress at work, but you could see new, exciting developments in your love life . Looks like we’ll both have interesting summers! 让我看看……你是摩羯座。上面说你在工作上会有压力，但你的爱情生活可能会出现新的、令人兴奋的发展。看来我们俩这个夏天都会很有趣！
Angela:  That’s bogus. I don’t feel any stress at work, and my love life is practically nonexistent. This zodiac stuff is all a bunch of nonsense. 那是胡扯。我在工作一点压力都没有，我的爱情生活几乎不存在。这些星座 stuff 全是胡说八道。
Lydia:  No it’s not, your astrology sign can tell you a lot about your personality. See? It says that an Aries is energetic and loves to socialize. 不是的，你的占星符号能告诉你很多关于你性格的事情。看？上面说白羊座精力充沛，喜欢社交。
Angela:  Well, you certainly match those criteria, but they’re so broad they could apply to anyone. What does it say about me? 好吧，你确实符合那些标准，但它们太宽泛了，谁都能套用。那说我是什么？
Lydia:  A Capricorn is serious-minded and practical. She likes to do things in conventional ways. * laughs * That sounds just like you! 摩羯座的人心思缜密且务实。她喜欢用传统的方式做事。* 笑 * 这听起来就像是你！
