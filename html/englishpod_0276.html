Daily Life - Telephone Services (C0276)

A:  Telco Mobile, how can I help you? Telco Mobile，有什么可以帮您？
B:  Yes, I’d like to activate my voice mail service please. 是的，我想开通语音信箱服务。
A:  Certainly sir, we currently have a special promotion where we include voice mail services ,call waiting and also three way calling. 当然，先生，我们目前有一个特别优惠活动，包含语音信箱、呼叫等待以及三方通话功能。
B:  Sure that sounds great! Are there any other fees? 太好了！还有其他费用吗？
A:  Not at all. No hidden fees or surcharges, it is a flat monthly rate. 完全没有。没有任何隐藏费用或附加费，只需支付固定的月费。
B:  Perfect. I also wanted to know if there is any call forwarding service? I am usually out of town and would like my calls to be forwarded to a local number. 完美。我还想了解一下是否有呼叫转移服务？我通常不在本地，希望来电能转移到本地号码。
A:  Yes of course. We can activate all these services in about an hour. 当然可以。我们大约一小时就能为您开通所有这些服务。
