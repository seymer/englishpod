The Weekend - Ordering Drinks (C0312)

A:  What Can I get you?我能为您点些什么？
B:  I’ll have a Cosmo please.我要一杯大都会鸡尾酒，谢谢。
C:  Dude! You can’t order a Cosmo! That’s a ladies drink, you’re embarrasing me!哥们儿！你不能要点大都会！那是女士喝的，你害我难堪了！
B:  What are you talking about? It’s a good drink!你在说什么呢？那是一款好酒！
C:  It’s too soft! Order something with a little more kick to it!太柔了！要点更有劲儿的！
B:  Fine! I’ll have a sex on the beach.行吧！我要一杯海滩性爱。
C:  You have to be kidding me!你是在开玩笑吧！
B:  Come on! It’s delicious! Especially when served in a pineapple or coconut.拜托！那味道绝了！尤其是用菠萝或椰子盛装的时候。
C:  Forget it, I’m ordering for you. I’ll have a Scotch on the rocks and my friend here will have a Manhattan. Put it on my tab. Here now this is a real drink!算了吧，我来给你点单。我点一杯加冰苏格兰威士忌，这位朋友点一杯曼哈顿。记在我的账上。现在这才是真正的酒！
B:  That’s strong! This is going to get me wasted!这烈得很！这下我要醉倒了！
C:  That’s the idea!就是这个！
