Global View - Understanding a Trial (C0204)

Lawyer:  Your honor, ladies and gentlemen of the jury, My client, Robert Malone, has been accused of a crime he did not commit. The prosecution has accused my client of being a pickpocket! I know we have heard the testimony of many people here today, …. people who claim the defendant, my client, stole their wallets. I feel sorry for these victims, I really do……… but my client is innocent! 法官大人，各位陪审员：我的当事人罗伯特·马洛恩被指控犯下他并未实施的罪行。检方指控我的当事人是一名扒手！我知道今天在这里听到过许多人的证词……他们声称被告、也就是我的当事人偷了他们的钱包。我对这些受害者感到同情，真的……但我方的当事人是无辜的！
Lawyer:  Let’s look at the facts… one: These 'so-called' witnesses did not actually see the defendant steal anything………. two: When the police stopped him, he did not have any of the stolen wallets. There is no evidence. 让我们看看事实……第一：这些“所谓的”证人实际上并没有看到被告实施任何盗窃行为……第二：当警察拦下他时，他身上没有任何被盗的钱包。没有证据。
Lawyer:  Therefore, Ladies and Gentlemen of the jury, I ask you to think carefully before giving your verdict. My client is innocent! 因此，各位陪审员，我在你们作出裁决前请求你们仔细思考。我的当事人是无辜的！
Judge:  Ladies and Gentlemen of the jury, have you reached a verdict? 各位陪审员，你们已经作出裁决了吗？
Jury member:  Yes, your honor. our verdict is……not guilty! 是的，法官大人。我们的裁决是……无罪！
Robert:  Thank you so much! You were great! Thank you for all your hard work! 非常感谢！你们太棒了！感谢你们的辛勤工作！
Lawyer:  You’re welcome Robert! I knew you were innocent so my job was easy. Take care of yourself, okay? 不客气，罗伯特！我知道你是无辜的，所以我的工作很轻松。照顾好自己，好吗？
Robert:  Thanks once again... 再次感谢……
Lawyer:  Hey! Where’s my wallet? 嘿！我的钱包在哪里？

Key Vocabulary

reach a verdict作出裁决succeed in making the finding of a jury in a trial成功地让陪审团在审判中做出裁决
guilty有罪的responsible for doing something bad为做坏事负责
your honor法官大人a respectful title used to or of certain judges某些法官所使用的尊称
jury陪审团a body of persons sworn to judge宣誓法官的团体
accuse of指控to charge someone with a crime指控某人犯罪
commit犯下to do something (usually a crime)做某事（通常是犯罪）
prosecution控方the lawyers against the defendant针对被告的律师
pickpocket扒手one who steals from pockets从口袋里偷东西的人
testimony证词a declaration of truth or fact真相或事实的声明
defendant被告the person being sued or accused被起诉或指控的人
victim受害者people affected by a painful or sad experience受到痛苦或悲伤经历影响的人
innocent无辜的not guilty无罪
so-called所谓的refer to something that is not suitable指不合适的东西
evidence证据something that shows the thing is true证据；凭证
witness证人one who is called on to testify before a court被传唤出庭作证的人

Supplementary Vocabulary

Acquittal无罪判决A jury verdict that a criminal defendant is not guilty, or the finding of a judge that the evidence is insufficient to support a conviction.陪审团裁定刑事被告无罪，或法官认定证据不足以支持定罪。
Affidavit宣誓书A written or printed statement made under oath.经宣誓后作出的书面或印刷声明。
Lawsuit诉讼A legal action started by a plaintiff against a defendant based on a complaint that the defendant failed to perform a legal duty which resulted in harm to the plaintiff.原告因指控被告未能履行法律义务而对原告造成损害而对被告提起的法律诉讼。
Plea答辩In a criminal case, the defendant's statement pleading "guilty" or "not guilty" in answer to the charges.在刑事案件中，被告针对指控作出的“有罪”或“无罪”陈述。
Warrant逮捕令Court authorization, most often for law enforcement officers, to conduct a search or make an arrest.法院授权（通常是执法人员）进行搜查或逮捕。
