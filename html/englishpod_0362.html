Daily Life - What Mood Are You In? (C0362)

A:  Are you ok? You seem a bit anxious. 你还好吗？看起来有点焦虑。
B:  Yeah I’m OK, I have been having a lot of mood swings lately. I think it has to do with the pills my doctor prescribed that are causing chaos on my hormones. 嗯，我没事，最近情绪波动很大。我想这跟我医生开的药有关，它们让我的激素乱了套。
A:  So you mean you feel ecstatic one minute and then blue the next? 你的意思是，一会儿兴高采烈，下一秒又垂头丧气？
B:  Yeah, it’s weird. For example just this morning I was feeling detached and lonely, even though there was really no reason to feel that way. 是啊，很奇怪。比如今天早上，我明明没什么理由，却感到疏离和孤独。
A:  Well, maybe your mood will swing positively and you will feel confident, brave and hopeful! 也许你的情绪会好转，你会变得自信、勇敢、充满希望！
B:  I hope you are right. 我希望你是对的。
