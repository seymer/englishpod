Global View - Homeschooling (C0354)

A:  I think we should home school our children when we decide to have kids. 我认为当我们决定要孩子时，应该在家教育他们。
B:  What? Why? 什么？为什么？
A:  Well, our public schools here are not very good and private school are just too expensive. I have been reading up on home schooling and it has a lot of advantages. 嗯，我们这里的公立学校不是很好，而私立学校又太贵了。我一直在了解在家教育的情况，它有很多优点。
B:  Like what? I think that by doing something like that we would be isolating our children from social interaction. 像什么？我认为这样做会把我们的孩子与社会隔离开来。
A:  Well, first of all, I would be able to teach them everything they learn in school in a more relaxed and fun way. I also think that having a one-on-one class is much better since you can focus more on his or her strengths or weaknesses. 首先，我可以以一种更轻松有趣的方式教他们学校里学的所有东西。我还认为一对一的课程更好，因为你可以更多地关注他们的优点或缺点。
B:  I think neither your parents or mine would agree to such an idea.我想你的父母和我的父母都不会同意这样的想法。
A:  I will bring it up over Sunday brunch. 我会在周日早午餐时提出来。
B:  Good luck with that! 祝你好运！
