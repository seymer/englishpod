Daily Life - Toothache (C0264)

A:  What seems to be the problem? 哪里不舒服？
B:  I have a really bad toothache! My cheek is swollen and I can't eat anything. 我牙疼得厉害！脸颊都肿了，什么都吃不下。
A:  Let's have a look. Hmmm. This doesn't look too good. I think we may have to pull out your wisdom tooth. It's pressing against your molars and that's one of the reasons you are experiencing so much pain. 让我看看。嗯……情况不太乐观。我想我们可能得拔掉你的智齿。它正顶着你的磨牙，这也是你感到剧痛的原因之一。
B:  When you pull my tooth will you also have to extract the nerve and the root?拔我的牙时，你也得把神经和牙根一起取出吗？
A:  First we will take some x-rays and see what we're dealing with. I also noticed a small cavity up front here, so you are going to need a filling. 我们先拍个 X 光片看看具体情况。我还注意到前面有个小蛀洞，所以你得补牙。
B:  I guess that's what I get for not flossing or brushing my teeth three times a day. 我想这就是我不每天刷三次牙、也不用牙线的原因吧。
A:  It could be that, or maybe you are eating too many sweets. In any case, I'll administer an anesthetic and you won't feel a thing! 可能是这样，也可能是你吃太多甜食了。不管怎样，我会给你打麻药，你一点都不会疼！
