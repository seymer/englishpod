A:  Okay, next question. If Eric asked you out on a date, what would you say?好的，下一个问题。如果埃里克约你出去约会，你会怎么说？

A:  Let's see.... If I won the lottery, I would buy two tickets for a trip around the world.让我想想……如果我中了彩票，我会买两张环球旅行的机票。
B:  If you buy me a ticket I will go with you for sure!如果你给我买一张票，我肯定跟你一起去！
A:  My dad will freak out if I even mention a trip like that!如果我爸听到我提这种旅行，他会气疯的！
B:  Alright this is a good one. What would your mom say if you told her you are going to get married?好吧，这个不错。如果你告诉妈妈你要结婚了，她会怎么说？
A:  If I told her that, she would faint and have me committed!如果我说那个，她会晕倒并把我送进精神病院！
