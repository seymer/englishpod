The Weekend - Family Barbecue (C0190)

A:  Is everything ready for the big family barbecue tomorrow?明天的家庭烧烤都准备好了吗？
B:  Yep. The steaks and chicken are marinated and I also bought hamburger buns. 是的。牛排和鸡肉已经腌好了，我还买了汉堡面包。
A:  We should also cook a couple dozen hot dogs and kebabs.我们还得烤几十个热狗和烤肉串。
B:  Yeah, good idea. We can put some lawn furniture outside next to the grill. I also set up the tent outside so we can hide from the sun if it gets too hot.好主意。我们可以把一些户外家具放在烤架旁边。我还在外面搭了帐篷，如果太热了我们就能躲到里面。
A:  Great! I asked Grace to bring cups and serviettes as she is also bringing two big coolers for the beers.太好了！我叫格蕾丝带杯子和餐巾纸，她还会带两个大冰桶装啤酒。
B:  This is gonna be a great barbecue!这一定会是一场很棒的烧烤！

Key Vocabulary

barbecue烧烤a meal cooked out of doors over an open fire在户外用明火烹制的饭菜
marinate腌制be soaked in marinade浸泡在腌料中
dozen一打a group of twelve十二人一组
kebab烤肉串pieces of roasted meat, fish, and vegetables on a skewer烤肉、鱼和蔬菜串在串上
grill烤架utensil with a metal grate (parallel metal bars) for cooking food over a fire带有金属格栅（平行金属条）的器具，用于在火上烹饪食物
tent帐篷portable shelter of canvas held up by poles, rope, and pegs由杆子、绳子和钉子支撑的便携式帆布庇护所
serviette餐巾a small square of cloth or paper一小块布或纸
cooler冷藏箱a container, vessel, or apparatus for cooling用于冷却的容器、器皿或装置
lawn furniture庭院家具furniture intended for use outside in the yard适合在院子外面使用的家具
hamburger bun汉堡面包a round bun shaped to hold a hamburger patty一个圆形的面包，形状可以容纳汉堡肉饼

Supplementary Vocabulary

tongs夹子device with two arms that are joined as a hinge at one end, used for grasping, holding, or lifting something具有两个臂的装置，其一端作为铰链连接，用于抓握、握住或举起某物
charcoal grill炭火烤炉grill used for barbecuing using charcoal as a fuel, which gives the food a smokey taste用于烧烤的烤架，使用木炭作为燃料，使食物具有烟熏味
gas grill燃气烤炉grill used for barbecuing using gas as a fuel, as from a propane tank or from a natural gas pipeline用于烧烤的烤架，使用来自丙烷罐或天然气管道的气体作为燃料
skewer烤肉签long pin of wood or metal for inserting through meat or other food to hold or bind it while cooking木制或金属长销钉，用于在烹饪时插入肉类或其他食物以固定或捆绑它
basting brush涂油刷brush used to moisten food periodically with a liquid or sauce, especially while cooking用于定期用液体或酱汁润湿食物的刷子，尤其是在烹饪时
