Daily Life - Text Me (C0358)

A:  Why didn’t you text me last night? 昨晚你为什么不给我发短信？
B:  What? I sent you three or four messages! 什么？我发了三四条信息给你！
A:  I didn’t get any of them. I was waiting for you to text me the address of where the party was and I never got your message. 我一条都没收到。我在等你发派对地址，结果一直没收到你的消息。
B:  Why didn’t you just call? I hate sending SMS messages. 你为什么不打电话呢？我最讨厌发短信了。
A:  Well, because I didn’t have any credit on my phone. I used it all up this month. 因为我的手机没话费了。这个月我用光了。
B:  I thought you had an unlimited SMS plan? 我以为你有无限短信套餐？
A:  I do, but if I don’t have any credit in my phone, it won’t let me call or send messages. 我有，但如果手机里没有余额，就无法通话或发送短信。
B:  No wonder you didn’t get my texts! 难怪你没收到我的短信！
