Daily Life - Where should we eat?(0024)

A:  Do you two have any plans for the evening?你们俩晚上有什么安排吗？
B:  We were thinking of checking out a restaurant in the neigbourhood. Do you have any suggestions?我们正打算去附近看看餐厅。你有什么建议吗？
A:  I know this really nice Italian place. The food is fantastic, and the décor is beautiful. I'd recommend giving it a try.我知道一家非常不错的意大利餐厅，食物很棒，装潢也很漂亮。我推荐你去试试。
C:  Actually, I'm not all that crazy aboutItalian Italian food; I’m in the mood for something a bit lighter.其实我对意大利菜不是很感冒；我现在想吃点清淡的。
A:  In that case, I know a great little bistro. They make a really tasty seafood platter; the fish is outstanding.那样的话，我知道一家很棒的小酒馆。他们做的海鲜拼盘特别美味，鱼更是出色。
B:  It sounds fantastic, but I’m allergic to seafood, so...听起来很棒，但我对海鲜过敏，所以……
A:  Okay, well, let me think... Oh, I know this great little place. It's just a hole in the wall, but they do the most amazing sandwiches. You gotta give them a try.好吧，让我想想……哦，我知道一家很棒的小店。它只是个不起眼的小馆子，但他们做的三明治简直绝了。你一定要去试试。
C:  Ella, you took me there last time I visited, and I got food poisoning, remember?艾拉，上次我来这儿你带我去过，结果我食物中毒了，记得吗？
