The Office - Sending A Quote Via Email (C0315)

Tracy:  Melanie, can you help me with something? We need to finalize the account with the Mexican Embassy and, I need some advice on phrasing this letter correctly in English. Melanie，能帮我个忙吗？我们需要和墨西哥大使馆敲定账户事宜，我还需要一些建议来确保这封信的英文措辞得当。
Melanie:  Sure Tracy, let me just get my laptop. all set. 没问题 Tracy，让我先拿上笔记本电脑。好了，准备就绪。
Tracy:  Okay, so……. toWhom It May Concern, I am writing. 好的，那么……致相关人士：我特此致函。
Melanie:  Um, Tracy? I think that’s a little too formal. I know you want to be polite but you’ve already made contact with them, so in English you can be more relaxed inthe opening…. 嗯，Tracy？我觉得这样有点太正式了。我知道你想保持礼貌，但你已经和他们联系过了，所以在英语中，开头可以更随意一些……
Tracy:  Okay, more relaxed. Got it…. Hey Sally, what’s up? It’s Tracy here, just. 明白了，更随意一点。懂了……嘿 Sally，最近怎么样？我是 Tracy，刚想找你聊聊天。
Melanie:  Okay Tracy, now it’s too relaxed! You’ve still got to show some respect. How about starting with “Dear Ms. Cooper, I’m writing to confirm…? 好的 Tracy，现在又太随意了！你还是要表现出一些尊重。不如这样开头："Dear Ms. Cooper, I'm writing to confirm…"？
Tracy:  Great, okay. “Dear Miss Cooper, I’m writing to confirm the final quotation forthe full page back cover color advertisement you requested forthe spring issue of Voila magazine. 太好了，好的。"Dear Miss Cooper, I'm writing to confirm the final quotation for the full-page back cover color advertisement you requested for the spring issue of Voila magazine."
Melanie:  That’s great…. 这很棒……
Tracy:  “ The final costing, including advert design and production, comes to forty-five thousand six hundred RMB. We want payment ten working days before publication or we will cancel the ad. Thanks for…” “最终费用，包括广告设计和制作费，共计人民币四万五千六百元。我们希望您在出版前两个工作日内付款，否则我们将取消该广告。谢谢……"
Melanie:  Woo, okay back up a second Tracy. That’s too direct. Can I suggest you say, " please note thatfinal payment is due two working weeks before publication? You don’t want to offend her. 哇，等等 Tracy，先退一步说。这样太直接了。我建议你说："请注意，最终款项需在出版前两周付清。”你不想冒犯她。
Tracy:  Oops okay. You are right. Then I can just end with “All the best, Tracy” 哎呀，好吧。你说得对，那我可以直接以“祝好，Tracy"结尾。
Melanie:  Hmmmm, maybe, but I’d play it safeand just finish with “Yours Sincerely”. That’s more professional. 嗯……也许吧，但我还是稳妥点，用“此致，Melanie"结尾更专业些。
Tracy:  Oh, Melanie you are a life saver, thank you! 哦，梅兰妮，你真是救星，太感谢了！
