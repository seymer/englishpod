Daily Life - Going On A Diet (C0187)

A:  Oh man! I've been starving myself for days now and I haven't lost an ounce! 天哪！我饿了好几天了，体重却一点都没减！
B:  Are you trying to lose weight? 你在减肥吗？
A:  Yeah, my friend is getting married next month and I'm supposed to be a bridesmaid. I have to fit into my dress and look nice for her wedding, but I haven't lost any weight! Look at these love handles. 是啊，我朋友下个月要结婚，我得当伴娘。我必须穿得进礼服，还要在她婚礼上看起来漂亮，可我一点都没瘦！看看我这腰上的赘肉。
B:  You don't have to starve yourself to lose weight. I think that's where you're going wrong. 你没必要靠挨饿来减肥。我觉得这就是你走错的地方。
A:  Why? If I eat less, then my body will start eating away at my fat reserves right? 为什么？如果我少吃点，我的身体就会开始消耗脂肪储备对吧？
B:  Not really. You should try to not eat foods high in calories, salts or saturated fats. Stay away from oily food and artificial flavors. 不完全是。你应该尽量避免高热量、高盐或高饱和脂肪的食物。远离油腻食品和人工香料。
A:  So you are saying that I should eat, but I should just watch what I eat? 所以你是说我可以吃，但要注意吃什么？
B:  Yes! You can also try to reduce your intake of carbohydrates and foods that are high in cholesterol. You can have steamed veggies or increase your protein intake found in chicken or fish. 没错！你也可以尝试减少碳水化合物的摄入，以及高胆固醇食物的摄入。你可以吃蒸蔬菜，或者增加鸡肉和鱼肉中的蛋白质摄入量。
A:  If I do all this do you think I can lose twenty pounds in four weeks? 如果我照做这些，你觉得我能在四周内减掉二十磅吗？
B:  Don't count on it. 别指望了。
