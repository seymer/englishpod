Daily Life - Vaccinations (C0351)

A:  Hello Mrs Parker, how have you been?你好，帕克太太，最近怎么样？
B:  Hello Dr Peters. Just fine thank you. Ricky and I are here for his vaccines.你好，彼得斯医生。我很好，谢谢。里基和我来打疫苗了。
A:  Very well. Let’s see, according to his vaccination record, Ricky has received his Polio, Tetanus and Hepatitis B shots. He is 14 months old, so he is due for Hepatitis A, Chickenpox and Measles shots.好的。让我看看，根据他的疫苗接种记录，里基已经接种了脊髓灰质炎、破伤风和乙型肝炎疫苗。他 14 个月大，所以该接种甲型肝炎、水痘和风疹疫苗了。
B:  What about Rubella and Mumps?那腮腺炎呢？
A:  Well, I can only give him these for now, and after a couple of weeks I can administer the rest.嗯，我现在只能给他打这些，过几周我再给他补剩下的。
B:  Ok great. Doctor, I think I also may need a Tetanus booster. Last time I got it was maybe fifteen years ago!好的，太好了。医生，我觉得我也可能需要加强一针破伤风疫苗。上次打大概是十五年前！
A:  We will check our records and I’ll have the nurse administer the booster as well. Now, please hold Ricky’s arm tight, this may sting a little.我们会查一下记录，然后让护士给您也打上加强针。现在请紧紧抱住里基的手臂，这可能会有点疼。

Key Vocabulary

vaccine疫苗a medical substance that protects people or animals保护人或动物的医疗物质
Polio小儿麻痹症acute viral disease, usually affecting children and young adults急性病毒性疾病，通常影响儿童和青少年
Tetanus破伤风an infectious, often fatal disease caused by a specific bacterium that enters the body through wounds一种传染性疾病，通常是由特定细菌引起的，这种细菌通过伤口进入身体
Hepatitis肝炎inflammation of the liver, caused by a virus or a toxin病毒或毒素引起的肝炎
Rubella风疹a mild contagious viral disease characterized by fever, mild upper respiratory congestion, and a fine red rash lasting a few days一种轻度传染性病毒性疾病，其特点是发热、轻度上呼吸道堵塞和细红色皮疹持续数天
Chickenpox水痘A disease common of children, characterized by mild headache and fever, and eruption of blisters on the skin儿童常见的疾病，其特点是轻度头痛和发热，皮肤喷发水泡。
Measles麻疹an acute infectious disease occurring mostly in children. red spots being a common sympton主要发生在儿童身上的急性传染病。 红色斑点是常见的韵母
Mumps腮腺炎characterized by inflammatory swelling of the usually other salivary glands通常其他唾液腺的炎症肿胀
