The Office - Interview Skills 8 - Discussing Salary and Benefits (C0256)

Mr. Parsons:  Okay, well I'm sure you have a number of questions to ask me regarding the position. 好的，我相信你肯定有很多关于这个职位的问题要问我。
Rebecca:  Yes, well Miss Childs did give me an overview of the position over the phone, but there were some details I'd like to clarify. 是的，确实如此。Childs 小姐在电话里给我介绍过这个职位的大致情况，不过有些细节我想再确认一下。
Mr. Parsons:  Well feel free to ask me anything, and I'll try to fill in the details.没问题，尽管问吧，我会尽量把细节补充完整。
Rebecca:  Could I first ask about the remuneration package ?那我先问问薪资待遇方面的事？
Mr. Parsons:  Yes of course. That's quite an important point isn't it? As a junior sub editor we offer a starting salary of 150,000 HK dollars per annum. This doesn't include a generous housing allowance also. 当然可以。这可是个很重要的问题，不是吗？作为初级副编辑，我们提供的起薪是每年 15 万港币，此外还包含一笔丰厚的住房津贴。
Rebecca:  I understand. And are there other bonuses included? 我明白了。那还有其他奖金吗？
Mr. Parsons:  Well apart from full health insurance we do offer a company staff bonus scheme linked to readership numbers. But we could go through all the details of that at a later date. 除了全额医疗保险外，我们还提供一项与公司读者量挂钩的员工奖金计划。不过我们可以稍后再详细讨论这部分内容。
Rebecca:  Well Mr. Parsons, I am flexible when it comes to salary. The opportunity to work in Hong Kong for you is the most important thing for me. 好吧，Parsons 先生，在薪资方面我很灵活。对我来说，能有机会在你这里工作才是最重要的。
Mr. Parsons:  Excellent. Well, what other questions do you have Rebecca? 太好了。那么，Rebecca，你还有什么其他问题吗？
