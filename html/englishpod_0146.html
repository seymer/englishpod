Global View - Animal Rights(0146)

A:  You should have seen the T.V. show that was on last night, the topic it covered was really interesting; animal rights.你应该看看昨晚播出的电视节目，它讨论的主题真的很有趣：动物权利。
B:  Do you really believe in that? If they are going to focus on something, they should do it on civil rights.你真相信那套吗？如果他们要关注什么，应该把重点放在民权上。
A:  Yes, but we can’t deny that animals are vulnerable, defenseless, and are completely at the mercy of human beings.是的，但我们不能否认动物是脆弱的、无助的，完全受制于人类。
B:  I understand your point, but we continue to have transgressions against human rights. If so much attention weren’t devoted to the topic of animals, we would then concentrate more on saving a human being instead of protecting a koala.我理解你的观点，但我们仍在不断侵犯人权。如果不过度关注动物议题，我们本可以更多地致力于拯救人类，而不是保护一只考拉。
A:  You can’t compare apples and oranges; I believe that both topics are important and that we can’t ignore them, the mistreatment of animals can cause a great environmental imbalance.I believe that governments should prohibit activities like poaching.你不能拿苹果和橘子比；我相信这两个话题同样重要，我们不能忽视它们，虐待动物会导致严重的环境失衡。我认为政府应该禁止偷猎等活动。
B:  Well, you are right on that point. This is the reason that I don’t buy leather and I try to buy synthetic products.没错，你在这点上是对的。这也是我不买皮革制品、尽量购买合成材料产品的原因。
B:  At least you’re doing your part. My contribution is to have a pet in the house that I treat like a member of the family.至少你在尽自己的责任。我的贡献是养一只宠物在家，把它当作家庭成员对待。
A:  As long as you don’t treat it better than your wife, it’s fine.只要你没把它比你的妻子待得更好就行，那就没问题了。
