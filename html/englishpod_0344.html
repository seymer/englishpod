Global View - Booking A Hotel Room (C0344)

A:  Madison Suites, how may I help you?Madison Suites，有什么可以帮您的？
B:  Yes, I'm calling from Mexico. I will be in town next week and would like to know if you have availability. 是的，我是从墨西哥打来的电话。我下周会到城里，想问问你们还有空房吗？
B:  OK ma’am, can you tell me the date you expect to check in? 好的，女士，能告诉我您预计哪天入住吗？
A:  Yes, July ninth. I will be there for seven nights. 好的，7 月 9 号。我会住七晚。
B:  We have a junior single suite or a superior double suite available for those dates. 那些日期我们有行政单人间或豪华双人套房可供选择。
A:  What’s the difference? 有什么区别呢？
B:  The junior suite is smaller and has one twin bed, while the superior suite has a double bed and mini-bar. 行政单人间面积较小，配有一张单人床；而豪华双人套房则配有双人床和迷你吧。
A:  OK, I would like to reserve the superior suite. Is breakfast included? 好的，我想预订豪华双人套房。早餐包含在内吗？
B:  Yes, a buffet breakfast is served every morning. I will need your name and your credit card details in order to complete the reservation. 是的，每天早上都供应自助早餐。完成预订我需要您的姓名和信用卡信息。
A:  Sure, my credit card number is... 好的，我的信用卡号码是……
