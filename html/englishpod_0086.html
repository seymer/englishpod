A:  Hi! We are the Christianson's! We are here to see the apartment.嗨！我们是克里斯蒂安森一家！我们来看这套公寓。
B:  Oh, hi! Sure, come on in! Well, as you can see, the place has just been renovated. The previous tenants left a huge mess here, so the landlord has redone everything.哦，你好！当然可以进来！你看，这里刚装修完。前租客把这儿弄得一团糟，所以房东重新翻修了所有地方。
A:  It looks great. It's so bright and airy! What great light! I really like these hardwood floors. What's the square footage of this place?看起来真棒！这么明亮通透！光线真好！我特别喜欢这些木地板。这房子多大？
B:  Well, it's about 120 square meters, or 1300 square feet, more or less. Oh, the landlord has also installed new kitchen appliances. There's a new dishwasher, and a professional-grade gas range. Really, at this price, this place is an amazing deal!嗯，大约 120 平方米，也就是 1300 平方英尺左右。哦，房东还装了全新的厨房电器。有崭新的洗碗机和专业级燃气灶。说实话，以这个价格，这套公寓真是超值！
A:  I love it! But what are the payment terms?我太喜欢了！不过付款条件是怎样的？
B:  First and last month rent as deposit and rent is due on the 1st of every month. Considering the amount of money invested into the apartment, it's a very good deal!首月租金加押金，每月一号付当月租金。考虑到在这套房子上投入的资金，这真是个不错的交易！
A:  Yes, it is! Too good to be true...是啊！好得有点不真实……
B:  The living room and dinning room are quite spacious as you can see, and down this hall, here's the master bedroom. It has a huge walk-in closet and an en suit bathroom. We can't go in there yet as the police... I mean the clean up crew hasn't finished.客厅和餐厅如你所见非常宽敞，顺着这条走廊走，就是主卧。它带一个超大步入式衣帽间和独立浴室。我们现在还不能进去，因为清洁团队还没完工。
