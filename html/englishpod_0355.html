Daily Life - Lending Money (C0355)

A:  Can I borrow five bucks? 能借我五块钱吗？
B:  No!不行！
A:  Come on! I’ll pay you back on Tuesday. 拜托，我周二就还你。
B:  Last time I lent you money, you never paid me back. 上次你借钱给我，到现在都没还。
A:  I promise if you lend me five dollars today, I will repay you in full next week. 我发誓，如果你今天借我五美元，下周我就全额还清。
B:  Ok, but I’m taking your skateboard as collateral. 行吧，但我得把你的滑板当抵押品。
A:  Fine! I can’t believe you don’t trust me. 算了！我真不敢相信你不信任我。
B:  It’s nothing personal, just business. 没什么私心的，只是公事公办。
