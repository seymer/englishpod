Global View - Immigration and Customs (C0347)

A:  Good afternoon, passport and arrival card please.下午好，请出示护照和入境卡。
B:  Here you are.给您。
A:  Where are you coming from?您来自哪里？
B:  China.中国。
A:  Is this your country of birth or residence.这是您的出生地还是居住地？
B:  I just work there.我只是在那里工作。
A:  What is the purpose of your visit to the United States?您访问美国的目的是什么？
B:  I’m here on vacation.我来度假。
A:  How long do you plan to stay in the United States?您计划在美国停留多久？
B:  Almost three weeks.差不多三周。
A:  Sir, you didn’t fill out the information on your arrival card of where you will be staying.先生，您没有填写入境卡上关于您将停留地点的信息。
B:  Oh, I’m sorry, but there are a couple of different places I will travel to within the United States, so I wasn’t sure what to put.哦，抱歉，因为我要在美国的几个不同地方旅行，所以我不确定该填哪里。
A:  You must specify an address of the place where you will spend most of your time.您必须明确填写将花费大部分时间的地点地址。
B:  Ok, here you are.好的，给您。
A:  Do you have enough means to support yourself while you are here?您在逗留期间有足够的资金维持生活吗？
B:  Yes. I have some travellers cheques and two credit cards.有的。我有一些旅行支票和两张信用卡。
A:  Very good. Do you have anything to declare?很好。您需要申报任何物品吗？
B:  Nope. I only have my clothes and camera!没有。我只带了衣服和相机！
A:  Very well sir, welcome to the United States, enjoy your visit.好的先生，欢迎来到美国，祝您旅途愉快。
