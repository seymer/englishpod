Global View - Nationalities (C0263)

A:  Hey! How was your first day of class? I'm in level two and I'm loving my class this semester! It's great being in a class of international students! 嘿！你的第一堂课过得怎么样？我在二班，这学期非常喜欢我的课！和一群国际学生在一起上课真是太棒了！
B:  Mine was ok, except that no one in my class speaks English. I guess it will force me to converse in Chinese more in class so at least I should improve a lot this semester. 我这边还行，除了班里没人说英语。我想这逼得我在课堂上得多用中文交流，至少这学期我能进步不少。
A:  That's both fortunate and unfortunate. It's the United Nations in my class! We have people from all over the world! There are three Germans, a Pole, a Scottish, two French, an American, a Brazilian, a Chilean, a New Zealander, though he prefers to call himself a Kiwi. Who else do we have? Oh, we also have a Moroccan, a Togolese, a Pakistani, and two Indonesians! 这既幸运又不幸。我的班简直就是联合国！我们来自世界各地！有三位德国人、一位波兰人、一位苏格兰人、两位法国人、一位美国人、一位巴西人、一位智利人、一位新西兰人（虽然他更喜欢自称“奇维”）。还有谁？哦，我们还有一位摩洛哥人、一位多哥人、一位巴基斯坦人和两位印尼人！
B:  That's quite the array of nationalities. Everyone in my class is from Asia, except me. There are a few South Koreans, several Japanese, Malaysian, Thai, Singaporean, Filipino, Kazakhstani, and one Russian. 这真是五花八门的国籍。我班里除了我，所有人都是亚洲人。有几位韩国人、几位日本人、马来西亚人、泰国人、新加坡人、菲律宾人、哈萨克斯坦人，还有一位俄罗斯人。
A:  Well, I think you're pretty lucky actually. You'll have the opportunity to learn so much about Asian culture. 嗯，我觉得你其实挺幸运的。你将有机会深入了解亚洲文化。
B:  I guess so, but I think it's going to be hard to relate to my classmates, especially with the language barrier. I think I might change classes. 我想是吧，但我觉得很难和同学们打成一片，尤其是语言障碍。我想我可能要换班了。
A:  Don't! Stay the course! Your spoken Chinese will be eternally grateful. I bet you it will even surpass mine with all that practice. 别！坚持到底！你的口语中文会感激不尽的。我相信经过这么多练习，你的水平甚至能超过我。
B:  I highly doubt it. Your girlfriend is Chinese. 我对此表示高度怀疑。你女朋友是中国人。
A:  Well, there is that, yes.嗯，确实如此。
