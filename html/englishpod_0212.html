Global View - Last Will And Testament (C0212)

A:  I, Luke Thompson, residing in California, being of sound mind, do hereby declare this instrument to be my last will and testament. 我，卢克·汤普森，现居加利福尼亚州，神志清醒，特此声明本文件为我之最后遗嘱及遗赠书。
A:  I hereby revoke all previous wills and codicils. 我特此撤销此前所有遗嘱及补充条款。
A:  I direct that the disposition of my remains be as follows: I am to be cremated and taken tothe summit of Mount Everest where my ashes will forever remain atthe ceiling of the Earth. 我指示我的身后事宜按如下方式处理：我将火化，骨灰将被运至珠穆朗玛峰顶，并永远安放在地球之巅。
A:  I give all the rest and residue of my estate to my spouse, Betty Thompson, should she survive me for days. If my spouse, Betty Thompson, does not survive me, I give all the rest and residue of my estate to EnglishPod. 若我的配偶贝蒂·汤普森在我之后尚存数日，我将把剩余的全部遗产留给她；若她未能先于我离世，则全部剩余遗产留给英语 Pod。
A:  If neither Betty Thompson nor EnglishPod survives me, I give all the rest and residue of my estate to my heirs as determined by the laws of the State of California, relating to descent and distribution. 若贝蒂·汤普森与英语 Pod 均未先于我离世，则将全部剩余遗产按加利福尼亚州关于继承与分配的法律规定，留给我依法确定的继承人。
A:  I appoint Robert Porter, to act as the executor of this will, to serve without bond. Should Robert Porter be unable or unwilling to serve, then I appoint Jason Smalls to act as the executor of this will. 我任命罗伯特·波特为本遗嘱的执行人，无需提供担保；若罗伯特·波特无法或不愿担任此职，则任命杰森·斯莫尔斯为本遗嘱的执行人。
A:  I herewith affix my signature to this will on this the twenty third of May two thousand ten inthe presence ofthe following witnesses, who witnessed and subscribed this will at my request, and in my presence. 我于二零一零年五月二十三日在此签署本遗嘱，见证人如下：他们应我的请求，并在我的面前见证了并签署了本遗嘱。
