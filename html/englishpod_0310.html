The Office - Small Talk 2 (C0310)

James:  So Michelle, let me introduce you to Maria. She's my colleague from Brazil. Maria, this is Michelle. 所以米歇尔，让我给你介绍一下玛丽亚。她是来自巴西的同事。玛丽亚，这位是米歇尔。
Maria:  Hi Michelle. So what do you do here in Shanghai? I mean, what work do you do? 嗨，米歇尔。那你在这里做什么工作呢？我是说，你具体从事什么职业？
Michelle:  I work in advertising right now. How about you? 我现在在广告行业工作。你呢？
Maria:  I'm actually inthe wine business. 我其实是在葡萄酒行业做事。
Michelle:  That sounds really great. I love wine myself! Is this your first time to Shanghai Maria? 听起来很棒！我自己也很爱酒！这是你第一次来上海吗，玛丽亚？
Maria:  No actually, I often visit. I usually come to China for business at least once a year. Also, I love the restaurants in Shanghai, so that's a good reason to come. 不，实际上我经常来。我通常每年至少会来中国一次出差。而且我也很喜欢上海的餐厅，这也是我来这里的一个好理由。
Michelle:  Me too. Actually, there's a great Brazilian restaurant I recommend. I mean, the food is delicious butthe service isn't so good. I often like to get together with friends and have a great barbecue there. 我也是。实际上，有一家巴西餐厅我非常推荐。我是说，食物很美味，但服务不太理想。我经常喜欢约上朋友在那里搞个不错的烧烤聚会。
James:  We should go together some time. 我们找个时间一起去吧。
Michelle:  Wonderful idea! I'd love that! 太棒了！我很乐意去！
