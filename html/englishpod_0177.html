The Weekend - Decorating a Christmas Tree (C0177)

HUSBAND:  I'm home! Everyone come here! I bought a Christmas tree! Look at this beautiful pine tree! 我回来了！大家都过来！我买了一棵圣诞树！看这漂亮的松树！
WIFE:  Wow, it's huge! Are you sure it will fit inthe living room? 哇，好大啊！你确定它能放进客厅吗？
KID:  Awesome! I'll go getthe Christmas lights! 太棒了！我去拿圣诞灯！
HUSBAND:  Of course it will. help me put it inthe living room. 当然能放得下。帮我把它搬到客厅去。
KID:  I found the lights! 我找到灯了！
WIFE:  I gotthe Christmas ornaments! We could also place these stockings next to the chimney. 我拿到圣诞装饰球了！我们还可以把这些长袜挂在壁炉旁边。
HUSBAND:  Great idea! While we decorate the tree, we can listen to some good old Christmas songs! 好主意！我们在装饰树的时候，可以听一些经典的圣诞歌！

Key Vocabulary

huge巨大的very big很大
ornament装饰品decoration, something used to beautify装饰，用来美化的东西
decorate装饰beautify美化
of course当然certainly true当然是真的
stocking圣诞袜large sock; traditionally Christmas gifts are put inside大袜子；传统上圣诞礼物都会放在里面
