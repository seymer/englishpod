Daily Life - Talking To Your Roomate (C0206)

A:  Charlie, do you have a second? Charlie，你有空吗？
B:  Yeah what's up? 有啊，怎么了？
A:  Well, I went and paid the bills today and you still haven't given me your half. 我今天去付账单了，可你还没给我你那一半。
B:  Yeah I wanted to talk to you about that. I agreed we would go halves on allthe bills, but frankly I think it's unfair. 对，我想跟你谈谈这个。我们说好所有账单 AA 制，但说实话我觉得这不公平。
A:  Unfair! Why? 不公平？为什么？
B:  Well, you have long hair and use the hairdryer every morning. I don't. You leave your computer on all night downloading torrents. I don't. You see what I'm getting at here? 你看，你留长发，每天早上都用吹风机，我没这习惯。你整夜开着电脑下载 torrent，我也没。你明白我的意思了吧？
A:  You leave the air conditioner on day and night! You also take 30 minute showers which means you are using way more gas and water than me! 你还整天把空调开着！而且你每次洗澡都要半小时，用的燃气和水比我多多了！
B:  Well, while we are at it, stop bringing your friends over for drinks every weekend. You always leave a mess and keep me up all night! 那既然说到这个，别再每个周末带朋友过来喝酒了。你总是留下一堆烂摊子，还把我吵得睡不着！
A:  Maybe you should just move out and find another place. 要不你就搬出去找个新地方吧。
B:  Maybe you should move out! 你也该搬出去！

Key Vocabulary

bill账单a document that shows how much you must pay显示您必须支付多少钱的文件
go halves平分divide equally平分
move out搬出to vacate a residence, cause to leave腾出住所，导致离开
what I am getting at我的意思是what I am trying to say我想说的话
way more多得多much more, a lot more更多，更多

Supplementary Vocabulary

Landlord房东One that owns and rents land, buildings, or dwelling units.拥有并租赁土地、建筑物或住宅单位的人。
Lease租赁A contract granting use or occupation of property during a specified period in exchange for a specified rent.授予在指定期限内使用或占用财产以换取指定租金的合同。
Utility公用事业A commodity or service, such as electricity, water, or public transportation, that is provided by a public utility由公用事业公司提供的商品或服务，例如电力、水或公共交通
Dower寡妇产The rights of a widow in the property of her husband at his death.寡妇对其丈夫去世时的财产的权利。
Breach Of Contract违约A legal violation of an established contract between two parties违反双方之间既定合同的法律行为
