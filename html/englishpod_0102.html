Global View - Global Warming(0102)

A:  And therefore, global warming is the greatest deception of the early 21st century. Questions?因此，全球变暖是 21 世纪最大的骗局。有问题吗？
B:  Uh… yeah. In the lecture you said there’s more evidence in the scientific record supporting global cooling?呃……有啊。你在讲座中提到科学记录中支持全球变冷的证据更多？
A:  Well, yes, essentially, the historical record supports a theory of climate cycles. Warming and cooling are cooperating processes in the planetary eco-system.嗯，基本上是这样，历史记录支持气候循环理论。变暖与变冷是行星生态系统中的协同过程。
B:  If that’s true and the planet is getting cooler, what explains the rapid melting of the polar ice caps and the dramatic rise in the global average temperatures?如果这是真的且地球正在变冷，那是什么解释了极地冰盖的快速融化以及全球平均气温的急剧上升？
A:  But are global temperatures rising? If you look at the data from nineteen seventy-five you’ll…但全球气温真的在上升吗？如果你查看 1975 年的数据……
B:  You’ll be misled. If you were serious, you would look at the record starting in the 1880’s. Then you would see how dramatically the earth’s temperature has changed.你会被误导。如果你认真的话，应该从 1880 年代开始看记录。那样你就能看到地球气温发生了多么剧烈的变化。
A:  Young lady, I beg to differ. Look, the point of the lecture was to emphasize that there is evidence for both sides, and I'm putting forth the argument that there's greater evidence in support of the global cooling hypothesis. Look, it's an indisputable fact that the public is being manipulated and scared into believing there’s some kind of climate crisis; this scaremongering is done, quite simply, for political reasons.这位女士，恕我异议。你看，讲座的重点是强调双方都有证据，而我提出的论点是支持全球变冷假说的证据更多。你看，无可争辩的事实是公众正被操纵和恐吓，让他们相信存在某种气候危机；这种危言耸听纯粹是为了政治目的。
B:  But even without the uncomfortable reality that greenhouse gases like carbon dioxide contribute to global warming, isn’t the topic appropriate for politicians to discuss?但即便不考虑温室气体如二氧化碳导致全球变暖这一令人不适的现实，讨论这个议题难道不适合政客吗？
A:  Not if they want to use your tax dollars and mine to fund completely unnecessary initiatives.除非他们想拿你的纳税款和我的钱去资助完全不必要的计划。
B:  Yeah, like conservation, protecting endangered species and investing in renewable energy. At the very least, you have to concede that this debate has the potential to end our dependence on foreign oil. Buying oil supports autocratic countries that use these revenues to devastating ends.是啊，比如环保、保护濒危物种和投入可再生能源。至少你得承认，这场辩论有可能终结我们对外国石油的依赖。购买石油就是在支持那些用这些收入干出毁灭性事情的独裁国家。
A:  Why, I’ve never been so disrespected in all of my days. I’m a professor, a scientist and researcher of high regard.天哪，我活了这么久还没受过这么大的侮辱。我可是位备受尊敬的教授、科学家和研究者。
B:  Yeah, and a duplicitous one at that. Everyone knows you’re in the pocket of the oil lobby. Why should we trust your so-called findings more than tobacco institute studies which say smoking doesn’t harm health? You’re full of it.是啊，而且你还是个两面派。大家都知道你被石油游说集团收买了。我们凭什么要比烟草研究所的研究更信任你的所谓发现？你们全是胡扯。
A:  Some people just cannot handle civil debate!有些人就是无法接受文明辩论！
