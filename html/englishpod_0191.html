Global View - Daylight Savings Time (C0191)

A:  Did you set your clock forward for daylight savings time? 你为夏令时把时钟拨快了吗？
B:  What? Why do we have to do that? 什么？我们为什么要这么做？
A:  Well, at the start of the spring we usually have more daylight in the mornings and less in the afternoon. This is basically due to our position on the planet and the rotation of the earth. In any case, to take better advantage of the daylight available, we compensate by moving our clocks forward one hour. 嗯，春天开始时，我们通常早晨有更多日光而下午较少。这基本上是由于我们在地球上的位置以及地球的自转造成的。总之，为了更好地利用可用的日光，我们通过将时钟拨快一小时来进行补偿。
B:  I see. That's convenient! I never understood things like this, such as GMT. I never know what time zone we are in or when to change my clock! 我明白了。这很方便！我从来不懂这类事情，比如格林尼治标准时间。我从来不知道自己在哪个时区，也不知道什么时候该调表！
A:  That just stands for Greenwich Mean Time. Here in California, we are in Pacific Standard Time, that is eight time zones west of Greenwich. Remember when we were in Beijing? Well, then we were in China Standard Time, and that's eight time zones east of Greenwich! 那只是代表格林尼治平均时间。我们在加州，处于太平洋标准时间，也就是格林尼治以西八个时区。还记得我们在中国的时候吗？那时我们处于中国标准时间，那是格林尼治以东八个时区！
B:  That's why it was so weird traveling from Beijing to LA! Because of the huge time difference, even though we left Beijing at noon and flew for more than eight hours, we still arrived in LA the same day at noon! It's like we went back in time! 这就是为什么从北京到洛杉矶旅行感觉如此奇怪的原因！由于巨大的时差，即使我们从中午离开北京并飞行了超过八个小时，我们还是在同一天中午抵达洛杉矶！就像我们回到了过去一样！
