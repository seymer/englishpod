Daily Life - Having Leftovers (C0338)

A:  What’s for dinner?晚饭吃什么？
B:  Leftovers.剩菜。
A:  What? Leftovers of what and from when?什么？什么菜的剩菜，哪天的？
B:  From last night! I took the left over turkey, mixed it with some diced peppers and onions, added a little bit of mayonnaise and made some sandwiches!昨晚的！我把剩下的火鸡切丁，拌了些洋葱和青椒，再加了一点蛋黄酱，做成三明治了！
A:  Isn’t that dangerous though? I mean bacteria and germs reproducing on food that was left out or reheated?但这不是挺危险的吗？我是说那些在室温下放置或重新加热的食物上滋生的细菌和微生物？
B:  Well, I didn’t leave the turkey out at room temperature for more than a an hour and I refrigerated it soon after we finished eating. Also, when reheating, I put it inthe oven for fifteen minutes at one hundred degrees Celsius. 嗯，我没让火鸡在室温下放超过一个小时，吃完后很快就放进冰箱冷藏了。而且加热时，我把它放进烤箱，用 100 摄氏度烤了十五分钟。
A:  Well ok, I am just afraid of getting food poisoning.好吧，我只是怕食物中毒。
B:  Don’t worry about it! Making a new meal out of leftovers is almost an art! Not only do you save money, but you also get to be creative and have something different to eat!别担心！把剩菜做成新菜肴几乎是一门艺术！不仅能省钱，还能发挥创意，吃到不一样的东西！
