Daily Life - Going to the Doctor (C0234)

Doctor Evans:  Good afternoon Chloe, I’m Doctor Evans. What seems to be the problem? 下午好，Chloe，我是 Evans 医生。哪里不舒服？
Chloe:  Hi, Dr Evans. Thanks for seeing me on such short notice. When I woke up this morning I had a really sore throat and a really bad cough. I think I am coming down with the flu. 你好，Evans 医生。真感谢您这么短的时间就给我看诊。今早醒来时我喉咙特别疼，咳嗽也厉害。我觉得我可能是得了流感。
Doctor Evans:  Ah I see, yes you do sound rather croaky. Well let’s have a look, shall we? Could you please open your mouth and say " ah".啊，我明白了，是的，你听起来嗓子确实很哑。好吧，我们检查一下，好吗？请张开嘴说"啊”。
Chloe:  "Ahhhhhhhh"“啊——"
Doctor Evans:  Good, yes, your tonsils are a little swollen and red. How are your ears, blocked at all? 好的，你的扁桃体有点红肿。耳朵怎么样，堵了吗？
Chloe:  A little actually. My sinuses are a little blocked up as well – I really feel terrible. 其实有点堵。我的鼻窦也有点不通气——我感觉糟透了。
Doctor Evans:  Ok Chloe, can you please breathe in and out slowly for me while I listen to your chest? You really are all bunged up, you don’t sound too good at all. Ok I’m going to set you up with a bunch of antibiotics. You will need to take these orange pills twice a day and these blue pills every evening. You will also have to take this cough medicine three times a day after meals. Finally, I am giving you an inhaler to use every time you feel breathless… just to clear up your lungs! 好的 Chloe，在我听诊时，请你缓慢地吸气呼气。你确实全身都堵得厉害，听起来状态很差。好，我给你开一些抗生素。你需要每天两次服用这些橙色药片，每晚一次服用蓝色药片。此外，饭后还要三次服用止咳药。最后，我给你的这个吸入器，每次感到气短时就用……帮你清理肺部！
Chloe:  Whoa! So many drugs…. I hate swallowing pills. Am I able to go to work? 哇！这么多药……我最讨厌吞药片了。我能去上班吗？
Doctor Evans:  Absolutely not! You are highly contagious! You don’t want to infect the rest of your co-workers do you? I recommend staying in bed for at least three days and drinking plenty of fluids so you don’t get weak and dehydrated. You can catch up on all the latest tv shows and movies! 绝对不行！你传染性太强了！你不想把病传染给其他同事吧？我建议你至少卧床休息三天，多喝点流食，以免身体虚弱和脱水。你可以趁机追最新的电视剧和电影！
Chloe:  Ok! Would you mind writing me a doctor’s note for work, otherwise they may think I am faking it! 好的！你能给我开张病假条吗？不然他们可能会以为我在装病！
Doctor Evans:  Ha-ha, sure not a problem! Here you are. Now off you go and away to bed. If you have any questions just give me a call! Feel better soon and take care. 哈哈，没问题！给你。现在赶紧回床上去吧。如果有任何问题随时打电话给我！早日康复，保重。
Chloe:  Thanks doc, bye! 谢谢医生，再见！
