Daily Life - Seafood Dinner (C0343)

A:  This is such a nice restaurant! I feel so classy!这餐厅真不错！感觉好有格调！
B:  Yeah, it’s a little bit pricey, but they serve the best seafood in town.是啊，价格有点贵，但他们的海鲜是镇上最好的。
C:  May I Take your order?请问您要点什么？
B:  Yes, I would like some marinated grilled shrimp for starters and I’ll also have the lobster.好的，我要一份腌烤虾做开胃菜，再来一只龙虾。
C:  Excellent choice sir. And for you madame?非常好的选择，先生。那您呢，女士？
B:  I would like the baked oysters and the seafood platter.我要烤牡蛎和海鲜拼盘。
C:  Very good madame.好的，女士。
B:  That seafood platter sounds good. Excuse me, what does the platter have?那个海鲜拼盘听起来不错。打扰一下，拼盘里都有什么？
C:  It’s a great combination of clams, scallops, squid mussels, calamari and fillets of salmon and tuna. It comes with a side of butter sauce and french fries.是蛤蜊、扇贝、鱿鱼、贻贝、鱿鱼圈以及三文鱼和金枪鱼鱼片的完美组合。配有一份黄油酱和炸薯条。
B:  That sounds great! Cancel the lobster and give me one of the same please.听起来很棒！取消龙虾，给我来一份一样的吧。
C:  Very well sir. Anything to drink?好的，先生，需要喝点什么吗？
A:  Can we get a bottle of your house white wine please?我们可以要一瓶你们家的白葡萄酒吗？
C:  Superb choice. I will be back shortly with the wine.非常好的选择。我马上回来给您拿酒。

Key Vocabulary

class课high class高级
pricey昂贵的expensive昂贵的
marinated腌制的To leave something to be soaked in a sauce for its flavour留下一些东西 浸泡在酱汁里 以待它的味道
starter开端as the first step or part作为第一步或部分
platter大浅盘a course of a meal, usually consisting of a variety of foods served on the same plate.通常由同一盘子上提供的各种食物组成。
clams蛤蜊Edible sea creature that buries it self in the sand.可食用海生物 把它自己埋在沙子里
scallops扇贝Edible sea creature similar to oysters that move in the water by opening and closing their shell.与牡蛎相似的食用海生物 通过打开和关闭它们的贝壳在水中移动.
squid鱿鱼a sea creature having a having a torpedo-shaped body有鱼雷形状的海生物
mussels贻贝Edible sea creature that attaches it to something such as a rock食用海生物把它附着在岩石上
calamari炸鱿鱼圈cooked squid cut into rings.煮的鱿鱼切成环状
