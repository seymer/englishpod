Daily Life - Christmas Traditions (C0336)

A:  What are you doing awake?你怎么还没睡？
B:  I can't sleep...我睡不着……
A:  But it's almost midnight!可是都快到午夜了！
B:  Exactly. I'm too excited for Christmas morning. Also, I thought I heard Santa.没错，我太期待圣诞早上了。而且我还以为听见是圣诞老人来了。
A:  Really? How do you know it was Santa?真的吗？你怎么知道是圣诞老人？
B:  Well I heard that naughty boys and girls get coal in their stockings, so I thought I'd be nice and make Santa cookies. I even left out some milk. I heard someone in the kitchen eating the cookies, so I came downstairs!嗯，我听说调皮的孩子和女孩会在袜子里收到煤块，所以我想做个乖孩子给圣诞老人做饼干。我还特意留了些牛奶。我在厨房听到有人吃饼干的声音，所以才下楼来的！
A:  Hmm... well I know that Santa won't come down the chimney with you hiding behind the tree, spying on him!嗯……好吧，我知道如果你躲在树后面偷看，圣诞老人可不会从烟囱里下来找你！
B:  Really?真的吗？
A:  Really! Let's go back upstairs and get back to bed. That way, we can let Santa do his job. Then when you wake up, it will be Christmas already!当然！我们回床上去吧，继续睡觉。这样圣诞老人就能安心工作啦。等你醒来时，圣诞节就已经开始了！
B:  O-K...好……
A:  Hey, honey! Is that you? Don't eat all the cookies - I want some, too!嘿，亲爱的！是你吗？别把饼干都吃光了——我也想吃点！
