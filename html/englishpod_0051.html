The Weekend - What a Bargain!(0051)

A:  Hello. May I help you?你好，有什么可以帮您的吗？
B:  Yeah, this dress is really nice! How much is it?嗯，这条裙子真不错！多少钱？
A:  That one is one hundred and fifty dollars.那条要一百五十美元。
B:  One hundred and fifty dollars? What about this other one over here?一百五十美元？那边那件呢？
A:  That's one hundred and forty dollars.这件只要一百四十美元。
B:  Hmm...that's a bit out of my price range. Can you give me a better deal?嗯……有点超出我的预算了。你能给个更优惠的价格吗？
A:  This is an exclusive design by DaMarco! It's a bargain at that price.这是 DaMarco 的独家设计！这个价格已经很划算了。
B:  Well, I don't know. I think I'll shop around.好吧，我还不太确定。我想再逛逛别的店看看。
A:  Okay, okay, how about one hundred dollars?行行行，那一百美元怎么样？
B:  That's still more than I wanted to spend. What if I take both dresses?这仍然比我原本想花的要多。如果我两件都买呢？
A:  Okay, I can give you a special discount, just because you seem like a nice person. One hundred and ninety dollars for both.好的，我可以给你个特别折扣，因为你看起来是个好人。两件一百九十美元。
B:  I don't know... It's still a bit pricey.... Thanks anyway.我不知道……还是有点贵……谢谢了。
A:  Okay, my final price! One hundred dollars for both! That's two for the price of one. That's my last offer!好吧，我的最终价格！两件只要一百美元！相当于买一送一！这是我的最后报价！
B:  Great! You've got a deal!太好了！成交了！
