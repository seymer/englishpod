Daily Life - Divorce (C0218)

Jeff:  Joanne, let’s not make this divorce any more acrimonious than it already is, okay? Let’s just get down to business and start dividing this stuff up fairly, so we can go our separate ways, alright? Joanne，咱们别再让这场离婚比现在更不愉快了，好吗？咱们干脆谈正事，公平地分掉这些东西，好让我们各奔东西，行吗？
Joanne:  Fine with me. I just want to get this over with. It’s important we make a clean break. I should have signed a pre-nup. 我没意见。我只想快点结束这事。我们必须彻底决裂。我本该签婚前协议的。
Jeff:  What was that? 你说什么？
Joanne:  Nothing! Anyway, you’re right, there’s no reason this has to be nasty. My lawyer tells me you’ve accepted our alimony proposal and the division of property, as well as the custody agreement- I keep the cat and you get the dog. So that’s done…. finally. 没什么！反正你说的对，没必要闹得这么难看。我的律师说你已经接受了我们的赡养费提议、财产分割方案以及抚养协议——猫归我，狗归你。所以这部分算是谈妥了……终于谈妥了。
Jeff:  Let’s not go there, Joanne! Ok, so let’s start with the record collection, I’ll take the albums I contributed and you can have your cheesy disco albums back. 别提那些，Joanne！好吧，咱们先从唱片集开始，我拿走我自己贡献的那些专辑，你把那些廉价的迪斯科专辑拿回去。
Joanne:  Fine, but I’m keeping the antique gramophone as my grandfather gave it to me. 行，但这台古董留声机我得留着，这是我爷爷送给我的。
Jeff:  I believe that was a wedding present to both of us, Joanne. And you hardly ever use it! 我记得那是送给我们俩的结婚礼物，Joanne。而且你几乎从不使用它！
Joanne:  He’s my grandfather, and he never really liked you anyway! 他是我爷爷，而且他从来就不喜欢你！
Jeff:  Whatever! Alright, I’ll concede the silly gramophone, if you’ll agree that I get the silver tea set. 随便吧！好吧，如果同意我把银制茶具拿走的话，我就让步让这台愚蠢的留声机归你。
Joanne:  How typical, when are you ever going to use a silver tea set? Fine! I don’t want to drag this out any longer than necessary. What’s next? What about these old photographs? 真典型，你什么时候会用上那套银制茶具？行！我不想再拖这么久了。接下来是什么？那些旧照片呢？
Jeff:  Which ones? Let me have a look. Wow, look at that! That brings back memories…. That? 是哪几个？让我看看。哇，看那个！这勾起回忆了……那个？
Joanne:  Our trip to Italy! I remember that day. We were going to visit the Trevi fountain, and we got caught in the rain…. 我们去意大利的旅行！我记得那天。我们要去参观特莱维喷泉，结果被雨困住了……
Jeff:  … and you looked so adorable with your hair all wet. I had to take a picture of you standing there in that little alley, smiling and laughing in the rain….……而且你湿着头发看起来可爱极了。我不得不拍了一张你在小巷里站着、雨中微笑大笑的照片……
Joanne:  Oh, we really did have fun back then didn’t we? 哦，我们那时候确实玩得很开心，不是吗？
Jeff:  Oh, Joanne, are we making a big mistake? I know our relationship has been on the rocks for sometime but are you sure we can’t reconcile and try again? I still love you. 哦，乔安妮，我们是不是犯了个大错？我知道我们的关系已经摇摇欲坠一段时间了，但你能确定我们不能和解再试一次吗？我依然爱你。
Joanne:  Oh Jeff! I love you too! I’m so glad we didn’t have to decide who keeps the motorcycle. 哦，杰夫！我也爱你！真高兴我们不用决定摩托车归谁了。
Jeff:  The motorcycle? But that’s mine! 摩托车？但那明明是我的！

Key Vocabulary

clean break彻底分手thorough or complete stop in a relationship彻底或完全停止一段关系
pre-nup婚前协议prenuptial agreement;a contract prior to marriage婚前协议；婚前合同
custody保管the act of keeping safe or guarding保持安全或守护的行为
concede承认to acknowledge, often reluctantly, as being true承认（常常不情愿）是真实的
drag this out拖长proceed for an extended period of time继续进行较长一段时间
go our separate ways分道扬镳set or keep apart in the relationship建立或分离关系
