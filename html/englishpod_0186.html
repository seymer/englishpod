The Office - Business Plan (C0186)

A:  I've had it! I'm done working for a company that is taking me nowhere!我受够了！我再也不给那种让我毫无进展的公司打工了！
B:  So what are you gonna do? Just quit? 那你打算怎么办？直接辞职？
A:  That's exactly what I am going to do! I've decided to create my own company! I'm going to write up a business plan, get some investors and start working for myself! 没错，我就是这么干的！我决定自己创业！我要写一份商业计划书，找些投资人，然后为自己工作！
B:  Have you ever written up a business plan before? 你以前写过商业计划书吗？
A:  Well, it can't be that hard! I mean, all you have to do is explain your business, how you are going to do things and that's it, right? 这能有多难啊！我的意思是，你只需要解释一下你的业务、打算怎么做就行了，对吧？
B:  You couldn't be more wrong! A well written business plan will include an executive summary which highlights the idea of the business in two pages or less. Then you need to describe your company with information such as what type of legal structure it has, history, etc. 你大错特错！一份优秀的商业计划书必须包含一份执行摘要，用两页或更少的篇幅概括整个商业理念。接着你需要描述你的公司，包括其法律结构类型、历史等信息。
A:  Well that seems easy enough. 听起来好像挺简单的。
B:  Wait, there's more! Then you need to introduce and describe your goods or services. What they are and how they are different from competitors'? Then comes the hard part, a market analysis. You need to investigate and analyze hundreds of variables! You need to take into consideration socioeconomic factors from GDP per capita to how many children on average the population has! All this information is useful so that you can move on to your strategy and implementation stage, where you will describe in detail how you will actually execute your idea.等等，还有更多！接下来你需要介绍并描述你的商品或服务。它们是什么，以及它们与竞争对手有何不同？然后是更困难的部分：市场分析。你需要调查和分析数百个变量！你必须考虑到各种社会经济因素，从人均 GDP 到人口平均生育数量！所有这些有用信息都是为了让你能进入策略与实施阶段，在那里详细描述你将如何实际执行你的想法。
A:  Geez. Is that all?天哪，就这些吗？
B:  Almost, the most important piece of information for your investors will be the financial analysis. Here you will calculate and estimate sales, cash flow and profits. After all, people will want to know when they will begin to see a return on their investment!差不多，对你投资者来说最重要的信息是财务分析。在这里你需要计算和估算销售额、现金流和利润。毕竟，人们想知道他们何时能看到投资回报！
A:  Umm. I think I'll just stick to my old job and save myself all the hassle of trying to start up a business! 嗯……我想我还是继续做原来的工作吧，省得自己折腾创业惹一身麻烦！
