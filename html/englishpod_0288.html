The Weekend - Getting A Tattoo (C0288)

A:  I have made up my mind. I am getting a tattoo. 我已经下定决心了，我要纹个身。
B:  Really? Are you sure? 真的吗？你确定吗？
A:  Yeah! Why not? They are trendy and look great! I want to get a dragon on my arm or maybe a tiger on my back. 当然！为什么不呢？现在很流行，看起来也很酷！我想在手臂上纹一条龙，或者在后背纹一只老虎。
B:  Yeah but, it is something that you will have forever! They use indelible ink that can only be removed with laser treatment. On top of all that, I have heard it hurts a lot!是啊，但这可是要伴随你一生的东西！他们用的是无法擦除的墨水，只能通过激光治疗去除。除此之外，我还听说纹身非常疼！
A:  Really? 真的吗？
B:  Of course! They use this machine with a needle that pokes your skin and inserts the ink. 当然！他们用一种带针头的机器，把针扎进皮肤里注入墨水。
A:  Oh, I didn’t know that! I thought they just paint it on your skin or something. 哦，我都不知道呢！我以为他们只是往皮肤上涂颜料之类的。
B:  I think you should reconsider and do some more research about tattoos. Also, find out where the nearest tattoo parlor is and make sure they used sterilized needles, and that the place is hygienic. 我觉得你应该重新考虑一下，多了解一下纹身。另外，查查最近的纹身店在哪里，确保他们使用消毒过的针头，并且环境卫生。
A:  Maybe I should just get a tongue piercing! 也许我应该直接做个舌钉穿孔吧！

Key Vocabulary

trendy时髦的following the latest trends跟踪最新趋势
on top of all that除此之外besides that除此之外
reconsider重新考虑to think carefully about something again再仔细考虑一下
hygienic卫生的clean and unlikely to cause illness干净又不可能生病
sterilized消毒的completely clean and free from germs完全干净，没有细菌

Supplementary Vocabulary

rebellious叛逆的defying authority, going against the mainstream违抗权威，与主流对抗
body art身体艺术used to describe tattoos, but also other, more temporary forms of decorating the body用来描述纹身，但也用于其他更临时的装饰身体的形式
temporary暂时的existing for only a short period of time仅短期存在
permanent永久的something that exists for a long time; does not leave长期存在的东西; 不离开
