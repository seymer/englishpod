The Weekend - Farm Animals (C0185)

A:  Isn't this great? I always wanted to own a farm, live out in the country, grow my own food! 这太棒了！我一直梦想拥有自己的农场，住在乡下，自己种粮食！
B:  This is very beautiful. Though I have to confess, I don't know the first thing about farming! 这里真美。不过我得坦白，我对 farming 一窍不通！
A:  That's fine! Don't worry about it! 没关系！别担心！
B:  What was that? 你说什么？
A:  Relax, it was just a goat! 放松点，只是一只山羊！
B:  And that? 那又是什么？
A:  It's just the cows that are grazing over there. We can milk them later. 那边吃草的是奶牛，我们稍后可以挤奶。
B:  What was that? 你说什么？
A:  Honey, seriously, It's just a sheep. Relax! 亲爱的，说真的，那只是一只绵羊。放松点！
A:  Relax, that was just the horses and donkeys that are in the stable . 放松点，那是马和驴，它们在马厩里呢。
B:  You know what? I don't think I can hack it here out in the countryside. I'm going back to the city! 你知道吗？我觉得我在这儿乡下待不下去了，我要回城里去！

Key Vocabulary

country乡村rural areas农村地区
graze吃草to feed in a meadow or pasture在草地或牧场觅食
milk挤奶get the milk from a cow从牛身上取牛奶
don't know the first thing about一无所知know little about知之甚少
stable马厩a building for the shelter and feeding of domestic animals, especially horses and cattle为家畜（尤其是马和牛）提供庇护和饲养的建筑物
hack it应付自如be able to manage or manage successfully能够管理或成功管理
