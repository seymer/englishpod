A:  Right. You have to turn it on and program it depending on what type of clothes you are washing. For example for delicates, you should set a shorter washing cycle. B:  So complicated! Ok, what about this red wine stain? How do I get it out?太复杂了！那这件红酒渍怎么办？怎么去掉？

B:  Cool. Then I can just throw everything in the dryer for an hour and its all set right?酷啊。那我可以直接把衣服都扔进烘干机烘一小时就万事大吉了吧？
A:  No! Since you are washing delicates and cotton, you should set the dryer to medium heat and for twenty minutes.不行！因为你洗的是 delicate 衣物和棉质衣物，所以要把烘干机调至中温，烘二十分钟。
B:  You know what? I'll just have everything dry cleaned.你说得对？那我就把所有衣服都送去干洗吧。
