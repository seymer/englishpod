Daily Life - Health Food (C0216)

John:  Ok darling, got some pizzas, potato chips, hot dogs and lots of cheese! 亲爱的，我买了些披萨、薯片、热狗和好多奶酪！
Kelly:  Oh John, I thought we said we would start eating right! Remember? Our new healthy lifestyle? That’s all junk food! 哦约翰，我以为我们说过要从健康饮食开始呢！记得吗？我们的新健康生活方式？那些全是垃圾食品！
John:  Hrumph! Right, so what did you get? 哼！好吧，那你买了什么？
Kelly:  Well, healthy food , of course! I got some whole wheat bread, skimmed milk, fresh fish and organic carrots…. 当然是健康食品啦！我买了全麦面包、脱脂牛奶、新鲜鱼类和有机的胡萝卜……
John:  Organic? What’s organic? Do we need organic carrots…? 有机的？什么是有机？我们需要有机胡萝卜吗……？
Kelly:  They were grown without using any chemicals that are harmful to our health. And yes, John, we need organic carrots…. 它们是在不使用任何危害健康的化学物质的情况下种植的。是的约翰，我们需要有机胡萝卜……
John:  Oh, so organic vegetables are the ‘green’ option, right? 哦，所以有机蔬菜就是“绿色”的选择，对吧？
Kelly:  Yup, better for the environment and better for us! 没错，对环境更好，对我们也有益！
John:  Wait a minute, that? …Doughnuts? they organic doughnuts, Kelly? 等等，那个……甜甜圈？它们是有机甜甜圈吗，凯莉？
Kelly:  …. I like doughnuts. ……我喜欢吃甜甜圈。
