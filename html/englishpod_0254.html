The Weekend - Making Breakfast (C0254)

A:  Smells good! What's for breakfast? 闻起来真香！早餐吃什么？
B:  Well, since we are getting up so late, I decided to make a big breakfast! 嗯，既然起这么晚，我决定做顿丰盛的早餐！
A:  Nice! Brunch! 不错！早午餐！
B:  Kind of, so I made scrambled and soft boiled eggs, some french toast and buttermilk pancakes! 算是吧，所以我做了炒蛋和半熟蛋、法式吐司和酪乳松饼！
A:  Wow! You really went all out! Did you make any coffee? 哇！你真下血本了！有咖啡吗？
B:  Yeah, just the way you like it! I also put out some cereal and muesli if you feel like having something more light. 有的，就按你喜欢的口味！我还放了些麦片和格兰诺拉麦片，如果你想吃点清淡的。
A:  Looks good! I'll squeeze us some fresh orange juice. 看起来不错！我去榨点新鲜橙汁。
B:  Getthe jam and butter while you are in there! Oh! And don't forget the syrup! 趁你在里面，把果酱和黄油也拿过来！哦！别忘了糖浆！
