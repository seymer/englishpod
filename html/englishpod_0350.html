Daily Life - Talking About Relatives (C0350)

A:  What are you doing this weekend?这个周末你在做什么？
B:  My brother in law is having a small get together at his house and he invited me.我姐夫要在家里办个小聚会，还邀请了我。
A:  Is it a family thing or just friends?是家庭聚会还是只是朋友？
B:  A bit of both. Some cousins, aunts and uncles will be there, but also some friends from the neighborhood.两者都有。一些表亲、阿姨和叔叔会来，但也有一些邻居朋友。
A:  Is your great uncle Rick going to be there? He is really funny.你大舅 Rick 会去吗？他特别有趣。
B:  Yeah he is going to be there with his step-son and his ex-wife.是的，他会带着他的继子和前妻一起去。
A:  You mean your sister?你是说你姐姐？
B:  No, Rick is actually my great uncle, so he is my grandmother’s brother.不，Rick 其实是我的大舅，也就是我奶奶的兄弟。
A:  You lost me.我听不懂了。
B:  I’ll explain later, let’s go.我待会儿再解释，我们先走吧。

Key Vocabulary

brother兄弟a male sibling一个男性兄弟
uncle叔叔a brother of one's father or mother.父亲或母亲的兄弟。
cousin堂表兄弟姐妹the son or daughter of an uncle or aunt.叔叔或姨妈的儿子或女儿。
aunt姑姑the sister of one's father or mother.父亲或母亲的妹妹
step son继子a son of one's husband or wife by a previous marriage.丈夫或妻子的儿子，通过以前的婚姻。
ex-wife前妻a woman who was formerly a particular man's wife曾经是男人妻子的女人

Supplementary Vocabulary

Niece侄女a daughter of a person's brother or sister.一个人的兄弟姐妹的女儿。
family reunion家庭聚会a gathering of relatives, friends, or associates at regular intervals or after separation定期或分居后举行亲属、朋友或同伙聚会;
biological生物的Being of the same genes and blood as another family member与另一家庭成员拥有相同的基因和血液
adopted被收养的to take and rear (the child of other parents) as one's own child.以自己的孩子的身份继承和继承(其他父母的子女)。
