Daily Life - Facial Hair (C0223)

Officer Brown:  Ok Sally, we have an artist here to help us. We’ll ask you questions about the bank robber you saw and Paul will draw a picture. Are you ready? 好的 Sally，我们请了一位艺术家来帮忙。我们会问你关于你目击的银行劫匪的问题，而 Paul 会为你作画。准备好了吗？
Sally:  Yes, hmmm. Well, he had brown hair…… long hair…… and he had some facial hair…… was brown, too. 是的，嗯……他有一头棕色的长发，还有一些面部的毛发……也是棕色的。
Officer Brown:  Good! Ok, the facial hair, was it a beard or a mustache? 很好！那么，面部毛发是胡须还是小胡子？
Sally:  Both! His mustache was very short and thin, …. on the top of his lip. 都有！他的小胡子非常短且细，就在嘴唇上方。
Paul:  un-uh hmmm…, like this? 呃……嗯，像这样吗？
Sally:  Yes, that’s the mustache! But the beard isn’t right, mean, it didn’t cover his whole face…. think it was just on his chin. 对，那就是小胡子！不过胡须不对，它并没有覆盖他的整张脸……我想应该只在下巴上。
Officer Brown:  A goatee? Was it like Paul’s? 山羊胡？是不是像 Paul 那样？
Sally:  Ah yes, that’s it, he had a goatee.........啊，对，就是这样，他留的是山羊胡。
Paul:  Okay, what about sideburns? Did he have sideburns? 好的，那鬓角呢？他有鬓角吗？
Sally:  Um, they were long and thick, yours! 嗯，又长又厚，你的！
Paul:  Alright, was this the man you saw? 好吧，这就是你看到的那个人吗？
Sally:  Yes, that’s him! Hmmmmm, he looks a lot like you. 是的，就是他！嗯……他跟你长得特别像。
Officer Brown:  Hmmm, why yes he does. Paul, where were you on Friday afternoon? 嗯，确实很像。保罗，周五下午你在哪儿？
Paul:  What? That’s ridiculous! It wasn’t me! I didn't do anything. 什么？这太荒谬了！不是我干的！我什么都没做！

Key Vocabulary

facial hair面部毛发hair on the face脸上的头发
beard胡须the hair growing on the lower part of a man's face长在男人脸下部的头发
mustache小胡子an unshaved growth of hair on the upper lip上唇长出未剃毛的毛发
goatee山羊胡a small chin beard trimmed into a point下巴上的小胡须修剪成一点
sideburns鬓角hair that grows on the sides of man长在男人两侧的头发
thick厚的not thin;great in extent from the surface不薄；距表面的距离很大
ridiculous荒唐的very unreasonable or silly非常不合理或愚蠢
why yes当然as a matter of fact事实上
