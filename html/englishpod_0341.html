Daily Life - Baking A Cake (C0341)

A:  Ok, so are you ready to learn how to bake a cake?好的，你准备好学习如何烤蛋糕了吗？
B:  Almost, let me just put my apron on.差不多了，让我先把围裙穿上。
A:  Ok, so the first thing we are going to do is preheat the oven, that way we have it at the desired temperature once we finish preparing everything. Set it to three hundred and seventy five degrees Fahrenheit.首先我们要预热烤箱，这样等所有材料准备就绪时，温度就已经达到了设定值。把它调到华氏三百七十五度。
B:  Got it.明白了。
A:  No we are gonna make the batter. Take some butter and sugar and mix it lightly until you have a nice consistency. Then add some vanilla extract and eggs and continue mixing.不，我们现在要做面糊。取一些黄油和糖，轻轻搅拌至质地顺滑。然后加入香草精和鸡蛋，继续搅拌。
B:  Do I have to use a whisk or can I use the electric mixer?我非得用打蛋器吗？还是可以用电动搅拌机？
A:  Go ahead and use the mixer, but put it on medium speed. I’m gonna sift the flour and baking powder separately and then we can mix it with milk and the rest of the ingredients.当然可以用搅拌机，但请调至中速。我会把面粉和小苏打分别过筛，然后再与牛奶和其他材料混合。
B:  Ok, so now we need a baking pan right?好的，现在我们需要一个烤盘对吧？
A:  Yeah, but grease and flour it first so the cake won’t stick to it when it bakes.是的，不过要先抹油并撒上面粉，这样蛋糕烤出来才不会粘在盘子上。
B:  Done. So how long do we bake it for?好了。那我们要烤多久呢？
A:  We can leave it in there for about twenty five minutes. Then we let it cool for ten minutes before we remove the cake from the pan.我们可以将其留在里面大约二十五分钟。然后我们让它在十分钟内冷却，再取出蛋糕。
B:  Wow! This was a lot easier than I thought!哇！这比我想象的要容易多了！
