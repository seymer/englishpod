The Office - Small talk 3 (C0313)

Grace:  Hey Michelle! Is that you? 嘿，米歇尔！是你吗？
Michelle:  Wow, Grace! Long time no see! Where have you been? 哇，格蕾丝！好久不见！你去哪儿了？
Grace:  Oh yeah. Well you see I got a promotion, so I moved tothe new Pudong office last September. 哦，是的。你看，我升职了，所以去年九月搬到了浦东新办公室。
Michelle:  You did? Congratulations!你做到了？恭喜啊！
Grace:  Thanks a lot. So how are things with you Michelle?太感谢了。那你最近怎么样，米歇尔？
Michelle:  Well, same old same oldyou know. Nothing much has changed here. 嗯，还是老样子，你知道的。这里没什么变化。
Grace:  Are you still seeing Chris?你还在和克里斯约会吗？
Michelle:  No, actually we split up last month.不，实际上我们上个月分手了。
Grace:  Oh dear. I'm sorry to hear that.真遗憾。听到这个消息我很抱歉。
Michelle:  But I met a really cute guy last night at a networking party so, ….....well, let's just wait and see......但我昨晚在一个社交聚会上认识了一个特别帅的男生，所以……好吧，我们就拭目以待吧……
Grace:  Good for you Michelle!干得好，米歇尔！
