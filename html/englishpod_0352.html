Global View - The 7 Wonders Of The World (C0352)

A:  Have you seen this news article? Apparently an organization has made a list to name the new seven wonders of the world and people could vote for them online.你看过这篇新闻吗？据说有个组织列出了新七大奇迹名单，人们可以在线投票。
B:  Wow, that’s really interesting. So who won?哇，真有意思。那谁赢了？
A:  Well, the Great Wall of China, the Taj Mahal in India.嗯，中国长城和印度的泰姬陵。
B:  I’ve been there! It really is an amazing work of architecture and art. The entire complex is made of white marble and in the interior of the tomb, the walls are covered with gems and emeralds!我去过！它真是建筑与艺术的杰作。整个建筑群由白色大理石建成，墓室内部墙壁镶嵌着宝石和绿松石！
A:  Cool! Also amongst the winners is Petra, in Jordan, Machu Picchu in Peru and the pyramid in Chichenitza in Mexico.太酷了！获胜者还包括约旦的佩特拉、秘鲁的马丘比丘以及墨西哥奇琴伊察的金字塔。
B:  Wait a minute! It also says that the Christ Redeemer statue in Brazil and the Colosseum in Rome are wonders. I would love to go to Italy and see the Colosseum, stand in the middle like a gladiator!等等！新闻还说巴西的基督救世主雕像和罗马斗兽场也是奇迹。我真想去意大利看看斗兽场，像角斗士一样站在中间！
A:  Well, let’s see if we can find some cheap airfare and we can go towards the end of the year.那我们先看看能不能找到便宜的机票，年底出发吧。
B:  Good idea!好主意！
