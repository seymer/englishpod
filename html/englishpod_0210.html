Daily Life - Meeting the In-laws (C0210)

Cindy:  Mother, father, I'd like to introduce you to my fiancé, Bob. 妈妈，爸爸，我想给你们介绍我的未婚夫鲍勃。
Miranda:  Hello, Bob. Welcome. 你好，鲍勃。欢迎。
Bob:  Thanks for having me. Nice to meet the both of you. I've heard so much! 谢谢你们的招待。很高兴见到你们二位。我听说太多了！
Thurston:  So Cindy told you about bringing home her last boyfriend, then? Hah, that idiot... 所以辛迪告诉过你带她最后一个男朋友回家的事吗？哈，那个蠢货……
Miranda:  Shhh! Thurston, you're going to scare the poor boy. Come in and have a drink. Dinner will be on in just a bit. 嘘！瑟斯顿，你会把可怜的男孩吓坏的。进来喝点东西吧。晚餐马上就好。
Thurston:  What'll you be having? Whiskey? Bourbon? Pick your poison! 你想喝什么？威士忌？波本酒？挑一个你喜欢的！
Bob:  If you have a lemonade that'd be great. 如果有柠檬汁那就太好了。
Thurston:  Lemona...? 柠檬汁……？
Miranda:  Why sure, there's some in the fridge! 当然有，冰箱里就有！
Cindy:  Mother makes her own lemonade from scratch. It's the best! 妈妈自己从头做起做柠檬汁。最好喝了！
Thurston:  So what are your hobbies, son? If you want, we can do some hunting tomorrow. I've just picked up a new rifle I've been meaning to try out. Should be a real hoot! 那么你的爱好是什么，孩子？如果你想的话，明天我们可以去打猎。我刚弄来一把新枪，一直想试试呢。那肯定特别有趣！
Bob:  Um. I'm not really. eh. I don't really hunt. 呃……其实我没什么爱好。呃……我也不怎么打猎。
Thurston:  You don't hunt? Well I'll be... 你不打猎？哎呀……
Cindy:  Bob is an animal rights activist. He doesn't believe in harming animals. 鲍勃是个动物权利活动家。他反对伤害动物。
Miranda:  Dinner's ready! Let's go out to the patio where the pig is roasting. 晚饭好了！我们到外面露台上去吧，那里正在烤猪。
Bob:  Roast pig? I'm a vegetarian. 烤猪肉？我是素食主义者。

Key Vocabulary

scratch挠to rub your skin with something sharp用尖锐的东西摩擦你的皮肤
pick your poison自己挑select whatever you like选择你喜欢的
pick up学会buy买
roast烘烤cook with dry heat用干火煮
activist活动家advocating or engaged in activism倡导或参与激进主义
well I'll be哎呀used to refer to show surprise用来指表示惊讶
