Daily Life - New Guy in Town II(0030)

A:  Oh, Armand, thank you for such a thoughtful invitation! It's really very nice of you to invite us over for dinner, don't you think so, Ellen?哦，阿曼德，这么体贴的邀请真是太感谢了！你请我们吃晚饭真是非常周到，对吧，艾伦？
B:  Oh, yes of course! We'd love to come over. Can I bring anything?哦，当然啦！我们很乐意过来。我能带点什么吗？
C:  No, don't worry about it; I'll take care of everything. I'll see you tonight. Come with an appetite... I know I will!不，别担心，我会安排一切的。今晚见。带着好胃口来……我知道我会的！
B:  I don't want go over to his place for dinner! He gives me the creeps! Why on earth did you accept?我可不想去他家吃晚饭！他让我浑身起鸡皮疙瘩！你到底为什么答应啊？
A:  Oh come on Ellen, it will be nice to get to know him. Besides, he's new to the neighborhood, and it would be rude to decline his invitation.哎呀，艾伦，认识一下他会不错的。再说了，他是新搬来的邻居，拒绝他的邀请太没礼貌了。
B:  I guess so... You always rope me into things like this!我想也是……你总是把我卷进这种事里！
C:  Ladies! Thank you for coming! You look delicious... I mean beautiful. Please come in.女士们！谢谢你们光临！你们看起来真美……我是说，非常漂亮。请进。
A:  Oh Oh Armand! You are too kind!哦，阿曼德！你真是太好了！
B:  How did I get myself into this...我怎么就把自己弄进这局了呢……
