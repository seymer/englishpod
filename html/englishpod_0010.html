The Office - Driving Sales(0010)

A:  All right, people. We're holding this meeting today because we've got to do something about our sales, and we need to do it NOW! I want concrete solutions. How do you intend to drive sales... Roger?好了各位，今天我们召开这次会议是因为我们必须采取一些措施来提升销量，而且必须立刻行动！我要具体的解决方案。你们打算如何提升销量……罗杰？
B:  Well, in fact, we're the most expensive in the market, so maybe we need to lower our prices to match the competitors?嗯，实际上我们在市场上是最贵的，所以也许我们需要降低价格以匹配竞争对手？
A:  Lower our prices? Not very creative. It'll never fly with Swan. What kind of thinking is that? Geez. Anybody else have a better plan? Natalie?降低价格？这没什么创意。斯万先生绝不会接受的。这是什么思路？天哪。还有谁有更好的计划？娜塔莉？
C:  Um, perhaps, um, a sales promotion. Maybe a two-for-one offer, or something like that!呃，或许，呃，搞个促销活动。比如买二送一之类的！
A:  What? That's the same thing. Bad idea. Really bad idea. Dammit people come on! Think! The CEO will be here any minute.什么？这和刚才说的一样。坏主意。非常坏的主意。该死，大家快点！动脑筋！CEO 随时都会到。
D:  Do we have any ideas yet?我们已经有想法了吗？
C:  Yes Mr. Swan, we were kind of considering a two-for-one offer to get more competitive.是的斯万先生，我们正考虑推出买二送一的活动以增强竞争力。
D:  A two-for-one promotion? Hmm. I kind of like the sound of that. It sounds like something we should consider.买二一促销？嗯。我听起来觉得不错。这似乎是我们应该考虑的事情。
A:  Yeah, exactly. Just what I was thinking! In fact, that's a brilliant idea! I'm glad we thought of that. Very creative.没错，正是我所想的！事实上这是个绝妙的主意！很高兴我们能想到这一点。非常有创意。
