Daily Life - Daddy Please!(0049)

A:  Hey daddy! You look great today; I like your tie! By the way, I was wondering can I…嘿，爸爸！你今天看起来真帅，我喜欢你的领带！顺便问一下，我在想我能不能……
B:  NO!不行！
A:  I haven’t even told you what it is yet!我还没告诉你是什么事呢！
B:  Okay, okay, what do you want?好吧，好吧，你想要什么？
A:  Do you think I could borrow the car? I'm going to a concert tonight.你觉得我能借你的车吗？今晚我要去听音乐会。
B:  Um.. I don't think so. I need the car tonight to pick up your mother.呃……我想不行。今晚我需要用车去接你妈妈。
A:  Ugg! I told you about it last week! Smelly Toes is playing, and Eric asked if I would go with him!哎呀！上周我就跟你说过这事了！Smelly Toes 乐队要演出，Eric 还问我能不能跟他一起去呢！
B:  Who’s this Eric guy?这个 Eric 是谁啊？
A:  Duh! He’s like the hottest and most popular guy at school! Come on, dad! Please!废话！他是学校里最酷、最受欢迎的那个男生！拜托啦，爸爸！求你了！
B:  No can do... sorry.不行……抱歉。
A:  Fine then! Would you mind giving me 100 bucks?好吧！你能借我一百块吗？
B:  No way!没门！
A:  That's so unfair!这也太不公平了！
