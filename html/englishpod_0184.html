Daily Life - Fresh Start (C0184)

A:  Now that it's the new year, I've decided to turn over a new leaf. 既然新年到了，我决定改过自新。
B:  Yeah? You finally decided to wipe the slate clean? 真的吗？你终于决定彻底重新开始了吗？
A:  You got it! I have a new job, I'm living in a new city, with new friends! This is my opportunity to make some small changes in the way I live my life. 没错！我换了工作，搬到了新城市，还结识了新朋友！这是我改变生活方式、做出一些小小调整的机会。
B:  So what are you going to do? Take up an art class or something? 那你打算做什么呢？去上艺术课之类的？
A:  Well, first of all, I've decided to stop smoking. It's not that I'm pinching pennies or anything, it's just that I've been smoking since I was sixteen, and I think it's time to stop. 首先，我决定戒烟了。并不是为了省钱什么的，只是我从十六岁就开始抽烟了，我觉得是时候戒掉了。
B:  I'm with you on that one. Anything else you're planning on doing? 我同意你的看法。你还计划做其他什么事吗？
A:  One last thing, I've decided to come out of the closet. 最后一件事，我决定公开自己的性取向。
B:  It's about time! 该你露一手了！
