B:  Someone get her out of here! Get me the defibrillator. Okay, clear! Again! Clear! Come on! dammit! I'm not letting you go! Clear! I've got a pulse!有人把她带出去！给我拿除颤器来。好了，闪开！再来一次！闪开！加油！该死！我不会让你们放弃的！闪开！我摸到脉搏了！
C:  Okay, what’s happening?好了，发生什么了？
B:  The patient is in acute respiratory failure, I think we’re going to have to intubate!病人处于急性呼吸衰竭，我想我们得插管了！
C:  Alright! Tube’s in! Bag him! Someone give him 10 cc's of adrenaline! Let’s go, people move, move!好！管子插进去了！给他用呼吸机！有人给 10 毫升肾上腺素！快点，大家让开，快让开！

B:  We managed to stabilize Frankie, but he’s not out of the woods yet; he’s still in critical condition. We’re moving him to intensive care, but…我们成功稳定了弗兰基的情况，但他还没脱离危险；他仍处于危重状态。我们要把他转去重症监护室，但是……
A:  Doctor, just do whatever it takes. I just want my little Frankie to be okay. I couldn’t imagine life without my little hamster!医生，不惜一切代价吧。我只想让我的小弗兰基平安无事。我无法想象没有我的小仓鼠的生活！
