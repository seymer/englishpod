Daily Life - Applying for a Visa(0043)

A:  So, you're applying for a B2 visa, where is your final destination and what's the purpose of your trip to the United States?所以，您正在申请 B2 签证，您的最终目的地是哪里？赴美旅行的目的是什么？
B:  I'm going to visit my brother; he's just had a baby. He lives in Minneapolis.我要去探望我的弟弟；他刚生了孩子。他住在明尼阿波利斯。
A:  And how long do you you plan to remain in the United States?那您计划在美国停留多久？
B:  I'll be here for approximately three weeks. See, here's my return ticket for the twenty-sixth of March.我会待大约三周。看，这是我的三月二十六日的返程机票。
A:  And, who is sponsoring your trip?那么，谁为您的旅行提供资助？
B:  My brother, here, this is an invitation letter from him. I will stay with him and his family in their home.是我的弟弟，这是他的邀请函。我会和他家人住在他家里。
A:  Alright, tell me about the ties you have to your home country.好的，请谈谈您与祖国有哪些联系。
B:  Well, I own a house; actually, I'm leaving my dog there with my neighbors. I have a car at home, and oh, my job! I'm employed by Tornel as an engineer. Actually, I only have three weeks' vacation, so I have to go back to work at the end of March.嗯，我拥有一栋房子；实际上，我把狗留给邻居照看。我在家有车，还有我的工作！我在 Tornel 公司担任工程师。实际上我只休三周假，所以三月末我必须回去上班。
A:  And what evidence do you have that you are financially independent?您有什么证据证明您在经济上是独立的？
B:  Well, I do have assets in my country; like I said, I own a house, and see, here's a bank statement showing my investments, and my bank balance.嗯，我在国内确实有资产；正如我所说，我拥有一栋房子，而且看，这是我的银行对账单，显示了我的投资和银行存款余额。
A:  I'm sorry, sir, we cannot grant you a B2 visa at this time, instead, you are granted a resident visa! Congratulations, you are the millionth person to apply for a visa! You win! Congratulations!抱歉，先生，我们目前无法为您签发 B2 签证，而是授予您居民签证！恭喜您，您是第 100 万位申请签证的人！您赢了！恭喜！
