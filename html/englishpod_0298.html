The Office - Presentation Series 10 - The Q and A Session (C0298)

Jonathan:  Well everyone, I'm sure you'd like to join me in thanking Michael for what was a really inspirational presentation. Sincere thanks Michael. 大家好，我相信大家都想和我一起感谢迈克尔，他刚才的演讲真的非常鼓舞人心。真诚地感谢迈克尔。
Jonathan:  Now, I'm sure many of you will be keen to ask some questions, so I'd like to open it up a Q and A session. Please raise your hand if you have any questions at all. Janice, go ahead. 现在，我相信在座的各位可能都迫不及待想提问了，所以我想开启一个问答环节。请大家举手示意任何疑问。简妮丝，请吧。
Janice:  Yes thank you Jonathan. I would just like to go back to the comment Mr. Ford made in regards to our competitors, particularly Orange. Now as you know, Orange has established themselves as the market leader in the high-end lap-top market. 是的，谢谢乔纳森。我想回到福尔先生关于我们竞争对手，特别是橙公司的评论上。众所周知，橙公司已经在高端笔记本电脑市场确立了领导地位。
Janice:  How does Mr. Ford expect to compete with a company that has such a huge reputation and huge resources? 福尔先生打算如何与一家拥有如此巨大声誉和资源的公司竞争呢？
Mr. Ford:  Well Janice, first of all, thanks for a very good question. I think you have hit the nail on the head actually. Orange are the global leaders precisely because of their size and power. 嗯，简妮丝，首先非常感谢你提出这个非常好的问题。我认为你确实抓住了重点。橙公司之所以成为全球领导者，正是得益于其规模和实力。
Mr. Ford:  But, although we can't compete in terms of size I do believe we hold an advantage in terms of dedication to customer service. Yes, I admit this is a David and Goliath battle,but don't forget who won that contest. 但是，虽然我们在规模上无法与之抗衡，但我相信我们在客户服务方面的投入上具有优势。是的，我承认这是一场大卫与歌利亚的较量，但别忘了那场较量中谁赢了。
Frank:  Ermmm, Mr Ford. Could you elaborate on the actual technical details of the x420 a little more? 呃……福尔先生，您能再多讲讲 x420 的具体技术细节吗？
Mr. Ford:  I'd love to but I think we are a little pressed for time right now. However Jonathan has all the technical specs for you on the powerpoint presentation, which you can look over in your own time. 我很乐意，但现在时间有点紧。不过乔纳森在 PPT 演示文稿里有所有的技术参数，你们可以有空时自己看看。
Marcie:  Mr. Ford. One final question. Would you like to join me for a game of golf this Sunday? 福特先生，最后一个问题。这周日想跟我一起打高尔夫吗？
