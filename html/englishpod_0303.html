Daily Life - Mexican Food (C0303)

A:  Hello sir, welcome to Pistolera restaurant. May I take your order? 您好，欢迎光临 Pistolera 餐厅。请问您想点什么？
B:  Yes, I would like the chicken cheese enchiladas with a side of guacamole.好的，我要一份鸡肉芝士卷饼，再配一份牛油果酱。
A:  I’m sorry sir, but we ran out of chicken. May I suggest our delicious beef burritos or cheese quesadillas? Both include a side of guacamole and jalapenos.抱歉先生，我们的鸡肉卖完了。建议您试试我们美味的牛肉卷饼或芝士烤饼，两者都配有牛油果酱和墨西哥辣椒。
B:  Sure I’ll have the burrito.. Do you have nachos?好的，我要卷饼。你们有炸玉米片吗？
A:  Of course sir. Our nachos come with melted cheese and chili.当然有，先生。我们的炸玉米片配有融化的芝士和辣酱。
B:  Sounds good.听起来不错。
A:  Would you like anything to drink?您想喝点什么吗？
B:  Sure, I’ll have a Corona.好的，我要一杯科罗娜啤酒。
