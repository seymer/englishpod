Intermediate - Chinese New Year(0037)

A:  I'm so excited about Chinese New Year! When do I get to visit Grandma? Grandma makes the best dumplings in the world!我对中国新年太兴奋了！我什么时候能去奶奶家？奶奶做的饺子是世界上最好吃的！
B:  Ha ha, right. Sounds to me like you're more excited about the dumplings than seeing your Grandma.哈哈，没错。听起来你更期待的是饺子而不是见奶奶。
A:  Of course I miss Grandma, too. I bet she's gonna teach me how to play Mahjong! Hey, Dad, are you going to buy me firecrackers this year? We're going to have the best fireworks! I'm really looking forward to lighting them!当然我也想念奶奶。她肯定会教我打麻将的！嘿，爸爸，今年你会给我买鞭炮吗？我们要看最棒的烟花！我真期待点燃它们的那一刻！
B:  Son, firecrackers aren't toys; they're dangerous!儿子，鞭炮不是玩具，它们很危险！
A:  No, fireworks are awesome!不，烟花太酷了！
B:  Whoa, don't you remember? Last year when I set off the firecrackers, you covered both your ears and hid behind your mother?哇，你不记得了吗？去年我放鞭炮时，你捂住了耳朵躲在妈妈身后？
A:  Dad! I was scared because... because I saw a bug. That's all.爸爸！我当时害怕是因为……因为我看见了一只虫子。就这些。
B:  Hahaha... really?哈哈哈……真的吗？
A:  Oh, and I can't wait to watch the dragon dance! Dad, can I sit on your shoulders this time?哦，我还迫不及待想看舞龙！爸爸，这次我能坐在你的肩膀上吗？
B:  Hey, I offered last year...嘿，去年我就提过这个了……
A:  Well, I... anyways, I was just thinking of the red envelopes. I wanna make a list of all the things I'm gonna buy with my red envelope money! I can't wait! I'm gonna have so much money! Mom, can I get a pen and a piece of paper?嗯，我……总之，我在想红包。我想列个清单，把要用红包钱买的东西都写下来！我等不及了！我会拿到好多钱的！妈妈，我可以要支笔和一张纸吗？
A:  I want a new transformer, no, two transformers...the Optimus Prime, and...maybe the wheeljack? I'll get a PSP game, hahaha, and I'll buy the entire class lunch at MacDonald's...我想要一个新的变形金刚，不，两个……擎天柱，还有……也许轮脚？我会买个 PSP 游戏，哈哈哈，我还要在麦当劳请全班同学吃午餐……
