Global View - Crime Scene (C0224)

Detective MeGee:  Alright, Officer McGraw, Give it to me straight, what are we looking at here? 好吧，麦格劳警官，直说，我们面对的是什么情况？
McGraw:  Detective MeGee! We’re glad to see you! We could sure use your expertise on this one. It’s a break-in, but nothing seems to have been stolen. We received a call from the Bear family at around ten thirty this morning. They had gone out for a walk before breakfast and came home to this mess! Broken chairs and porridge all over the place! Apparently, Momma Bear had made the porridge a little too hot, you see, and they were waiting for it to cool down. 梅吉侦探！很高兴见到你！这件案子特别需要你的专业知识。这是一起入室盗窃，但看起来什么都没丢。今天早上十点半左右，熊家给我们打了电话。他们吃完早饭出去散步，回来后发现一片狼藉！椅子全碎了，粥洒得到处都是！听说是熊妈妈把粥煮得太烫了，所以他们等粥凉下来才回家。
Detective MeGee:  Okay then, let’s start examining the evidence…. Have the forensics team been in yet? 好的，那我们开始检查现场吧……法医组到这儿了吗？
McGraw:  Yes sir. They found some fingerprints on the bowls and are analyzing them back at the lab as we speak. Hopefully, they will be able to identify the burglar soon. 是的，长官。他们在碗上发现了指纹，目前正在实验室进行分析。希望他们很快就能锁定嫌疑人。
Detective MeGee:  Hmmmm,Ah ha! What’s this? A strand of golden hair…… this is a very important piece of trace evidence McGraw. It tells me the suspect has long golden hair……. very few men have long golden hair……. our criminal could be a woman…… 嗯……啊哈！这是什么？一缕金发……这可是麦格劳，非常重要的微量物证。这说明嫌疑人的头发又长又金黄……很少有男性留这么长的金发……我们的罪犯可能是个女人……
McGraw:  A woman? Was she working alone? Did she have an accomplice? 是个女人？她是单独作案的吗？有没有同伙？
Detective MeGee:  An accomplice? No, no McGraw, she was definitely working alone. See here, there are footprints in the porridge, here on the floor…. footprints, tells me that our suspect is small…. could possibly be a child. 同伙？没有，没有，麦格劳，她肯定是单独行动的。你看这里，粥里和地上都有脚印……脚印表明我们的嫌疑人个子很小……甚至可能是个孩子。
McGraw:  A child? Surely not, sir… 孩子？不可能吧，长官……
Detective MeGee:  We must follow the clues, McGraw! The evidence doesn’t lie! Now, let’s reconstruct the crime…… the suspect came in, sat in each chair breaking the smallest one into little pieces. Next, the porridge. she obviously tried to eat it and because it was so hot, she dropped it on the floor…. this mess. interesting. These footprints seem to lead upstairs. McGraw, did your officers clear the scene? 我们必须顺着线索走，麦格劳！证据不会撒谎！现在我们来重建案发现场……嫌疑人进来后，坐在每把椅子上，把最小的一把拆成了碎片。接着是粥。她显然试图吃它，但因为太烫了，把它掉在了地板上……这真是一团糟。有趣。这些脚印似乎通向楼上。麦格劳，你的警察清理完现场了吗？
McGraw:  Well, there was no one down here… and maybe we forget to check upstairs. 嗯，楼下没人……也许我们忘了检查楼上。
Goldylocks:  Hey! What’s with all the noise? I’m trying to sleep up here! 嘿！这么吵干什么？我在楼上睡觉呢！
Detective MeGee:  There she is! Get her! 抓住她！
