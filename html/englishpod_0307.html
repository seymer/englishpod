The Office - Small Talk Series - Showing Interest (C0307)

Tina:  Hey Michelle, this is my friend James. He's visiting Shanghai from New York. 嘿，Michelle，这位是我的朋友 James。他刚从纽约来上海旅游。
Michelle:  Oh, hi James. Nice to meet you. So, uh. you visiting for business or pleasure? 哦，嗨，James。很高兴认识你。那么，你是为了商务还是休闲来访？
James:  Well, actually a little of both. I'm meeting some business contacts but I'm also taking some Mandarin classes too. 嗯，其实两者都有。我会见一些商务伙伴，同时也上几节中文课。
Michelle:  That's cool! How's it going? 太棒了！最近怎么样？
James:  Well, I'm finding the classes pretty tough actually, but I'm having a great time in Shanghai. It's really an amazing city. 嗯，我觉得课程挺难的，不过在上海玩得很开心。这座城市真的很棒。
Michelle:  It sure is. Are you staying for long? 确实如此。你会待多久？
James:  Only two weeks unfortunately. I wish I could stay longer but. 很遗憾只有两周。我希望能多待几天，但是……
Michelle:  Well listen, if you need someone to show youthe sights then just call me. I'm having a little get together at my new apartment next week so if you want to drop bythen. 好吧，听着，如果你需要人带你逛逛景点，就给我打电话。我下周在新公寓有个小聚会，如果你想顺道过来也可以。
James:  That sounds great. I'd love to! Let me take down your number Michelle. 听起来很棒。我很乐意！让我记一下你的号码，Michelle。
