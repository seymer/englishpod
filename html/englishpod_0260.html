The Weekend - Guns (C0260)

A:  Hey Nick, what are you up to?嘿尼克，你在忙什么呢？
B:  Not much, just heading over to the shooting range. You wanna come? 没什么，正要去靶场。你要来吗？
A:  Seriously? You mean to fire a real weapon? I don't know man. 真的？你是说要用真枪实弹？我不知道啊兄弟。
B:  Yeah it will be fun! I have a 9mm pistol that is really easy to shoot. I also have a revolver that's really fun too! They have big targets at the range that we could use to practice and improve your aim. 没错，肯定很有趣！我有一把 9mm 手枪，非常好上手。我还有一把左轮手枪，也特别好玩！靶场里有大靶子，我们可以用来练习和提高你的瞄准能力。
A:  Yeah that would be cool! Maybe I can also have a try at other weapons like a machine gun or a shotgun! Maybe even a rocket launcher or an anti tank missile! Or what about a flame thrower! 哇，那太酷了！也许我也能试试其他武器，比如机枪或霰弹枪！甚至火箭筒或反坦克导弹！或者火焰喷射器怎么样？
B:  Whoa, take it easy there Rambo. Don't get carried away. These weapons are not toys, and you must first learn how to handle them properly. There are basic rules that you must abide by in order to be safe. For example, never handle a weapon that you haven't inspected yourself. Always make sure there isn't anything in the chamber, and never put your finger on the trigger unless you are ready to shoot! 喂，别这么激动，兰博。别过头热了。这些可不是玩具，你必须先学会如何正确操作。有一些基本规则你必须遵守以确保安全。例如，永远不要触碰未经自己检查过的武器。务必确保枪膛里没有东西，除非你准备射击，否则手指绝不能放在扳机上！
A:  Wow, I didn't know! It always looks so cool and easy in the movies! 哇，我都不知道！电影里看起来总是那么酷又简单！
B:  The reality is different you know, running and firing a weapon is a lot harder than in the movies! So are you ready? 现实情况可不一样，你知道吗，奔跑中开枪比电影里难多了！那你准备好了吗？
A:  Let's do it! 我们开始吧！
