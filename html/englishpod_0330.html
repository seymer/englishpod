The Weekend - Fortune Telling (C0330)

A:  Look at this newspaper article about this famous local medium. It says that she is really gifted and so popular now, that she is booked solid with appointments for the next twelve months!看看这篇关于这位著名本地灵媒的新闻报道。文章说她天赋异禀，如今人气爆棚，接下来的十二个月预约已经全部排满！
B:  You don’t really believe in all that hocus pocus mumbo jumbo do you?你真信那些神神叨叨的鬼把戏吗？
A:  Well I have had many friends that went to a psychic and got their palms read and most of the things the psychic told her came true!其实我有很多朋友都去找过通灵者看手相，他们说的很多事后来都成真了！
B:  Of course it does! They tell you general and obvious things like that you will be successful or have a big house. I think most of the times they are just scam artists.当然啦！他们只会说些笼统又显而易见的话，比如“你会成功”或“拥有大房子”。我觉得大多数时候他们就是骗子。
A:  Well historically it is a practice that many cultures share. Reading the tarot cards, in the east they would even read tea leaves! I even heard that there are people that make you smoke a cigar, and then read your ashes.从历史上看，这是许多文化共有的习俗。塔罗牌占卜是其中一种，在东方甚至还会通过茶叶渣占卜！我还听说有人让你吸雪茄，然后解读烟灰来预测未来。
B:  All superstitious nonsense! I would still like to go to one and see what he or she has to say, just for kicks.全是迷信 nonsense！不过我还是想去试试，看看他或她能说什么，就当图个乐子。
A:  Great! I’ll make an appointment!太棒了！我这就去预约！
