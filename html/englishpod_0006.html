The Weekend - Road Trip(0006)

A:  So, are we all ready to go?所以，大家都准备好了吗？
B:  Yup, I think so. The car's packed; we have munchies and music, and the map's in the car.嗯，我想是的。车已经收拾好了，我们有零食、音乐，地图也在车里。
A:  Did you get the camera?你拿到相机了吗？
B:  Got it! Did you fill up the tank?拿到了！你加满油了吗？
A:  Yup, it's all set.是的，都准备好了。
B:  You're sure we're not forgetting anything?你确定我们没落下什么东西吗？
A:  I'm sure... we've got all our bases covered.我确定……我们已经把该准备的都备齐了。
B:  Well… let's get going then! I love road trips!好吧……那我们出发吧！我喜欢公路旅行！
B:  Um... do you think we can make a pit stop?呃……你觉得我们可以中途休息一下吧？
A:  But we've only been on the road for ten minutes.可我们上路才十分钟啊。
B:  I know, but I forgot to go to the bathroom before we left.我知道，但我们出发前我忘了上厕所。
