Daily Life - Registering for University(0110)

A:  Excuse me? Is this where I register? I'd like to sign up for my courses for next semester.打扰一下，这里是办理注册的地方吗？我想为下学期选课。
B:  Yes, of course. I need your student ID please.是的，当然可以。请出示你的学生证。
A:  Here you are.给您。
B:  Okay, Susan. It says here that you are a business major and you are in your second year. Is this information correct?好的，苏珊。这里显示你是商科专业，二年级学生。这些信息对吗？
A:  Yes. I do want to take some additional credits this year to get a minor in psychology.对的。我想今年多修一些学分，辅修心理学。
B:  Sure. That's not a problem. Do you have the list of courses you want to take this semester?没问题。你有本学期想上的课程列表吗？
A:  Yeah. Here's my list. I'm not sure if the class schedule will allow me to take all of them though.有的。这是我的课表。不过我不确定时间安排上能否全部修完。
B:  Yeah, that's perfect. What about the subjects for your minor?太好了。那你的辅修科目呢？
A:  Oh yeah! Almost forgot! I need to take fundamental linguistics, consumer psychology and neuroanatomy.哦对了！差点忘了！我需要修基础语言学、消费心理学和神经解剖学。
B:  Wow, you are going to be busy this semester! Okay, here you go. You are registered now; you'll have to make your first tuition payment before classes start.哇，这学期你肯定很忙了！好的，给你。你已经注册成功了；请在上课前缴纳第一笔学费。
