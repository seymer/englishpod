Global View - Taking The GMAT (C0320)

Ash:  I can’t seem to progress up the career ladder no matter how hard I try and I have been here for 2 years already!我怎么努力都感觉在职业阶梯上无法晋升，我在这里已经待了两年了！
Mindy:  Well, have you thought of getting an MBA? I heard it does wonders in getting you to the top.嗯，你考虑过读个 MBA 吗？听说那能帮你迅速登顶。
Ash:  An MBA hey… well my degree wasn’t in business, the business schools won’t be interested in me.MBA？呃……我的学位不是商科，商学院不会对我感兴趣。
Mindy:  Nonsense! The business schools measure your ability through a test called GMAT.胡说！商学院是通过一个叫 GMAT 的考试来衡量你的能力的。
Ash:  GMAT? What does that stand for and what will the test contain?GMAT？那是啥缩写，考试都考些什么？
Mindy:  Graduate Management Admission Test, it contains three parts; Analytical Writing Assessment, the Quantitative section, and the Verbal section. Not only does the test mark you on the number of questions answered but also on the difficulty.研究生管理专业入学考试，包含三个部分：分析写作评估、定量部分和言语部分。评分不仅看你答对多少题，还看题目的难度。
Ash:  Okay this sounds a little tough, how am I supposed to practice for this?好吧，听起来有点难，我该怎么准备呢？
Mindy:  Up to you, you could have a one on one session with a tutor or group sessions, you can also use free or private computer software. Going to church might help as well!随你选择，可以找导师一对一辅导，也可以参加小组课程，还能用免费或付费的软件练习。去教堂说不定也有帮助！
Ash:  No matter what I do I’m going to ace this test and go on to become a corporate fat cat!不管怎么做，我都要考高分，然后成为企业里的肥猫！
Mindy:  Umm…That’s the spirit!呃……这才是有干劲的表现！
