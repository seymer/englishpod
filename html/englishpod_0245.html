The Weekend - Getting A Subscription (C0245)

A:  Good afternoon Ma'am, My name is Mike and I am selling subscriptions to all sorts of periodicals. 下午好，女士。我是迈克，我在推销各类期刊的订阅服务。
B:  No thank you, I am not interested. 不用了，我不感兴趣。
A:  Please ma'am , if you could spare five minutes of your time, I am sure we could find something that interests you! 女士，如果您能抽出五分钟时间，我相信我们一定能找到您感兴趣的刊物！
B:  I wish I could, but Ihave to walk the dog and finish cooking so if you would excuse me. 我很想答应，但我得遛狗并做完晚饭，所以请原谅我。
A:  We have a great variety of magazines all about cooking! This one for example, is a bi monthly publication with recipes from all over the world! 我们有各种各样的烹饪杂志！比如这本，是双月刊，收录来自世界各地的食谱！
B:  Wow, that would be kind of useful, do you have any other cooking magazines? 哇，那还挺实用，你们还有其他烹饪杂志吗？
A:  Sure do! This one is a quarterly publication, but each issue has over 200 color pages of recipes and also many home decorating ideas! 当然有！这本是季刊，每期都有超过 200 页彩色食谱，还有许多家居装饰点子！
B:  Wow, this is nice! Ok, sign me up for both publications. 哇，这个不错！好，我把这两本都订了。
A:  You mentioned you have a dog, most pet owners sign up for this weekly newsletter that has information on dog care, pet shops and even pet sitters! 您提到您养狗，大多数宠物主都会订阅这份周报，里面有关于狗狗护理、宠物店甚至宠物保姆的信息！
B:  That is exactly what I needed! What else do you have? 这正是我需要的！你们还有什么别的吗？
A:  Well, I also have.... 嗯，我还有……
