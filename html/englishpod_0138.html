Daily Life - Renting A Car(0138)

Man:  Hi, I made a reservation for a mid-size vehicle. The name is Jimmy Fox.你好，我预订了一辆中型车，名字是吉米·福克斯。
Agent:  I'm sorry, we have no mid-size available at the moment.抱歉，目前我们没有中型车了。
Man:  I don't understand, I made a reservation, do you have my reservation?我不明白，我都预订了，你们有我的预订吗？
Agent:  Yes, we do, unfortunately we ran out of cars.有的，很遗憾我们车已经租光了。
Man:  But the reservation keeps the car here. That's why you have the reservation.但预订就是要把车留在这儿。这就是为什么你们有预订的原因。
Agent:  I know why we have reservations.我知道你们为什么有预订。
Man:  I don't think you do. If you did, I'd have a car. See, you know how to take the reservation, you just don't know how to hold the reservation and that's really the most important part of the reservation, the holding. Anybody can just take them.我想你并不知道。如果你知道的话，我就能有车了。你看，你知道怎么“拿”预订，但你不知道如何“保留”预订，而这正是预订中最关键的部分——保留。任何人都可以随便拿走预订。
Agent:  But we do have a compact or an SUV if you'd like.不过我们确实还有紧凑型车或 SUV，如果您愿意的话。
Man:  Fine. I'll take the compact.好的。我要那辆紧凑型车。
Agent:  Alright. We have a blue Ford Focus for you Mr. Fox. Would you like insurance?好的。福克斯先生，我们为您准备了一辆蓝色的福特福克斯。您需要购买保险吗？
Man:  Yeah, you better give me the insurance, because I am gonna beat the hell out of this car.是啊，你最好把保险给我，因为我打算狠狠修理这辆破车。
