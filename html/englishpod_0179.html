Key Vocabulary

infant婴儿baby宝贝
quake at the sight望而生畏shake or tremble when see something看到某物时颤抖或颤抖
afar远处from or to a great distance从或到很远的距离
saviour救星a person who rescues people from harm or danger将人们从伤害或危险中拯救出来的人
thy你的（古语）an old-fashioned, poetic, or religious word for 'your'表示“你的”的老式、诗意或宗教词
