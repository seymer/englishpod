Global View - The World Cup (C0257)

A:  What are you doing? 你在干嘛？
B:  What am I doing? What am I doing? Don't you know what day it is? 我在干嘛？我在干嘛？你不知道今天是什么日子吗？
A:  Ummm. no. 呃……不知道。
B:  It's only the day when the world's biggest sporting event is kicking off. 今天是全球最大体育赛事开幕的日子。
A:  What? 什么？
B:  The World Cup! The first match is today! It's Mexico vs. South Africa! It's going to be a really good match! Both teams have a very strong offense and have skilled players. I think that South Africa will probably dominate the first half since they are the host country, and all. 世界杯！首场比赛就在今天！墨西哥对阵南非！这将是一场非常精彩的比赛！两队都有很强的进攻能力和技术娴熟的球员。我认为南非队可能会主导上半场，毕竟他们是东道主，等等。
A:  I have no idea what you're talking about. The only sporting event we watch at home is the Super Bowl. 我完全听不懂你在说什么。我们在家看的唯一体育赛事就是超级碗。
B:  This is bigger than the Super Bowl, man! Teams from 32 countries compete against each other every four years and fight to win that trophy. They first start in a group stage with bigger groups, each group having 4 teams. The top 2 teams pass on to an elimination stage before going on to the quarterfinalsand semifinals.这比超级碗大得多，伙计！来自 32 个国家的球队每四年相互角逐一次，争夺那座奖杯。他们首先从小组赛开始，每组有四支球队，然后前两名晋级淘汰赛阶段，接着是四分之一决赛和半决赛。
A:  Sounds interesting, but soccer doesn't really appeal to me. 听起来挺有意思，但足球对我来说没什么吸引力。
B:  Are you kidding? Over seven hundred million people watched the final match of the World Cup! It's a very exciting and nerve wracking sport! Each nation is cheering on their team, hoping they will become the next champion. So far Brazil is the team with the most titles under their belt; they're really good! 你在开玩笑吗？超过七亿人观看了世界杯决赛！这是一项非常刺激又让人紧张的运动！每个国家都在为自己的球队加油助威，希望他们能成为下一届冠军。到目前为止，巴西队拥有的冠军头衔最多；他们真的很强！
A:  All I know about soccer is that you can't use your hands and that players are always falling down, trying to get a free kick or penalty kick. It seems like a sissy sport to me! 关于足球我全都知道：不能用手，而且球员老是倒地争任意球或点球。在我看来这简直是个娘炮运动！
B:  Whatever dude, I'm going to go watch the opening match. 得了吧哥们儿，我要去看揭幕战了。
