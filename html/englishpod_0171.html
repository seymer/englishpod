The Weekend - Buying a Camera (C0171)

A:  Hello, ma'am, can I help you find something? 你好，女士，有什么可以帮您？
B:  Yes, actually I'm looking to buy a camera. 是的，其实我想买台相机。
A:  We've got a wide selection do you know if you'd like a point-and-shoot, or something a little fancier? Are you shopping for yourself or for someone else? 我们这儿选择很多，您是想买个傻瓜机还是更高级一点的？您是给自己买还是给别人买？
B:  Actually I'm buying a camera for my husband. 实际上我是给我丈夫买的。
A:  Ah, well then I'd recommend a nice entry-level digital SLR. 啊，那我会推荐一款不错的入门级数码单反。
B:  Yeah? Can I take a look at the SLRs you carry? 是吗？我能看看你们这里的单反吗？
A:  Sure thing, follow me. This here is the. 当然可以，请跟我来，这是……
B:  The Canon Eos. Yeah it's ok, but I'm looking for something that performs better in low light, has a better display panel, and longer battery life. 佳能 EOS。嗯，还可以，但我想要低光环境下表现更好、屏幕更清晰、电池续航更长的。
A:  Oh, ah, umthe Nikon D60 is a nice option. 哦，呃，尼康 D60 是个不错的选择。
B:  Yeah, but what kit lens does this camera come with? I don't want some bulky telephoto lens. 是啊，但这台相机配的是什么镜头？我可不想买那种笨重的长焦镜头。
A:  Oh, well this one has the, uh. 哦，好吧，这个有那个，呃。
B:  Looks to me like an 18-55mm lens. pretty standard, that will do. Not like my husband will be stalking celebrities or anything! 看起来像是 18-55mm 镜头，很标准，这就够了。不像我丈夫会跟踪名人什么的！
A:  So, ahem, can I interest you in any acc...那么，咳咳，我能向您推荐任何附...
B:  Accessories? Do you carry polarizing filters? 配件吗？你们带偏振镜吗？
A:  Polarizing filter um we should! I'm sorry, ma'am. looks like we're sold out. 偏振镜啊，我们有！抱歉，女士。好像我们卖完了。
B:  No you're not! There are some right here! 不，你没有！这里就有几个！
A:  Oh, well, would you look at that! Po-la-ri-zing filters. 哦，天哪，你快看！偏振镜。
B:  Thanks for your help, Ralph! 谢谢你的帮助，拉尔夫！
A:  No problem, ma'am. 没问题，女士。

Key Vocabulary

point-and-shoot傻瓜相机Any compact non-SLR camera with non-interchangeable lenses, using automatic settings and simplified controls. Generally desired by novices and casual users.任何带有不可更换镜头的紧凑型非单反相机，使用自动设置和简化的控制。通常是新手和临时用户所需要的。
interest someone in使…感兴趣try to persuade someone to buy, do, or eat something尝试说服某人购买、做或吃某物
lens透镜a transparent optical device used to converge or diverge transmitted light and to form images用于会聚或发散透射光并形成图像的透明光学装置
polarizing filter偏振滤镜a camera lens filter used to control the plane of polarization of light entering the lens.用于控制进入镜头的光的偏振平面的相机镜头滤镜。
entry-level入门级simple and appropriate to an inexperienced person简单且适合没有经验的人

Supplementary Vocabulary

tripod三脚架a three-legged stand for a camera相机的三脚支架
filter滤镜a lens screen of dyed gelatin or glass for controlling color or for diminishing the intensity of light染色明胶或玻璃制成的透镜屏，用于控制颜色或减弱光强度
exposure曝光the process of light hitting a photosensitive surface, such as film or a digital image sensor光照射到感光表面（例如胶片或数字图像传感器）的过程
shutter快门a mechanical device for opening and closing the aperture of a camera lens to expose film用于打开和关闭相机镜头光圈以曝光胶片的机械装置
wide-angle lens广角镜头a lens which has a wide coverage angle so it can take in a great deal of scene, usually any lens shorter than 42mm具有宽覆盖角度的镜头，因此可以拍摄大量场景，通常是短于 42 毫米的镜头
