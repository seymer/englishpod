Daily Life - Pizza Delivary(0052)

A:  Good evening, Pizza House. This is Marty speaking. May I take your order?晚上好，披萨屋。我是马蒂，请问您需要点什么？
B:  Um yes… I’d like a medium pizza with pepperoni, olives, and extra cheese.嗯，好的……我想要一份中号披萨，上面放意大利辣香肠、橄榄和加量芝士。
A:  We have a two-for-one special on large pizzas. Would you like a large pizza instead?我们有大号披萨买一送一的活动。您要不要换成大号披萨？
B:  Sure, that sounds good.当然，听起来不错。
A:  Great! Would you like your second pizza to be the same as the first?太好了！您的第二份披萨要和第一份一样吗？
B:  No, make the second one with ham, pineapple and green peppers. Oh, and make it thin crust.不，第二份用火腿、菠萝和青椒。哦，还要薄底。
A:  Okay, thin crust. Your total is $21.50 and your order will arrive in thirty minutes or it's free!好的，薄底。您的总额是 21.50 美元，三十分钟内送达，否则免费！
B:  Perfect. Thank you. Bye..完美。谢谢。再见。
A:  Sir, wait!! I need your address!先生，等等！我需要您的地址！
