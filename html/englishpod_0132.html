Daily Life - Mechanic(0132)

A:  Howdy! Nice car! What seems to be the problem?嗨！好车啊！出什么问题了？
B:  I don't know! This stupid old car started spewing white smoke and it just died on me. Luckily, I managed to start it up and drive it here. What do you think it is?我不知道！这该死的破车突然冒出白烟，然后就在半路上熄火了。幸运的是，我勉强把它发动起来开到了这儿。你觉得会是什么毛病？
A:  Not sure yet. How about you pop the hood and we can take a look. Hmmm, it doesn't look good.现在还不确定。你先把引擎盖打开，我们看看情况。嗯……看起来不太妙。
B:  What do you mean? My daddy gave me this car for my birthday last month. It's brand new!什么意思？我爸爸上个月送给我当生日礼物的。这可是新车！
A:  Well missy, the white smoke that you saw is steam from the radiator. You overheated your engine so now the pistons are busted and so is your transmission. You should have called us and we could have towed you over here when your car died.小姐，你看到的白烟是散热器里的蒸汽。你把发动机过热了，现在活塞和变速箱都报废了。你应该早点叫我们的，那样车熄火了我们可以直接拖过来。
B:  Ugh... So how long is this going to take? An hour?唉……那要多久能修好？一个小时？
A:  I'm afraid a bit more than that. We need to order the spare parts, take apart your electrical system, fuel pump and engine and then put it back together again. You are going to have to leave it here for at least two weeks.恐怕不止。我们需要订购备件，拆下你的电气系统、燃油泵和发动机，然后再重新组装。你得把车留在这儿至少两周。
B:  What! How am I supposed to get to school or go shopping? This is not happening!什么！那我怎么去学校或者购物啊？这不可能！
