Global View - New Years Resolution (C0175)

A:  Did you read this? It says that the number one new years resolution make is to spend more time with friends and family. 你看过这个吗？上面说新年首要的决议就是多花时间和朋友家人在一起。
B:  Why would that be funny? 这有什么好笑的？
A:  Well, think about it. We are a society that is always on the go, not because we have to, but because we want to. 嗯，仔细想想吧。我们是一个总是忙忙碌碌的社会，不是因为必须如此，而是因为我们自己就想这样。
B:  Why? 为什么？
A:  We work hard and spend less time at home because we are trying to provide for our family with goods and services that are usually unnecessary. 我们努力工作却很少待在家里，因为我们试图用一些通常不必要的商品和服务来供养家人。
B:  I don't agree, but anyways, I think you should start thinking of a new year's resolution yourself. 我不这么认为，不过话说回来，我觉得你应该自己定个新年目标。
A:  What's the point? We always make a new year's resolution and by February we will have forgotten about it. It's pointless. 这有什么意义？我们每年都立新年决心，可到了二月就忘得一干二净了。毫无意义。
B:  Well then maybe you should resolve to sticking to your goals and objectives. 那也许你该下定决心坚持你的目标和计划。
A:  What about you? Your gym bag is gathering dust and you still have brand new running shoes that are yet to be jogged in. 那你呢？你的健身包都积灰了，那双崭新的跑鞋连一次都没穿过。
B:  Well, I would go if you kept your promise of going tothe gym with me everyday! 嗯，如果你能兑现每天和我一起去健身的承诺，我就去了！
A:  Yeah yeah whatever. 是啊是啊随便吧。
