Global View - Happy New Year! (C0340)

A::  It’s almost midnight! We are about to start a brand new year!快午夜了！我们要开始崭新的一年了！
B:  I know it’s so exciting! A new year is always like a clean slate.我知道这太令人兴奋了！新一年总是像一张白纸。
A::  fresh start to accomplish any dreams, objectives and goals.全新的开始，去实现任何梦想、目标和愿望。
A:  Do you have a New Year’s resolution?你有新年决心吗？
B:  I was thinking about it, but I’m never able to keep my New Year’s resolution. Last year for example I joined a gym and only went twice.我考虑过，但我从来无法坚持我的新年决心。比如去年我加入健身房，只去了两次。
A:  Yeah I know what you mean. That’s why this year I am keeping things more simple. Maybe like getting together with friends I haven’t seen in a long time, or doing some volunteering work.是啊，我明白你的意思。所以今年我把事情变得更简单。也许像和很久没见的朋友们聚会，或者做一些志愿工作。
B:  That seems reasonable. We should get together and watch the ball drop in Times Square.这听起来很合理。我们应该聚在一起去时代广场看倒计时球落下。
A:  Sure, as long as you don’t try to kiss me at midnight!当然，只要你不在午夜时试图吻我！
B:  Well, we can’t break tradition! It’s bad luck!好吧，我们不能打破传统！那是不吉利的！
