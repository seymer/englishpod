Daily Life - Building Your Dream Home (C0193)

A:  Mr. and Mrs. Robinson! Let's get straight to it. You have saved up your money for years and are now ready to build your dream home. What did you have in mind? 罗宾逊先生和太太！咱们直接切入正题。你们攒了多年的钱，现在终于准备好建造梦想中的家园了。你们心里想的是什么？
B:  A suburban bungalow straight out of the sixties! A perfect lawn with minimal landscaping. A brick patio in the backyard with an old-fashioned grill, quaint lawn furniture, and a swimming pool. A two-car carport, pastel siding and a gable roof. Completed with white shutters and a white picket fence !六十年代的郊区平房！完美的草坪，只需最少的景观布置。后院有个砖砌露台，配上复古烤架、古朴的庭院家具和一个游泳池。带两个车位的停车棚，淡彩色的外墙和山墙屋顶。再配上白色的百叶窗和白色栅栏！
C:  Uh, honey? 呃，亲爱的？
B:  In the living room we would have moss-green rugs and a fireplace with a stone mantle and wood paneling on the walls. In the kitchen, the cupboards would be a pale yellow and we would have a turquoise metal oven and vinyl flooring - 客厅里我们会铺上苔藓绿的地毯，壁炉配有石制壁炉架，墙壁采用木质护墙板。厨房里橱柜是淡黄色的，我们还会装一个青绿色的金属烤箱和乙烯基地板——
C:  Umm, sweetie, but I was thinking of a more modern style house. An open concept house, all glass, wood, metal, and concrete. 嗯，宝贝，不过我在想的是更现代风格的房子。开放式概念的房子，全玻璃、木材、金属和混凝土结构。
B:  But sweetums, there is always a lot of wasted space in those kinds of homes. Besides, it's just a fad. It doesn't have the homey feeling the old homes do. 但亲爱的，那种类型的房子总是有很多浪费的空间。而且，这不过是昙花一现的潮流罢了。它没有老房子那种温馨的感觉。
C:  Sweetie-pie it's not a lot of wasted space. It is relaxing and the house would be eco-friendly with an in-floor heating system and designed to retain the heat of the sun in the winter and keep the house cool in the summer. We would have solar panels on the roof - 甜心，那不是很多浪费的空间。那样很放松，而且房子是环保的，配有地暖系统，设计成冬季能保留阳光的热量，夏季保持室内凉爽。我们还会在屋顶安装太阳能板——
B:  Do you know how much those things cost? 你知道那些东西要多少钱吗？
C:  What about your vintage furniture, dearest? And instead of a lawn, which is also a lot of wasted space and would require environmentally harmful pesticides, we would have a fish pond in the backyard and a garden that would cover the whole yard so we could grow our own food! 你的古董家具怎么样，亲爱的？还有，与其留一片草坪（那既浪费空间又需要对环境有害的杀虫剂），不如在后院建个鱼池，再种满整个庭院的花园，这样我们就能自己种菜了！
B:  But buttercup, I thought you always said that you loved visiting your grandmother's house! 可是，金盏花，你不是常说最喜欢去奶奶家吗？
C:  And I thought you, Mr. Scientist, were all up on saving the planet with your technological advancements! 我还以为你这位“科学家先生”最擅长用科技拯救地球呢！
A:  Umm well I am just going to go get some coffee while you two keep discussing. 嗯，那我就先去喝杯咖啡，你们俩继续聊吧。
