Daily Life - Applying CPR (C0198)

A:  Hello everyone and welcome to our CPR for beginners course. First of all, does anyone know what CPR stands for? 大家好，欢迎来到我们的基础心肺复苏课程。首先，有人知道 CPR 代表什么吗？
B:  Cardiopulmonary resuscitation! 心肺复苏！
A:  That's right! We apply CPR in the case of cardiac arrest or pulmonary arrest.没错！我们在心脏骤停或呼吸停止的情况下实施心肺复苏。
B:  What does that mean? 那是什么意思？
A:  Well, basically if your heart stops pumping blood, or your lungs stop pumping air, then we need to get them going again! That's when we have to apply this procedure. Let's begin! I need a volunteer. 嗯，基本上如果你的心脏停止泵血，或者你的肺部停止泵气，那么我们需要让它们重新运作！这时候我们才需要执行这个程序。让我们开始吧！我需要一位志愿者。
B:  Me! Me! 我！我！
A:  Alright, come here and lay flat on your back. Let's suppose this young woman has stopped breathing. We must lift the person's chin so that we clear a pathway for air to get into the lungs. Then we place our mouth over the other person's mouth and blow air two or three times, like this. 好的，过来平躺。假设这位年轻女性已经停止呼吸。我们必须抬起患者的下巴，以清理气道让空气进入肺部。然后我们将嘴巴覆盖在另一人的嘴上，吹气两到三次，就像这样。
B:  Wait, what are you doing? I'm a married woman! You can't just try to kiss me like this! 等等，你在干什么？我是个已婚妇女！你不能这样对我做亲吻的动作！
A:  Ma’ am I'm not trying to kiss you! I am trying to demonstrate how to apply CPR in the case of an emergency. 女士，我不是想吻你！我是在演示紧急情况下如何实施心肺复苏。
B:  Well, ok. But no French kissing! 好吧，好的。但禁止法式热吻！
A:  As I was saying, we blow air through the mouth in this manner. Once this is done, we must try to get the heart going again. To do this, we place our hands over the person's chest, and press down firmly two or three times. 正如我刚才所说，我们像这样通过嘴吹气。完成这一步后，我们必须尝试让心脏再次跳动。为此，我们将手放在这个人的胸部，用力按压两到三次。
B:  Wait, what are you doing! You can't just kiss me then go for second base! 等等，你在干什么！你不能先吻我然后就去二垒了！
