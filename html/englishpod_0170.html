Daily Life - Wedding Doubts (C0170)

A:  Man, I'm freaking out! You gotta help me! 哥们儿，我吓坏了！你得帮我！
B:  Whoa whoa take it easy, relax. Geez, you're sweating like a pig! What's going on? 哎哟别慌，放松点。天哪，你汗流浃背的像头猪！到底怎么了？
A:  I can't go through with this! I just can't! I'm not ready for marriage! What was I thinking? I'm only thirty five years old! I've got my entire life ahead of me, adventures waiting! I can't settle down yet! 我真没法继续这场婚礼了！我就是不行！我还没准备好结婚呢！我当时在想什么啊！我才三十五岁！我还有大把人生在前方，等着我去冒险呢！我现在还不能安定下来！
B:  What are you talking about? It wasn't more than a month ago that you were rambling on about how you are tired of living the life of a bachelor and how you envy your friends that have a family! 你在说什么胡话？还不止一个月前你就唠叨着说受够了单身生活，羡慕那些有家庭的朋友！
A:  I know I know! 我知道我知道！
B:  Let's think this through. First of all, you cannot leave Amy atthe altar. Not only will she be humiliated and kill you, but she is the woman that you love andthe woman of your dreams! Why would you want to end that or jeopardize it like this? Second of all, you are just getting cold feet. You know deep down inside that you want to marry her, so cut the crap and do it! 咱们好好想想。首先，你不能在婚礼上甩了艾米。她不仅会丢脸，还会恨死你，而且她是那个你爱的人，是你梦寐以求的女人！你怎么可能想结束这段感情或者像这样毁掉它呢？其次，你只是有点临阵退缩。你知道内心深处你是想娶她的，所以别扯那些没用的，赶紧办吧！
B:  You’re right. I'm being crazy! Ok, I'm ready, lets do this! 你说得对。我疯了！好吧，我准备好了，咱们就这么办！
A:  Great! Phew! That was a close one ! You had me going there for a minute I thought I was gonna have to slap some sense intoyou 太好了！呼！真是虚惊一场！刚才可把我吓得不轻，我以为我得给你上一课呢
