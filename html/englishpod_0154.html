The Weekend - Rock Band (C0154)

A:  I’m forming a music band. 我要组建一个乐队。
B:  Do you already know how to play an instrument? 你懂乐器吗？
A:  Uh... Yeah! I've told you a thousand times that I'm learning to play the drums. Now that I know how to play well, I would like to form a rock band. 呃……对！我跟你说过无数次了，我正在学打鼓。现在我打得不错了，我想组个摇滚乐队。
B:  Aside from yourself, who are the other members of the band? 除了你，乐队里还有谁？
A:  We have a guy who plays guitar, and another who plays bass. Although we still haven’t found anyone to be our singer. You told me that you had some musical talent, right? 我们有个弹吉他的，还有个弹贝斯的。不过还没找到主唱。你不是说你有点音乐天赋吗？
B:  Yes, I’m a singer. 对，我是歌手。
A:  Perfect. So you can audition this weekend here at my house. 太好了。那这周末在我家试试音吧。
B:  Great! Wait here? You don't have enough room for the amplifiers, microphones or even your drums! By the way where do you keep them or practice? 太棒了！等等我？你家没地方放音箱、麦克风，甚至我的鼓啊！顺便问一下，你的乐器和练习场在哪？
A:  Dude? What are you talking about? It's right here! All we need is my Nintendo Wii and we are set! 哥们儿，你在说什么呢？就在这儿！我们只需要一台任天堂 Wii 就够了！
