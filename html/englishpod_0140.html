Daily Life - Buying a Computer(0140)

Customer:  So can you fix it?那能修好吗？
Sales Clerk:  I'm sorry sir. This computer is not broken or damaged. It's simply just too old! That's why your programs and applications are running slow. There really isn't much I can do.抱歉先生，这台电脑没坏也没受损，只是太旧了！这就是为什么您的程序和应用程序运行缓慢。我实在没什么办法。
Customer:  What do you mean? I bought this computer just three years ago!您这是什么意思？我才买了三年啊！
Sales Clerk:  Yes, but technology is ever changing and technology is becoming obsolete faster and faster!是的，但技术日新月异，设备淘汰的速度也越来越快！
Customer:  Ok, I know where this is going. How much will it cost me to get a new computer?好吧，我知道你要说什么了。换台新电脑要多少钱？
Sales Clerk:  Well, this desktop over here is our latest model. It has a four gigahertz processor with sixteen gigabytes in RAM and a hard disk with one terabyte. Of course, it includes a mouse, keyboard and desk speakers.嗯，这边这台台式机是我们最新的型号。它配备四千兆赫处理器、十六 GB 内存和一块一 TB 的硬盘。当然还包括鼠标、键盘和桌面音箱。
Customer:  I have no idea what you are talking about. I just want to know if it's good and if I will be able to play solitaire without the computer crashing or freezing all the time!我完全听不懂您在说什么。我只想知道这玩意儿好不好用，能不能让我打纸牌时不卡机、不死机！
Sales Clerk:  This PC is top of the line and I guarantee it will never freeze! If it does, we'll give you your money back!这款电脑是顶级配置，我保证绝对不会死机！如果真出了状况，我们全额退款！
