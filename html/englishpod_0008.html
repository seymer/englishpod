Daily Life - What's your name again?(0008)

A:  Nick! How's it going?尼克！最近怎么样？
B:  Oh, hey...哦，嗨……
A:  What are you doing in this neighbourhood? Do you live around here?你怎么会出现在这个街区？你住这儿附近吗？
B:  Actually, my office is right around the corner.其实，我的办公室就在街角。
A:  It was great to meet you last week at the conference. I really enjoyed our conversation about foreign investment.上周在会议上见到你真高兴。我特别喜欢我们关于外国投资的谈话。
B:  Yeah, yeah, it was really interesting. You know, I'm in a bit of a hurry, but here's my card. We should definitely meet up again and continue our discussion.是啊，是啊，真的很有意思。你知道的，我现在有点赶时间，这是我的名片。我们肯定得再聚聚，继续聊聊。
A:  Sure, you still have my contact details, right ?当然，你还有我的联系方式吧？
B:  You know what, this is really embarrassing, but your name has just slipped my mind. Can you remind me?你知道吗，这真让人尴尬，但我刚把你的名字忘了一时半会儿。你能提醒我吗？
A:  Sure, my name is Ana Ferris. Don't worry about it; it happens to me all the time. I'm terrible with names too.当然，我叫安娜·费里斯。别担心；这种事常发生在我身上。我也记不住名字。
