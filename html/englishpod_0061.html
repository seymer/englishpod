The Weekend - Swim faster!(0061)

A:  This is such a beautiful day! Great for sailing!今天天气真美！特别适合帆船运动！

B:  I get too tired! I'll just backstroke, it's easier!我太累了！我只会仰泳，那个轻松点！
A:  Try kicking your legs more. That's good. Don't go out too far!多踢腿试试。这样很好。别游太远！
B:  It's Jump in!快跳下去！
A:  Kathy! Get back here! I see a shark!凯西！回来！我看见鲨鱼了！
B:  Ahhhh!!!! Help me! Help! Bring the boat closer! The shark is coming straight towards me!啊啊啊！！救命！救命！把船划过来！鲨鱼正朝我冲过来！
A:  It's right under you! Kathy!!!!!它就在你下面！凯西！！
