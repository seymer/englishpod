The Weekend - Getting a manicure (C0174)

A:  Honey I'll be right back! 亲爱的，我马上回来！
B:  Where are you going? 你去哪儿？
A:  I told you already! I'm going to. get my nails done. 我都说过了！我要去做美甲。
B:  Again? You just went last week! You spend more time atthe nail salon than you do here at home! Honestly, why do you need a manicure every week?又去？你上周才去过！你在美甲沙龙待的时间比在家还多！老实说，你为什么要每周都做美甲？
A:  Well, first of all, I like to pamper myself, and my nails look great. You should come with me! 首先，我喜欢犒劳自己，而且我的指甲看起来很棒。你应该跟我一起去！
B:  Why? I don't want to have nail polish or anything like that! 为什么？我不想涂指甲油之类的东西！
A:  They don't only paint my nails! The manicurist will remove my cuticles, file my nails, and apply at least nails coats of nail polish! 他们不只是给指甲上色！美甲师会帮我修剪死皮、打磨指甲，还要至少涂三层指甲油！
B:  Yeah, sounds like something I should definitely do. 嗯，听起来是我绝对应该做的事。
