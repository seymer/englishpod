Daily Life - What If? Part 2(0141)

A:  This is the good life! We have it good don't you think?这就是美好的生活！我们过得很好，不是吗？
B:  Yeah of course! Although, don't you ever wonder what "could have been"?当然！不过，你有没有想过“本可以怎样”？
A:  What do you mean?你是说真的？
B:  Well, sometimes I think of how things could have turned out if I had done things a little differently.嗯，有时候我会想，如果当初我稍微做点不一样的选择，事情会有怎样的不同。
A:  For example?比如呢？
B:  Like for example, if I hadn't studied architecture, I would have become an artist like I wanted to.比如说，如果我没学建筑，我就能成为我想当的画家了。
A:  I see. Yeah now that I think of it, I wouldn't have gotten married if I hadn't moved to this town and met Sally.我明白了。是啊，这么一想，如果我没搬来这个小镇遇到莎莉，我就不会结婚了。
B:  You see! Everything happens for a reason! We wouldn't even have met if I hadn't been in that car accident ten years ago!你看！凡事都有原因的！要不是十年前那场车祸，我们甚至都不会相遇！
A:  Well, I have no regrets!好吧，我毫无遗憾！
B:  I'll drink to that!那就为这干杯！
