Daily Life - Dr. Plumber(0112)

A:  Good afternoon! Did you call for a plumber?下午好！您叫了水管工吗？
B:  Yes, yes I did. Please come in! I'm so glad you came! This old house is falling apart! Come on into the bathroom. See, here, there's water leaking everywhere!是的，我确实叫了。请进！真高兴您能来！这老房子都快散架了！快进来看看浴室。瞧，到处都是漏水！
A:  I see. Let me have a look. It seems that your toilet is clogged, and that's why it won't flush. Let me just get my plunger. No, that's not working either. I suspect that there's some sort of foreign object in the pipes that's causing a blockage. That's what's making your toilet overflow.明白了。让我检查一下。看来您的马桶堵住了，所以冲不下去。我先拿个皮搋子试试。不行，这也没用。我怀疑管道里有什么异物造成了堵塞。这就是导致马桶溢水的原因。
B:  Oh, that must be because of my four-year-old daughter. She is always flushing things down the toilet. You know how kids are.哦，那肯定是我四岁的女儿干的。她总往马桶里扔东西。您知道孩子都这样。
A:  Yeah, I have a little one myself. Anyway, these water pipes are really rusty, so they also should be changed. That could be causing water to not drain completely; that might lead to more problems in the future. I would also suggest fixing this faucet that isn't shutting off properly. I could have it all finished by today if it's urgent.是啊，我自己也有个小孩。不过，这些水管真的生锈了，也该更换一下。这可能导致水排不干净；将来可能会引发更多问题。我还建议修一下那个关不严的水龙头。如果情况紧急，我今天就能全部完工。
B:  That would be great! Is it expensive?那太好了！贵吗？
A:  Let's see... I would say about eight hundred dollars.让我想想……大概八百美元。
B:  What? That's more than I make in a day and I'm a heart surgeon!什么？这比我一天挣的还多，我可是心脏外科医生！
