Global View - At The Car Dealership (C0278)

A:  Hi there! I am looking for a new car. I have this old Ford Pinto that I would like to trade in. 你好！我想买辆新车。我有一辆旧福特 Pinto，想把它置换掉。
B:  I see. You are in luck this month because all of our models are on sale! it is a perfect time to buy a new car since it’s the end of the year, 明白。您运气真好，本月所有车型都在促销！年底了，正是买新车的完美时机。
A:  Perfect! I like this one. 太好了！我喜欢这一款。
B:  That is the Ford Focus. A very light but powerful vehicle. It comes with dual side airbags, power steering and power windows, tinted windows and your choice of either automatic or manual transmission. 这是福特 Focus。一款非常轻便但动力强劲的车型。配备双侧安全气囊、动力转向和电动窗、深色车窗，以及自动或手动变速箱供您选择。
A:  Sounds like a good car! How many miles to the gallon? 听起来不错！每加仑能跑多少英里？
B:  It is a very fuel efficient vehicle giving you about 34 miles in the city and 40 on the highway. 这是一款燃油效率很高的车，市区约 34 英里，高速约 40 英里。
A:  That is really convenient. Especially now that fuel prices are so high! What’s under the hood? 真方便。尤其是现在油价这么高！引擎怎么样？
B:  A very powerful 2.5-liter turbocharged engine, Trust me, this car is fast! 一台非常强劲的 2.5 升涡轮增压引擎。相信我，这车很快！
A:  Now for the most difficult question. What is the price tag for this lovely vehicle? 最难的问题来了：这辆可爱的车标价多少？
B:  Very affordable sir. You can take it out of this lot today with 0% down payment and no interest for the first year! You can test drive it now and we can sign the papers when we get back. 价格很实惠，先生。今天就能把车开走，首付零元，第一年免息！您可以先试驾，我们回来后再签字。
A:  Great! Let’s do it! 太棒了！就这么办吧！
