Elementary - Difficult Customer(0001)

A:  Good evening. My name is Fabio. I'll be your waiter for tonight. May I take your order?晚上好，我是法比奥。今晚由我为您服务。请问可以点餐了吗？
B:  No, I'm still working on it. This menu isn't even in English. What's good here?不，我还在想呢。这菜单上连英文都没有。你们这儿有什么好吃的？
A:  For you sir, I would recommend spaghetti and meatballs.先生，我推荐您意大利肉酱面。
B:  Does it come with coke and fries?会配可乐和薯条吗？
A:  It comes with either soup or salad and a complimentary glass of wine, sir.我们搭配的是汤或沙拉，还有一杯免费葡萄酒，先生。
B:  I'll go with the spaghetti and meatballs, salad and the wine.那就来份意大利肉酱面、沙拉和那杯酒吧。
A:  Excellent choice. Your order will be ready soon.很好的选择。您的餐点很快就好。
B:  How soon is soon?“很快”是多久？
A:  Twenty minutes?二十分钟？
B:  You know what? I'll just go grab a burger across the street.你知道的，我还是去街对面买个汉堡算了。
