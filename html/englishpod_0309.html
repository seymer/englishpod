Global View - Indian Food (C0309)

A:  So where is this mystery restaurant that we are going to? 我们要去的那家神秘餐厅在哪里？
B:  It’s an Indian restaurant! I know you have never had Indian food, so I thought you might want to try. 那是一家印度餐厅！我知道你从未吃过印度菜，所以我想你可能想尝尝。
A:  That sounds great! I am craving some type of beef dish. 听起来很棒！我特别想吃某种牛肉料理。
B:  Well, Indian cuisine actually doesn't serve beef. You see, cows are a sacred animal, a very important element inthe Hindu religion, so beef is not eaten. 嗯，印度菜实际上不提供牛肉。你看，牛是神圣的动物，在印度教中是非常重要的一部分，所以不吃牛肉。
A:  I see, so what are we having? Chicken? 我明白了，那我们吃什么？鸡肉吗？
B:  There are many amazing dishes to choose from. We can havesome chicken tikka masalawhich is an amazing curry. It’s a bit spicy, but I think you can handle it. 有很多美味的菜肴可供选择。我们可以点一些咖喱鸡 Tikka Masala，这是一道非常棒的咖喱。有点辣，但我想你能应付得来。
A:  Sounds good! I have always heard that Indian spices give a rich flavor to food. 听起来不错！我一直听说印度的香料能给食物带来丰富的风味。
B:  Yeah. Also, we can have some Naan bread which is baked in a tandoori oven. Since you don’t use any utensils to eat, you can use this bread to scoop upthe curry or rice. 是的。另外，我们还可以吃烤馕 Naan 面包，这是在塔多里烤箱中烤制的。既然你们不用餐具进食，可以用这种面包舀起咖喱或米饭。
A:  What about veggies? 蔬菜呢？
B:  They have a good variety of vegetable based dishes like palak paneer, vegetable samosas or Daal. 他们有很多以蔬菜为主的菜肴，比如菠菜奶酪 Palak Paneer、蔬菜萨莫萨 Samosas 或者扁豆 Daal。
A:  It all sounds exquisite! I can’t wait! 听起来太棒了！我等不及了！
