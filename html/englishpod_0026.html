Daily Life - New Year's Resolution(0026)

A:  So, did I tell you about my New Year's resolution? I've decided to go on a diet.所以，我跟你说过我的新年决心了吗？我决定节食了。
B:  And you're going to completely transform your eating habits, right?那你打算彻底改变饮食习惯对吧？
A:  Exactly! I'm going to cut out all that junk I eat; no more chips, no more soda, no more fried food.没错！我要戒掉所有那些垃圾食品；再也不吃薯片、不再喝汽水、再也不吃油炸食物。
B:  I've heard this one before.我以前也听过这种说法。
A:  But this time I'm going to stick to it. I really mean it! Trust me, Carol, I'm going to be a new man in one year's time!但这次我会坚持下去的。我是认真的！相信我，卡罗尔，一年后我会变成一个全新的男人！
B:  Well, I guess we'll just have to wait and see.好吧，我想我们只能拭目以待了。
A:  Thanks, honey, that was a great meal. I'm stuffed. Do we have any chips left?谢谢亲爱的，这顿饭太棒了。我饱了。我们还剩下薯片吗？
