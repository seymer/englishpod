Daily Life - Getting A Nanny (C0272)

Grace:  Hey Mel! Are you up for some tennis today? 嘿，梅！今天有空打网球吗？
Mel:  Sorry, I can’t! I have to go to work, pick up Jake and Maddie from school, and make them an afternoon snack, then take Jake to soccer practice and Maddie to dance class. 抱歉，不行！我得去上班，接杰克和麦迪放学，给他们做下午点心，然后送杰克去足球训练，再送麦迪去舞蹈课。
Grace:  You sound exhausted. Maybe you should hire a nanny to help you out! She can pick the kids up and take them to their after-school activities. She can also help you do some household chores, and run some errands. 你听起来累坏了。也许你应该请个保姆帮你一把！她能接送孩子，带他们参加课后活动。她还能帮你做些家务，顺便跑跑腿。
Mel:  Oh, I don't know... it's hard to find the right nanny .You have to consider her previous work experience, the responsibilities you give her, and how she interacts with the kids. I would love to have someone to help me out, though. 哦，我不知道……找个合适的保姆很难。你得考虑她之前的工作经验、你要交给她的职责，以及她如何与孩子相处。不过，我还是希望能有人帮我分担一下。
Grace:  I think you should definitely consider it! This way you won’t have to juggle such a busy schedule, and you’ll still get to spend time with the kids in the evenings. I can refer you this great nanny Amy. She used to work for my neighbors, before they moved away. She’s very responsible, a good cook, and great with kids. 我觉得你绝对应该认真考虑这件事！这样你就不用手忙脚乱地应付这么忙的时间表了，晚上还能陪孩子们。我可以介绍给你一位很棒的保姆艾米。她以前在我邻居那儿工作，后来他们搬走了。她很有责任心，厨艺好，也很会带孩子。
Mel:  Oh, that’s great. Thanks Grace. Can you give me her number? I’ll talk it over with Dan and give her a call tomorrow. Maybe this way I won’t be so tired every day, and Dan and I might even get to go on a date once in a while .哦，那太好了。谢谢格蕾丝。你能给我她的电话吗？我会和丹商量一下，明天给她打个电话。也许这样我就不会每天都这么累了，说不定丹和我偶尔还能约个会呢。
