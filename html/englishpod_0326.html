Daily Life - Report Card (C0326)

A:  Look, Jimmy’s report came today.瞧，吉米的报告单今天到了。
B:  Let’s have a look. What is this? Where are all the grades?咱们看看。这是什么？所有的成绩都在哪儿呢？
A:  He’s in the third grade Sam! You see under each subject that he is being taught in school, he receives a mark from one to three. A one means his achievement or work is excellent. Here in Science for example he got a two, which means its satisfactory.他可是三年级的学生啊！你看，在每门他在校学习的科目下面，都会给出一个从一到三的评分。一分代表他的表现或作业非常出色。比如这里的科学课，他得了两分，意思是“令人满意”。
B:  What about here in physical education?那体育呢？
A:  He got a three here which means it’s unsatisfactory. We should work on that with him.他这儿得了三分，意思是不令人满意。我们得跟他一起努力改进一下。
B:  So confusing! In my day we got an A or B if we were doing well and if we failed an exam we would get an F!真让人摸不着头脑！我上学的时候，表现好就是拿 A 或 B，要是考试不及格才会拿到 F！

satisfactory令人满意的fulfilling all demands or requirements体育课呢？
exam考试formal test taken at the end of a course课程结束时进行的正式测试
