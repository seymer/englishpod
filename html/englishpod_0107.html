Daily Life - Complaining at a Restaurant(0107)

A:  Excuse me, waiter? Waiter!打扰一下，服务员？喂！
B:  Yes, sir? What can I do for you?哎，先生？有什么可以帮您？
A:  I've been sitting here for the past twenty minutes and no one has offered me a glass of water, brought any bread to the table and our appetizers haven't been served yet! You know, in this kind of establishment, I'd expect much better service.我已经坐在这儿二十分钟了，没人给我倒杯水，也没人把面包端上桌，开胃菜也还没上！您知道，在这种餐厅，我本该期待更好的服务。
B:  I am sorry, sir. I'll check on your order right away.抱歉，先生。我马上为您检查一下订单。
C:  Relax honey, the place is busy tonight, but I've heard the food is amazing. Anyway...别急亲爱的，今晚店里很忙，不过听说食物很棒。总之……
B:  Here you are, sir. The foie gras for the lady, and a mushroom soup for you.给您，先生。这是那位女士的鹅肝，您的蘑菇汤。
A:  Waiter, I ordered a cream of mushroom soup with asparagus. This soup is obviously too runny, and it's over-seasoned. It's completely inedible!服务员，我要的是奶油蘑菇汤配芦笋。这汤明显太稀了，而且调味过重。完全没法吃！
B:  Okay, I do apologize for that. Can I bring you another soup, or would you like to order something else?抱歉，确实是我的错。我可以再给您上一碗汤，还是您想点别的？
A:  Take this foie gras back as well, it's rubbery and completely overcooked. And look at the portion size! How can you charge twenty-five dollars for a sliver of duck liver?这道鹅肝也退了吧，口感像橡胶一样，完全煮老了。再看看分量！怎么敢收二十五美元就只给这么一小片鸭肝？
B:  Right away... sir.马上为您处理……先生。
C:  Honey come on! The foie gras was fine, why are you making such a big deal? Are you trying to get our meal comped again?亲爱的，别闹了！鹅肝本来就很不错，你干嘛这么小题大做？你是想让我们这顿饭再免费吗？
A:  What do you mean? We are paying for this. If I'm shelling out my hard earned bucks, I expect value for money!什么意思？我们可是付了钱的。如果我掏腰包赚来的辛苦钱，我当然期望物有所值！
B:  Here you are, sir. I hope it is alright now. The chef has prepared it specially for you.先生，给您。希望您现在满意了。厨师特意为您重新做的。
A:  Yes, fine.好的，没问题。
C:  Honey, are you alright?亲爱的，你没事吧？
