Daily Life - Making A Dinner Reservation (C0357)

A:  Bruno Bistro, how may I help you? Bruno Bistro，您好，有什么可以帮您的吗？
B:  Yes hello, I would like to make a reservation please. 是的，你好，我想预订一个座位。
A:  Certainly sir, For which day and time please? 当然，先生，请问是哪一天哪个时间？
B:  Tonight at seven. 今晚七点。
A:  I’m sorry sir, but we are fully booked tonight until eight. 抱歉先生，我们今晚直到八点都客满了。
B:  In that case, eight o'clock is fine. 那八点钟没问题。
A:  Very well, and how many people will attend tonight? 好的，请问今晚几位用餐？
B:  Four people. 四位。
A:  Lastly, may I please know what name I should make the reservation under? 最后，请问我应该用哪个名字为您预订？
A:  Mark. 马克。
