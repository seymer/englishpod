The Office - Interview Skills 6 - Describing Ones Strengths (C0250)

Mr. Parsons:  Okay Rebecca. Now youyou've given me a good idea of your work and academic background, but what about you as a person? How would you describe your key strengths? 好的，Rebecca。现在你已经让我对你的工作和学术背景有了很好的了解，那作为一个人呢？你会如何描述你的主要优势？
Rebecca:  Well Mr. Parsons, as I mentioned before I'm someone who needs new challenges. I'm really focused and hard hard-working. I think my academic results prove this. 好吧，Parsons 先生，正如我之前提到的，我是一个需要新挑战的人。我非常专注且勤奋。我认为我的学业成绩可以证明这一点。
Mr. Parsons:  Yes, true, but how about other personal qualities? 是的，确实如此，但其他个人品质呢？
Hmmmmm, that's a tough question. But I would have to say my passion. I'm really passionate about journalism and passionate about my career.嗯……这是个棘手的问题。但我不得不说，是我的热情。我对新闻事业充满热情，也对职业生涯充满热情。
Rebecca:  Well I believe I'm a good team-player, but I can also work well independently. I'm very enthusiastic and , well I hope my colleagues would agree, I'm fun to work with.我相信我是个优秀的团队合作者，同时也能很好地独立工作。我非常热情，而且——希望我的同事们会同意——我很合群、容易相处。
Mr. Parsons:  What would you say is your most positive quality? 你会说你最积极的品质是什么？
Rebecca:  Hmmmmm, that's a tough question. But I would have to say my passion. I'm really passionate about journalism and passionate about my career. 嗯……这是个棘手的问题。但我不得不说，是我的热情。我对新闻事业充满热情，也对职业生涯充满热情。
