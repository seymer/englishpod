Daily Life - Cut In Line(0005)

A:  I can't believe it took us two hours to get here. The traffic in New York is unbelievable.我真不敢相信我们花了两个小时才到这儿。纽约的交通简直没法形容。
B:  Yeah, but just relax honey, we're here and we're going on vacation. In a few hours we'll be in Hawaii, and you'll be on the golf course.是啊，但亲爱的，放松点，我们已经到了，马上要开始度假了。几小时后我们就在夏威夷，而你将在高尔夫球场打球。
A:  Oh no! Look at that line! It must be a mile long! There's no way I'm waiting for another two hours.哦不！看那条队伍！肯定有一英里长！我绝不可能再等两个小时。
B:  Honey... don't...亲爱的……别……
C:  Hey man, the end of the line is over there.嘿，伙计，队伍的尽头在那边。
A:  Yeah...是啊……
C:  No seriously, I was here first, and you can't cut in line like this.说真的，我先到的，你不能这样插队。
A:  Says who?谁说的？
C:  I do!是我！
A:  So sue me!那就告我吧！
C:  Alright...that's it....好吧……就这样吧……
