Global View - Presidential Speech II(0129)

A:  We now stand ten years past the midpoint of a century that has witnessed four major wars among great nations. Three of these involved our own country. Despite the carnage of these conflicts, America is today the strongest, the most influential and most productive nation in the world. We are understandably proud of this pre-eminence, yet we realize that America's leadership and prestige depend, not merely upon our unmatched material progress, riches and military strength, but on how we use our power in the interests of world peace and human betterment.我们现在站在一个世纪的中间点之后十年，这个世纪见证了四大强国之间的四次主要战争。其中三次涉及我们自己的国家。尽管这些冲突造成了巨大伤亡，但美国如今仍是世界上最强大、最具影响力且生产力最高的国家。我们对这种领先地位感到自豪是理所当然的，但我们认识到，美国的领导地位和声望不仅取决于我们无与伦比的物质进步、财富和军事实力，更取决于我们如何运用权力以维护世界和平与人类福祉。
A:  Throughout America's adventure in free government, such basic purposes have been to keep the peace; to foster progress in human achievement, and to enhance liberty, dignity and integrity among peoples and among nations.在美国追求自由政府的历程中，这些基本目标始终如一：保持和平；推动人类成就的进步；提升各国人民之间的自由、尊严与正直。
A:  We pray that peoples of all faiths, all races, all nations, may have their great human needs satisfied; that those now denied opportunity shall come to enjoy it to the full; that all who yearn for freedom may experience its spiritual blessings; that those who have freedom will understand, also, its heavy responsibilities; that all who are insensitive to the needs of others will learn charity; that the scourges of poverty, disease and ignorance will be made to disappear from the earth, and that, in the goodness of time, all peoples will come to live together in a peace guaranteed by the binding force of mutual respect and love.我们祈愿所有信仰、种族和国家的人民，其伟大的人类需求都能得到满足；那些目前被剥夺机会的人能够充分享受它；所有渴望自由的人都能体验其精神福祉；拥有自由的人也能理解其所肩负的重任；对所有他人需求漠不关心的人将学会慈善；让贫困、疾病和无知的灾难从地球上消失，并在适当的时候，所有人民将在相互尊重与爱的约束力保障下和平共处。
A:  Now, on Friday noon, I am to become a private citizen. I am proud to do so. I look forward to it. Thank you, and good night.现在，周五中午，我将成为一名普通公民。我为此感到自豪。我对此充满期待。谢谢，晚安。
