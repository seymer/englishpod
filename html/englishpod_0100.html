Daily Life - Where are you from?(0100)

A:  Where to, miss?去哪，小姐？
B:  Hi! Crenshaw and Hawthorne, at the Holiday Inn that is on that corner.嗨！克伦肖和霍桑酒店，就在拐角处的那家假日酒店。
A:  Sure thing. So, where are you flying in from?没问题。您是从哪儿飞来的？
B:  From China.中国。
A:  Really? You don't look very Chinese to me, if you don't mind me saying so.真的吗？恕我直言，您看起来不太像中国人。
B:  It's fine. I am actually from Mexico. I was in China on a business trip, visiting some local companies that manufacture bathroom products.没关系。我其实来自墨西哥。我去中国出差，拜访了一些生产卫浴产品的当地公司。
A:  Wow sounds interesting! Excuse me if I am being a bit nosy but, how old are you?哇，听起来很有趣！如果我有点冒昧的话请原谅，请问您多大年纪了？
B:  Don't you know it's rude to ask a lady her age?难道你不知道问女士年龄是不礼貌的吗？
A:  Don't get me wrong! It's just that you seem so young and already doing business overseas!别误会！只是觉得您这么年轻就已经在海外做生意了！
B:  Well thank you! In that case, I am 26 years old, and what about yourself?谢谢！既然如此，我今年 26 岁，那您呢？
A:  I am 40 years old and was born and raised here in the good old U.S of A, although I have some Colombian heritage.我今年 40 岁，出生并成长于美国这片古老而美好的土地，尽管我有一些哥伦比亚血统。
B:  Really? That's great! Do you speak some Spanish?真的吗？太棒了！你会说西班牙语吗？
A:  Uh... yeah.. of course!呃……是的，当然会！
B:  Que bien! Entonces podemos hablar en espanol!太好了！那我们可以用西班牙语交流了！
