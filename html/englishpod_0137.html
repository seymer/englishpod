Daily Life - Getting Internet Service(0137)

A:  Welcome to Galanet. How can I help you?欢迎来到 Galanet，有什么可以帮您的吗？
B:  Hi. I would like to get an internet plan for my house.你好，我想给我的房子办一个上网套餐。
A:  Of course. We have three different plans with different prices you can choose from. The first one is the cheapest but most basic plan which is thirty dollars a month. This is for broadband internet with a download speed of five hundred and twelve kbps.当然可以。我们有三种不同价格的套餐供您选择。第一种是最便宜但也是最基础的套餐，每月三十美元。这是宽带网络，下载速度为五百一十二 kbps。
B:  I have no idea what kbps means. I just want to be able to get online, play games and chat with my friends. Oh, and watch movies online as well.我不明白 kbps 是什么意思。我只想能够上网、玩游戏和跟朋友聊天。哦，还要在线看电影。
A:  Well, this connection might be a bit too slow for your needs. I suggest you get the premium package for fifty dollars a month which includes a connection speed of two megabytes. That way you can play games online without any lag. This package also includes a wireless router and a personal firewall absolutely free!嗯，这个连接速度可能有点太慢，无法满足您的需求。我建议您选择每月五十美元的优质套餐，包含两兆的网速。这样您就能流畅地在线玩游戏了。该套餐还免费赠送无线路由器和个人防火墙！
B:  Do I have to pay an installation fee?我需要付安装费吗？
A:  Lucky for you, this month we aren't charging our normal installation fee. You are saving yourself 100 bucks right there! And we'll throw in this pen drive!幸运的是，本月我们免收常规安装费。您直接省了一百块！我们还额外送您这个 U 盘！
B:  Awesome!太棒了！
