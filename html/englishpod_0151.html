Advanced - Darwin's Theory Of Evolution (E0151)

A:  It’s been a long time since I last saw you. Where have you been? 好久不见，你这段时间去哪了？
B:  The exams and plans I have to turn in in are driving me crazy, I don’t even have time to sleep. 考试和要交的计划让我快疯了，我连睡觉的时间都没有。
A:  It’s the same for me. I'm up to my neck in work, but at least finals are coming soon and we’ll have a vacation. Where are you going now? 我也一样，工作忙得不可开交，不过好在期末考试快到了，我们就能放假了。你现在要去哪？
B:  I’m going to Anthropology class and now with the year anniversary of Darwin, it’s the only thing we study. Frankly, I'm sick and tired and tired of hearing about this guy. 我去上人类学课，现在正值达尔文诞辰周年纪念日，所以这是我们唯一研究的课题。说实话，我已经听腻了这个家伙了。
A:  What? Why? How can you not like Darwin? I mean the man changed the entire perception of how things came to and his theory is backed by pretty solid evidence! 什么？为什么？你怎么会不喜欢达尔文？我是指，这个人彻底改变了我们对事物起源的认知，而且他的理论有相当扎实的证据支持！
B:  I don’t like him. His theory of human evolution and natural selection is full of holes. It lacks the solid evidence of which you speak of. 我就是不喜欢他。他关于人类进化和自然选择的理论漏洞百出。根本没有什么你所说的那种确凿证据。
A:  That statement puts you at odds with half of the academy. Not to mention your professors! Furthermore, the explanation proposed by Darwin about the origin of species and the mechanism of natural selection constitutes a grand step toward a coherent understanding of the world and evolutionist ideas. 这话可就把你和半个学术界对立起来了，更别提你的教授们了。此外，达尔文提出的物种起源和自然选择机制的解释，构成了对世界和进化论思想连贯理解的一大步。
B:  I'm not minimizing his grand contributions, it’s just that his theory reminds of the conundrum of the chicken and the egg. 我并没有贬低他的巨大贡献，只是他的理论让人联想到“先有鸡还是先有蛋”的难题。
A:  What are you talking about? 你在说什么呢？
B:  The question is, which was first? The chicken or the egg? I feel the same regarding his theory. How does the first cell of life come to be? 问题是，哪个先来？鸡还是蛋？我对他的理论也有同样的疑问。生命的第一细胞是如何产生的？
A:  Interesting. I think that question is better suited for my philosophy class. In the meantime, how about we settle this... with a due! 有趣。我觉得这个问题更适合在我的哲学课上讨论。趁现在，要不我们用一顿饭来解决这件事吧……
