The Weekend - Playing Chess(0139)

Daddy:  Bobby! Come here, look what I got you!Bobby！过来，看看我给你买了什么！
Bobby:  What is that?那是什么？
Daddy:  A chess board! Daddy is going to teach you how to play!一副棋盘！爸爸要教你下棋了！
Bobby:  Cool!太酷了！
Daddy:  Ok, each player gets 16 pieces. You can be the white ones and I'll play with the black pieces. Now in the front, you set up the pawns. Those are the least valuable pieces and can only move one space forward. When you are about to capture another piece, it can move one space diagonally.好，每位玩家各得 16 枚棋子。你可以执白子，我执黑子。现在在前排摆上兵。它们是最不值钱的棋子，只能向前直走一格。当你准备吃掉对方棋子时，可以斜着走一格。
Bobby:  What about all these other pieces?那其他这些棋子呢？
Daddy:  See this one that looks like a tower? It's called the rook. The one with the tall hat is called the bishop. See this little horsey? This is called the knight, it's a very important piece so it's best to not let your opponent capture it.看这个像塔一样的，叫车。戴高帽的叫象。看见那个小马蹄了吗？这叫马，是个非常重要的棋子，最好别让对手吃掉它。
Bobby:  And these two? They are husband and wife?那这两个呢？是夫妻吧？
Daddy:  That's right! That's the queen and that's the king. If the other player captures your king, he will say "Check Mate" and the game is over! Doesn't this sound fun?没错！那是王后和国王。如果对方把你的国王吃掉了，他就会说“将死”，游戏结束！这听起来不有趣吗？
Bobby:  Nah! This is boring! I'm gonna go play Killer Zombies on my PlayStation!没趣！真无聊！我要去 PlayStation 上玩《杀手僵尸》了！
