The Office - Asking For A Raise (C0188)

A:  Excuse me sir, may I talk to you? 打扰一下，先生，我可以和您谈谈吗？
B:  Bill! Sure, come on in. What can I do for you? 比尔！当然，请进。有什么事需要我帮忙？
A:  Well sir, as you know, I have been an employee of this prestigious firm for over ten years. 先生，如您所知，我在这里这家知名公司已经工作了十多年了。
B:  Yes. 是的。
A:  I won't beat around the bush. Sir, I would like a raise. I currently have three companies after me and so I decided to talk to you first. 我不绕弯子了。先生，我想加薪。目前有三家公司在挖我，所以我决定先来找您谈。
B:  A raise? Son, I would love to give you a raise, but this is just not the right time. 加薪？孩子，我很乐意给你加薪，但这确实不是合适的时机。
A:  I understand your position, and I know that the current economic downturn has had a negative impact on sales, but you must also take into consideration my hard work, pro-activeness and loyalty to this company for over a decade. 我明白您的立场，我也知道当前的经济低迷对销售产生了负面影响，但您也必须考虑到我为这家公司工作十多年来所付出的辛勤努力、积极主动和忠诚。
B:  Taking into account these factors, and considering I don't want to start a brain drain, I'm willing to offer you a ten percent raise and an extra five days of vacation time. How does that sound? 综合考虑这些因素，并且考虑到我不希望人才流失，我愿意给您提供百分之十的加薪以及额外的五天假期。您觉得怎么样？
A:  Great! It's a deal! Thank you, sir! 太好了！成交！谢谢您，先生！
B:  Before you go, just out of curiosity, what companies were after you? 在您离开之前，出于好奇，是哪家公司在挖您？
A:  Oh, the electric company, gas company and water company! 哦，电力公司、燃气公司和自来水公司！

Key Vocabulary

beat around the bush拐弯抹角avoid the subject by talking about something else通过谈论其他事情来回避这个话题
raise加薪increase in salary工资增加
take into consideration考虑到to think about something before making a decision在做出决定之前考虑某事
offer优惠give with the choice of accepting or rejecting给予，可以选择接受或拒绝
loyalty忠诚the quality of being loyal忠诚的品质
