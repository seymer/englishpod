The Office - Malfunction(0057)

A:  Hey Carl, can you make a copy of this contract for me please? When you have it ready, send it out ASAP to our subbranch.嘿卡尔，能麻烦你帮我复印一下这份合同吗？弄好后尽快发给我们的分部。
B:  Sure! Um... I think I broke this thing. Maxine, can you help me out here? I'm not really a tech guy.没问题！呃……我想我把这台机器弄坏了。麦克斯，你能帮帮我吗？我真不是搞技术的。
C:  Yeah, sure. I think it's just out of toner. You can go use the other one upstairs. On your way up, can you fax this while I try and fix this thing?行，没问题。我觉得只是没墨了。你可以去楼上用另一台。上楼的时候，你能帮我传真这份文件吗？我试试修好这台机器。
B:  Sure! Dammit! Everything in this office seems to be breaking down! Never mind. I'll send this stupid fax later. Oh great! Is someone playing a practical joke on me? This is ridiculous!好的！该死！这办公室里的东西好像都在坏掉！算了，我等会儿再发这个破传真。哦天哪！是不是有人在跟我开玩笑？这也太荒谬了！
D:  The elevator has some sort of malfunction. Just take the stairs dude. What floor are you going to?电梯出故障了。伙计，直接走楼梯吧。你要去几楼？
B:  I have to go up fifteen floors! Never mind. Made it! There is the copier!我得往上爬十五层！算了，到了！复印机在那儿！
