Daily Life - At The Airport(0089)

A:  Next please! Hello sir, may I see your passport please?下一位！您好，先生，可以看一下您的护照吗？
B:  Yes, here you go.好的，给您。
A:  Will you be checking any bags.请问您有托运行李需要办理吗？
B:  Yes, I'd like to check three pieces.是的，我想托运三件。
A:  I'm sorry, sir. Airline policy allows only two pieces of checked luggage, at twenty kilograms each, plus one piece of carry-on luggage. I will have to charge you extra for the additional suitcase.抱歉，先生。航空公司规定每件托运行李限重 20 公斤，最多两件，外加一件随身行李。多出的行李箱需要额外收费。
B:  What? Why! I am taking an intercontinental flight! I'm flying sixteen thousand kms! How am I supposed to only take two, twenty kilo bags? That's absurd!什么？为什么！我要坐的是跨洋航班啊！我飞了整整一万六千里！凭什么只能带两个、每个才二十公斤的包？这太荒谬了！
A:  I am sorry, sir, there's nothing I can do. You cannot board the flight with that large bag either. Carry-on bags must fit in the over-head compartment or under your seat. That bag is clearly too big.抱歉，先生，我也没办法。您也不能带着那个大包登机。随身行李必须能放进头顶行李舱或放在座位下方。那个包明显太大了。
B:  Now I see. You charge next to nothing for an international ticket, but when it comes to charging for any other small thing, you charge an arm and a leg! So tell me, miss, how much will I have to pay for all of this.现在我明白了。你们卖国际机票便宜得几乎不要钱，可一旦涉及其他小费用，那就贵得离谱！那么小姐，请问我总共要付多少钱？
A:  Let's see... six hundred and twenty-five US dollars.让我算一下……625 美元。
B:  That's more than my round-trip ticket!这比我往返机票还贵！
