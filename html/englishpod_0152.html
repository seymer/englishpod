The Office - Cut It Out (C0152)

Ed:  Hey, Mary, can you cut that out? 嘿，玛丽，你能别再那样了吗？
Mary:  Cut what out I'm not doing anything. 让我别做什么？我什么都没做。
Ed:  The tapping of your pen on your desk. It’s driving me crazy. 你笔敲桌子的声音。吵死我了。
Mary:  Fine! By the way would you mind not slurping your coffee every time you have a cup! 行！顺便问一下，每次喝咖啡时能不能别吸溜吸溜的？
Ed:  I don’t slurp my coffee. And plus, how can you hear it when you're shouting into your phone all the time? 我没吸溜咖啡。再说，你整天对着手机大喊大叫，我怎么听得见那声音啊？
Mary:  You 've got to be kidding me! You're complaining about me talking on the phone when you go out for a cigarette break ten times a day to shoot the breeze? 你在开玩笑吧！你抱怨我打电话，可你自己每天出去抽十次烟，闲扯个没完！
Ed:  Look, we have a lot of accumulated anger from working in these conditions, and it's probably okay to let off steam once in a while But, it's probably not a good idea to keep it up I’m willing to forgive and forget and if you are. 听着，我们在这样的环境下工作积累了不少怨气，偶尔发泄一下也许没问题，但一直这样下去恐怕不好。我愿意既往不咎，如果你也愿意的话。
Mary:  Fine. Let’s call a truce. I’ll try to more considerate and to keep the noise down 行。我们休战吧。我会尽量体贴些，把噪音降下来。
Ed:  Yeah, I'll try to do the same. So, I was wondering you wanna go out to dinner Friday night? 嗯，我也会尽量做到。对了，我想问问你，周五晚上想不想出去吃晚饭？
