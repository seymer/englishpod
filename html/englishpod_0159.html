Daily Life - Getting Flowers (C0159)

A:  Hello sir, how may I help you? 您好，请问有什么可以帮您？
B:  I would like to buy some flowers, please. Something really nice. 我想买些花，拜托。要特别漂亮的。
A:  I see, may I ask whatthe occasion is? 明白了，请问是有什么特别的场合吗？
B:  It's not really an occasion, it's more like I'm sorry. 其实没什么特别的场合，更像是我想道歉。
A:  Very well. This arrangement here is very popular among regretful husb ands and boyfriends. It has a dozenlong stem red roses with a couple of sunflowers and a single orchid that stands out. It includes a small teddy bear to achievethe effect of immediate forgiveness. 好的。这款花束在愧疚的丈夫和男朋友中很受欢迎。它包含一支长茎红玫瑰、一束向日葵和一朵突出的兰花。还配有一只小熊，以达到立即原谅的效果。
B:  I think I'm gonna need more than just a dozen red roses and a bear. What else do you recommend? 我觉得光是一打红玫瑰加一只熊还不够。您还有什么推荐的？
A:  Mmm, well this is our " I'm sorry I cheated on you" package. Two dozen red roses lined with tulips, carnati ons and lilies. The fragrance and beauty of this flower arrangement is sure to make her forgive you. 嗯，这是我们“对不起我背叛了你”套餐。两打红玫瑰，搭配郁金香、康乃馨和百合。这束花的香气和美丽肯定能让她原谅你。
B:  I don't think that's gonna cut it. I need something bigger and better! 我想那还不够。我需要更大更好的！
A:  I'm sorry sir but, what exactly did you do? 抱歉先生，但您到底做了什么？
B:  Well, I may have accidentally insinuated that she is getting chubbier .呃，我可能不小心暗示她变胖了。
A:  Get out of my store you jerk! 滚出我的店来，你这个混蛋！

Key Vocabulary

occasion场合occasion场合
dozen一打a word used to describe twelve units用于描述十二个单位的词
recommend推荐say something deserves to be chosen说某件事值得被选择
fragrance香味a scent or smell气味或气味
chubby胖乎乎的more polite way of calling someone fat称呼某人胖子的更礼貌的方式
