The Office - First Paycheck (C0317)

Emily:  Hey, Susan. Have you got a sec? I have some questions about my paycheck. 嘿，苏珊。有空吗？我有些关于工资单的问题。
Susan:  You bet, Emily. Pull up a chair. 当然可以，艾米丽。过来坐。
Emily:  Well, this is my first paycheck here inthe States and there are a few things I don’t understand. First off, what is this FICA, and SUI Y tax, and why are there deductions both for Medicare and for my health insurance plan? 嗯，这是我在这里收到的第一份工资条，有几处我不太明白。首先，这个 FICA 和 SUI Y 税是什么？为什么还要扣除 Medicare 和我的健康保险计划的费用？
Susan:  OK, let’s start from the top of your pay stub. This number here represents your gross pay. 好，我们从你的工资条顶部开始看起。这里的数字代表你的税前总收入。
Emily:  Yes, that’s easy enough to understand. 没错，这部分很容易理解。
Susan:  Then here we have a series of deductions. First off are the federal ones. FICA stands for Federal Insurance Contribution Act, or something like that. It’s your federal income tax. And then there’s Social Security and Medicare, which are both federal programs to help you out after you retire or if you were unable to work.接下来这里有一系列扣款。首先是联邦层面的扣款。FICA 全称是联邦保险贡献法案，或者类似的名字。它其实就是你的联邦所得税。然后是社会保障和医疗保险，这两项都是联邦项目，旨在在你退休或因故无法工作时提供帮助。
Emily:  All right, I see. So the Medicare isn’t actually a health insurance I can use now.明白了。所以 Medicare 并不是我现在就能使用的医疗保险？
Susan:  That’s right. Below the federal deductions are the state deductions. There’s the state income tax, and then this SUI SDItax you were asking about is paying into an unemployment and disability fund that our state has set up, but you can see it’s a pretty small quantity that they take.没错。在联邦扣款之下的是州级扣款。这里有州所得税，而你刚才问到的 SUI SDItax，则是向州政府设立的失业和残疾基金缴费，不过你可以看到他们扣除的金额相当小。
Emily:  Yeah, I don’t mind giving them a dollar fifty for that. So there are two separate income taxes – one at a state level and one at a federal level?好吧，我付给他们一块五也没关系。所以有两种不同的所得税——一种在州层面，一种在联邦层面？
Susan:  That’s right. Not all states have an income tax. Some use higher property taxes or sales taxes instead.没错，并非所有州都征收所得税。有些州则采用更高的房产税或销售税来替代。
Emily:  I see. All right, well I think everything else I can figure out on my own. The deductions for health insurance and my 401(K) are pretty self-explanatory. Thanks for your help, Susan.我明白了。好吧，我想其他部分我自己也能搞懂。健康保险和 401(k) 的扣除项都很直观易懂。谢谢你的帮助，苏珊。
Susan:  No problem! All those deductions do add up, and nobody’s net pay is as high as they’d like. I can understand why you’d want some explanation.没问题！那些扣除项加在一起确实不少，没人拿到手的净工资能像他们期望的那样高。我能理解你为什么想要一些解释。
Emily:  Yeah, I guess it’s the same in the UK, I just never paid much attention. See you later!是啊，我想英国也是这么回事，我只是从来没怎么留意过。回头见！
