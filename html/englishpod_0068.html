The Office - Groundbreaking Research(0068)

A:  We've been over this a thousand times. The data is irrefutable! Look, we've done extensive research, built studies, and read the literature, and there is conclusive evidence to support my theory!我们已经讨论过这个问题上千遍了。数据无可辩驳！看，我们做了广泛的研究，建立了多项研究，阅读了大量文献，有确凿的证据支持我的理论！
B:  Horowitz, I beg to differ. Even in your most recent study, the investigative approach was flawed! You know as well as I do that the collection of data was not systematic, and there is a large margin of error. To draw a definitive conclusion based on that data would be misleading霍洛维茨，我持不同意见。即使在你最近的研究中，调查方法也是有缺陷的！你也和我一样清楚，数据的收集并不系统，存在很大的误差范围。基于这样的数据得出一个确定的结论会具有误导性。
A:  That is preposterous!这简直荒谬至极！
B:  You are trying to single-handedly solve one of the world's greatest mysteries, and yet you are oblivious to the fact that you are wrong!你试图独自解开世界上最伟大的谜团之一，却对事实视而不见：你是错的！
A:  I am not wrong! The chicken came first!我没有错！鸡先出现！
B:  No! The egg came first!不！蛋先出现！
