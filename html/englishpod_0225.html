The Weekend - Planning A Crime (C0225)

Sammy:  Alright, let’s run through this one more time from the top. I will be positioned here, across from the bank on this park bench. Now, according to the intel we got from Jimmy… 好，咱们从头再演练一遍。我会坐在这张公园长椅上，正对着银行。根据我们从吉米那里得到的情报……
Ralph:  ah, who’s Jimmy? 啊，谁是吉米？
Sammy:  Jeez Ralph! Pay attention, will ya? Jimmy’s our mole, you know…. the guy on the inside… He’s been snooping and passing on the info to us so we can pull this heist off! 天哪，拉尔夫！专心点行不行？吉米是我们的内鬼，你知道的……就是里面的人……他一直在打探消息并传给我们，好让我们能成功实施这次抢劫！
Frankie:  Yea, Ralph, clean the moth balls outta your ears and listen up. This here is important , you don’t wanna end up back in the slammer, do ya? Your role is pretty important here, we’re depending on you, man. 对，拉尔夫，把那些樟脑丸从耳朵里掏出来好好听着。这可是关键，你不想再回牢房蹲大狱吧？你的角色很重要，我们全靠你了，伙计。
Ralph:  Ok, ok! I’m listening! moth balls, hrumph… 行，行！我在听！樟脑丸，哼……
Sammy:  Alright then, …. was I? Oh yeah, ok, so I’ll be the lookout…. here on the bench across from the bank. Nobody moves until I give the go-ahead, Alright? And what’s the go-ahead? …Ralph? 好吧，我刚才说到哪了？哦对，好，那我就负责望风……就坐在这张正对银行的长椅上。在我发出信号前谁也不许动，明白吗？那什么才是“出发信号”呢？……拉尔夫？
Ralph:  You, umm… ah…. yeah, you’ll take off your hat and scratch your head! 你，呃……啊……对，你把帽子摘下来挠挠头！
Sammy:  Right. When I take my hat off and scratch my head, you do what? 行。当我摘下帽子挠头的时候，你做什么？
Ralph:  I get in the box. 我钻进箱子里。
Frankie:  Right, you get in the box. I’ll make sure it’s all sealed and then, posing as a delivery guy, I'll drop off a ‘special package’ for the manager. Now, according to Jimmy, the bank manager is leaving early on Tuesday 'cause it’s his wedding anniversary. He and the wife are having a romantic rendezvous in the country, so any packages delivered will be left unopened in his office until he gets back late on Wednesday…. 好，你钻进箱子里。我会确保箱子封好，然后伪装成快递员，给经理送一份“特别包裹”。根据吉米所说，银行经理周二会提前离开，因为是他结婚纪念日。他和妻子会在乡下浪漫约会，所以任何送来的包裹都会在周三晚上他回来之前一直未开封地留在他的办公室里……
Sammy:  ……Which gives us access to his office for at least Come hours…. Ralph, this is where you come in…. where are you? ……这给了我们至少几个小时进入他办公室的机会……拉尔夫，现在轮到你了……你在哪儿？
Ralph:  I’m standing right next to you Sammy, Sorry Sam, I’m in the box. Right there… in that box. 我就站在你旁边，萨米。抱歉，萨米，我在箱子里。就在那儿……那个箱子里。
Frankie:  …. what do you do once I deliver you to the manager’s office? ……等我送到经理办公室后，你该怎么做？
Ralph:  I stay in the box until the bank has closed, . I get out of the box. 我会待在箱子里直到银行关门，然后从箱子里出来。
Sammy:  …. then? What next, Ralph? Oh for Pete’s sake! This is never going to work.……然后呢？接下来做什么，拉尔夫？真是见鬼了！这计划绝对行不通。
Ralph:  Hey, give me a chance here, fellas! I, um, I crack the safe . then, thenI take the money…. then I… ummmmm, I get back in the box. 嘿，伙计们，给我个机会吧！我，呃，我打开保险柜，然后拿走钱……接着我……呃……我再钻回箱子里。
Frankie:  'Atta boy Ralph! In the morning I come back to the bank, say there’s been a mix-up with the delivery I made and take the ‘special package’ back here. “干得漂亮，拉尔夫！明天早上我会回到银行，说我在送货时出了差错，把那个‘特别包裹’带回这里。”
Sammy:  Alright, let’s get some sleep… it’s a big day tomorrow fellas! 好吧，我们该去睡觉了……明天是大日子，伙计们！
Frankie:  A perfect plan, Sammy! It went off without a hitch! 完美计划，萨米！一切顺利，毫无差错！
Sammy:  Let’s open this up and get Ralph out here so we can start counting the money! 把这儿打开，把拉尔夫弄出来，咱们开始数钱吧！
Ralph:  Phew! I sure am glad to see you guys! I was sure getting lonely with no one to talk. 呼！真高兴见到你们啊！没人说话我都快憋死了。
Frankie:  That’s nice, ok how much! 不错嘛，那有多少？
Ralph:  Huh?Uh,, really, really, really glad? 啥？呃……真的、真的、真的很高兴吗？
Sammy:  Money, Ralph! Money! 钱，拉尔夫！钱！
Ralph:  Oh man, I knew I forgot something……. 哎呀，我知道我忘带什么东西了……
