Daily Life - I'm in Debt(0014)

A:  Hello, I’m here to see Mr. Corleone.你好，我来找科莱昂先生。
B:  Right this way, sir.这边请，先生。
C:  Charlie! What can I do for you?查理！有什么事需要我帮忙？
B:  Mr. Corlone, I’m really sorry to trouble you, but I need your help.科莱昂先生，真不好意思打扰您，但我需要您的帮助。
C:  Anything for you, Charlie! Your father was like a brother to me.为你效劳，查理！你父亲就像我的亲兄弟一样。
B:  Well, sir, you see, this recession has hit me pretty hard; I lost my job and I’m in a lot of debt.好吧，先生，您也知道，这次经济衰退对我打击很大；我丢了工作，现在欠了一屁股债。
C:  I see……我明白……
B:  Yeah, you know, I’ve got credit card bills, car payments, I’ve got to pay my mortgage; and on top of all that, I have to pay my son’s college tuition.是啊，你知道的，我有信用卡账单要还，有汽车贷款要付，还得还房贷；除此之外，我还得给我儿子交大学学费。
C:  So you’re asking for a loan.所以你是想借笔钱？
B:  Well, I just thought maybe you could help me out.嗯，我只是想看看您能不能帮帮我。
C:  What? At a time like this? I’m broke too, you know! You’re not the only one who has been hit by the recession! I lost half my money in the stock market crash! Go on! Get outta here!什么？都这时候了？我也没钱了，你知道的！你不是唯一一个受经济衰退影响的人！我在股市崩盘时损失了一半的钱！走开！滚出去！
