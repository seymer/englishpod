A:  ...like I told you before, we just don't have the resources to hire you an assistant.就像我之前告诉你的，我们确实没有资源给你配个助理。

A:  The timing is just not right. The economy is bad, and it's too risky to take on new staff.时机不对。经济不好，招新人风险太大。
