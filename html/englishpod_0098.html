A:  Yes, my little Paul is finally a doctor. They grow up so fast you know.是的，我的小保罗终于当上医生了。他们长大得真快，你知道的。
B:  He is such a handsome guy. He gets his looks from his mother of course!他真是个帅气的小伙子。他的长相当然遗传自妈妈啦！

B:  But of course! Well, it's been great talking to you, but I have to get going.当然会有的！嗯，和你聊天真开心，但我得先走了。
A:  Same here! We will catch up soon, maybe over coffee!我也是！我们很快会见面，也许一起喝咖啡！
B:  That would be great! Give me a call!那太好了！给我打电话吧！
