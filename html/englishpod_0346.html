Global View - All About Wines (C0346)

Salesperson:  Hello there, welcome to Wine World. Let me know if I can help you out at all. 你好，欢迎来到葡萄酒世界。有什么我可以帮你的吗？
Customer:  Um, yes, please, I could really use some help. I’m going over to my boss’ house for dinner tonight and don’t know what kind of wine I should bring. 嗯，是的，请吧，我真的很需要一些帮助。今晚我要去老板家吃晚饭，不知道带什么酒合适。
Salesperson:  OK, do you know what kind of food will be served? 好的，你知道会提供什么食物吗？
Customer:  Well, his wife is Japanese. He said she makes really good sushi. 嗯，他妻子是日本人。他说她做的寿司特别好吃。
Salesperson:  Hmm, that’s a bit of a challenge. Sushi is notoriously difficult to pair with wine. Well, let’s see. have to be a white wine, of course. 嗯哼，这有点棘手。寿司 notoriously 很难搭配葡萄酒。好吧，我们看看。必须是白葡萄酒，当然。
Customer:  Why? Wouldn’t a red wine go well with sushi? 为什么？红葡萄酒不也能配寿司吗？
Salesperson:  No, I don’t think so. Sushi is a very delicately flavored food, and red wine would be a jarring contrast. You need a white wine, which has more subtle flavors, to complement the fish. 不，我不这么认为。寿司是非常精致的食物，红葡萄酒会显得格格不入。你需要一种风味更细腻的白葡萄酒来衬托鱼肉。
Customer:  I see. So should I get a bottle of Chardonnay? That’s a white wine, right? 我明白了。那我该拿一瓶霞多丽？那是白葡萄酒，对吧？
Salesperson:  Yes, Chardonnay is a white wine, but I’m not sure it’d be your best bet. Chardonnay is one of the more full-bodied whites, and tends to be a bit oaky. I’d suggest that you go for something brighter, like this Sauvignon Blanc from New Zealand.是的，霞多丽是白葡萄酒，但我不确定它是不是你的最佳选择。霞多丽属于酒体较饱满的白葡萄酒，而且往往带有一些橡木味。我建议你选一款更清爽的，比如这款来自新西兰的长相思。
Customer:  Sauvignon Blanc? What’s that?长相思？那是什么？
Salesperson:  That’s another varietal, or type of grape, just like Chardonnay.那是另一种葡萄品种，就像霞多丽一样。
Customer:  Let’s see. The label says it’s got "attractive citrus and grassy aromas that give way to crisp, mineral flavors and a bone-dry finish. Serve chilled." Oh, no, how long will it take to chill the wine? I’m on my way to the dinner now.让我看看。标签上写着：“具有迷人的柑橘和青草香气，随后转为清爽、矿物风味，余味干爽。请冰镇后饮用。”哦，不，把酒冰多久才能喝？我现在正要去赴晚宴呢。
Salesperson:  It’s OK, don’t worry, we’ll just choose a wine from the cooler. We don’t have quite as extensive a selection over here, but...this Rhone Valley white would be lovely.没关系，别担心，我们直接从冷藏柜里选一瓶吧。这里的酒款选择没有那边那么丰富，但……这瓶来自罗纳河谷的白葡萄酒会很棒。
Customer:  All right. What varietal is that?好的。这是什么品种？
Salesperson:  Well, this is a French wine, so they don’t always specify the varietal on the label. The French believe that the soil a grape is grown in is one of the most important factors in the final flavor of the wine. This wine is probably a blend of a few different types of grapes, mostly Viognier, I’d guess.嗯，这是法国葡萄酒，所以标签上并不总是标明具体品种。法国人认为，葡萄生长的土壤是决定最终风味的最关键因素之一。这款酒可能是由几种不同的葡萄混酿而成，我猜主要是维欧尼耶。
Customer:  And you think this is a good wine?你觉得这酒不错吗？
Salesperson:  Yes, this is one of our best-sellers. It’s not quite as dry as the Sauvignon Blanc we were looking at earlier, which means it’s more approachable. It’s light and crisp, with a bit of a vanilla aroma.是的，这是我们最畅销的酒款之一。它不像我们刚才看的那款长相思那么干，这意味着它更容易入口。口感轻盈清爽，带有一丝香草香气。
Customer:  Perfect! I’ll take it!完美！我要了！
