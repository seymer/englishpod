The Office - Presentation Series 6 - Addressing the Audience (C0286)

Mr. Ford:  The campaign that we have in store for the x420 is exciting, imaginative and revolutionary. We have spent two years listening to and responding to feedback from customers and staff alike. 我们即将推出的 x420 营销活动令人兴奋、富有想象力且极具革命性。我们花了两年时间倾听并回应来自客户和员工的反馈。
Mr. Ford:  I would like to say that without the assistance and support of each and every one of you we really could not have devised this campaign. I'd like to take my hat off and really thank you all for the wonderful work you've done so far, not only in helping support our marketing efforts, but also in your continuing your commitment to Alpha computers. 我想说，没有你们每一个人的协助与支持，我们真的无法策划出这项活动。我要向各位脱帽致敬，衷心感谢大家迄今为止所付出的卓越工作——不仅在于支持我们的营销努力，更在于你们对 Alpha 电脑持续不变的承诺。
Mr. Ford:  There's no doubt in my mind that we have a great workforce here and together we can really push Alpha computers to a whole new level of success. 毫无疑问，我们拥有一支优秀的团队，携手合作，我们定能将 Alpha 电脑推向全新的高度，取得巨大成功。
Mr. Ford:  On the subject of the campaign let me ask you all a question. How do we define the perfect lap-top? Is it about affordability, quality, speed, reliability? What do you look for in a consumer? Well, I believe the answer lies in a combination of all of these elements. 关于这项活动，我想问大家一个问题：如何定义完美的笔记本电脑？是价格实惠、品质优良、运行迅速还是可靠耐用？消费者最看重什么？我认为答案在于所有这些要素的完美结合。
Mr. Ford:  Our campaign will really hammer home the point that the x420 is a state-of-the-art laptop for all of your computing needs. With our television campaign we hope to really reach out to a huge audience. 我们的活动将有力传达一个核心观点：x420 是一款满足您所有计算需求的尖端笔记本电脑。通过电视广告活动，我们希望能触达更广泛的受众群体。
Mr. Ford:  We have a great ad campaign planned focusing on the fantastic USP' s of the x420. We have hired one of the best PR companies to work with us on the campaign, and have already completed three separate TV adverts, all focusing on one key feature of the x420. 我们已策划了一场出色的广告活动，重点突出 x420 的卓越独特卖点。我们聘请了顶尖公关公司合作开展此次活动，并已制作完成三部独立的电视广告，每部均聚焦于 x420 的一项核心功能。
Mr. Ford:  I'm excited to say that today, for the first time, we will unveil to all of you here the first of these advertisements! 我很高兴地向在座的各位宣布，今天我们将首次揭晓这些广告中的第一部！
