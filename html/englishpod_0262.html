The Office - Interview Skills 10 - Concluding The Interview (C0262)

Mr Parsons:  Well Rebecca, is there anything else you need to know for now? Well Rebecca, is there anything else you need to know for now?
Rebecca:  I don't think so Mr. Parsons. I think you have covered all the main points for me. 我想没有，Parsons 先生。我觉得您已经把主要要点都讲清楚了。
Mr Parsons:  Okay well listen, here is my business card with my mobile number. If any other questions spring to mind don't hesitate to contact me. Of course you can also call Miss Childs too. 好的，听着，这是我的名片，上面有我的手机号码。如果之后想到其他问题，随时联系我。当然，你也可以联系 Childs 小姐。
Rebecca:  Great. Ermm, when can I expect to hear from you? 太好了。呃，我什么时候能收到您的消息？
Mr Parsons:  Well, we are finishing the shortlist interviews tomorrow, so we will certainly have a decision made by early next week. Miss Childs will call you to discuss more on Monday or Tuesday. How does that sound? 嗯，我们明天会结束最后一轮面试，所以最迟下周一之前就会有结果。Childs 小姐会在周一或周二给您打电话进一步沟通。这样安排可以吗？
Rebecca:  That sounds perfect. Thank you very much for taking the time to speak to me Mr. Parsons. 听起来完美。非常感谢您抽出时间和我交谈，Parsons 先生。
Mr Parsons:  The pleasure's all mine Rebecca. 这是我的荣幸，Rebecca。
Rebecca:  I hope to hear from you very soon.希望很快能收到您的消息。
Mr Parsons:  Absolutely. Thanks for coming Rebecca. Goodbye.没问题。谢谢你过来，Rebecca。再见。
