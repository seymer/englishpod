Daily Life - Winter Clothes (C0183)

A:  Bye, mom! 再见，妈妈！
B:  Wait, Jimmy, it's cold outside. Put a hat on! 等等，吉米，外面很冷。戴上帽子！
A:  Ok. Bye! 好的。再见！
B:  No, wait, you will be too cold without mittens. 不行，等下，你不戴手套会冻着的。
A:  Alright. See ya! 好吧。回头见！
B:  Hold on, with that wind, you're going to catch a cold. Wear this scarf. 稍等，有这阵风，你会感冒的。戴上这条围巾。
A:  Ok, see you after school... 好的，放学见……
B:  Oh... and ear muffs! Put these on... here we go. 哦……还有耳罩！戴上这些……好了。
A:  Mom? 妈妈？
B:  Yes, honey...是的，亲爱的……
A:  I... I can't breathe. 我……我喘不过气来。

Key Vocabulary

mitten连指手套hand covering separating thumb from fingers手覆盖将拇指与手指分开
hold on等一下stop, wait停下来，等待
scarf围巾a long narrow piece of cloth worn around the neck戴在脖子上的一块狭长的布
ear muff耳罩a warm tubular covering for the ears耳朵的温暖管状覆盖物
honey亲爱的name you call someone you love你称呼你爱的人的名字
hat帽子head covering头套

Supplementary Vocabulary

snowsuit滑雪服a one- or two-piece outer garment for cold weather, often consisting of heavily lined pants and jacket寒冷天气用的一件或两件式外衣，通常由厚衬里的裤子和夹克组成
gloves手套hand covering separating all fingers and thumb手部覆盖物将所有手指和拇指分开
coat涂层an outer garment with sleeves, covering at least the upper part of the body有袖的外衣，至少覆盖身体的上半部分
mitts手套short for mittens手套的缩写
boots靴子a covering of leather, rubber, or the like, for the foot and all or part of the leg脚和整个或部分腿的皮革、橡胶或类似材料的覆盖物
