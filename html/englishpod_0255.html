Global View - The Miracle Of Life (C0255)

A:  Continuing with our class, today we are going to study briefly the miracle of life. Many of you may think you already know how babies come to be, but I am sure that some of the things that we will be talking about today may surprise you. Billy can you turn on the projector please? Thanks. Ok, does anyone know what this is? 继续我们的课程，今天我们将简要探讨生命的奇迹。你们中许多人可能认为自己已经知道婴儿是如何诞生的，但我确信我们今天讨论的一些内容可能会让你们感到惊讶。比利，你能打开投影仪吗？谢谢。好的，有人知道这是什么吗？
B:  Looks like a goat head to me! 在我看来这像个羊头！
A:  Nice try, but this is a woman’s womb which contains her uterus and ovaries. The ovaries. are packed with eggs and each month during the middle of the menstrual cycle, the ripest one will be sucked up by one of the fallopian tubes. This is called ovulation and the exact time of ovulation depends on the length of your cycle. In an average 28 day cycle, ovulation will most likely happen between the 12th and 15th days, counting day 1 as the first day of your last period. 不错的尝试，但这其实是女性的子宫，里面包含卵巢。卵巢里充满了卵子，每个月在月经周期的中期，最成熟的卵子会被输卵管吸走。这叫做排卵，而排卵的具体时间取决于你的周期长度。在一个平均 28 天的周期中，排卵最可能发生在第 12 天到第 15 天之间，将第一天算作你上一次月经的第一天。
B:  That's amazing! So each month, the woman produces these eggs and then waits for them to be fertilized? 太神奇了！所以每个月女性都会产生这些卵子，然后等待它们受精？
A:  Actually, every woman is already born with over four hundred thousand eggs! Some will start dying off immediately and others released during her fertile period. 实际上，每位女性在出生时就已经拥有超过四十万个卵子！其中一些会立即开始死亡，另一些则会在她的生育期释放。
B:  What about the guys? I know they produce sperm and stuff. 那男性呢？我知道他们会产生精子和之类的东西。
A:  That’s right! The man’s body has a tiny factory that produces sperm twenty four hours a day! Each ejaculation will release about a hundred million sperm so the factory is always pretty busy. The sole purpose of a sperm's life is to fertilize the woman's egg. 没错！男人的身体里有个微型工厂，24 小时不间断地生产精子！每次射精会释放约一亿个精子，所以这个工厂总是忙个不停。精子的唯一使命就是让女人的卵子受精。
B:  So, then we basically need to put one-and-one together so we can have babies right? 那这么说，我们基本上就是把一加一凑在一起就能生孩子了，对吧？
A:  Yes, the man will have an orgasm during intercourse and ejaculate sperm and semen. Now this is where the race begins and all those millions of sperm will race and swim from the cervix, through the uterus to the fallopian tubes. This could take anywhere from forty five minutes to twelve hours! Not all of them make it, since some go the wrong way and get lost or simply die. Many will actually reach the egg but only one will penetrate it and fertilize it. Once this happens, the egg instantly changes and creates a protective shield once the sperm is safely inside. 是的，男人会在性交中达到高潮并射出精液和精子。现在比赛开始了，那些数以百万计的精子会从子宫颈出发，游过子宫进入输卵管。这可能需要 45 分钟到 12 小时！并非所有精子都能到达终点，因为有些会走错方向迷路或直接死亡。许多精子确实能抵达卵子旁，但只有一个能穿透它并完成受精。一旦成功，卵子会立即改变并生成保护盾，确保精子安全进入体内。
B:  And then? That's it? 然后呢？就这样结束了吗？
A:  Well, the egg will be fertilized within about 24 hours of its release. The genetic material from the sperm combines with the genetic material in the egg to create a new cell that will rapidly start dividing. The woman is not actually pregnant until that bundle of new cells, known as the embryo, travels the rest of the way down the fallopian tube and attaches itself to the wall of her uterus. Any other questions? Then let's move on. 好吧，卵子排出后大约 24 小时内就会受精。精子的遗传物质与卵子的遗传物质结合，形成一个新的细胞，并开始迅速分裂。实际上，女性要等到这一团新细胞——也就是胚胎——沿着输卵管继续下行并附着在子宫壁上时，才算真正怀孕。还有其他问题吗？那我们接着往下讲。
