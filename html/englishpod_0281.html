The Weekend - Breaking Up (C0281)

Lydia:  Hello? 喂？
Maggie:  Hey! Do you want to go out tonight? 嘿！今晚想出去吗？
Lydia:  No, I think I’ll pass. Mark broke up with me. I feel awful. 不了，我想我就算了。马克跟我分手了。我感觉糟透了。
Maggie:  What?!? What happened? Just last week you were talking about going on vacation together! 什么？！发生什么事了？就上周你还在说要一起去度假呢！
Lydia:  I don't know what I did wrong. He said he needed some space to figure things out... He said I didn’t do anything wrong, that I’m a great person... just not the one for him... 我不知道自己做错了什么。他说他需要一些空间来理清思绪……他说我没做错什么，我是个很棒的人……只是不是他的那个人……
Maggie:  Ugh! That’s so cliché. Mark’s not worth your time, Lydia. You deserve so much better! 唉！这太老套了。马克不值得你花时间，莉迪亚。你值得更好的！
Lydia:  I know we had hit a rough patch but I had hoped we could work it out next weekend, when we both had some time off... I think he found someone else. I had been getting suspicious because he had been spending a lot of time with a coworker... 我知道我们最近遇到了一些困难，但我原本希望下周末能解决，因为那时我们都有空……我觉得他找到别人了。我一直有点怀疑，因为他花了很多时间和一个同事在一起……
Maggie:  Whatever. He’s a jerk, forget about him! There are many more fish in the sea. 随便吧。他就是个混蛋，忘了他！海里还有更多鱼呢。
Lydia:  Talk about being cliché, Maggie! But you’re right. There’s no use for me to sulk around... Let’s go out tonight! 说老套倒是你，玛吉！不过你说得对。我在这生闷气也没用……今晚我们就出去吧！
