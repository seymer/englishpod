Daily Life - High School Reunion (C0287)

A:  I hate coming to high school reunions. 我讨厌参加高中同学聚会。
B:  It will be great honey. We will get to see your old classmates and catch up to see how they have been doing. 亲爱的，这会很棒的。我们可以看看你的老同学，聊聊他们都过得怎么样。
A:  Yeah I guess so. Oh look! There is Robert Matthews! Rob! 嗯，我想是吧。哦，看！那是罗伯特·马修斯！罗布！
C:  Hey Bill! Wow great to see you! 嘿，比尔！哇，见到你真高兴！
A:  Likewise! It’s been a long time! This is my wife Dorthy. 我也是！好久不见了！这是我妻子多萝西。
C:  Pleasure to meet you. So Bill, how have you been? 很高兴认识你。那比尔，你最近怎么样？
A:  Can’t complain! We have 2 children who are in college and my business is going well. What about you? 没什么可抱怨的！我们有两个孩子在读大学，生意也做得不错。你呢？
C:  Ah you know me! I am a dedicated bachelor. I never married although I do have a beautiful daughter with Mary, you remember her? We were high school sweetheart, didn’t really work out between us, but I really can’t complain either. 啊，你了解我！我是个专注的单身汉。虽然没结婚，但我有个漂亮的女儿和玛丽生的，你还记得她吗？我们是高中时的恋人，不过后来没能走到一起，但我也没什么好抱怨的。
A:  That’s good. Have you seen Frank? I was hoping he would come tonight. 那挺好的。你见到弗兰克了吗？我还希望他能今晚来呢。
C:  You didn’t hear? Frank passed away last year. 你没听说吗？弗兰克去年去世了。
A:  Are you serious? 你是认真的吗？
C:  Nah! I’m just yanking your chain. He’ll be here soon. I saw him just last week and he told me he would show up. 别逗了！我就是在拿你开玩笑。他马上就到了，上周我还见过他，他说会来的。

Key Vocabulary

catch up叙旧meet each other相见
dedicated忠诚的have strong loyalty to someone or something忠于别人或什么的
likewise同样also(单位：千美元)
yank your chain开玩笑骗人deceive someone in a friendly or playful way以友善或玩弄的方式欺骗别人
high school sweetheart高中恋人the person who you loved in high school你高中时所爱的人
