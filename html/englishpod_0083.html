Daily Life - Using The ATM(0083)

A:  Stupid girl, making me spend so much money, now I have to get it from the ATM...笨蛋女孩，害我花这么多钱，现在还得去 ATM 取……
B:  Hello, welcome to Universal Bank. Please insert your card into the slot.您好，欢迎光临环球银行。请将您的卡片插入卡槽。
A:  I know where to put my card! Stupid machine, talking to me like I'm an idiot...我知道该把卡插哪儿！这破机器，跟我说话像我是个白痴……
B:  Please input your 6 digit PIN code followed by the pound key. Thank you. Please select an option. Thank you. You have selected withdrawal.请输入六位 PIN 码，然后按英镑键。谢谢。请选择一项。谢谢。您已选择取款。
A:  Yeah, yeah, I know what I selected. Just gimme my money!对对对，我知道我选了啥。快给我钱！
B:  Please type the amount you would like to withdraw. Thank you, you want to transfer 10000 USD to the World Wildlife Foundation. If this is correct please press 1.请输入您想取款的金额。谢谢，您想向世界野生动物基金会转账 10000 美元。如果正确请按 1。
A:  No, no! Stupid machine, what are you doing! No!不，不！这破机器你在干什么！不行！
B:  Confirmed. Thank you for using our bank! Please remove your card from the slot. Goodbye!确认。感谢您使用我们的银行！请从卡槽中取出您的卡片。再见！
C:  Danger, danger! The exits have been sealed and the doors will remain locked in until the local authorities arrive. Thank you for using our bank. Have a nice day.危险，危险！出口已被封锁，门将在当地当局到达前保持锁定状态。感谢您使用我们的银行。祝您愉快。
