Daily Life - Planning a Bank Robbery(0056)

A:  All right, so this is what we are going to do. I've carefully mapped this out, so don't screw it up. Mr. Rabbit, you and Mr. Fox will go into the bank wearing these uniforms. We managed to get replicas of the one the guards wear when they pick up the money.好的，这就是我们要做的。我已经仔细规划好了，别搞砸了。兔子先生，你和狐狸先生穿着这些制服进银行。我们弄到了和守卫取钱时穿的一模一样的复制品。
B:  Got it.明白了。
C:  No problem, boss.没问题，老板。
A:  When you get inside, tell them that you are filling in for Carl and Tom, and say that they are on another route today. Don't lose your cool. Just act natural.进去后告诉他们你们替卡尔和汤姆来上班，说他们今天走另一条路线。别慌，表现得自然点。
B:  What if they want to call and confirm?如果他们打电话确认怎么办？
A:  You let him.随他去吧。
C:  What!?什么？！
A:  Don’t worry, we have the phones tapped, so the call will be patched through to me, and I’ll pretend to be the transport company.别担心，电话已经被窃听了，他们会把通话转接给我，我会假装是运输公司的人。
B:  Ha ha, you are so clever boss!哈哈，老板你真聪明！
A:  Okay, shut up. Only take as much money as you can fit in these bags. Don’t get greedy! Are you ready? Let's go.好了，闭嘴。只拿能塞进这些袋子的钱就行，别太贪心！准备好了吗？我们走。
