Daily Life - Getting A Hair Cut(0081)

A:  Hello there! Come on in! Don't just stand there! Come and take a seat!你好！请进！别站在那儿了，快过来坐下吧！
B:  Um, okay. Well, I just want a trim. Nothing too fancy.嗯，好的。我想简单修一下，不要太复杂。
A:  Oh my gosh! Your hair is amazing! So silky, so shiny! I am going to work my magic on your hair! You hear me? You are going to look like a million bucks!天哪！你的头发太棒了！又柔顺又有光泽！我要施展魔法让你的头发焕然一新！你听到了吗？你会帅得不得了！
B:  Okay. Um... can you make sure my sideburns are even and you just take a little off the top.好的。呃……你能确保我的鬓角对称，再把头顶稍微剪短一点吗？
A:  Don't you worry, I'll take care of everything! ( starts cutting) Oh my god! I just love your curly hair! It's so fluffy and cute! You should totally let it grow out. An afro would look great on you!别担心，一切交给我！（开始剪）天哪！我太喜欢你卷发了！蓬松又可爱！你应该让它自然生长，非洲式发型很适合你！
B:  Um... no.呃……不行。
A:  Okay, but you are going to be my masterpiece!好吧，但你将成为我的杰作！
