The Weekend - Golf(0111)

A:  Good morning golf aficionados! My name is Rick Fields, and you guessed it, I am here with my main man, Bob Copeland.大家早上好，高尔夫爱好者们！我是里克·菲尔兹，正如你们所料，我和我的头号搭档鲍勃·科佩兰德一起在这里。
B:  Thank you, Rick! As you can see, ladies and gentleman, we are here in beautiful Pebble Beach where the top golfers in the world are trying to win the grand prize of one million dollars!谢谢，里克！各位女士、先生，我们正身处美丽的 Pebble Beach，世界顶尖的高尔夫球手们正在这里争夺价值一百万美元的巨额奖金！
A:  Whoa, that's a lot of cash! Let's go to the course and see how Tiger Woods is doing.哇，这么多钱！让我们去球场看看泰格·伍兹的表现如何吧。
B:  All right, were're here at the eighth hole. It's a par four, and has some very difficult hazards which many golfers find difficult to avoid. Although, I did see Jack Nicklaus hit a hole in one on this very same hole!好的，我们现在来到了第八洞。这是一个标准杆四杆洞，而且有一些非常危险的障碍区，许多高尔夫球手都难以避开。不过，我确实看到杰克·尼克劳斯在这个洞上打进了一个一杆进洞！
A:  Tiger Woods is about to tee off, and let's see if he has the same luck as Jack. Tiger is asking his caddie for his driver and, he seems to be very nervous.泰格·伍兹即将开球，让我们看看他是否能有和杰克一样的运气。泰格正让他的球童递给他一支木杆，看起来他非常紧张。
B:  Oh no! Not a good swing at all! It's definitely not his day today. On the seventh hole he got a bogey and before that he barely made par. He will definitely not get a birdie on this shot.糟糕！这完全不是一个好挥杆！今天肯定不是他的幸运日。在第七洞他打出了一个柏忌，在此之前他也只是勉强抓到标准杆。这一杆他绝对不可能打出小鸟球。
A:  It seems that his ball has flown somewhere deep in the trees. He is having a hard time finding it and even his caddie has climbed a tree to try and spot it.看来他的球飞到了树林深处。他很难找到球，甚至他的球童都爬上了树试图寻找它。
B:  Oh no! A bear! Run, Tiger, run! Somebody call animal control!糟糕！一只熊！快跑，泰格，快跑！有人叫动物控制中心吗？
