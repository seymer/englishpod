Global View - Volunteering  (C0217)

Mark:  Thanks a lot for pitching in once again Judy, we really appreciate your help. It seems that at this time of year there are more and more people who are struggling to make ends meet. There aren’t many professional chefs like you who are so generous with their time. 再次感谢你伸出援手，朱迪，我们真的很感激你的帮助。看来每到这个时候，越来越多的人难以维持生计。像你这样慷慨奉献时间的专业厨师可不多见。
Judy:  Don’t be silly Mark, I’m more than happy to donate my time to a good cause. Volunteering at the soup kitchen has been really rewarding for me. You know, it’s satisfying to provide good meals for those who are less fortunate, I feel like I’m really making a difference in some small way. 别傻了，马克，我很乐意把时间捐给公益事业。在施粥处做志愿者让我受益匪浅。你知道的，能为那些不如我们幸运的人提供美味的饭菜，我感到自己也在以某种方式真正发挥作用。
Mark:  Well, your skills are definitely appreciated here! The people who come here have fallen on hard times and a delicious hot meal can really bolster their spirits. That smells great! The needy are sure lucky to have you! 你的手艺在这里确实很受欢迎！来这里的人们都遭遇了困境，一顿热腾腾的美味佳肴真的能提振他们的精神。闻起来真香！有需要的人有你真是幸运！
Judy:  Thanks Mark! 谢谢马克！
Judy:  Here you go, enjoy your meal! 请慢用，祝用餐愉快！
Old lady:  Thank you my dear, Oh this looks lovely. 谢谢你，亲爱的，哦，这看起来真不错。
Judy:  You’re welcome, Hello sir, today we have…. are you doing here? 不客气，先生您好，今天我们提供……您想在这里用餐吗？
George:  Hey Judy! I’ll have a little of everything, thanks…. smells great! 嘿，朱迪！我要来一点所有的菜，谢谢……闻起来真棒！
Judy:  George, seriously …what are you doing here? I haven’t seen you since our divorce was finalized. You’ve got no right to be here, you’re hardly homeless! 乔治，说真的……你怎么会在这里？自从我们的离婚手续办完我就再没见过你。你没权利待在这儿，你根本不算无家可归！
George:  Don’t be like that Judy, I really miss your home cooking! 别那样，朱迪，我真的怀念你做的家常菜！
