The Weekend - Buying Jewelery (C0290)

Shop assistant:  Good afternoon, sir, is there anything I can help you with today? 下午好，先生，今天有什么可以帮您的吗？
Mark:  umm... yeah!I’m looking for a nice gift to give my girlfriend. Our fifth anniversary’s next Friday. 呃……有！我在找一份送给女朋友的礼物。我们下周五就是五周年纪念日了。
Shop assistant:  Well, I would be happy to assist you in choosing the perfect gift for her. Is there anything particular that you have in mind? 我很乐意帮您挑选一份完美的礼物。您心里有特别想好的款式吗？
Mark:  No, not really... I'm completely at a loss. 没有，不太确定……我完全不知道选什么。
Shop assistant:  Well, you can give her a set of pearl earrings, or this beautiful heart-shaped pendant. What is her favorite gemstone? 嗯，您可以送她一套珍珠耳环，或者这款精美的心形吊坠。她最喜欢的宝石是什么？
Mark:  That purple one. I’m sorry...I've never bought jewelery for anyone and I’m kind of nervous. 紫色那种。抱歉……我从没给任何人买过珠宝，有点紧张。
Shop assistant:  Don’t worry, we specialize in providing our customers a relaxed, pressure-free shopping environment. That stone is an amethyst. We have a range of beautiful amethyst pieces. Take a look at this bracelet. It’s 18K rose-gold, studded with amethyst and blue topaz. It’s a great statement piece. 别担心，我们专为顾客提供轻松无压力的购物环境。那块石头是紫水晶。我们有各种漂亮的紫水晶饰品。看看这条手链吧。它是 18K 玫瑰金镶嵌紫水晶和蓝托帕石，非常抢眼的款式。
Mark:  Oh...wow. That’s really pretty. Jess would love that. But... I was thinking of something a little more delicate, perhaps a necklace? 哦……哇。真漂亮。Jess 一定会喜欢的。不过……我在想送点更精致的东西，比如项链？
Shop assistant:  We have this beautiful platinum pendant, or you could also get her a locket. You could also get her a timepiece---it's both glamorous yet functional. If you tell me a little more about your girlfriend, maybe I can help you find something for her. 我们有这款精美的白金吊坠，或者您也可以送她一个胸针。您甚至可以送她一块手表——既时尚又实用。如果您能多告诉我一些关于女朋友的信息，也许我能帮您找到更合适的礼物。
Mark:  Jess? Well, she’s very smart, and has a great sense of humor. She’s very feminine...Jess？她非常聪明，而且很有幽默感。她很女性化……
Shop assistant:  Perhaps you could give her a ring? 或许你可以给她打个电话？
Mark:  Well...actually...I was thinking about asking Jess to marry me...I've just been so nervous.嗯……其实……我在考虑向 Jess 求婚……我一直很紧张。
Shop assistant:  Well sir, I believe your fifth anniversary is a great time to propose! 先生，我相信您的五周年纪念日是个求婚的好时机！
Mark:  Okay, I’ve decided. I’m going to pop the question! 好的，我决定好了。我要去求婚了！
Shop assistant:  Fabulous! We should look at engagement rings then! Now that’s a whole other section. 太棒了！那我们看看订婚戒指吧！那可是另一个专区呢。
