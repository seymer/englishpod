The Weekend - Baseball(0085)

A:  Hello baseball fans, and welcome back to today's game! My name is Rick Fields and of course, I am here, once again, with the man that seals the deal, Bob Copeland.大家好，棒球迷们！欢迎回到今天的比赛现场！我是里克·菲尔兹，当然，我再次与那位能锁定胜局的鲍勃·科佩兰德一起。
B:  It's a beautiful day to see two world class teams face each other and fight for their right to be called champions.今天是个美好日子，两支世界级的球队相遇并争夺冠军的资格。
A:  Well, the national anthem has just been sung, and the umpire has started the game. It's time to play ball!好了，国歌刚刚奏完，裁判已经开始了比赛。是时候开打了！
B:  Roger Vargas is up at bat. The pitcher winds up and strike one!罗杰·瓦尔加斯准备击球。投手挥臂投球，一好球！
A:  A very nice curve ball by the pitcher. The catcher gives him the sign, he winds up and Vargas gets a line drive!投手投出了一个非常漂亮的曲球。捕手给他打了个手势，他再次挥臂，瓦尔加斯打出了一记直线球！
B:  The players are scrambling to get the ball. Vargas gets to first base and he's still going! The outfielder throws it to second! Vargas slides! He's safe!球员们争抢着球。瓦尔加斯跑向一垒，他还不停下！外野手将球传向二垒！瓦尔加斯滑垒！安全上垒！
A:  Great play!精彩的防守！
B:  We have a runner on third and up at bat is Brian Okami! There's the pitch, he hits it! It's going, going, that ball is gone!三垒有跑垒员，轮到布莱恩·奥卡米击球！投手投球了，他打中了！球飞出去了，飞远了，那球没了！
A:  Home run by Okami! That puts this team ahead by two as we are at the bottom of the fifth inning here at Richie Stadium!奥卡米本垒打！这使我们的球队领先两分，现在比赛进行到第五局下半，地点在里奇体育场！
