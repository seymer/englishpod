Daily Life - Going To The Tailor (C0196)

A:  Welcome to Bill's Fabric World. What can I do for you today? 欢迎光临比尔的布料世界。今天我能为您做些什么？
B:  I was wondering if you guys also tailor clothes? 我想知道你们也提供服装定制服务吗？
A:  Sure we do! We have the best tailors in the country! What is it that you need exactly? 当然！我们拥有全国最好的裁缝！您具体需要什么呢？
B:  Well, I'm looking to get a custom-made suit. 嗯，我想定做一套西装。
A:  Excellent! We have the finest cashmeres at affordable prices. How about we get you measured? Let's start off by measuring the width of your shoulders. Now, let’s measure the length of your arms and this bit around your neck here. 太好了！我们有价格实惠的优质羊绒面料。我们要不要先给您量一下尺寸？我们先从测量您的肩宽开始。接下来测量手臂长度以及脖子周围的围度。
B:  Can you make sure you leave a little extra space in the collar? My neck gets easily irritated. 麻烦您在领口处留一点额外空间好吗？我的脖子很容易发痒。
A:  No problem! Now for your pants, let me just measure your waist and the inseam. 没问题！现在来量裤子，让我为您测量腰围和裤长。
B:  You might also want to leave a little extra room in the waist area. I tend to gain a few pounds over the holidays. 您也可以在腰部区域多留一点空间。我每逢节假日体重都会增加几磅。
A:  OK. Now you can pick your fabric and pattern design. Please follow me. 好的。现在您可以挑选面料和图案设计了。请跟我来。
