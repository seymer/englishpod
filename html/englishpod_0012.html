Daily Life - Cleaning the House(0012)

A:  Honey, the house is such a mess! I need you to help me tidy up a bit. My boss and her husband are coming over for dinner and the house needs to be spotless!亲爱的，家里太乱了！你得帮我收拾一下。老板和她丈夫要过来吃晚饭，房子必须一尘不染！
B:  I'm in the middle of something right now. I'll be there in a second.我正忙着呢，马上就好。
A:  This can't wait! I need your help now!这可等不了！我现在就需要你的帮忙！
B:  Alright, alright. I'm coming.行行行，我来了。
A:  Ok, here's a list of chores we need to get done. I'll do the dishes and get all the groceries for tonight. You can sweep and mop the floors. Oh, and the furniture needs to be dusted.好，这是我们需要完成的任务清单。我来洗碗并买今晚需要的所有食材。你去扫地拖地。哦对了，家具也得擦灰。
B:  You know what, I have to pick something up at the mall, so why don't you clean the floors and I’ll go to the supermarket and get all the groceries.哎，我得去商场买点东西，要不你负责打扫地板，我去超市把食材全买了？
A:  Sure that's fine. Here is the list of all the things you need to get. Don’t forget anything! And can you pick up a bottle of wine on your way home?没问题。这是你需要购买的所有物品清单。别忘了任何东西！顺便在回家的路上带一瓶红酒回来好吗？
B:  Hey, honey I'm back. Wow, the house looks really good!嘿亲爱的我回来了。哇，房子看起来真干净！
A:  Great! Can you set the table?太好了！你能摆一下餐桌吗？
B:  Just a sec I'm just gonna vacuum this rug real fast稍等一下，我正快速吸一下这块地毯呢。
A:  Wait! Don't turn it on...等等！别把它打开……
