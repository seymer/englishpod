Daily Life - Trying To Sleep (C0236)

Jill:  Alex, what's up with you? You look dreadful! Alex，你怎么了？看起来糟透了！
Alex:  Hey Jill, I don't know. I've been having trouble sleeping these past few weeks. I usually lie in bed for hours trying to get to sleep . I've tried stretching and different breathing techniques before going to bed . I've tried eating and not eating different foods. I've even tried counting sheep! And then when I finally get to sleep , I have these really disturbing nightmares, so I usually wake up in a panic and more tired than before I went to sleep . 嘿 Jill，我不知道。最近几周我一直睡不着。我通常躺在床上几个小时试图入睡。睡前我试过拉伸和不同的呼吸技巧。我也试过吃和不吃各种食物。甚至试过数绵羊！然后当我终于睡着时，我会做非常可怕的噩梦，所以我通常会惊慌醒来，比睡觉前更累。
Jill:  Wow, maybe you should get that checked out. Maybe you're stressed? 哇，也许你应该去检查一下。也许你压力太大了？
C:  Just take some sedatives! Works for me! Every so often having some melatonin on hand helps me when I have trouble sleeping . It works on all kinds of sleeping disorders . It's the stuff pilots use to regulate their sleeping patterns . 吃点镇静剂吧！对我很管用！偶尔备点褪黑素在我失眠时很有帮助。它对各种睡眠障碍都有效。那是飞行员用来调节睡眠模式的药物。
Jill:  I heard of that. But does that apply to Alex's situation? 我听说过这个。但这适用于 Alex 的情况吗？
C:  Ya sure, why not ? Sounds like he only has transient insomnia since it's a recent thing so taking melatonin do the trick. 当然可以，为什么不呢？听起来他只是暂时性失眠，因为这是最近才发生的，所以吃褪黑素应该能奏效。
Jill:  But shouldn't he be looking into WHY it's been happening? 但他不应该去探究为什么会发生这种情况吗？
C:  Well aren't youthe little psychologist? Our buddy's having trouble sleeping , it's easy and curable. It's not something freakish like if he was a sleepwalker. 你不是那个小心理学家吗？我们的朋友睡不着，这很容易治愈。不像他如果是梦游者那样奇怪的事。
Alex:  Well, there's that too. 嗯，那也是个问题。
