Daily Life - The Butcher(0143)

Butcher:  Hi. What can I get for you?嗨，请问您想买点什么？
Gina:  I’d like a half a pound of ground beef, please.请给我来半磅绞肉。
Butcher:  Good choice! Our ground beef is extra lean, if you know what I mean.好选择！我们的绞肉特别瘦，懂我意思吧。
Gina:  Could I also have half a dozen pork chops and two pounds of boneless chicken breasts?那再给我六块猪排和两磅去骨鸡胸肉可以吗？
Butcher:  No, no no no chicken breasts at the moment, but we have some nice chicken thighs.不行，现在没鸡胸肉了，不过我们有不错的鸡腿。
Gina:  No, that won't do. I'll take this smoked ham you have here.不，那样不行。我要你这里的熏火腿。
Butcher:  Okay, is there anything else?好的，还需要别的吗？
Gina:  Do you have any other cold cuts? Is this salami and bologna you have here?你们还有其他冷切肉吗？就是你们这里的萨拉米香肠和博洛尼亚香肠？
Butcher:  Yes! It's very fine meat! Made it myself...没错！这可是上好的肉啊！我自己做的……
Gina:  Sounds good. Okay, that's it.听起来不错。好了，就这些了。
Butcher:  Wait! We have T-bone, rib eye, and sirloin steaks. They are very fresh! Just came from the slaughter house...等等！我们有 T 骨牛排、肋眼牛排和牛柳牛排。非常新鲜！刚从屠宰场运来的……
Gina:  Mmm... No that's okay, really. I think that's all for today.嗯……不用了，真的。我想今天就这些就够了。
Butcher:  Okay. That will be thirty-four dollars and fifty cents.好的。一共三十四美元五十美分。
