Daily Life - Buying A Pair Of Jeans (C0327)

A:  Excuse me, can I try on this pair of jeans?打扰一下，我可以试穿这条牛仔裤吗？
B:  Sure. Let me see... I'm afraid we don't have any size eights left.当然可以。让我看看……恐怕我们没有 8 码了。
A:  What are you talking about? I'm always a size four. Here, I'll try these.你在说什么呢？我一直都是 4 码。来，我试试这条。
B:  They seem a bit too tight. Shall I find you a larger size?看起来有点太紧了。我去给你找个大一点的尺码吧？
A:  No, they fit fine! They show off my curves perfectly!不，它们很合身！完美地展现我的曲线！
B:  Yeah, your love handles. Yeah, they sure do, although... here, you forgot to close this button.是啊，你的赘肉。确实如此，不过……嘿，你忘了扣上这个纽扣。
A:  Yeah right, I'll do it now...对啊对啊，我现在就扣上……
