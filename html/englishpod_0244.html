The Office - Interview Skills 4 - Talking About Work Experience (C0244)

Mr. Parsons:  Right Rebecca. Now I see that after graduating from University your first job was....... 好的，Rebecca。现在我看到你大学毕业后的第一份工作是……
Rebecca:  For a local paper in York called the York Herald. Actually, I started with them as an intern in the beginning. I was really keen on getting some experience in the journalistic world, and this seemed like a good first step. 在约克当地的一家报纸《约克先驱报》。实际上，我是以实习生的身份最初加入他们的。我真的很渴望在新闻界积累一些经验，这似乎是个不错的起步。
Mr. Parsons:  Certainly. And after your internship 当然。那之后呢？
Rebecca:  They seemed impressed, and offered me a position as a junior local news reporter. I ended up staying two years there actually. I was in charge of the sports news section of the newspaper. I really enjoyed it there, and it really helped me build my skills. 他们似乎很欣赏我，并给了我一个初级地方新闻记者的职位。事实上，我在那里待了两年。我当时负责报纸的体育新闻版块。我在那里过得非常愉快，这也帮助我提升了技能。
Mr. Parsons:  Yes I see. But you decided to leave them in 2006 right是的，我明白了。但你在 2006 年决定离开他们，对吗？
Rebecca:  Yes, that's right. My husband and I moved to London, and so I managed to find a position with a National newspaper based in London 没错。我和丈夫搬到了伦敦，因此我设法找到了一份在伦敦出版的全国性报纸的工作。
Mr. Parsons:  The London Weekly right 《伦敦周报》，对吗？
Rebecca:  Yes, in some ways it was a step down from my previous job but it did offer me much better prospects for the future. 是的，从某些方面来说，这份工作比之前的职位要低一些，但它确实为我提供了更好的未来前景。
