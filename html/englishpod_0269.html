The Office - Marketing Plan (C0269)

A:  Okay everyone, let’s begin. I called you here today to evaluate our marketing strategy during this recession. I wanted to re-emphasize our corporate mission of Aiming to give our customers the best coffee and service in a clean and welcoming atmosphere. 大家好，我们开始吧。今天把大家召集起来，是为了评估我们在经济衰退期间的营销策略。我想再次强调我们的企业使命：在干净、温馨的氛围中，为客户提供最好的咖啡和服务。
B:  Several other shops have reduced the prices for their coffees and are drawing in more customers. Why aren’t we doing the same thing?其他几家店铺已经降低了咖啡价格，吸引了更多顾客。为什么我们不这么做呢？
A:  I know that recent sales have been slow, but we are not going to reduce our prices to the level of our competitors. We offer a superior product and our focus is on long-term growth rather than short-term sales. If we lower our prices, we run the risk of devaluing our product. 我知道最近销量确实放缓了，但我们绝不会将价格降到与竞争对手相同的水平。我们提供的是优质产品，我们的重点在于长期增长，而非短期销售。如果降低价格，就有贬低我们产品的风险。
B:  Customers don’t care about the coffee anymore. They only care about the price. 客户不再关心咖啡本身了，他们只在乎价格。
A:  I disagree. Highly discerning customers know that our coffee is far better than the coffee you buy at the other places. Our coffee bean are artisan roasted and we use state-of-the-art equipment to brew our coffees. When you compare the coffees side-by-side our coffee wins the taste test every time. We have never sought to appeal to the mass market with cheap coffee drinks, and we will not do so now. 我不同意。那些眼光挑剔的客户清楚知道，我们的咖啡远比其他地方的好。我们的咖啡豆采用手工烘焙，并使用最先进的设备冲泡。如果将咖啡并排对比，我们的咖啡在口味测试中每次都胜出。我们从未试图用廉价咖啡饮品吸引大众市场，现在也不会这么做。
C:  That’s true. We’ve certainly achieved top of mind awareness when it comes to the best best tasting brews and it’s important to distinguish ourselves from our competitors. I think the main question is how we can show our appreciation to our customers.确实如此。我们在“最佳口感咖啡”这一认知上已占据领先地位，因此区分自己与竞争对手至关重要。我认为核心问题在于：我们该如何向客户表达感激之情。
A:  That’s the main question I would like to discuss today. 这正是我今天想重点讨论的问题。
B:  Money is tight for everyone these days so even our most loyal customers may be reconsidering the money they pay for their morning coffee. Since the superiority of our coffee beans is one of our core competencies why don’t we sell the beans for people to brew coffee at home.如今大家手头都紧，就连最忠实的顾客也可能重新考虑为早晨那杯咖啡支付的费用。既然我们咖啡豆的优越性是我们的核心优势，为什么不直接卖豆子，让人们在家自己冲泡呢？
C:  That could definitely be a way we could expand our company, but would we be undermining the essence of the company that way?这确实能帮我们拓展业务，但这样做会不会损害公司的本质？
A:  Let’s brainstorm some more ideas, and do some research. The customer always comes first, and what the customer wants, the customer gets. Maybe it’s time we started selling coffee beans. 让我们再头脑风暴一些点子，并做点调研吧。客户永远第一，顾客想要的，我们就给。也许该开始销售咖啡豆了。
