The Office - Interview Skills Part 1 - The Introduction (C0235)

Mr. Parsons:  Come in. 请进。
Rebecca Carlyle:  Mr Parsons ? 帕森斯先生？
Mr. Parsons:  Ah, you must be Rebecca. Please do come in. 啊，您一定是丽贝卡了。请进。
Rebecca Carlyle:  Thank you for making some time to see me Mr Parsons. It's a pleasure to meet you finally. 感谢您抽出时间见我，帕森斯先生。很高兴终于见到您。
Mr. Parsons:  The pleasure's all mine Rebecca. Have a seat please . Now would you like any refreshments? Tea or coffee? 我也很高兴，丽贝卡。请坐。想喝点什么吗？茶还是咖啡？
Rebecca Carlyle:  A coffee would be lovely thank you. Black, no sugar. 来杯咖啡就好，谢谢。黑咖啡，不加糖。
Mr. Parsons:  No problem. Sally can we have two coffees please One, no milk or sugar? 没问题。萨莉，给我们两杯咖啡好吗？一杯，不要牛奶或糖？
Sally:  Certainly Mr Parsons . 当然可以，帕森斯先生。
Mr. Parsons:  So Rebecca, I understand you had a first interview with Miss Childs last week. 那么，丽贝卡，我听说您上周和奇尔德斯小姐进行了第一次面试。
Rebecca Carlyle:  Yes that's correct. She filled me in onthe details ofthe job onthe telephone. 是的，没错。她通过电话向我介绍了工作的具体细节。
Mr. Parsons:  Great. Well, I'm glad to say she recommended you for a 2nd interview, and here we are. Perhaps we can start by discussing your background and resume details a little? 太好了，很高兴地告诉你她推荐你参加第二轮面试，我们现在就在这里。或许我们可以先聊聊你的背景和简历细节？
Rebecca Carlyle:  Yes , of course. 当然可以。
