The Office - Interview Skills 5 - Discussing Reasons For Leaving Previous Position (C0247)

Mr. Parsons:  Okay, now I'd like to find out more about your last job. I see you spent almost four years at the London Weekly , is that right? 好的，我想多了解一下你上一份工作的情况。我看到你在《伦敦周刊》工作了将近四年，是这样吗？
Rebecca:  Yes, that's right. To be honest, the first year was quite tough for me. I was really just treated more like an intern. I didn't have many responsibilities and I found it quite frustrating. 是的，没错。说实话，第一年对我来说相当艰难。我基本上只被当作实习生对待。我没有多少职责在身，这让我感到相当沮丧。
Mr. Parsons:  So, what changed? 那么，是什么发生了变化呢？
Rebecca:  Well slowly but surely I proved myself, and the new editor liked me so he promoted me to features writer . 嗯，慢慢地但肯定地，我证明了自己的能力，新编辑喜欢我，于是将我晋升为特稿记者。
Mr. Parsons:  Wow, a real step up! 哇，真是个大飞跃！
Rebecca:  Yes I was responsible for restaurant and food reviews mostly. I spent restaurant years in that position, but to be honest it wasn't an area of journalism I wanted to stay in long-term. 是的，我主要负责餐厅和美食评论。我在该职位上待了好几年，但说实话，那不是我想长期从事的新闻领域。
Mr. Parsons:  I see, so why did you decide to leave finally? 我明白了，那你最终决定离开的原因是什么？
Rebecca:  I just felt that the paper couldn't offer me any new opportunities. I really needed a more challenging role to be honest. 我只是觉得那家报纸无法为我提供新的机会。老实说，我真的需要一个更具挑战性的角色。

frustrating令人沮丧to cause feelings of discouragement or bafflement在餐馆批评食物的人

to prove oneself证明自己to show to have the character or ability expected晚餐软件
long-term长期的covering a relatively long period of time亲爱的，你可以摆桌子吗？
challenging有挑战性的a test of one's abilities or resources in a demanding but stimulating undertaking当然 我们吃什么？需要我做点什么吗？
