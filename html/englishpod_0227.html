Daily Life - Wedding Planning (C0227)

A:  Trina, will you marry me? Trina，你愿意嫁给我吗？
B:  Yes! Yes! And yes! Jared of course I'll marry you! 当然愿意！我愿意！Jared，我当然会嫁给你！
A:  Oh Babe, I can't wait to spend the rest of my life with you! I can't wait for all the adventures we're going to have, for all the fights and the laughter. I can't wait to grow old and wrinkly with you.亲爱的，我等不及和你共度余生啦！我等不及去经历我们所有的冒险、争吵和欢笑。我等不及和你一起变老、长皱纹。
B:  Oh Jared! I can't wait for our wedding! I hope you don't mind, but I've already chosen a date! Six months from now in the summer! Melissa saw you buying the ring last month so I've had plenty of time to start planning! Oh Jared！我等不及我们的婚礼了！我希望你不介意，但我已经选好了日期！六个月后的夏天！Melissa 上个月看到你买戒指，所以我已经有足够的时间开始筹备了！
A:  She what? 她什么？
B:  Oh don't worry sweetie, I didn't know when you were going to propose. It was still a nice surprise! As I was saying, I've got it all planned out. There's almost nothing left to do! I wrote up our guest list and we will have roughly four hundred guests attending. 亲爱的别担心，我不知道你什么时候会求婚，所以这仍然是一个惊喜！正如我所说，我已经把一切都计划好了。几乎没什么可做的了！我列出了宾客名单，我们将邀请大约四百位客人参加。
A:  four hundred? 四百人？
B:  No need to sweat it My parents agreed to pay for most of the wedding, which is going to be low-budget anyway. So roughly four hundred people, which means that the hall at Northwood Heights will be our reception venue. I thought it would be nice if we had the wedding at your parents' church and my uncle of course would be officiating. We'll meet with him soon for some pre-wedding counseling. The music for the wedding ceremony was a no-brainer. My step-sister and her string quartet will take care of that. My cousin will be the official photographer. I thought it would also be nice if his daughter could sing a solo. Did you know that she's going to be a professional opera singer? 不必为此烦恼，我父母同意承担大部分婚礼费用，而婚礼本身也会是低预算的。大约会有四百人参加，这意味着北伍德高地的大厅将作为我们的宴会场地。我想如果能在你父母的教堂举行婚礼会很好，当然我的叔叔会担任证婚人。我们很快就会与他见面进行婚前辅导。婚礼仪式的音乐安排很简单：我继姐妹和她的弦乐四重奏将负责演奏。我的表亲将是官方摄影师。我还觉得如果能让他女儿独唱会很棒，你知道她将成为职业歌剧歌手吗？
A:  Ah... 啊……
B:  And then of course the ladies at the church would love to be our caterers for the banquet and we'll get the Youth Group to serve us. I was thinking that your friend's band could be our entertainment for the night. though they might have to tone it down a bit. Or we could hire a DJ. Your sister's husband could get us a discount with that company that does the decor at weddings. what's their name again? I was thinking that we could have an island paradise- themed wedding and our theme color would be a soothing blue like Aquamarine. And there will be a huge seashell on the wall behind the podium where we'll make our toasts! What do you think of small packages of drink mixes for our wedding favors? Who else am I missing? Oh, your uncle could be our florist and his wife could make our wedding cake! 当然，教堂的女士们很乐意为我们承办宴会，我们可以让青年团来帮忙服务。我在想，你朋友的乐队可以负责当晚的娱乐，不过他们可能得稍微低调一点。或者我们也可以请个 DJ。你姐夫可以通过那家做婚礼布置的公司帮我们拿折扣，那家公司叫什么来着？我在想办一个热带海岛主题的婚礼，主色调就用像海蓝宝石那样舒缓的蓝色。讲台后面墙上会放一个巨大的贝壳装饰，到时候我们就在那里举杯祝酒！你觉得用些小包装的饮料混合包作为回礼怎么样？我还漏了谁吗？哦，你叔叔可以做我们的花艺师，他老婆可以给我们做婚礼蛋糕！
A:  Wow. 哇。
B:  See? It's going to be wonderful! Oh this wedding is going to be everything I ever dreamed of.看吧，一定会很棒！这场婚礼将是我梦寐以求的一切。
A:  If I survive the next six months. 如果我能撑过接下来的六个月的话。
