Global View - Capital Punishment(0144)

Professor:  That's all for today's class. We will continue our lecture on crime and punishment tomorrow.今天的课就到这里，明天我们继续讲解犯罪与刑罚的话题。
A:  Do you think we should be tougher on crime?你觉得我们应该对犯罪采取更严厉的措施吗？
B:  Well, it depends on what you mean.嗯，这得看你具体指什么。
A:  For example, we could bring back the death penalty for murder, give longer prison sentences for lesser offences and lock up juvenile offenders.比如说，我们可以恢复死刑来惩罚谋杀罪，给较轻的罪行判更长的刑期，把未成年罪犯关起来。
B:  Those really sound like Draconian measures. Firstly, what do you do about miscarriages of justice if you've already put innocent people to death?这些听起来真是 draconian 的措施啊。首先，如果已经处死了无辜的人，那司法不公怎么办？
A:  You'd only use capital punishment if you were absolutely sure that you'd convicted the right person.只有在绝对确定你抓对了人的情况下，才会使用死刑。
B:  But, there've been many cases of wrongful conviction where people have been imprisoned for many years. The authorities were sure at the time, but later it was shown that the evidence was unreliable. In some cases, it'd been fabricated by the police.但是，历史上有很多冤假错案，人们被冤枉入狱多年。当局当时是确信无疑的，但后来证明证据不可靠。有些情况下，甚至是警察伪造了证据。
A:  Well, no system of justice can be perfect, but surely there's a good case for longer prison sentences to deter serious crime.嗯，没有哪个司法体系是完美的，但至少有更长的刑期来威慑严重犯罪是有道理的。
B:  I doubt whether they could act as an effective deterrent while the detection rate is so low. The best way to prevent crime is to convince people who commit it that they're going to be caught. It doesn't make sense to divert all your resources into the prison system.我怀疑在破案率这么低的情况下，它们能起到有效的威慑作用。预防犯罪的最好办法是让罪犯相信他们一定会被抓到。把所有资源都投入到监狱系统里是没有意义的。
A:  But if you detect more crimes, you'll still need prisons. In my reckoning, if we could lock up more juvenile criminals, they'd learn that they couldn't get away with it. Soft sentences will merely encourage them to do it again.但如果你发现更多犯罪，你仍然需要监狱。依我看，如果我们能关押更多的未成年罪犯，他们就会明白自己无法逍遥法外。轻判只会鼓励他们再次犯罪。
B:  Yes, but remember that prisons are often schools for criminals. To remove crime from society, you really have to tackle its causes.是的，但要记住，监狱往往是罪犯的学校。要从社会中消除犯罪，你必须真正解决其根源。
A:  Well, if I were president, I would impose tougher laws and punishment. I would have a peaceful society based on fear of punishment, not consciousness of doing the right thing.好吧，如果我是总统，我会颁布更严厉的法律和刑罚。我会建立一个基于对惩罚的恐惧而非对做正确之事认知的和平社会。
B:  You sound like a dictator!你听起来像个独裁者！
A:  Well if it works, why not?好吧，如果有效，为什么不呢？
