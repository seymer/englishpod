Daily Life - Christmas Day (C0181)

A:  Dad, dad, dad! Wake up! It's Christmas! 爸爸，爸爸，爸爸！快醒醒！圣诞节到了！
B:  Timmy. It's too early for this. Look, it's six in the morning! Go back to bed! 蒂米。这太早了。看，才早上六点！回床上去！
A:  No way! Santa already came and left all our presents! Can we go open them? Please! Please! 没门！圣诞老人已经来过并留下所有礼物了！我们能打开看看吗？求你了！求你了！
C:  Of course we can honey. Bill, come on, get dressed. 当然可以啦，亲爱的。比尔，快点，穿衣服。
B:  Fine! Not like Santa brought me any gifts! 行吧！不像圣诞老人给我带了任何礼物！
C:  Bill! Honestly, you can be such a grouch sometimes. 比尔！说实话，你有时候真像个爱抱怨的家伙。
A:  Look at all these presents under the Christmas tree! Awesome! 看看圣诞树下这些礼物！太棒了！
B:  Alright Timmy, knock yourself out. We should get ready and head to the market to buy everything for the Christmas dinner tonight. 好吧蒂米，随你便。我们应该准备好，然后去市场买今晚圣诞晚餐需要的东西。
C:  Yeah you're right. It's the first time we are hosting Christmas dinner at our house so everything has to be perfect. 对，你说得对。这是我们第一次在家里举办圣诞晚餐，所以一切必须完美。
B:  I got the list right here. Ham, turkey, mashed potatoes, ingredients for the gravy and of course, yams! 清单就在这儿。火腿、火鸡、土豆泥、肉汁配料，当然还有红薯！
C:  My dad offered to bring the eggnog so we should be set! 我爸爸主动提出带蛋酒过来，这样我们就万事俱备了！

host主办hold an activity举行活动
set托球ready for something (informal)准备做某事（非正式）
