Daily Life - Setting Up Your Voice mail Message (C0332)

A:  Can you help me set up my voicemail message? I just got this service and I am not really sure what I am supposed to say.能帮我设置一下语音信箱吗？我刚开通这项服务，不太确定该说什么。
B:  Sure! You just basically gotta let the caller know who they called, and ask them for their contact information so you can call them back.没问题！你基本上得让来电者知道他们打给了谁，并请求他们的联系方式，以便你回电。
A:  Ok, so can I say, “ This is Abby’s voicemail. I will call you later, so leave me your name and number”.好的，那我可以说：“这是艾比的语音信箱。我稍后会回电，所以请留下你的姓名和电话号码。”
B:  That’s more or less the idea, but try something that sounds more friendly.大体意思对，但试着说得更友好些。
A:  Ok, so how about this, “ This is Abby and I am really happy you called! I promise I will give you a ring as soon as I can, so please leave me your name and number. Talk to you soon!”.那这样呢：“你好，我是艾比，很高兴你打来！我保证一有空就给你回电话，所以请留下你的姓名和电话号码。回头聊！”
B:  A little too friendly Abby. Just say this, “ Hi, you have reached Abby. I am unable to answer your call right now, but if you leave me your name and phone number, I will get back to you as soon as possible. Thanks”.有点太热情了，艾比。就说这个：“你好，你已接通艾比的语音信箱。我现在无法接听，但如果你留下姓名和电话号码，我会尽快回电。谢谢。”
A:  That’s perfect! Can you say that again and record it for me?完美！你能再说一遍并录下来给我吗？
