Daily Life - You're Grounded! (C0258)

A:  Do you know what time it is? 你知道现在几点了吗？
B:  Um. Ten? 嗯。十点？
A:  Get in this door young man. It's midnight, you are two hours past curfew. 快进屋，年轻人。都半夜了，你已经晚归两个小时了。
B:  I know, but it wasn't my fault! I told Jennifer she had to drop me off before ten, but she wouldn't leave the party! 我知道，但这不是我的错！我告诉詹妮弗要在十点钟前送我回家，可她不肯离开派对！
A:  I don't care! You are grounded for life mister! 我才不在乎！你被终身禁足了，先生！
B:  Mom! That is so unfair! 妈妈！这太不公平了！
A:  You know the rules and you broke them. No allowance and no TV for a week. I usually never ground you but this time I have to put my foot down! 你知道规矩的，你也违反了。一周内不许零花钱，也不许看电视。我通常从不把你禁足，但这次我必须强硬到底！
B:  What! For being a couple of hours late? You have to be kidding! 什么！就因为晚回来几个小时？你在开玩笑吧！
A:  I don't want to hear it! Now go to your room! 我不想再听你说什么了！现在回你房间去！
