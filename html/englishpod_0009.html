The Weekend - Silence please!(0009)

A:  Those people in front of us are making so much noise. It’s so inconsiderate!前面那些人太吵了，真是不体谅别人！
B:  Don’t worry about it; it's not such a big deal.别担心，没什么大不了的。
A:  Oh... I can’t hear a thing! Excuse me, can you keep it down?哦……我什么都听不见！不好意思，能小声点吗？
C:  Sure, sorry 'bout that!当然可以，抱歉啦！
A:  Someone’s phone is ringing!有人在打电话！
B:  Honey, I think it’s your phone. Did you forget to switch it off?亲爱的，我想是你的手机响了。你忘记关机了吗？
A:  Oh, no! You’re right. That’s so embarrassing!哎呀，糟糕！你说得对，太尴尬了！
C:  Do you mind keeping it down? I’m trying to watch a movie here!能小声点吗？我正在这儿看电影呢！
