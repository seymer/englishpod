Daily Life - I Can See Clearly Now(0020)

A:  Hello, Arthur. What seems to be the problem?你好，亚瑟。怎么了？
B:  Hey doc. Well, I think I might need glasses. I'm getting headaches, and I really struggle to see things that are far away. But I have always had 20/20 vision.嘿，医生。我想我可能需要配眼镜了。我最近经常头痛，看远处的东西也很吃力。但我以前视力一直很好啊。
A:  Sounds like you may be far-sighted. OK, then, cover your left eye and read the chart in front of you.听起来你可能是远视。好的，那就遮住左眼，读一下眼前的视力表吧。
B:  Mmm… X, E, R, 3, a question mark, and I can't quite make out the other symbol but I think it's the peace sign.嗯……X、E、R、3、一个问号，另一个符号看不太清，但我觉得像是和平标志。
A:  Wow, Arthur! You're as blind as a bat!哇，亚瑟！你简直瞎得像只蝙蝠！
B:  Yeah, I know, my vision is really blurry at times.是啊，我知道，有时候我的视力真的很模糊。
A:  Ok, then, head on over to the other room and pick out some frames while I fill out your prescription.好的，那你到隔壁房间去挑一副眼镜框吧，我顺便给你开处方。
B:  Thanks, doc!谢谢，医生！
A:  Arthur, that's the bathroom.亚瑟，那是浴室。
