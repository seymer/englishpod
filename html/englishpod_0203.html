Daily Life - Calling Tech Support (C0203)

Tech guy:  Hello, thanks for calling 123TechHelp, I’m Todd. How can I help you? 你好，感谢致电 123TechHelp，我是托德。请问有什么可以帮您？
Client:  Hello? Can you help me? My computer! Oh man... 喂？能帮帮我吗？我的电脑！天哪……
Todd:  It’s okay sir, calm down. What happened? 没事的先生，请冷静。到底发生什么事了？
Client:  I turned on my laptop and it broke! I mean, the monitor went black! 我打开笔记本电脑它就坏了！我是说，屏幕变黑了！
Todd:  Ok, sir, it sounds like you might have a virus. 好的先生，听起来您可能中了病毒。
Client:  I don’t feel sick,...let me check... Nope! No fever, I’m fine. 我没觉得生病……让我检查一下……没有！没发烧，我没事。
Todd:  No, your computer might have a virus, I mean, it has a bad program on it. Maybe that’s why it crashed. I recommend that you run an antivirus program in order to safely remove any unwanted spyware or Trojans. 不，您的电脑可能中了病毒，我是说，它感染了恶意程序。也许这就是它崩溃的原因。建议您运行杀毒软件，以安全地清除任何不需要的间谍软件或特洛伊木马。
Client:  Phew! ……Wait a minute, CRASH??!! Spyware? Trojans! What? where? when?! 呼……等等，崩溃？间谍软件？特洛伊木马？什么？在哪？何时？

I mean我是说to express ones own opinion, meaning表达自己的观点、意思

virus病毒a program designed to harm a computer旨在损害计算机的程序
nope不no (informal)否（非正式）
program程序a set of instructions to perform一组要执行的指令
crash碰撞stop working or functioning properly停止工作或正常运作
antivirus杀毒软件a program designed to identify and remove computer viruses旨在识别和删除计算机病毒的程序
run运转to work or be in operation工作或正在运作
spyware间谍软件software that secretly gathers information秘密收集信息的软件
