Daily Life - Funeral Arrangements (C0213)

A:  Hi Daniel, how are you holding up? I am greatly sorry for your loss. 嗨，丹尼尔，你最近怎么样？我对你的遭遇深感悲痛。
B:  Thank you, I'm doing much better. I've begun organizing everything for the funeral. 谢谢，我好多了。我已经开始安排葬礼事宜了。
A:  How's that going ?进展如何？
B:  It's a lot harder than I imagined. There are many things that you have to to arrange. I booked a time and date with the funeral home, but I still have a lot of things to do. 比我想象的要困难得多。有很多事情需要操办。我已经和殡仪馆约好了时间和日期，但还有很多事要做。
A:  Have you bought a burial plot and a casket? 你买墓地了吗？买了棺材吗？
B:  No. Wendy is being cremated. She always talked about how she didn't want to be buried. I already chose a cremation urn and we plan to spread the ashes in the ocean. 没有。温迪选择火化。她生前一直说她不想被埋葬。我已经选好了骨灰瓮，我们计划把骨灰撒进海里。
A:  I see, that sounds like something she would have really liked. I am sure the memorial service will be tasteful. You are doing a great job. 我明白了，这听起来正是她会喜欢的。我相信追悼会一定会庄重得体。你做得很好。
B:  Thanks, it hasn't been easy, but luckily we have life insurance and Wendy left behind a detailed will that will sort out any other legal matters. 谢谢，这并不容易，但幸运的是我们有寿险，而且温迪留下了详细的遗嘱，可以处理其他法律事宜。

Key Vocabulary

hold up延误continue to live through hardship继续经历苦难
burial埋葬the act of burying, esp the interment of a dead body埋葬的行为，尤指埋葬尸体的行为
cremate火化burn up something and reduce to ash烧掉某物并化为灰烬
tasteful有品位being in keeping with good taste符合良好品味
detail细节having many details有很多细节
