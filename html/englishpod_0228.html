The Weekend - Going to the Beach (C0228)

A:  Oh, George, what a beautiful day it is today! The sun is hot and there are just a few clouds scattered here and there! What a perfect day to be at the beach! The kids are going to have so much fun! And we'll be able to relax in the sun while they're playing. 哦，乔治，今天天气真美！太阳很热，天上只零星飘着几朵云！真是去海滩的完美日子！孩子们会玩得很开心！我们也能在阳光里放松，让他们尽情玩耍。
B:  It does seem like the perfect day! I'm glad we chose to get out of the city and enjoy the nice weather! This looks like the perfect spot! Ok kids, put on your sunscreen while your mom and I set up camp. Here, Mary, help me lay down these beach towels. 确实像是完美的一天！我很高兴我们选择离开城市，享受这好天气！这里看起来是绝佳地点！好了孩子们，穿上防晒霜吧，等我和你们妈妈搭好帐篷。玛丽，帮我把这些沙滩巾铺好。
A:  There we go. Can you help me with the umbrella? Perfect. 搞定啦。能帮我撑一下遮阳伞吗？太好了。
B:  Ok kids, here'sa beach ball and a Frisbee, a pail and a shovel. I want to see an impressive sandcastle by the time we leave. Don't stray too far. Wait! Leave your sandals here or put on your wet shoes. 好了孩子们，这是沙滩球、飞盘、水桶和铲子。在我们离开前，我要看到一座令人惊叹的沙堡。别跑太远。等等！把凉鞋留在这儿，或者穿上防水鞋。
A:  And stay in the shallow area. I don't want to see you go any farther than that sandbar! It's too deep out there and we didn't bring your floaties. 待在浅水区。我不想让你们游到那块沙洲以外！那边水太深了，我们也没带你们的游泳圈。
B:  You're back already? The water was too cold, huh? I'll tell you a secret. Do you see that small pool of water over there? It'll be warmer in there. Go see if you can find some seashells or catch some minnows. 你们怎么这么快就回来了？水太冷了吧？我跟你们说个秘密。看见那边那小片水域了吗？那里水温会暖和些。去看看能不能找到些贝壳或抓几条小鱼。
A:  What is that? A jellyfish? Jeremy, put that down right now! It could sting you! 那是什么？水母？杰里米，立刻放下！它会蛰你的！
B:  Ah! Not onme! Ow! 啊！别扎我！哎哟！
