Daily Life - Small Talk(0044)

A:  Morning.早上好。
B:  Hi there, Mr. Anderson! How are you on this fine morning?嗨，安德森先生！这大早晨您还好吗？
A:  Fine, thank you.挺好的，谢谢。
B:  It sure is cold this morning, isn't it? I barely even got out of bed!今早可真冷啊，不是吗？我差点都没起床！
A:  Yeah. It’s pretty cold, alright.是啊，确实挺冷的，没错。
B:  Did you catch the news this morning? I heard that there was a fire on Byron Street.今早您看新闻了吗？听说拜伦街着火了。
A:  No, I didn't hear about that.没，我没听说那事儿。
B:  Did you happen to watch the football game last night? The Patriots scored in the last minute!昨晚您看橄榄球赛了吗？爱国者队最后一分钟得分了！
A:  No, I don’t like football.不，我不喜欢橄榄球。
B:  Oh… By the way, I saw you with your daughter at the office Christmas party. She is really beautiful!哦……对了，我在办公室圣诞聚会上看见您和女儿了。她真漂亮！
A:  She’s my wife! Oh, here’s my floor! Nice talking to you. Goodbye.她是我的妻子！哦，这是我的楼层！很高兴和你聊天。再见。
B:  Sir, this is the fifty-sixth floor! We are on the seventieth!先生，这是五十六楼！我们在七楼！
A:  That’s okay, I'll take the stairs!没关系，我走楼梯吧！
