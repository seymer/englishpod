Daily Life - Handyman (C0285)

A:  The air conditioning is not working! We need to call a handyman before we start to fry in here! 空调坏了！我们得赶紧请个维修工，不然快要在里面热死了！
B:  Dan is on top of that. I think they are also getting the handyman to fix the bathroom toilet that keeps clogging up. 丹正在处理这事。我想他们也会请维修工来修那总是堵塞的卫生间马桶。
A:  That would be convenient. They might as well ask him to fix the electrical wiring. The circuit breakers keep going out all the time. It’s really annoying! 那倒方便。不如让他顺便把电路也修了。断路器老跳闸，烦死人了！
B:  Yeah you are right. This office is falling apart! Frank told me the other day that the gutters outside were clogged and that’s why the parking lot was flooded. 对，你说得没错。这办公室简直要散架了！弗兰克前几天告诉我，外面的排水沟堵了，所以停车场才淹水。
A:  I know! I was in ankle deep water trying to get to my car that day! The handyman definitely has his work cut out for him. 我知道！那天我脚踝深的水里挣扎着去取车呢！维修工的工作量肯定不小。
