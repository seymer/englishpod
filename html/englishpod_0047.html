Advanced - Just In Time Strategy(0047)

A:  I called this meeting today in order to discuss our manufacturing plan. As I'm sure you're all aware, with the credit crunch, and the global financial crisis, we're obligated to look for more cost efficient ways of producing our goods. We don't want to have to be looking at redundancies. So, we've outlined a brief plan to implement the just-in-time philosophy.今天我召开这次会议，是为了讨论我们的制造计划。正如大家所知，受信贷紧缩和全球金融危机的影响，我们有义务寻找更高效的成本生产方式。我们不想被迫进行裁员。因此，我们制定了一个简要计划来实施准时制理念。
B:  We have two basic points that we want to focus on. First of all, we want to reduce our lead time.我们要重点关注的有两个基本点。首先，我们要缩短交货提前期。
C:  Why would want to do that? I think this is not an area that really needs to be worked on.为什么要这样做呢？我认为这不是一个真正需要改进的领域。
B:  Well, we want to reduce production and delivery lead timesfor better overall efficiency.嗯，我们希望缩短生产和交付提前期，以提升整体效率。
A:  Right, production lead times can be reduced by moving work stations closer together, reducing queue length, like for example, reducing the number of jobs waiting to be processed at a given machine, and improving the coordination and cooperation between successive processes. Delivery lead times can be reduced through close cooperation with suppliers, possibly by inducing suppliers to locate closer to the factory or working with a faster shipping company. 对，生产提前期可以通过将工作站靠得更近、减少排队长度来缩短，例如减少在特定机器上等待处理的作业数量，并改善连续工序之间的协调与合作。交付提前期可以通过与供应商紧密合作来缩短，可能包括诱导供应商靠近工厂选址或合作使用更快的航运公司。
C:  I see… That makes sense.我明白了……这很有道理。
B:  The second point is that we want to require supplier quality assurance and implement a zero defects quality program. We currently have far too many errors that lead to defective items and therefore, they must be eliminated. A quality control at the source program must be implemented to give workers the personal responsibility for the quality of the work they do, and the authority to stop production when something goes wrong.第二点是我们要求供应商提供质量保证并实施零缺陷质量计划。目前我们因错误导致的次品实在太多，必须予以消除。必须在源头实施质量控制计划，让工人对所做工作的质量承担个人责任，并在出现问题时拥有停止生产的权力。
C:  I'm with you on this one. It’s essential that we reduce these errors; we've got to force our suppliers to reduce their mistakes.我同意这一点。减少这些错误至关重要；我们必须迫使供应商减少失误。
