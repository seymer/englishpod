Daily Life - Homesick (C0153)

Sarah:  Tom! How are you? We missed you at the party last night. Are you ok? 汤姆！你好吗？昨晚派对上我们可把你盼坏了，你还好吧？
Tom:  I don’t know. I didn't really feel like going out. I guess I'm feeling a little homesick. 我不知道。我其实不太想出门。我想我是有点想家了。
Sarah:  Come on We've been through this already! Look, I know the adjustment was hard when you first got here, but we agreed that you were gonna try and deal with it. 得了吧，我们都经历过这个了！听着，我知道你刚来这儿时适应起来很难，但我们说好你要努力应对的。
Tom:  I was. It’s just that the holidays are coming up and I won’t be able to home because I can’t afford the airfare. I'm just longing for some of the comforts of home, like my mom’s cooking and being around my family. 是的，我就是这么做的。只是假期快到了，而我付不起机票钱回不去家。我只是怀念家里的舒适，比如妈妈做的饭菜和家人的陪伴。
Sarah:  Yeah, it can get pretty lonely over the holidays. When I first got here, I’d get depressed and nostalgic for anything that reminded me of home. I almost let it get to me, but then I started going out, keeping myself busy and before I knew it, I was used to to it. 是啊，过节确实挺孤独的。我刚来的时候，只要看到任何让我想起家的东西就会抑郁、怀旧。我差点就被它击垮了，但后来我开始出门，让自己忙起来，不知不觉就习惯了。
Tom:  I see what you mean, but I 'm still bummed out. 我明白你的意思，但我还是心情低落。
Sarah:  Ok how does this sound: let's get you suited up and hit the dance club tonight. I hear that an awesome DJ is playing and there will be a lot of pretty single girls there! 那这样行吗：今晚我们给你打扮一下，然后去夜店嗨皮？听说有个超棒的 DJ 在打碟，而且会有很多漂亮的单身女孩在那儿！
Tom:  You know, I could really go for that. You don’t mind being my wingman for tonight?你知道吗，我特别想去。你今晚愿意做我的“僚机”吗？
Sarah:  Not at all! It be fun! It will be like a boys night out... well kinda... 完全没问题！肯定很有趣！就像男生们出去放松……嗯，差不多吧……
Tom:  Great! I must warn you though, whatever happens, don't let me go on a drinking binge. Trust me, it's not a pretty picture! 太好了！不过我得提醒你，无论发生什么，千万别让我喝成烂醉。相信我，那场面可不好看！
