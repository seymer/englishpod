The Weekend - Asking For Directions (C0202)

A:  We have been going around in circles for the past hour! Will you just please stop and ask for directions? 我们兜圈子已经一个小时了！你就不能停下来问路吗？
B:  We are not lost! I'm just taking the scenic route. 我们没迷路！我只是想走风景好的路线。
A:  Yeah, whatever. I told you we should buy that GPS that was on sale, but would you listen to me? No! This is so typical. 行吧，随便你。我说过应该买那个打折的 GPS，但你听不听？不！这太典型了。
B:  Fine! I'll ask this guy for directions if it will shut your trap! Excuse me, sir. Can you tell me how to get to Saint Mary's Church? 好吧！如果这样能让你闭嘴，我就问这位先生指路！打扰一下，先生。请问去圣玛丽教堂怎么走？
C:  Sure! Go down Park Road. turn left, go up as far as the set of traffic lights and turn left. The place you are looking for isthe second building on the right. 当然！沿着公园路往下走，左转，一直走到红绿灯处再左转。你要找的地方就在右边的第二个建筑。
A:  Thanks! 谢谢！
B:  See? Was that hard? If you would only listen to me more often, you would be better off. 看到了吗？难吗？如果你多听我的话，情况会好得多。
