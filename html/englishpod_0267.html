Daily Life - Making A Bank Transfer (C0267)

A:  Good Morning welcome to Bank of the USA. How may I help you today?早上好，欢迎光临美国银行。今天有什么可以帮您的？
B:  Hi I need to transfer some money to another account. It’s urgent. 嗨，我需要把钱转到另一个账户上。很紧急。
A:  Okay, have you made a wire transfer at our bank before ?好的，您以前在我们银行做过电汇吗？
B:  No. I've never made a transfer before. 没有。我从来没转过账。
A:  It’s alright, I will take you through the procedure. Are you transferring funds to a company or an individual account?没关系，我会带您完成整个流程。您是向公司账户还是个人账户转账？
B:  A company account. I need to pay a bill. 是公司账户。我要付一笔账单。
A:  Okay, I’ll need the name of the company and their bank routing number as well as their bank's address and phone number. 好的，我需要公司的名称、他们的银行路由号，以及银行的地址和电话号码。
B:  I have all the information in this folder. 我所有的信息都在这份文件夹里。
A:  Well You've come prepared .You have all the necessary materials so we can go ahead and make the transfer right now. It’s a simple transaction, and we can process it today. 嗯，您准备得很充分。既然您带齐了所有必要材料，我们现在就可以立即办理转账。这是一笔简单的交易，今天就能处理完成。
B:  Oh, that’s such a relief. I didn’t want the payment to be overdue. Thank you so much . 哦，这真是如释重负。我可不想让付款逾期。非常感谢。
A:  It’s my pleasure. 这是我的荣幸。
