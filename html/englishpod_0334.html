The Office - Small Talk 10 - General Talk (C0334)

Mr. Campbell:  Hi ladies. Thanks for picking me up. It's awful weather out there!嗨，女士们。谢谢你们来接我。外面天气真糟糕！
Michelle:  Absolutely. It's been raining for hours.是啊。已经下了好几个小时的雨了。
Mr. Campbell:  How are you Melissa? Are you okay?你好吗，梅丽莎？你还好吧？
Melissa:  I'm great thanks, Mr. Campbell.我很好，谢谢，坎贝尔先生。
Michelle:  Do you have any business trips planned soon Mr. Campbell?最近有什么商务行程计划吗，坎贝尔先生？
Mr. Campbell:  Of course. I'm always travelling! I will leave for London next Monday, and then I'll fly to Boston from there. It's going to be a busy month. How about you Michelle? Any vacation plans?当然有。我一直都在出差！下周一我就要去伦敦，然后从那里飞往波士顿。这个月会很忙。你呢，米歇尔？有什么度假计划吗？
Michelle:  Yes. Mike and I will travel to Beijing to see Mikes parents for Spring festival, and hopefully next year we will visit London. I hear it's a wonderful city.有的。我和迈克要去北京过春节，看看迈克的父母，希望明年我们也能去伦敦。我听说那是一个很棒的城市。
Mr. Campbell:  I couldn't agree more. London is really fantastic. It's my favorite city. I'm sure you'll have a great time.我非常同意。伦敦真的很棒。那是我最喜欢的城市。我相信你会度过一段愉快的时光。
