A:  Welcome back movie lovers to another " Premier Movie Review". My name is Richard Clarke and I am joined today by the very erudite David Watson.欢迎回到“首映电影评论”节目。我是理查德·克拉克，今天和我一起的是学识渊博的大卫·沃森。
B:  Thank you Dick. Today we are going to talk about the movie " Lion King". Tell me Dave, what is your impression of this film?谢谢迪克。今天我们聊聊《狮子王》这部电影。大卫，你对这部影片有什么印象？
A:  Well, I think this film is simply a fable, depicting man's eternal greed for power, and in my opinion, it's a very fine film. Even despite the accusations of plagiarism traditional folk tales from other countries. The musical score was amazing, the animation was very well done, and the story was simply enchanting.嗯，我觉得这部电影简直就是一部寓言，描绘了人类对权力永恒的贪婪。在我看来，它是一部非常出色的电影。即便有人指控它抄袭其他国家的民间传说，它的配乐依然令人惊叹，动画制作精良，故事更是引人入胜。
B:  I think otherwise. Even though the animation was technically strong, and as you say, the score and songs performed by Elton John were great, the film lacks a certain originality; it lacked heart. And I would dare to say, it was too predictable.我持不同看法。虽然从技术角度看动画非常出色，正如你所说，埃尔顿·约翰演唱的配乐和歌曲也很棒，但这部电影缺乏原创性；它缺少灵魂。我甚至敢断言，剧情太过 predictable（可预测）。
A:  Predictable! How! Come on Dick, It's a G-rated movie! It's for the kids! It's not a thriller!可预测？怎么可能！拜托了迪克，这可是 G 级电影啊！是给小孩子看的！又不是惊悚片！
B:  Well, that's just it. It did have some very dramatic and intense scenes For example when Mufasa dies, or the dark, grim portrayal of Scar. Even so, the film is linear. Mufasa dies, Simba runs away thinking it's his fault. Falls in love and returns to retake what is rightfully his. It's just too cliché.问题恰恰就在这里。它确实有一些非常戏剧化、紧张激烈的场景。比如木法沙去世那段，或者对刀疤阴暗冷酷的刻画。即便如此，整部电影的叙事依然线性：木法沙死了，辛巴误以为是自己害了他而逃亡；后来他坠入爱河，又回来夺回本该属于自己的东西。这简直太老套了。
A:  How can it be cliché? It's a fable! It's telling a time-honoured story! The movie make a point of how the hunger for power leads to corruption, and teaches children the value of respect, life and love.这怎么能算老套？这是个寓言！它在讲述一个历久弥新的故事！电影着重阐述了权力欲望如何导致腐败，并教导孩子们尊重、生命和爱的价值。
B:  You have always been so soft, Dave!你向来如此心软，戴夫！
A:  Open your heart Dick. Don't shut us out.敞开心扉吧，迪克。别把我们拒之门外。
B:  Anyway... That's all for today folks! Join us next time as we talk about "How to lose a guy in 10 days" I'm sure you'll love that one Dave!总之……今天的节目就到这里了，各位！下次我们将聊聊《十天甩掉一个男人》，我相信戴夫你会喜欢的！
