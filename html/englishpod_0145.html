Daily Life - Chicken Pox(0145)

A:  What's wrong with you? Why are you scratching so much?你怎么了？怎么抓得这么厉害？
B:  I feel itchy! I can't stand it anymore! I think I may be coming down with something. I feel lightheaded and weak.好痒啊！我实在受不了了！我觉得我可能生病了。头晕又虚弱。
A:  Let me have a look. Whoa! Get away from me!让我看看。哇！离我远点！
B:  What's wrong?怎么了？
A:  I think you have chicken pox! You are contagious! Get away! Don't breathe on me!我想你得了水痘！你有传染性！快滚！别对着我呼气！
B:  Maybe it's just a rash or an allergy! We can't be sure until I see a doctor.也许只是皮疹或过敏！在去看医生之前我们还不能确定。
A:  Well in the meantime you are a biohazard! I didn't get it when I was a kid and I've heard that you can even die if you get it as an adult!那眼下你就是个生物危害源！我小时候没得过，听说成年人得了甚至会死！
B:  Are you serious? You always blow things out of proportion. In any case, I think I'll go take an oatmeal bath.你是认真的吗？你总是把事情夸大其词。不管怎样，我想去泡个燕麦浴。
A:  Ewww!呕！
