Daily Life - Buying a TV(0134)

A:  Seriously, I don't know why we need to get a new TV.说真的，我真不明白我们为什么要买台新电视。
B:  Honey I told you already. I can't appreciate the graphics level and detail of the games on my Playstation 3 on our old TV.亲爱的，我之前跟你说过啦。在旧电视上看我的 PS3 游戏，根本欣赏不到画面的精细度和细节。
C:  Good afternoon folks! How can I be of service today?各位下午好！有什么可以帮您的吗？
B:  I'm looking to upgrade to a newer, bigger television set.我想升级一台更新、更大的电视机。
C:  You've come to the right place! What size are you looking for?您来对地方了！您想要多大尺寸的？
A:  Just a normal sized TV for our living room.只要客厅用的一般尺寸就行。
C:  I see. Well this set here is on sale. It's a forty six inch HDTV screen and has all the works. Three HDMI connectors, USB, VGA and S - Video ports. It even has a DVI port so you can hook up your PC or laptop! This is without a doubt the complete home theater experience!明白了。这款正在促销，是 46 英寸的 HDTV 屏幕，功能齐全。三个 HDMI 接口、USB、VGA 和 S-Video 端口。甚至还配有 DVI 接口，您可以连接电脑或笔记本电脑！这绝对是完美的家庭影院体验！
B:  This is exactly what I need! Can you imagine watching movies or playing video games on this thing?这正是我需要的！你能想象在这上面看电影或玩游戏吗？
A:  Honey, I think it's a bit too big. I don't even think it will fit in our living room.亲爱的，我觉得有点太大了。甚至可能放不进我们的客厅。
C:  Not to worry, we will deliver and install it in your home. It comes with a wall mount so you can just hang it on the wall like a picture!别担心，我们会送货上门并安装。还附带壁挂支架，您直接把它挂在墙上就像挂幅画一样！
B:  This is great! How much will this set me back?太棒了！这套多少钱？
C:  Lucky for you, this is the last one we have in stock so it's half off!幸好这是店里最后一台现货，所以半价出售！
B:  I'll take it!我要了！
