A:  Those are today’s top stories. Now let’s go to John for the weather. John, what does the forecast look like for our weekend travelers? 以下是今天的头条新闻。现在把时间交给约翰，播报天气情况。约翰，周末出行的朋友们会看到怎样的天气预报？
B:  I’m afraid we’re in for a rough weekend, Mark. There is a storm system moving through the East Coast. It will be drizzling all day today, and there’s a 60 percent chance of thunderstorms this evening. It will be warm and humid all weekend. A:  That’s pretty chilly for the summer! Will it rain on Saturday? 夏天这么冷可不太常见！周六会下雨吗？
B:  Unfortunately, yes. It will be clear early Saturday morning but there is a high chance of showers and thunderstorms later in the day. A:  That certainly sounds like a dreary Saturday. 这听起来确实是个阴沉的周六。
B:  It gets better on Sunday, though. The storm systems move east and the skies will clear up at night. It will still be rather cool, with highs in the low 50s. The West Coast will be experiencing some unusually chilly weather, but at least the sun will come out. I advise weekend travelers to be careful, especially while driving. Back to you, Mark. 不过周日情况会好转。风暴系统向东移动，夜间天空将放晴。气温依然偏凉，最高温在 50 华氏度出头。西海岸将出现一些异常寒冷的天气，但至少太阳会出来。我提醒周末出行的朋友们务必小心，尤其是驾车时。交还给你，马克。
A:  Thanks John, and there you have it! Looks like it’s a weekend to stay at home! 谢谢约翰，就是这样！看来这个周末适合待在家里！
