The Weekend - Stir Fry (C0194)

A:  Oh, man. I had the best supper last night. My wife made a stir fry and it was amazing! 哦，天哪。昨晚我吃了一顿绝佳的晚餐。我老婆做了个炒青菜，简直太棒了！
B:  I love stir fry Crispy bite-sized vegetables covered in a mixture of soy sauce and oyster sauce. Wilted greens and fresh bean sprouts. Throw in some onion and garlic and ginger! Mmm! Mmm! It's almost lunchtime. I would die for a plate of stir fry right now! 我爱炒青菜——脆嫩的块状蔬菜裹着酱油和蚝油的混合酱汁。还有软塌的绿叶菜和新鲜的豆芽。再加点洋葱、大蒜和生姜！嗯……嗯……都快到午饭时间了。我现在要是能来一盘炒青菜，我非死不可！
A:  Well, you can keep the vegetables, I'll take the meat. The stir fry my wife made was really hearty, with chunks of beef and slivers of bell peppers and onion... 好吧，蔬菜你留着，肉我要了。我老婆做的炒青菜分量十足，有大块的牛肉、细条的青椒和洋葱……
B:  What? You call that a stir fry? More meat than vegetables? That's the worst insult you could throw at a Chinese stir fry What a disgrace to the wok she fried it in! What you had is equivalent to a fajita without the wrap! Silly Americans! 什么？你管那叫炒青菜？肉比菜多？这简直是对中式炒青菜最恶毒的侮辱！她用的锅都被丢尽了脸面！你吃的那东西跟没有卷饼的墨西哥辣炒鸡丝一模一样！愚蠢的美国佬！
