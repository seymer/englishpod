Daily Life - Describing Someone's Face (C0261)

A:  Let's play a game! 我们来玩个游戏吧！
B:  Ok! How about Scrabble? 好啊！要不来拼字游戏？
A:  No no, a friend of mine taught me this really fun game. I'm going to describe someone's face, and you guess who it is! 不不，我朋友教了我一个特别有趣的游戏。我来描述某人的脸，你来猜是谁！
B:  Ok! 好！
A:  Let's see. He has a roman nose, bushy eyebrows and dimples! 让我想想。他有个罗马鼻、浓眉和酒窝！
B:  Our cousin Pete! My turn! She has a pointy nose, sunken eyes and a mole on her chin! 我们的堂弟皮特！轮到我了！她有个尖鼻子、深陷的眼睛和下巴上有一颗痣！
A:  Aunt Rose! That mole is so huge! Ok, my turn. He has a crooked nose and full lips. He has quite a few freckles and an oval face. Oh, he is also bald! 罗丝阿姨！那颗痣也太大了吧！好了，轮到我了。他有个歪鼻子和丰满的嘴唇。他脸上有不少雀斑，脸型是椭圆形的。哦，他还秃顶！
B:  Your future husband! 你未来的丈夫！
A:  Not funny. 真没劲。

Key Vocabulary

dimple酒窝a small natural indentation in the flesh肉体中的小自然缩合
mole痣a small, dark brown spot on a person's skin一个小的深褐色的斑点 在一个人的皮肤上
freckle雀斑a small, brownish spot on someone's skin皮肤上一个褐色的小斑点
oval椭圆形having the shape of an egg有蛋的形状

Supplementary Vocabulary

forehead前额a part of the face above the eyes眼睛上的脸的一部分
eyelash睫毛the hair on the eyelid that protects the eye保护眼睛的眼皮上的头发
chin下巴the lower portion of the face including the jaw脸部下部，包括下巴
