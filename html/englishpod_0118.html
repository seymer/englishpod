A:  Honey, the baby is up again.亲爱的，宝宝又醒啦。
B:  It's your turn! I went last night.轮到你了！昨晚是我换的。
A:  Fine! Hello widdle baby! Why are you crying widdle baby? Oh, I see, you made a doo-doo!行吧！你好呀，小宝贝！你为什么哭呀？哦，我明白了，你拉臭臭了！

A:  The widdle baby made a doo -doo!小宝贝拉臭臭了！
B:  What a good boy! Lets get this icky diaper off you.真是个好孩子！快把这条脏兮兮的尿布给你脱下来。

A:  Hold still while I change this yucky diaper.别动，让我给你换这条恶心的尿布。
C:  What going on in here?里面怎么了？
A:  Oh look it's nana! Say hi to nana!哦，看！是奶奶！跟奶奶打招呼吧！
C:  He's so adorable! I could just eat him up!他太可爱了！我简直想把他吃掉！
A:  Ok, say bye to nana! Time to go beddy - bye!好了，跟奶奶说再见吧！该上床睡觉啦！
