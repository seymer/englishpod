The Weekend - Bug Spray (C0150)

A:  The mosquitos are biting me! 蚊子在咬我！
B:  Me too, I can’t stop scratching. They are everywhere! Sneaky little jerks.我也是，痒得停不下来。它们到处都是！这些狡猾的小混蛋。
A:  Do you have any bug spray? 你有驱蚊水吗？
B:  No, I forgot to buy some. 没有，我忘了买。
A:  Then we’ll have to put up with it. 那我们就只能硬扛了。
B:  We can cover ourselves with beer! That way if they bite us, they'll get drunk and fall asleep 我们可以用啤酒涂满全身！这样就算被咬，它们也会醉倒睡着的。
A:  That's without a doubt, the best idea youve had! Lets do it! 这绝对是你想出的最棒的主意！就这么办！
B:  Run! They are thirsty for more! 快跑！它们还想再来一口！

Key Vocabulary

scratch挠to rub your skin with something sharp用尖锐的东西摩擦你的皮肤
sneaky偷偷摸摸的behaving in a secret and usually dishonest manner行事隐秘且通常不诚实
put up with忍受to allow to exist or happen允许存在或发生
bug spray驱虫喷雾a liquid used to prevent being bitten by bugs用于防止被虫子叮咬的液体
mosquito蚊子a small flying insect that bites the skin一种咬皮肤的小飞虫

Supplementary Vocabulary

malaria疟疾a disease with recurring attacks of fever, caused by the bite of some types of mosquito一种反复发烧的疾病，由某些类型的蚊子叮咬引起
bug zapper灭蚊灯a device that attracts and kills insects that are attracted by light一种吸引并杀死被光吸引的昆虫的装置
pesticide杀虫剂a chemical used to destroy pests, esp. insects一种用于消灭害虫的化学物质，尤其是。昆虫
