Daily Life - Finding the perfect present (C0178)

A:  Bill. Bill! You gotta help me! 比尔！比尔！快帮帮我！
B:  What's wrong? Slow down or you are gonna give yourself a heart attack. 怎么了？慢点，不然你非得把自己吓出心脏病不可。
A:  Tomorrow is Christmas and I haven't bought my mom anything! I'm such a bad son! 明天就是圣诞节了，我还没给我妈买礼物呢！我真是不孝子！
B:  Take it easy! Let's go tothe mall, window shop a little and see if there is anything she might like. 别急！我们去商场逛逛，看看有没有她喜欢的东西。
A:  That's just it! I don't know what to get her! Last year I got her a ring that was two sizes too big and a pair of shoes five sizes too small! I suck at getting presents for people. 问题就在这儿！我不知道该给她买什么！去年我送她的戒指大了两个码，鞋子小了五个码！我根本不会挑礼物。
B:  That's where you're making a big mistake! You can't just guess peoples' likes or sizes! Especially with clothes or jewelry. On top of that, I think that you should get your mom something that shows how much you love her. Atthe same time you should show her that you took the time and effort tolook for something that she would really like! 你错得离谱！你不能光靠猜别人的喜好或尺码！尤其是衣服和首饰。再说了，我觉得你应该送点能体现你对她多深爱的东西。同时，也要让她知道，你花了不少时间和精力去找她真正喜欢的东西！
A:  Yeah you're right. When it comes down to it, I can be pretty tacky. 你说得对。归根结底，我确实挺俗气的。
B:  Yeah tell me about it. I know! Your mom is trying to learn Spanish right? Why don't you get her a gift certificate for this great website I saw called SpanishPod. 没错，我也深有同感。我知道啦！你妈不是在学西班牙语吗？不如送你妈一张这家叫 SpanishPod 的网站的礼品券吧。
A:  Now that's a great idea! 这主意真棒！
