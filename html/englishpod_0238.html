The Office - Interview Skills Part 2 - Discussing Your Background (C0238)

Mr. Parsons:  Now, Miss Childs passed on your resume to me and I've had the chance to look it over and I must say I'm quite impressed. 现在，Childs 小姐把你的简历转交给我，我有机会仔细看过之后，必须说我很印象深刻。
Rebecca:  Thank you very much. I've tried to keep it short and clear. If there's any questions please feel free to ask me. 非常感谢。我尽量让它简短明了。如果有任何问题，请随时问我。
Mr. Parsons:  Well yes, I do have a number of questions, but perhaps first you could give me a brief overview I'd like to get a little bit of an idea of your background. 嗯，是的，我确实有几个问题，但或许首先你能给我一个简要概述？我想对你有个大致的了解。
Rebecca:  yes of course. Well as you can see from the resume I'm up and grew up in Brooklyn, New York, although our family moved to London when I was quite young, at around rook. 当然可以。正如你在简历上看到的，我在纽约布鲁克林长大，虽然我家在我大约六岁时搬到了伦敦。
Mr. Parsons:  Ah I see, so you were actually educated in Europe? 啊，我明白了，所以你是实际上在欧洲接受的教育？
Rebecca:  yes precisely. Although I was born in the US, I would definitely call London home. But as you see I've actually spent a lot of my life moving from country to country. My Father was inthe oil business before he retired so we also spent a number of years in Saudi Arabia too. 没错。虽然我出生在美国，但我一定会称伦敦为家。但如你所见，我一生中大部分时间都在从一个国家搬到另一个国家。我的父亲在退休前从事石油行业，所以我们也在沙特阿拉伯度过了数年时光。
Mr. Parsons:  Very interesting. So it seems you had quite an adventurous childhood. 非常有趣。看来你有一个相当冒险的童年。
Rebecca:  Absolutely! We were never still for too long. But now I'm really looking to settle down. 绝对如此！我们从未长时间停留。但现在我真的想安定下来。
Mr. Parsons:  I see. Okay, well let's move on to discuss your education shall we? 我明白了。好的，那我们接着谈谈你的教育经历，好吗？
Rebecca:  Sure. 当然。
