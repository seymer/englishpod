The Office - Interview Skills 7 - Describing Ones Weaknesses (C0253)

Mr Parsons:  Okay Rebecca, well I think you'vegiven me a clear impression of your positive qualities, but let's talk a little bit about your weaknesses. 好的，Rebecca，我觉得你已经让我对你的优点有了清晰的了解，但让我们稍微谈谈你的弱点吧。
Rebecca:  Okay, well it's always more difficult to describe them isn't it? 嗯，说实话，描述起来总是更难一些，不是吗？
Mr Parsons:  Definitely, but if you had to pinpoint one weakness what would it be? 当然，不过如果你必须指出一个缺点，那会是什么？
Rebecca:  Well as I mentioned before, I do tend to get frustrated if I don't see progress in my work or career. I suppose I'm quite a restless character. My father always taught me to be a high achiever so. 正如我之前提到的，如果我在工作中或职业生涯中看不到进展，我确实容易感到沮丧。我想我是个坐不住的人。我父亲一直教导我要追求卓越。
Mr Parsons:  So would you say if things don't go your way at work it could easily get you down?那么你会说，如果工作不顺利，很容易让你情绪低落吗？
Rebecca:  Well, in a way yes. But I must say that even if I'm not completely happy in my work I always give 110% I would never shirk my responsibilities. I suppose sometimes Iexpect too much too soon. 嗯，某种程度上是的。但我必须说，即使我对工作不完全满意，我也总是全力以赴，绝不会推卸责任。我想有时候我期望过高、求成太急。
Mr Parsons:  Well, you know journalism is a highly competitive world, so you do need to keep pushing yourself it's true. Okay well lets move on to talk about the job position here shall we? 好的，你知道新闻界是个竞争激烈的领域，所以确实需要不断鞭策自己，这没错。好，那我们接下来聊聊这个职位吧，可以吗？
Rebecca:  Yes please.好的，请讲。
