Global View - Chinese Medicine  (C0349)

A:  What's wrong?怎么了？
B:  I have a headache. These past few days I've been living off painkillers. Man, I feel like my head is going to explode.我头疼。这几天我一直靠止痛药度日。天哪，我感觉脑袋快要炸了。
A:  You should get acupuncture treatment. My mom was always having headache issues and it was acupuncture that cured her.你应该试试针灸治疗。我妈以前总是头疼，就是针灸治好了她。
B:  The results are too slow. On top of that, just the thought of smoking needles poking into my flesh frightens me.见效太慢了。而且光是想到要让人用针扎我的肉就让我害怕。
A:  They don't just randomly stick you, they find your pressure points. The heat allows the body to immediately respond to the treatment, restoring the body's "chi".他们不会随便扎的，他们会找准你的穴位。热力能让身体立刻对治疗做出反应，恢复身体的“气”。
B:  But I get scared the moment I see a needle. How could I stand having needles in my body for hours on end?可我一看到针就怕。我怎么受得了身上插着针几个小时？
A:  The needles are very thin, and as long as the doctor's technique is good, and the patient himself is relaxed, it won't hurt--on the contrary it will actually alleviate pain. Now there are high-tech needles that are micro thin; they don't hurt at all. However, if you are really scared of acupuncture, scraping or cupping are also options.针很细，只要医生手法好，病人自己也放松，就不会疼——反而能缓解疼痛。现在还有高科技的微细针，一点都不疼。不过如果你真的怕针灸，拔罐或刮痧也是选项。
B:  Scraping is too terrifying. When they finish scrapping, your body is all red, as if you were just tortured. Cupping is the same, your body ends up with red circles all over it--looks like someone beat you up.刮痧太可怕了。刮完之后全身通红，好像刚被折磨过一样。拔罐也一样，身上全是红圈——看起来像是被人打了一顿。
A:  This only signifies that the toxins have left the body. Actually, there is only discomfort during the treatment process. Once it's over you feel very comfortable.这仅仅意味着毒素已经排出体外。实际上，治疗过程中只会感到不适。一旦结束，你会感觉非常舒适。
B:  Chinese medicine is strange. The patients are already ill, and then the doctor makes them suffer more.中医很奇怪。病人本来就已经病了，医生却让他们更痛苦。
A:  This is the only way to get at the problem. Anyway, if you want to relieve the pain,You are just going to have to be tough and do it.这是解决问题的唯一途径。总之，如果你想缓解疼痛，你就必须坚强并坚持下去。
B:  Forget it. I don't want to inflict any more pain on myself. In a little while I'll go and buy some more painkillers and take a nap.算了吧。我不想再给自己增加痛苦了。一会儿我去买点止痛药，然后睡个觉。

acupuncture针灸Chinese Medical practice or procedure that treats illness or provides local anesthesia by the insertion of needles at specified sites of the body.通过在身体特定地点插入针头治疗疾病或提供局部麻醉的中医执业或手术.
pressure point压觉点a point on the skin that is extremely sensitive to pressure because of the presence of pressure-sensing organs.由于压力感应器官的存在，皮肤上的一个点对压力极为敏感.
treatment治疗an act or manner of treating.一种治疗行为或方式。
