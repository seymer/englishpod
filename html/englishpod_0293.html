A:  Hello everyone my name is Laurie and I want to welcome you to this course. We will learn all about one of the oldest yet most delicious foods on this planet; cheese! Let’s get started! 大家好，我是劳里，欢迎来到这门课程。我们将学习地球上最古老却最美味的食物之一——奶酪！让我们开始吧！
A:  Cheese is usually categorized intofour types: soft, semi- soft semi-hard and hard. The designation refers to the amount of moisture in the cheese, which directly affects its texture. Making cheese is an ancient practice, dating back thousands of years, and the home cheese maker can usually find recipes for cheese that falls into any of the four categories. 奶酪通常分为四类：软质、半软质、半硬质和硬质。这种分类指的是奶酪中的含水量，这直接影响其质地。制作奶酪是一项古老的技艺，可追溯数千年前，家庭制作者通常能找到适用于这四类中任何一类的奶酪食谱。
A:  Soft cheese includes cottage cheese, cream cheese, ricotta, brie, bleu, roquefort, mozzarella, meunster and similar cheeses. These cheeses generally pair well with fruit or meats, or can be used as breakfast cheeses in an omelette Nor as pasta fillings. They are usually mildly flavored and very high in moisture. 软质奶酪包括新鲜干酪、奶油干酪、里科塔、布里、蓝纹、罗克福尔、马苏里拉、穆斯特以及类似奶酪。这些奶酪通常与水果或肉类搭配食用，也可用作早餐蛋饼中的奶酪，而非意面馅料。它们通常味道温和且含水量很高。
A:  American, Colby, co-jack and similar cheeses are inthe semi-soft category. These are slightly stronger in flavor and cover a wide range of uses. Co-jack cheese, a blend of Colby and Monterrey jack is one of the most popular. This allows the sharper flavor of Colby to be combined with the milder jack cheese, and also melts better than plain Colby. Grilled cheese sandwiches often use American cheese, and Mexican cheeses such as Asadero and Queso Fresco are becoming more popular. 美国、科比、科杰克及类似奶酪属于半软质类别，风味稍强且用途广泛。其中，由科比和蒙特雷杰克混合而成的科杰克奶酪最为流行，它将科比的浓郁风味与杰克的温和口感相结合，且比普通科比奶酪更易融化。烤奶酪三明治常使用美国奶酪，而墨西哥奶酪如阿萨德罗和菲索奶酪也日益受欢迎。
A:  Hard cheeses include Parmesan, Romano, Asiago, Swiss, Gruyere and others. Parmesan and Romano are most familiar as the grated powder used to top spaghetti, but they are also used as accompaniments for fruit, wine, nuts and other appetizer items. Swiss is a popular sandwich cheese and melts well, unlike some other hard cheeses. 硬质奶酪包括帕尔马干酪、罗马诺、阿夏戈、瑞士、格鲁耶尔等。帕尔马干酪和罗马诺奶酪最为人熟知的是作为意面顶部的擦丝粉末，但也常用于搭配水果、葡萄酒、坚果及其他开胃菜。瑞士奶酪是流行的三明治用奶酪，且融化性良好，不同于某些其他硬质奶酪。
