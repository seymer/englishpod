Global View - Phobias (C0302)

A:  Are you excited about your trip next month?下个月的旅行你兴奋吗？
B:  Yes and no. I can’t wait to go to Europe, but at the same time I am terrified.又兴奋又不安。我迫不及待要去欧洲，但同时我又非常害怕。
A:  Why?为什么？
B:  Well, I have aerophobia. I have a chronic fear of flying.嗯，我有恐高症……不对，是飞行恐惧症。
A:  Oh really? I have an uncle who is also terrified of flying. It’s not that bad though, I mean, it is pretty scary to be in this big machine flying through the air at seven hundred miles per hour. I actually have arachnophobia. 真的吗？我有个叔叔也特别怕坐飞机。不过也没那么糟，我是说，待在那架时速七百英里的大型机器里确实挺吓人的。其实我有的是蜘蛛恐惧症。
B:  You’re scared of spiders? I actually have two more phobias. Acrophobia and glossophobia.你怕蜘蛛？我还有另外两种恐惧症。恐高症和公开演讲恐惧症。
A:  I guess that explains why you are afraid of flying, but public speaking is not that bad.我想这解释了为什么你害怕飞行，但公开演讲倒没那么可怕。
B:  Are you kidding? When I get on stage, my palms start to sweat, I get really nervous and I can hardly speak.你在开玩笑吧？当我站上舞台时，手心就开始冒汗，变得非常紧张，几乎说不出话来。
A:  Well, I must confess I am a bit claustrophobic. I hate being in an elevator for more than 5 seconds.好吧，我必须承认，我也有点幽闭恐惧症。我讨厌在电梯里待超过五秒。
B:  We are such weirdos right?我们真是怪人吧？
