The Office - Out Of Control Spending(0013)

A:  OK, so now the last point on our agenda. Jill, let's go over the profit and loss statement.好的，现在我们要讨论议程上的最后一项。吉尔，我们过一下损益表吧。
B:  Great. Well, the main issue here, as you can see, is that our expenses are through the roof.太好了。正如你看到的，这里的主要问题就是我们的开支高得离谱。
A:  Let's see... These numbers are off the charts! What's going on here!让我看看……这些数字简直超出天际了！到底怎么回事啊！
B:  Well, um, sir, the company expenditures on entertainment and travel are out of control. Look at these bills for example. Just this month we've paid over twenty thousand dollars for hotel charges!呃，先生，公司在娱乐和旅行方面的支出完全失控了。比如看这些账单。光是这个月我们就付了超过两万美元的酒店费用！
A:  OK, thank you. I'll look into it.好的，谢谢。我会去调查一下。
B:  The list goes on and on. Here, this is a bill for five thousand dollars for spa treatments!清单没完没了。这里是一张五千美元的 SPA 护理账单！
A:  Thank you; that will be all. I'll take care of it.谢谢；就这些了。我会处理好的。
B:  Look at this one sir, eight thousand dollars were spent in one night at a place called "Wild Things"?!先生，你看这一项，光是在一个叫“狂野地带”的地方一晚就花了八千美元？！
A:  OK, I get it!! Thank you for your very thorough analysis!哦，我明白了！！非常感谢你如此详尽的分析！
