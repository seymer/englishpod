Global View - At The Library (C0342)

A:  Wow! Look at all these books! I bet I can find a book about anything here!哇！看看这些书！我肯定能在这里找到关于任何主题的书！
B:  Shhh!! Please keep your voice down. There are people reading and studying here.嘘！！请小声点。这里有人在读书和学习呢。
A:  Ok, I’m sorry. Are you the librarian? Maybe you can help me, I am looking for a book.好的，对不起。你是图书管理员吗？也许你能帮我，我在找一本书。
B:  Yes I am. You can check our online catalog to search the book you want based on the genre, title or if you know the author, I can point you towards the right direction.是的，我就是。你可以查看我们的在线目录，根据类型、书名或作者来搜索你想找的书，我可以为你指明方向。
A:  I am looking for a book that has nursery rhymes.我在找一本有童谣的书。
B:  That would be in our children’s section. That book shelf there on the right.那应该在儿童区。就是右边那个书架。
A:  Ok, I would like to check out these books.好的，我想借阅这几本书。
B:  Do you have a library card?你有图书馆卡吗？
A:  No. How do I get one?没有。我该怎么办一张？
B:  I just need to see your drivers license or utility bill to prove that you a resident of this state.我只需要看一下你的驾照或水电账单，证明你是本州居民即可。
A:  Here you go.给你。
B:  So you are all set. You can have these books for two weeks. If you need to have them longer, you can bring them here to renew them. If you don’t, you get charged ten cents a day for each book.好了，你办妥了。这两本书你可以借两周。如果需要延长借阅时间，可以带到这里续借。如果不续借，每本书每天会收取十美分。
A:  Ok, thanks!好的，谢谢！
