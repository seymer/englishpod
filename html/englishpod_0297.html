The Weekend - Ordering At An Italian Restaurant (C0297)

A:  Good evening ladies. My name is Josh and I'll be your server tonight. May I take your order? 各位女士晚上好，我是 Josh，今晚由我为您服务。请问可以点餐了吗？
B:  Do you have any recommendations? 有什么推荐吗？
A:  Well, I personally like the chicken penne with cream mushroom sauce, but the prawn fettuccine is also very nice. 嗯，我个人比较喜欢奶油蘑菇酱鸡意面，不过虾子宽面也很不错。
B:  Hmm. I'd like to have the grilled chicken, but can I have spaghetti instead of penne? 嗯……我想要烤鸡肉，但可以把意大利面换成通心粉吗？
A:  Of course, mam. And for you? 当然可以，女士。那您呢？
C:  I... ah..I'll have the horse tripe. 我……啊……我要马鞭草。

Key Vocabulary

server服务员a waiter or waitress服务员或服务员
recommendation建议a suggestion or proposal建议或提案
prawn大虾a large shrimp大虾
fettuccine意大利宽面pasta in the shape of long narrow strips长窄条形面条
spaghetti意大利面pasta dish originally from Italy面条菜原产于意大利
penne笔管面a type of Italian pasta一种意大利面条
horse tripe马肚the stomach of a horse used as food一匹马作为食物的胃
