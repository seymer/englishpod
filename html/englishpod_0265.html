The Weekend - Pest Control (C0265)

A:  Hi, did you call for an exterminator? 嗨，你叫了除虫公司吗？
B:  Yes! Thank goodness you're here. These bugs are driving us crazy! 是的！谢天谢地你来了。这些虫子简直把我们逼疯了！
A:  What sort of pest are we dealing with?我们遇到的是什么害虫？
B:  We just bought this house and it is infected with just about everything. We have termites in the wood, cockroaches all over the place, and last night I saw a huge rat out in the backyard! 我们刚买下这栋房子，里面几乎什么都有。木头里有白蚁，蟑螂到处都是，昨晚我还看见后院有一只大老鼠！
A:  Well, there's nothing we can't handle. I'll spray the floorboards and walls to get rid of the cockroaches, but the termites will be harder to get rid of. We will have to cover the entire house and fumigate it. Unfortunately that means you will have to find a place to stay for the next three days. 别担心，我们都能搞定。我会给地板和墙壁喷药消灭蟑螂，但白蚁更难处理。我们需要把整栋房子密封并熏蒸消毒。不幸的是，这意味着你得在这三天内找个地方住。
B:  No problem, just get rid of the bugs! 没问题，只要把虫子弄走就行！

Key Vocabulary

exterminator灭虫专家a professional who kills pests杀死害虫的专业人员
pest害虫an annoying and harmful insect or animal令人烦恼和有害的昆虫或动物
fumigate熏蒸to spray with smoke or other chemicals用烟雾或其他化学品喷雾
can't handle无法应付not be able to deal with无法处理
just仅仅simply简单
