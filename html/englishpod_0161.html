Daily Life - Computer Games (C0161)

A:  MarkWhere have you been? I've been calling you all morning. Mark 你去哪儿了？我整个早上都在给你打电话。
B:  I've been playing computer games. 我在玩电脑游戏。
A:  What? So you blew me off yesterday and today over a stupid video game? What game is so important that you have no time for me anymore? What are you playing? 什么？所以昨天和今天你都因为一个愚蠢的电子游戏放了我鸽子？有什么游戏重要到让你没时间理我了？你在玩什么？
B:  It's called Counter Strike It's a first person shooter game. It's awesome. It's a multi player game where you can go online and compete against players from all over the world. 它叫《反恐精英》，是一款第一人称射击游戏。太棒了。这是一款多人在线游戏，你可以联网与来自世界各地的玩家对战。
A:  You've been wasting your time on this? I can't believe it! It doesn't even look fun or challenging! 你把时间都浪费在这上面了？我真不敢相信！这看起来一点都不有趣或具有挑战性！
B:  My laptop is on my bed. If you think it's so easy then get onlineand try to beat me. 我的笔记本电脑在床上。如果你觉得这很简单，那就上线来试试能不能打败我。
A:  Fine! 好吧！
B:  Dammit! Howare youkilling me with a single shot? It's not fair! I don't want to play anymore! Let's go get something to eat. 该死！你是怎么只用一枪就干掉我的？这不公平！我不想再玩了！我们去吃点东西吧。
A:  Can you bring me something? I am totally hooked on this game! 你能给我带点吃的吗？我已经完全沉迷于这个游戏了！
