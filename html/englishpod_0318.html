Global View - Allergies (C0318)

Jim:  Argh...I feel terrible, I keep sneezing and my eyes are all watery, what’s wrong with me?哎呀……我感觉糟透了，一直打喷嚏，眼睛也水汪汪的，我怎么了？
Tom:  Wow, you’re not dying are you, it looks like you have a cold, you should take some medicine.哇，你不会要死了吧？看起来像是感冒了，你应该吃点药。
Jim:  I don’t think it’s a cold, I feel fine if I move a few feet away from my desk.我觉得不是感冒，只要离我的办公桌远几步我就感觉好多了。
Tom:  Maybe we should put you into quarantine ha ha, jokes aside, I think you might have an allergy.也许我们应该把你隔离起来吧，哈哈，开玩笑的，我想你可能过敏了。
Jim:  An allergy? I never thought about that, I don’t think I’m allergic to pollen though and I’m desensitized to bee stings after being stung so many times, Hmm…过敏？我从没想过这个，不过我不觉得我对花粉过敏，而且我被蜜蜂蛰过那么多次，现在对蜂蛰已经脱敏了，嗯……
Jim:  Ow! Why did you chuck that peanut at me?哎哟！你为什么朝我扔那颗花生？
Tom:  Just checking if you’re allergic to peanuts, I guess not.只是想看看你是不是对花生过敏，看来不是。
Jim:  Not funny! I could have gone into Anaphylactic Shock.一点都不好笑！我差点就过敏性休克了。
Tom:  Okay my bad, how about dust? This office is full of it.好吧，是我的错，那灰尘呢？这办公室里到处都是。
Jim:  Yes the whole is office is dusty yet I only feel affected near our desks!是的，整个办公室都积满了灰，可只有靠近我们办公桌的地方我才感到不适！

allergic过敏的have allergy towards something没错。 并非所有州都有所得税. 有些人则使用较高的财产税或销售税。
affect影响to act on; produce an effect or change in产生效果或改变
dust灰尘dry fine powdery material, such as particles of dirt, earth or pollen干细粉末材料，如土、土或花粉颗粒
anaphylactic shock过敏性休克severe and sometimes fatal allergic reaction to a foreign substance such as bee venom对诸如蜜蜂毒液等外来物质的重度和有时是致命的过敏反应
quarantine隔离a strict isolation imposed to prevent the spread of disease.为防止疾病蔓延而实行严格的隔离。
