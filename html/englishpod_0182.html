Global View - Boxing Day (C0182)

A:  What do you think of this one? 你觉得这件怎么样？
B:  Eh, so so. 嗯，也就那样。
A:  And this one? Too flashy? 那这件呢？太花哨了吧？
B:  Nah, not too flashy. 不，没那么花哨。
A:  Uhg! And this sweater from my aunt? Isn't it hideous? 哎哟！还有我阿姨送的那件毛衣？难看不难看？
B:  I guess. 大概吧。
A:  Are you even listening? I'm trying to have a conversation with you. 你听得进话吗？我想跟你好好聊聊天。
B:  And I'm trying to watch the game, but you're yapping on about your new clothes! 我也想看看比赛，可你一直在絮叨你的新衣服！
A:  Well I have to decide which gifts to keep and which to exchange for better ones when I go to the Boxing Day sales this afternoon! 那我得决定哪些礼物留着，哪些去换更好的啊，今天下午就要去 Boxing Day 大甩卖了！
B:  Well could you do me the favor of making this quick? It's the third quarter and you've been blabbering on since the first! 那你倒是快点啊，都第三节了，你从第一节开始就没停过嘴！
A:  Oh, your precious game. You watch the same game every year, and each year your beloved hometown team loses by at least three goals! 哦，你那宝贝的球赛。你每年看同一场比赛，而你心爱的家乡队每场至少输三个球！
B:  Oh no you didn't. You didn't just insult the Salsbury Seals, did you? Why don't you just. just go and return all of those stupid clothes and not come back until the sales are over? 哎呀你可别这样。你难道不是刚骂了塞尔伯里海豹队吗？你干脆把那些蠢衣服全退了，等促销结束再回来吧？
A:  I might just! Enjoy your stupid game! 我可能就这么干！祝你这愚蠢的球赛愉快！
B:  And Merry Christmas! 圣诞快乐！
A:  Merry Christmas! 圣诞快乐！
