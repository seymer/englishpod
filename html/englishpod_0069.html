Daily Life - How Would You Like Your Eggs?(0069)

A:  Wow, you're up early today! What's for breakfast?哇，你今天起得真早！早餐吃什么？
B:  Well, I felt like baking, so I made some muffins.我想做点烘焙，所以烤了些松饼。
A:  Smells good! I'll make some coffee. Do you want me to make you some eggs?闻起来不错！我去煮咖啡。要我给你煎个蛋吗？
B:  Sure, I’ll take mine, sunny side up.好的，我要单面煎的。
A:  Eww, I don't know how you can eat your eggs like that! Ever since I was small, I've had eggs and soldiers.天哪，你怎么能那样吃鸡蛋啊！从我小时候开始，我就只吃水煮蛋配吐司条。
B:  You know, my dad had scrambled eggs eggs every morning for twenty years. It drove my mom crazy!你知道吗？我爸爸每天早上都吃炒蛋，整整吃了二十年，把我妈烦死了！
A:  You know what really drives me crazy? When I ask for soft boiled eggs, and they overcook them, so they come out hard boiled! How can you dip your toast into a hard boiled egg?你知道什么最让我抓狂吗？当我要求煮软一点的鸡蛋时，他们却煮过头了，结果变成了硬壳蛋！你怎么能把烤面包蘸进硬壳蛋里啊？
B:  You're so picky sometimes.你有时候真挑剔。
A:  Here you go, honey, fried eggs.给你，亲爱的，这是煎蛋。
B:  Dammit! I asked for sunny side up! How many times do I have to tell you.该死！我要的是单面煎的！我到底得说多少次才行。
