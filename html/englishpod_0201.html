Daily Life - At The Post Office (C0201)

A:  Welcome to the National Post. How may I help you? 欢迎光临国家邮局，有什么可以帮您？
B:  Hi, I would like to send this package to China, and these postcards as well. 你好，我想把这个包裹寄往中国，还有这些明信片。
A:  Very well. You will need some stamps for the postcards and I need to weigh that package, too. 好的。明信片需要贴邮票，这个包裹我也得称重。
B:  Great. How much is this going to cost? 太好了。请问一共要多少钱？
A:  Well, it depends. Do you want to send it via priority, express or standard mail? 这得看情况。您想通过优先、特快还是普通邮件寄送？
B:  What's the difference? 有什么区别吗？
A:  Well, standard mail can take up to fifteen working days . Priority is a bit faster and will arrive in about five to eight working days. Express is the fastest, but it's also the most expensive. It only takes three days and you can track your package online. 嗯，普通邮件可能需要十五个工作日才能送达。优先邮件稍快一些，大约五到八个工作日就能到达。特快是最快的，但也是最贵的，只需三天，而且您可以在网上追踪包裹。
B:  I see. Well, there's no rush. Please send it via priority mail. Please be extra careful, the contents of the package are fragile. 我明白了。不急，那就用优先邮件寄吧。请格外小心，包裹里的东西很易碎。
