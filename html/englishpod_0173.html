The Office - Preparing a Budget (C0173)

A:  Welcome Mr and Mrs Carnwell, please take a seat. 欢迎卡恩韦尔先生和夫人，请坐。
B:  Thank you. 谢谢。
A:  So I understand that your family spending has sky rocketed and you want to start budgeting. 我了解到你们家的开销急剧增加，想要开始制定预算。
C:  Yes thats correct. Frankly speaking our household income is relatively high and we have never had any money problems, but I think this isthe main reason as to why our spending has gone out of control. 是的，确实如此。坦率地说，我们家庭收入相对较高，从未有过经济问题，但我认为这正是导致我们支出失控的主要原因。
B:  We have two kids and with allowances, paying their credit cards, ours and not to mention our mortgage and car payments. With the recent economic downturn, my husband's business has suffered and now we need some advice as to how we can prepare a family budget. 我们有两个孩子，除了他们的零花钱、信用卡还款（包括他们和我们自己的），还有房贷和车贷。最近经济衰退，我丈夫的生意也受到了影响，现在我们急需一些建议，了解如何制定家庭预算。
A:  I see. Well you have come tothe right place. First what we need to do, is determine your cash flow. Knowing how much money is coming in will help us allocate spending to different categories such as mortgage, education, groceries, etc. 我明白了。那么您来对地方了。首先我们需要做的是确定你们的现金流。了解有多少钱流入，将有助于我们将支出分配到不同的类别，比如房贷、教育、杂货等。
B:  Yes that makes sense. 是的，这很有道理。
A:  Secondly, I need you to bring all of your receipts forthe last two to three months. That way, we can determine what your average expenditures are and see which category you are spending money onthe most. Usually, your fixed costs are higher and we cant do much about that, but we can usually trim your variable costs such as entertainment or clothing. 其次，我需要您带上最近两到三个月的所有收据。这样我们可以确定您的平均支出是多少，并看看您在哪个类别上的花费最多。通常，固定成本较高，我们对此无能为力，但我们可以削减可变成本，比如娱乐或服装方面的开支。
C:  Great! We will do that then! Now how about we treat you to a nice dinner? 太好了！那我们就这么办吧！现在我们要请您吃顿丰盛的晚餐，好吗？
A:  That's another thing. If you really want to stop spending so much money, throw away at least half of your credit cards! 那是另一回事。如果你真的想停止花这么多钱，那就扔掉至少一半的信用卡！
