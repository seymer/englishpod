Global View - College Life (C0353)

A:  Hey, Jordan, is that you? Long time no see! 嘿，乔丹，是你吗？好久不见！
B:  Oh, hey, no kidding! I haven’t seen you since orientation three months ago! So how’ve you been? Settling into college life OK? 哦，嗨，可不是嘛！自从三个月前的新生导览我就没见过你了！你最近怎么样？适应大学生活还好吧？
A:  Yeah, I think so! I pledged Phi Iota Alpha, so I’m living atthe frat house now. 嗯，我想还不错！我加入了 Phi Iota Alpha 兄弟会，所以现在住在兄弟会宿舍。
B:  Oh, so you’re a frat boy now, huh? 哦，那你现在是个“兄弟会男孩”了，对吧？
A:  Yeah, yeah, I know, it’s totally cliché, but really, I think it’s been a good decision. I’ve got a lot of support and good suggestions from the guys. What about you? What have you been up to? 对对，我知道这很老套，但说实话，我觉得这是个不错的决定。兄弟们给了我很多支持和好的建议。你呢？最近在忙什么？
B:  Not much. I’m still living at home and commuting to school. I ended up dropping that metalworking class I was so excited about. It just wasn’t as interesting as I’d hoped. The guidance counselor suggested that I focus on my prerequisite courses so that I can make sure the credits count. 没什么特别的。我还是住家里，每天通勤上学。最后我退掉了那门金属加工课，当时我还挺期待的。结果它没像我希望的那样有趣。指导老师建议我把精力放在先修课上，这样才能确保学分有效。
A:  That sounds smart…but kind of boring. 听起来很明智……但也挺无聊的。
B:  Yeah, it is, a little bit. I joined the Great Outdoors Club, though, which has been a lot of fun. We’ve gone on two camping trips already, and I’ve made some good friends. 是啊，确实有点。不过我加入了“户外探险俱乐部”，特别有意思。我们已经去了两次露营，还交到了不少好朋友。
A:  That’s cool. Hey, so have you decided on your major yet? 真不错。嘿，那你决定好主修专业了吗？
B:  Definitely pre-med. What about you? 肯定是医学预科。你呢？
A:  I still have no clue…but we don’t have to declare a major ‘til our sophomore year, so I’ve got time! Oops, I’m late for class. Gotta run! 我还是没什么头绪……不过我们不用到大二才确定专业，所以我还有时间！哎呀，我要迟到了。得赶紧跑！
B:  OK, take care! Hey, nice running into you!好的，保重！嘿，真巧又碰到你了！
A:  Yeah, you too!是啊，你也是！
