The Office - Making an Appointment(0023)

A:  Hello, Fairbrook Consulting, how may I help you?你好，Fairbrook 咨询公司，有什么可以帮您的吗？
B:  Yes, this is Julianne Horton, and I'm calling to arrange an appointment with Ms. McNealy.是的，我是朱莉安·霍顿，我打电话是想和麦克纳利女士预约。
A:  Certainly, what day were you thinking of?当然，您想安排在哪一天呢？
B:  How's Thursday? Does she have any time available then?周四怎么样？她那天有空吗？
A:  Um... let me double check...unfortunately, she's booked solid on Thursday, how does next Monday work for you?呃……让我再确认一下……很抱歉，她周四已经排满了，下周一对您来说方便吗？
B:  Actually, I've got something scheduled on Monday. Can she do Tuesday?其实我周一已经有安排了。她能周二吗？
A:  Sure, Tuesday's perfect. May I ask where you're calling from?好的，周二没问题。请问您是哪里打来的电话？
B:  Sure, Merton Financial Advisors.当然，默顿金融顾问公司。
A:  Oh, actually, Tuesday's no good. Sorry 'bout that.哦，实际上周二不行。抱歉啊。
