Daily Life - Buying Women's Shoes (C0231)

Mom:  Hi, excuse me Miss? I'm looking for a dress shoe. My usual pair that I've had for years have finally been stretched out of shape. They don't provide any support anymore. 嗨，打扰一下，小姐？我在找一双女式皮鞋。我穿了好多年的那双旧鞋终于被撑变形了，现在完全没法提供支撑力了。
Salesgirl:  Sure, what kind of shoe are you looking for? We've got strappy sandals, sleek high heels, edgy pumps, or if you're looking for something a little more practical, we've got Mary Janes, ballerinas. 好的，您想看看什么款式的鞋呢？我们有带细带的凉鞋、时尚的高跟鞋、前卫的尖头鞋；或者如果您想要更实用一点的款式，我们有玛丽珍鞋和芭蕾舞平底鞋。
Mom:  Show me some classic high heels, please. 给我看看经典款的高跟鞋吧。
Salesgirl:  Ok, right this way. What color did you have in mind? 好的，这边请。您心里有想好的颜色吗？
Mom:  Black. Classic. 黑色。经典的款式。
Salesgirl:  Of course. We've got this style here that is very popular. Because it's an open-toe shoe, you can wear it any time of the year. They look great on everyone. 当然可以。这款在这里很受欢迎。因为它是露趾鞋，所以一年四季都能穿。谁穿都好看。
Mom:  Umm. too shiny. And I wear pantyhose with my shoes so let's look for a closed-toe shoe. 呃……太亮了。而且我穿鞋时会搭配连裤袜，所以我们还是看看带封闭鞋头的款式吧。
Salesgirl:  Ok, these are a very nice pair of leather shoes with a two-inch heel so they are very comfortable. 好的，这是一双非常不错的皮鞋，两英寸的鞋跟，穿起来很舒服。
Mom:  I don't like the pointed toes. Let me take a look at what else you have. Too high. That one looks like the back would cut into my heel. I have a high instep so I doubt that one will fit properly. I don't want bows. I find slingbacks very uncomfortable. Those might as well be stilettos. Too modern. Ah, finally, this is what I'm looking for. 我不喜欢尖头设计。让我再看看你们还有别的什么。太高了。那双看起来后跟会硌着我的脚后跟。我的脚背比较高，所以我觉得那双可能不太合脚。我不要蝴蝶结装饰。我发现后绊带鞋非常不舒服。那些简直就像细高跟一样了。太现代了。啊，终于找到我想要的了。
Salesgirl:  What size? 您穿多大码？
Mom:  Seven-and-a-half. 七点五码。
Salesgirl:  Here we are How does it fit? 在这儿呢，试试合不合脚？
Mom:  Hmmm. not good. They're too tight. The length is right, but the shoe is too narrow and it's pinching my toes. And there'd be no room for my insoles. You know what? I don't think I have the patience for this today. They just don't make shoes like they used to. I'll come back another time. 嗯……不太合适。太紧了。长度刚好，但鞋子太窄了，夹脚趾。而且连鞋垫都没地方放。你说什么？我今天没耐心试鞋了。现在根本做不出以前的那种鞋。我改天再来吧。
Salesgirl:  Have a nice day, Ma'am. 祝您有愉快的一天，女士。
