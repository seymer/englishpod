Global View - Understanding The Stock Market (C0208)

A:  Sorry to bother you sir, but I have some bad news. 抱歉打扰您，先生，但我有个坏消息。
B:  What is it? 怎么了？
A:  Well, the stock market just took a huge plunge and we've lost a lot of money! 唉，股市刚刚暴跌，我们损失惨重！
B:  What do you mean? What happened? 你什么意思？发生什么事了？
A:  There are many factors that weigh in, but NASDAQ is down 200 points, the DOW JONES indicator also suffered! Our portfolio is worth half of what it was worth points week ago. 影响因素很多，但纳斯达克指数下跌了 200 点，道琼斯指标也遭殃了！我们的投资组合价值只剩上周的一半。
B:  How is this possible? You are supposed to be talking to our stockbrokers and making sure that our securities and investments are safe and making a profitable return! 这怎么可能？你们不是该和我们的股票经纪人沟通，确保我们的证券和投资安全并带来盈利回报吗？
A:  I know sir! We didn't expect a bull market to become a bear market all of a sudden. On the other hand, you still have some high yield trash bonds and government bonds that will give us enough liquidity to cut our loses and reinvest in emerging markets. We could potentially make this tragedy work for us and make us think outside the box. 我知道，先生！我们没料到牛市会突然变成熊市。不过，您手里还有些高收益垃圾债和政府债，能提供足够的流动性来止损，然后重新投资新兴市场。我们或许能把这场悲剧转危为机，换个思路应对。
B:  Do what you have to do! One other thing, don't tell the rest of the stockholders about this. If they find out, it's the end of this company! 该怎么做就怎么做！还有一件事，别告诉其他股东。要是他们知道了，这家公司就完了！

Key Vocabulary

plunge骤降a steep and rapid fall急剧而快速的下降
weigh in发表意见to contribute, especially in a discussion做出贡献，尤其是在讨论中
profitable盈利的affording gain, benefit, or profit提供收益、好处或利润
yield产量the amount of something that is produced所生产的某物的数量
think outside the box跳出定式take another look at something再看看某事
