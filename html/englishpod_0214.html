Daily Life - Describing Personalities (C0214)

A:  OK class, settle down. I have the results of your individual personality tests. I am going to hand them out and if you'dlike, you can read them out loud to the rest of the class. 大家安静，把注意力集中过来。我这儿有你们个人性格测试的结果。我会把它们发给大家，如果愿意的话，你可以大声读出来让全班同学听听。
B:  I'll read mine! 我要读我的！
A:  OK, go ahead. 好的，请吧。
B:  It says here that I am adventurous, outgoing and easy-going. It says that I am a little superstitious and occasionally naive! That's not true! 上面写着我是个爱冒险、外向又随和的人。还说我是个有点迷信、偶尔天真的人！这可不是真的！
A:  The test isn't one-hundred percent accurate. Is that all it says? 这个测试并不百分之百准确。就这些了吗？
B:  No! It also says that I am open-minded with great ambition but that I can also be reckless and clumsy. This is stupid! 不！它还说我心胸开阔、志向远大，但也可能鲁莽且笨手笨脚。这太荒谬了！
A:  Ok, anyone else want to read theirs? 还有谁想读自己的结果吗？
C:  I'll go! It says that I am an extroverted, well-balanced person. It says I am generous, outspoken, and very diligent. This is so true! It also says that I am magnanimous, eloquent and daring! This is totally me! 我来！上面写着我是个外向、性格平衡的人。还说我很慷慨、爱说话、非常勤奋。这简直太对了！还说我宽宏大量、口才出众、敢于冒险！这完全就是我！
A:  Pfft whatever, these tests are bologna! 切，这些测试全是扯淡！
