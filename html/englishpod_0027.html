The Office - Asking for Time Off(0027)

A:  Mr. McKenna, do you have a second? I need to talk to you about something.McKenna 先生，您现在有空吗？我得跟您说件事。
B:  Sure, Liv, what can I do for you?当然可以，Liv，我能为你做什么？
A:  Well, I was just wondering... you see, I know I've used up all my vacation days this year, but my sister is getting married, and the wedding is overseas, and, well...嗯，我有点想问……你看，我知道今年我的年假已经用完了，但我妹妹要结婚，婚礼在国外，而且……
B:  You wanna take some time off, is that right?你是想请假对吧？
A:  Well, sir, I was just hoping that I might be able to take some unpaid leave this year.是的，老板，我只是希望能今年请些无薪假。
B:  What dates are you planning on taking off? I'll need at least two months notice, so that I can plan for your absence.你打算什么时候休假？我需要至少提前两个月通知，以便安排你的缺席事宜。
A:  I was thinking of taking off from September first until the thirtieth. Would you be okay with that?我想从 9 月 1 日到 30 日休假。您能同意吗？
B:  Well, I guess so.嗯，我想可以。
