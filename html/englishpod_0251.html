The Weekend - Buying Makeup  (C0251)

A:  I'm hungry, let's go grab a bite to eat. 我饿了，咱们去吃点东西吧。
B:  Yeah me too. Oh! Can we stop at the shop really fast? I lost my makeup bag at the airport and I want to pick up a few things. 我也是啊！哦！我们能顺路快速去趟商店吗？我在机场把化妆包弄丢了，想补买几样东西。
A:  Will you take long? 你要花很久吗？
B:  No! Five minutes I promise! 不会！就五分钟，我保证！
A:  Come on! We have been here for almost an hour! I thought you said you were only going to get a few things! How long does it take you to pick out a lipstick and some nail polish! 拜托！我们在这儿都快一个小时了！我还以为你说只买几样呢！你挑一支口红和一点指甲油要多久啊！
B:  Are you crazy! You have no idea what you are talking about! Just for my eyes I have to get eyeliner, an eyelash curler, eye shadow, an eyebrow pencil and mascara. Then I need to get foundation, liquid foundation. 你疯了吗！你根本不知道你在说什么！光是为了我的眼睛，我就得买眼线笔、睫毛夹、眼影、眉笔和睫毛膏。然后还得买粉底液、液体粉底。
A:  Whoa whoa whoa! Are you nuts? How much is all this going to cost? I'm looking at the price at each one of these little things and it's outrageous! This is basically a crayon! 哇哦哇哦！你疯了吧？这一堆加起来得多贵啊！我看了一下每个小东西的价格，简直离谱！这玩意儿基本上就是一根蜡笔嘛！
B:  What about you? You spend as much or more money on your razors, after shave, cologne and gel! Not to mention how much you spend on clothes and...那你自己呢？你在剃须刀、须后水、古龙水和发胶上花的钱也一样多甚至更多！还不算你在衣服和……上花了多少钱。
A:  Fine! Get the stupid thirty dollar crayon! 行吧！买那个该死的三十美元的“蜡笔”好了！
