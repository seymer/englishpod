Daily Life - Junk Food (C0200)

A:  I'm hungry, let's grab a bite to eat. 我饿了，咱们去吃点东西吧。
B:  Sure! How about we go home and prepare a couple of sandwiches? 好啊！要不回家做点三明治？
A:  Nah! Let's go get a burger and fries. 不了！我们去吃汉堡和薯条吧。
B:  All you ever do is have unhealthy fast food Pizza, fries, burgers and hot dogs! You have to start eating better! 你总是吃不健康的快餐！披萨、薯条、汉堡和热狗！你得开始好好吃饭了！
A:  What are you talking about? I have salads sometimes. 你在说什么呢？我也偶尔吃沙拉的。
B:  Yeah right! I'm serious! You should also cut down on your sugar intake as well. You drink carbonated drinks that are high in fructose syrup! It's really not healthy! 得了吧！我是认真的！你也得减少糖分摄入。你喝的那些高果糖浆碳酸饮料真的不健康！
A:  Fine! I'll start drinking and having home cooked meals that are low in fat. Are you happy now? 行！我会开始喝低脂饮品，吃低脂家常菜。现在满意了吧？
B:  It's a start, but I'll be happy when I see you stick to your promise! 这只是个开始，但当你真正坚持承诺时，我才会开心！
