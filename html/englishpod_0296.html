Global View - Vegan Or Vegetarian? (C0296)

A:  Hey Julie, you want to go grab something to eat? 嘿 Julie，要不要出去吃点东西？
B:  Sure! What do you feel like having? 好啊！你想吃什么？
A:  I really feel like having a big juicy steak! 我特别想吃一大块多汁的牛排！
B:  Oh. ok. I don’t eat meat, but that’s fine, I am sure wherever we are going they will have other options right? 哦。好吧。我不吃肉，不过没关系，我相信我们要去的地方肯定有其他选择对吧？
A:  I didn’t know you were a vegetarian! 我不知道你是素食主义者！
B:  I’m not, I am a vegan. 我不是，我是纯素者。
A:  A what? 什么？
B:  A vegan. I don’t eat or use any animal based products. I don’t wear leather, eat eggs, drink milk or anything that comes from an animal. I used to be a pescatarian before, which basically means you don’t eat meat, but still have fish and seafood. 纯素者。我不吃也不用任何来自动物的产品。我不穿皮革，不吃鸡蛋，不喝牛奶，也不吃任何来自动物的东西。我以前是 pescatarian，基本上就是不吃肉，但还是会吃鱼和海鲜。
A:  Wow! That’s interesting! It must be tough! 哇！这真有趣！肯定很难吧？
B:  It’s a bit difficult to find vegetarian friendly restaurants sometimes, but since more and more people are vegetarians or vegans nowadays, it’s getting a bit less difficult. 有时候找素食友好的餐厅有点难，但如今越来越多的人是素食者或纯素者，所以情况正变得稍微容易一些。
