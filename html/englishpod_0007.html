The Office - Virus!(0007)

A:  Oh great! This stupid computer froze again! That’s the third time today! Hey Samuel, can you come take a look at my PC? It’s acting up again. It must have a virus or something.哦，太棒了！这破电脑又卡住了！今天已经是第三次了！嘿，Samuel，你能过来帮我看看我的电脑吗？它又出问题了。肯定是有病毒或者别的什么毛病。
B:  Just give me a second; I’ll be right up.稍等一下，我马上就来。
B:  I ran a virus scan on your computer, and it turns out that you have a lot of infected files!我在你的电脑上运行了病毒扫描，结果发现你有很多被感染的文件！
A:  But I’m quite careful when I’m browsing the internet, I have no idea how I could have picked up a virus.但我上网浏览时一直都很小心啊，我真不知道是怎么染上病毒的。
B:  Well, you have to make sure that your anti-virus software is updated regularly; yours wasn't up to date, that's probably what was causing your problems.嗯，你得确保你的杀毒软件定期更新；你的没更新，这大概就是导致问题的原因。
A:  Ok. Anything else?好的。还有其他问题吗？
B:  Yeah, try not to kick or hit the computer!对，别再踢打或者撞击电脑了！
A:  Um yeah… Sorry about that.呃，是的……对不起啦。
