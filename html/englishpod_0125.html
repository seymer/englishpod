Daily Life - No Smoking! (0125)

A:  It smells like an ashtray in here!这里闻起来像个烟灰缸！
B:  Hi honey! What's wrong? Why do you have that look on your face?嘿亲爱的！怎么了？你脸上怎么那副表情？
A:  What's wrong? I thought we agreed that you were gonna quit smoking.怎么回事？我以为我们说好你要戒烟的。
B:  No! I said I was going to cut down which is very different. You can't just expect me to go cold turkey overnight!不行！我说的是要减少，这俩完全不一样。你不能指望我一下子彻底戒掉！
A:  Look, there are other ways to quit. You can try the nicotine patch, or nicotine chewing gum. We spend a fortune on cigarettes every month and now laws are cracking down and not allowing smoking in any public place. It's not like you can just light up like before.听着，戒烟还有其他办法。你可以试试尼古丁贴片或者口香糖。我们每个月在烟上花一大笔钱，现在法律也收紧了，任何公共场所都不允许吸烟。不像以前那样想抽就抽。
B:  I know, I know. I am trying but, I don't have the willpower to just quit. I can't fight with the urge to reach for my pack of smokes in the morning with coffee or after lunch! Please understand?我知道，我知道。我在努力，但我没有意志力直接戒掉。我没法抵抗早上喝咖啡或午饭后伸手去拿烟包的冲动！请理解？
A:  Fine! I want a divorce!行吧！我要离婚！
