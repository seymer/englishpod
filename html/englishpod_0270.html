Daily Life - Buying A Suit (C0270)

A:  Hello sir, what can I do for you today? 您好，先生，今天有什么可以帮您的吗？
B:  Hi, I need a new suit. I have an important interview next week, so I really need to look sharp. 嗨，我需要一套新西装。下周有个重要面试，所以我真的需要看起来精神点。
A:  No problem! We have a broad selection of suits, all tailored made so that it will fit perfectly. 没问题！我们有很多款式的西装，都是量身定制的，保证合身。
B:  Great! I want a three piece suit, preferably made from Italian cashmere or wool. 太好了！我想要一套三件套西装，最好是意大利产的羊绒或羊毛材质。
A:  Very well sir. Would you like to have some shirts made also? 好的，先生。您也需要定制衬衫吗？
B:  Sure. I’ll also take some silver cuff link and a pair of silk ties. 当然。我还想买一对银色袖扣和一条真丝领带。
A:  Very good. Now, if you will accompany me, we can take your measurements and choose the patterns for your suit and shirts. 非常好。如果您愿意跟我来，我们可以量您的尺寸，并挑选西装和衬衫的款式。
