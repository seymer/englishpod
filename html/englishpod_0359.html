Global View - E-mail Scam (C0359)

A:  I got an urgent email from Tom! He says he is in London and got robbed and needs us to wire him some money for his hotel. 我收到汤姆发来的紧急邮件！他说他在伦敦，被抢了，需要我们要给他汇钱住酒店。
B:  What? That sounds really dodgy tome. 什么？这听起来太可疑了。
A:  No way, Tom is an honest person, he wouldn’t lie tome. 不可能，汤姆是个诚实的人，他不会骗我的。
B:  No I mean, it seems like someone may have hacked his email account and sent that out. I mean think about it, why would he email you instead of calling you. 不，我是说，好像有人黑进了他的邮箱发了那封邮件。我想想，他为什么给你发邮件而不是打电话呢？
A:  Do you really think someone is trying to scam people into sending money? 你真觉得有人在试图诈骗人们把钱吗？
B:  For sure! There are so many con artists out there, you never really know. 当然！外面骗子那么多，你永远不知道会发生什么。
