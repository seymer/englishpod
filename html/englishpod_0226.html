Global View - Fundraiser (C0226)

A:  Ok Mark, it's your turn to ring the doorbell. I did it last time. 马克，该你按门铃了。上次是我按的。
B:  I hate going door to door, and I hate asking for money. 我讨厌挨家挨户地推销，也讨厌要钱。
A:  But we need to raise enough money for the school fundraiser so that our class can win the pizza party! You do want to have a pizza party, don't you? 但我们得为学校的筹款活动凑够钱，这样我们班才能赢得披萨派对！你是想参加披萨派对的，对吧？
B:  Yes, but... 是，但是……
A:  Just go already! 赶紧去啊！
B:  No one's coming. 没人会来。
A:  Try again. 再试一次。
B:  Maybe there's no one home. 也许家里没人。
A:  Of course there's someone home! There are two cars in the driveway and I see lights on inthe house! Hello! Anybody home? We would like to know if you want to sponsor us in our school fundraiser. Fifty percent of the profits go towards the new school playground! 当然有人在家了！车道上有两辆车，而且我看见屋里灯还亮着呢！喂！有人在吗？我们想问问您是否愿意赞助我们的学校筹款活动。所得利润的百分之五十将用于建设新操场！
B:  I don't know why anyone would want what's in this catalog anyway. It's just a bunch of tacky Christmas ornaments, Cd's of old people singing Christmas songs, and special crackers and cheeses and boxes of chocolates. 我真不知道谁会想要目录里的东西。那只是一堆俗气的圣诞装饰品、老人在唱圣诞歌的 CD 碟，还有特制的小饼干奶酪和巧克力盒。
A:  You don't like chocolates? 你不喜欢巧克力？
B:  Not this kind. They've got weird names like ganache and praline. 不是这种。那些名字怪怪的，像甘纳许和太妃糖。
A:  Look! I just saw someone walking around inside! These people are being very rude! 看！我看见有人在里面闲逛了！这些人真没礼貌！
A:  Finally, someone's coming! 终于有人来了！
B:  They don't look too happy. 他们看起来不太高兴。
A:  Hi, sir. Would you like to sponsor us or make a donation to. 您好，先生。您愿意赞助我们或者捐款吗？
C:  What grade are you kids in? 你们这些孩子上几年级？
A:  Grade seven. 七年级。
C:  Then for goodness sake, don't you see this sign? Can't you read? 拜托，你没看见这个牌子吗？不会读吧？
A:  No soliciting. 禁止推销。
B:  What does that mean? 那是什么意思？
A:  No idea. 没概念。
