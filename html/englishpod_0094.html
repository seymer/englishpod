Global View - Big Bang Theory(0094)

A:  What's up? You don't look too good.怎么了？你看起来不太对劲。
B:  Yeah, my head hurts, that's all. I've been in physics class all day. It's killer!是啊，头有点疼，就这些。我上了一整天物理课，简直要命！
A:  I liked physics. It's all math, really; arcs, curves, velocity, cool stuff.我喜欢物理。其实全是数学：弧线、曲线、速度，挺酷的。
B:  Yeah, yeah, but today's lesson was all about the creation of the universe.是啊，是啊，但今天的课讲的是宇宙的创生。
A:  A physics class about the creation of the universe? That's some pretty unscientific language there. Sounds more religous to me.讲宇宙创生的物理课？这措辞可不科学啊。听起来更像宗教。
B:  It's all religion. Take the theory of the Big Bang. How is it possible that all of the stuff in the universe comes from an explosion? That's no better than Atlas carrying the globe on his back or African myths about turtles and stuff.全是宗教。就拿大爆炸理论来说吧。怎么可能说宇宙里所有的物质都来自一次爆炸呢？这跟阿特拉斯背着地球球体或者非洲神话里的乌龟之类的故事没什么两样。
A:  Turtles? Whatever... Look, all that's required for the creation of matter an imbalance of particles and anti-particles. At least, that's what the math says.乌龟？随便……听着，创造物质只需要粒子和反粒子的不平衡。至少数学是这么说的。
B:  Math, shmath. What's the evidence?数学？去你的。有什么证据吗？
A:  There is evidence! You know Edwin Hubble? He's the guy who in the early twentieth century was the first scientist to measure the drift of matter in the universe, thus advancing notions of an expanding universe. What would it be expanding from? Well, the Big Bang... DUH!有证据！你认识埃德温·哈勃吗？他是二十世纪早期的第一位测量宇宙中物质漂移的科学家，从而提出了宇宙膨胀的概念。那它从什么膨胀而来呢？嗯，大爆炸……废话！
B:  Anyway, it's just a theory. Why do people go around touting theories? Where's the scientific rigor in that?总之，这不过是个理论。人们到处宣扬理论做什么？科学严谨性在哪里？
A:  Dude, don't equivocate. A theory only becomes a theory after withstanding rigorous testing. You slept through class, didn't you?哥们儿，别含糊其辞。理论只有在经过严格检验后才能成立。你上课睡觉了吧？
B:  Agh! You're making my head hurt again! Quit with the questions!啊！你又让我头疼了！别再问问题了！
