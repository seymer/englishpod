The Weekend - Going To The Playground (C0335)

A:  Hey honey! Where were you?嘿亲爱的！你去哪儿了？
B:  I decided to take Kenny to the park and get some fresh air.我决定带肯尼去公园透透气。
A:  How was it? Were there a lot of kids?怎么样？孩子多吗？
B:  It wasn’t too crowded, but we had a great time! We got on the see-saw together, the went on a couple of different slides and then I tried to go with him in the jungle gym, but I didn’t fit.人不算太挤，但我们玩得很开心！我们一起玩了跷跷板，还滑了几个滑梯，然后我想和他一起玩攀爬架，但我身材太高进不去了。
A:  Sounds like fun! When we go he always just likes to play in the sandbox.听起来真有趣！我们下次去的时候他总是只想在沙坑里玩。
B:  Yeah, but today he was really hyper. He even got on the monkey bars and then he went on to go on the swings for a half hour. I’m exhausted!是啊，但今天他特别兴奋。他甚至爬上了单杠，接着又在秋千上荡了半小时。我累死了！
A:  You should go to the park more often since you don’t go to the gym anymore!既然你不再去健身房了，你应该更常去公园啊！
