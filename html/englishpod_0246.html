Daily Life - At The Train Station (C0246)

A:  Hi, I would like to purchase a one way ticket to Brussels please. 你好，我想买一张去布鲁塞尔的单程票。
B:  Certainly sir, this is our train schedule. We have an express train departing every morning and an overnight train that departs at nine pm. 当然可以，先生，这是我们的列车时刻表。我们每天早上有一班快车，晚上九点有一班夜间列车。
A:  How long does it take to get there? 到那里需要多长时间？
B:  About twelve hours. We currently have tickets available only for first class on the express train. If you'd like, you can choose a sleeper on the overnight train which is a bit less expensive. 大约十二个小时。目前快车只有一等座有票。如果您愿意，可以选择夜间列车的卧铺，价格会便宜一些。
A:  Yeah, I think that is the best option. Do you serve food on the train? Twelve hours is such a long time! 好的，我觉得这是最好的选择。火车上有提供食物吗？坐十二个小时太久了！
B:  Yes of course. There is a dining car towards the front of the train where they serve meals at all times. We do provide complimentary water and coffee for all of our passengers. 当然有。在列车前部有一个餐车，随时供应餐食。我们为所有乘客免费提供水和咖啡。
A:  Great! I'll take it. 太好了！我就选这个了。
B:  Here you are sir. Your train leaves from platform number nine at nine on the dot. Remember to be here at least thirty minutes before your scheduled departure time or else you might miss your train! 给您，先生。您的列车将在九点整从九号站台出发。请至少在发车时间前三十分钟到达，否则可能会错过火车！
A:  I understand. Thank you very much ! 我明白了。非常感谢！
B:  Have a great trip. 祝您旅途愉快。
