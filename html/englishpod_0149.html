Daily Life - Moving (C0149)

A:  Ok, that's fine. Bye. 好的，没问题。再见。
B:  What happened? 发生什么事了？
A:  That's it, my lease is up. I have to move. 就是这样，我的租约到期了，我得搬家。
B:  What? Why? Can't you renew it? 什么？为什么？你不能续租吗？
A:  The owner apparently is selling this place to make way for the construction of a parking lot 房东显然要把这里卖掉，以便为建造停车场腾出空间。
B:  Well, I can help you pack. We should start looking for a new place for you ASAP. 嗯，我可以帮你打包。我们应该尽快开始为你寻找新住处。
A:  I think I might move in with my parents for a couple of months until I can find something. You know how hard it is to find a decent place around here. I'm gonna have to put most of my stuff in storage for a while. 我想我可能会和父母住上几个月，直到找到合适的地方。你知道在这附近找个好地方有多难。我得把大部分东西先寄存在仓库里一段时间。
B:  Well, let me know if there's anything I can do to help out. 好吧，如果有什么我能帮忙的，请告诉我。
A:  Actually, would you mind looking after my pet tarantula and snake for a couple of weeks? 实际上，你介意帮我照看我的宠物捕鸟蛛和蛇几周吗？
B:  hehe.. sure 嘿嘿……没问题。
