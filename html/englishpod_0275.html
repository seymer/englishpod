Daily Life - Kitchen Appliances (C0275)

A:  I have been looking at this online catalog for over an hour and I still haven’t finished getting all the kitchen appliances that we need! 我在网上浏览这个目录已经超过一个小时了，还是没买齐我们需要的所有厨房电器！
B:  What are you getting? 你要买什么？
A:  Well, the first thing on my list is a new blender. I decided to also get a juicer and a new coffee maker. 嗯，我清单上的第一样是新的搅拌机。我还决定买个榨汁机和一台新咖啡机。
B:  Don’t forget to also get a new mixer. I lent the old one to my brother and he broke it. 别忘了再买个新的打蛋器。我把旧的那个借给我哥哥了，结果被他弄坏了。
A:  Yeah I know. I also decided to throw away the old toaster and get a new one. I am also getting a rice cooker and steamer to make some nice steamed fish or veggies. 是啊我知道。我也决定把旧烤面包机扔掉换个新的。我还要买个电饭煲和蒸锅，用来做点清蒸鱼或者蔬菜。
B:  I’m actually thinking of completely refurnishing the kitchen and getting a new stove, oven, dishwasher and trash compacter. 我其实正打算彻底重新装修厨房，换上新炉灶、烤箱、洗碗机和垃圾处理器。
A:  That’s a good idea ! The kitchen will look amazing! 好主意！厨房会看起来棒极了！
