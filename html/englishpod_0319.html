Daily Life - Small Talk 5 - Brief Talk With A Stranger (C0319)

Older gentleman:  Oh dear Miss, you are soaked! Wow, it's really raining heavily outside.哎呀，小姐，您都湿透了！哇，外面雨下得真大。
Michelle:  Yes, it sure is. I had to run here from work! I need to rush as I'm on my lunch break.是啊，确实如此。我从公司跑过来的！我得赶紧去，因为我在午休时间。
Older gentleman:  Well please, why don't you go ahead of me in line? I'm in no hurry.那好吧，请让我先排吧？我不着急。
Michelle:  Oh, that's so nice of you! Thank you very much.哦，您真是太好了！非常感谢。
Older gentleman:  My pleasure Miss. Actually, could you recommend what to eat here? I've never been here before.不客气，小姐。对了，能推荐一下这里吃什么吗？我还没来过这儿呢。
Michelle:  Sure. Well, the avocado sandwich is delicious, and it's the healthiest thing on the menu. Personally, I think the beef salad is the tastiest choice. I usually get that. Also, the milkshakes are the best milkshakes in town!当然可以。嗯，牛油果三明治很好吃，而且菜单上最健康。就我个人而言，我觉得牛肉沙拉最好吃。我通常都点那个。还有，这里的奶昔是镇上最好的！
Older gentleman:  Well, thanks for the suggestions.好的，谢谢您的建议。
Michelle:  Oh, don't mention it.哦，别客气。
