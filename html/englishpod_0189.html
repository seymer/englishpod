Daily Life - Buying A New Mobile Phone (C0189)

A:  Hello sir, may I help you? 您好，有什么可以帮您？
B:  Yeah, I accidentally dropped my phone in the toilet. 是的，我不小心把手机掉进马桶里了。
A:  I see. Well, you have come to the right place. We have over one hundred models of more than twenty leading mobile phone manufacturers. 明白了。您来对地方了。我们有一百多种型号，来自二十多家主流手机制造商。
B:  Sounds good. I don't want it to be too expensive, maybe something mid-range. 听起来不错。我不想买太贵的，也许中档的就行。
A:  We have this new HTC smart phone. It comes with the Android OS so you can download applications. It also has a built-in camera, mp3 player and touch screen. It works on the 3G network so you have fast access to the internet wherever you are. 我们有这款新的 HTC 智能手机。它搭载安卓系统，可以下载应用。它还内置摄像头、MP3 播放器和触摸屏。支持 3G 网络，无论您在哪里都能快速上网。
B:  What about Wi-fi? 那 Wi-Fi 呢？
A:  Of course! You can access the internet from any hotspot as well as from home. 当然！您可以在任何热点以及家中上网。
B:  One last thing. Is it waterproof? 最后问一句，它是防水的吗？
