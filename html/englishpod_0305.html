The Weekend - Bowling (C0305)

A:  Alright, so the first thing that you need to know about bowling is that you should never cross that line where the lane begins. 好了，关于保龄球你需要知道的第一件事就是：永远不要越过球道起始的那条线。
B:  Why not?为什么不能？
A:  Because they polish and oil it to make the ball slide down. If you step there you will slip and fall.因为他们会打磨并给那条线涂油，让球滑下去。如果你踩上去就会打滑摔倒。
A:  OK, so I got my bowling shoes, my ball, our names on the scorecard, so now, how the heck do I play this?好的，那我有了保龄球鞋、我的球、还有我们名字在记分牌上，那么现在我到底该怎么玩呢？
A:  You throw the ball down the lane and try to knock down all the pins. If you do, that is called a strike. If you don’t knock them all down on the first try, then you get a chance to get the spare. After ten frames, we add up the points and see who has the most. Three hundred is a perfect score, but very hard to get.你把球沿着球道扔过去，尽量把所有的球瓶都撞倒。如果全撞倒了，那叫全中（strike）。如果你第一次没全撞倒，那你还有机会补一个余数（spare）。十局结束后，我们加总分数，看谁最高。满分是三百分，但非常难拿到。
B:  Got it! OK, I’m gonna give it a go. Oh no! My ball went in the gutter!明白了！好的，我要试试了。哦不！我的球掉进沟里了！
A:  I told you, its harder than you think. Now let a pro show you how it’s done.我早说过，这比你想的要难得多。现在让一位专业人士给你演示一下怎么做。
