The Office - Small Talk 7 - Talking About A Trip (C0325)

Jim:  Hey Michelle. Good to see you. Are you at lunch?嘿，米歇尔。见到你真高兴。你在吃午饭吗？
Michelle:  Oh hi Jim. No I just got back. I thought you were on vacation now.哦，嗨，吉姆。不，我刚回来。我以为你现在在度假呢。
Jim:  No, I wish I was! I just got back from Spain actually.没有，真希望我在！其实我刚才刚从西班牙回来。
Michelle:  Oh wonderful! Have you been there before or was it your first time?太棒了！你以前去过那里还是第一次去？
Jim:  My first time. I've traveled around Europe a lot, but this was my first time to Spain. It was amazing, and the weather was just beautiful! No rain, and just sun, sun, sun....我第一次去。我去过欧洲很多地方，但这是第一次来西班牙。太神奇了，天气也美极了！没下雨，全是阳光，阳光，阳光……
Michelle:  I'm so jealous of you. I've never been anywhere in Europe. I've always dreamed of traveling around and seeing the sights.我真羡慕你。我从没去过欧洲的任何一个地方。我一直梦想着四处旅行看看风景。
Jim:  Well, I really recommend Spain. You really should go.Anyway, it's been great to catch up, but I must be going, this is my floor. Speak again soon I hope.嗯，我真的很推荐你去西班牙。你真应该去看看。总之，能叙旧真好，但我得走了，这是我的楼层。希望不久后还能再聊。
Michelle:  For sure. Take care.没问题。保重。
