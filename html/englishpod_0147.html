Daily Life - The Argument (C0147)

A:  Wow, that terrible movie is finally over. Next time I'm picking the film, because I don't want to end up seeing a chick flick.哇，那部烂片终于看完了。下次选电影我来挑，我可不想再看什么“少女爱情片”。
B:  Well you should have picked, in the end you always complain about everything.哼，你早该自己选了，到头来你总是对什么都抱怨。
A:  Not everything, just this film. Even the title is ridiculous; and it's so long, those are the two and a half most wasted hours of my life, so much so that I'm thinking about asking them to give me my money back.不是所有东西都这样，就这部片子而已。连标题都荒谬可笑；而且拖得太长，那是我人生中被浪费的两个半小时，简直离谱到我想找他们退钱。
B:  I'm thinking of taking you back home. I thought we could have a nice evening, but you're always so negative.我正打算送你回家。本想着能有个愉快的夜晚，可你总是这么消极。
A:  I'm only complaining about a movie that I could have rented or bought and then thrown in the garbage.我只是在抱怨一部我本来可以租或买、然后直接扔进垃圾桶的电影。
B:  You see, that's what I'm talking about, I can't stand your sarcastic jokes anymore瞧吧，这就是我要说的，我真的受够了你的讽刺笑话。
A:  Next time, go with your gay friend who is more in touch with his feelings.下次跟那个更懂感情的同性恋朋友去吧。
B:  Well he's more of a man than you are; at least he appreciates love stories.哼，他比你更像个男人；至少他还懂得欣赏爱情故事。
A:  Love? More like one-night-stands.爱情？那不过是露水情缘罢了。
B:  Don't criticize Mario or else I'll start on those fat, drunk friends of yours; they're no saints.别拿马里奥说事，否则我就开始吐槽你那群又胖又喝醉的朋友了；他们可算不上什么圣人。
A:  My friends? Fat? What about those whales you call friends?我那些朋友？胖？那你那些你叫朋友的鲸鱼又算怎么回事呢？
B:  You're unbearable; you can walk home, I'm leaving.你真让人受不了，你自己走回家吧，我走了。
