Daily Life - Hiring Help (C0364)

A:  Can you help me write a newspaper ad? 能帮我写一则报纸广告吗？
B:  Sure, what are you looking to buy or sell? 当然，你想买什么或卖什么？
A:  Actually, I want to hire someone to help me around the house. 其实我想雇个人来帮忙打理家务。
B:  Oh, you want to get a maid? 哦，你是想请个女佣？
A:  Well, I think it’s better if you call her a cleaning lady or domestic help. 嗯，我觉得最好还是叫她清洁工或家政帮手。
B:  Ok, so what do you wanther to do? 好的，那她具体要做什么呢？
A:  Well, let’s see. I wanther to come in three times a week for a couple of hours to clean the kitchen, bathroom and maybe do some cooking. 嗯，让我想想。我希望她每周来三次，每次几个小时，负责打扫厨房、浴室，顺便做点饭。
B:  Got it. And how much do you offer per month? 明白了。那你打算每月付多少钱？
A:  I would pay her hourly, I don’t know what the going rate is though. 我想按小时付给她，不过我不清楚市场行情。
B:  I know for sure it’s more than minimum wage but maybe you should just negotiate with the person that answers the ad. 我确定肯定高于最低工资标准，但也许你可以直接和刊登广告的人商量一下。
A:  Great! Thanks for your help! 太棒了！谢谢你的帮助！
