Daily Life - Office Gossip (C0209)

Pam:  Psssst! Pssssssssst! Hey! Eric, have you heard? 嘘！嘘！嘿！Eric，你听说了吗？
Eric:  Hm? No… go on, tell me, what’s the latest office gossip? 嗯？没有……快说，最新的办公室八卦是什么？
Pam:  Well, you didn’t hear this from me but the rumor is………. is getting a promotion! 呃，这个你可别从我这儿听去的，但传闻是……她要升职了！
Eric:  No way! But… she’s a terrible worker… and you can’t trust her… she’s so two-faced – you can’t believe anything she says! 没门儿！可是……她工作一塌糊涂……而且你也信不过她……她两面三刀——她说的话一句都不可信！
Paula:  Hey guys, what are you two whispering about? 嘿伙计们，你们俩在嘀咕什么呢？
Eric:  Oh Hi Paula! How are you? 哦嗨，Paula！你怎么样？
Paula:  I’ve got some good news! I’m getting a promotion! 我有好消息要告诉你！我要升职啦！
Pam:  Congratulations! Eric and I were just saying that you are the best person forthe job…. 恭喜啊！Eric 和我刚才还说，你是最合适的人选……
Eric:  Yes! You’re the best! 没错！你就是最棒的！

Key Vocabulary

hear听说learn something by the ear (usually in surprise)通过耳朵了解一些东西（通常是在惊讶中）
gossip八卦rumor or talk of a personal, sensational nature涉及个人、耸人听闻的谣言或谈话
whisper低语soft speech produced without full voice没有完整语音的轻柔语音
rumor谣言a mixture of truth and untruth passed around真相与谎言的混合体流传开来
promotion促销act of raising in rank or position提升级别或职位的行为

Supplementary Vocabulary
