The Office - Presentation Series 3 - Making Comparisons (C0277)

Mr. Ford:  Now a key question you might ask yourself is what differentiates the new x420 line with our previous models, and also of course with some of our competitors. 现在您可能要问的一个关键问题是，新的 x420 系列与我们之前的型号以及部分竞争对手有何不同。
Mr. Ford:  In other words what makes the x420 stand out from all the others? This is a key question, and is something I'd like to explore in a little depth. Firstly, the x420 has a range of USPs that really make it a cut above the rest. 换句话说，是什么让 x420 在众多产品中脱颖而出？这是一个关键问题，我想对此进行深入的探讨。首先，x420 拥有一系列独特的卖点，使其真正超越其他所有产品。
Mr. Ford:  The first thing to mention is that the x420 is the first in a new generation of ultra-light laptop computers. It is only 2lbs, which compares very favorably with all our key competitors. In terms of computer performance, for such a light machine it’s very powerful. 4Gb of RAM, with an ultra-fast processor. 首先要提到的是，x420 是新一代超轻笔记本电脑的首款产品。它仅重 2 磅，与我们的主要竞争对手相比具有显著优势。就计算机性能而言，对于这样一台轻便的机器来说，它的性能非常强大，配备 4GB 内存和超高速处理器。
Mr. Ford:  The most advanced video and sound cards on the market are installed with a crystal-clear 15-inch LCD display. The x420 really stands out as next generation laptop. Compared with our previous x540 range it really is in a league of its own .市场上最先进的视频和声卡已安装其中，并搭配一块清晰锐利的 15 英寸 LCD 显示屏。x420 真正彰显了其作为下一代笔记本电脑的地位。与之前的 x540 系列相比，它确实处于一个完全不同的档次。
Mr. Ford:  Now, if we go on to look at projected sales for the x420 we can see that sales revenue for 2010 is expected to hit at least 20 million dollars. Now this is really a conservative estimate. 现在，如果我们进一步查看 x420 的预计销售额，可以看到 2010 年的销售收入预计至少达到 2000 万美元。这实际上是一个非常保守的估计。
Mr. Ford:  If our marketing campaign is successful I'm confident that we could see a doubling of this figure at the very least. Now please bear in mind that this is only for the first year of production. 如果我们的营销活动取得成功，我确信至少能看到这一数字翻倍。现在请注意，这仅针对生产第一年。
Mr. Ford:  I'm certain that in the coming three years the x420 will actually overtake all our existing products, both in terms of sales and revenue. Okay, now let's move on to discuss our marketing concept and look more closely at our key competitors. 我确定在接下来的三年里，x420 将在销售额和收入方面实际上超越我们现有的所有产品。好的，现在让我们继续讨论我们的营销理念，并更深入地分析我们的主要竞争对手。
