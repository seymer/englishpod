Daily Life - Ordering Chinese Food (C0291)

Waitress:  Hi, welcome to Happy Buddah! Can I get you anything to drink? 嗨，欢迎光临幸福佛！想喝点什么吗？
Manny:  A Coke for me, please. 给我来杯可乐吧。
Andrea:  I’ll have a Sprite. 我要一杯雪碧。
Waitress:  Okay, I’ll go get that for you. Are there any questions with the menu? 好的，我去给您拿。菜单上有什么需要问的吗？
Andrea:  Do you use MSG? 你们用味精吗？
Waitress:  No ma’am, we are MSG-free. 女士，我们不用味精。
Andrea:  Oh man, I haven’t had Chinese food in so long! I want everything! This place has the BEST sesame chicken. 天哪，我好久没吃中餐了！什么都想吃！这家的芝麻鸡最好吃了。
Manny:  Yeah, I’ve been craving Chinese for such a long time. I used to get take-out all the time. It’s definitely been a while. Let’s start off with some crab rangoon. 是啊，我好久了就想吃中餐了。以前我经常叫外卖。确实有一阵子没吃了。我们先来点蟹肉春卷吧。
Andrea:  Ooh yeah, that sounds good. I think I’m going to get the sesame chicken with fried rice, a spring roll, and egg drop soup. 哦，听起来不错。我想点芝麻鸡配炒饭、春卷和蛋花汤。
Manny:  It’s so tempting to order everything on the menu, it all looks so appetizing! I think I’ll get General Tso’s chicken, hot and sour soup, fried wontons, and white rice. 菜单上的菜太诱人了，看起来都让人食欲大开！我想点左宗棠鸡、酸辣汤、炸馄饨和白米饭。
Andrea:  Aren’t you supposed to be on a diet? You should at least get brown rice.你不是在节食吗？至少该点糙米饭。
Manny:  I don’t think so! I hate brown rice, and I’m so sick of eating healthy all the time. I’ve been eating so much salad I swear I’ve forgotten what meat tastes like! There’s no better remedy than some nice, greasy, calorie-laden Chinese food. I might even get an order of broccoli beef! 我才不这么想！我讨厌糙米饭，而且受够了天天吃健康餐。我吃沙拉吃到怀疑人生，发誓都快忘了肉是什么味道了！没有什么比来顿香喷喷、油腻腻、热量爆棚的中餐更解馋的了。我甚至可能要点一份西兰花牛肉！
Andrea:  Gosh, I’m so hungry! Let’s call the waitress over! 天哪，我好饿啊！叫服务员过来吧！

Key Vocabulary

craving渴望a very strong desire for something非常强烈的渴望
tempting诱人的attractive有吸引力的
remedy补救办法a medicine or treatment药品或治疗
take-out外卖food that is sold by a store to be eaten elsewhere由商店出售给其他地方食用的食物
appetizing开胃的delicious不错

Supplementary Vocabulary

adaptation改编something that is changed/alerted to suit a new form更改主题
guilty pleasure带罪感乐趣an indulgence,; something that you eat, drink, or do that you shouldn't, but makes you happy稍后再联系或写信给你
hits the spot恰到好处just right; just what you were looking for (often refers to food or a drink)请休息
