Global View - Thai Food (C0321)

A:  What did you cook? 你做了什么菜？
B:  Well, as you know I was in Thailand last month, and I took a cooking class! So I prepared some of my favorite dishes. 嗯，你知道我上个月去了泰国，还上了一堂烹饪课！所以我准备了一些我最喜欢的菜肴。
A:  Great idea! As long as I don’t get food poisoning! So what is onthe menu tonight? 好主意！只要别食物中毒就行！那今晚的菜单上有什么？
B:  Ok, for starters we have Tom Yam soup. It’s a bit spicy, but really good! 好的，先来一道汤——冬阴功汤。有点辣，但真的很好吃！
A:  This is delicious! The ginger and lemongrass really gives it a nice taste! 太美味了！姜和柠檬草赋予了它独特的风味！
B:  Now this next dish is one of the most famous. Foreigners call it papaya salad butthe proper name is Tom Sam. It is a spicy salad made from a mix of fresh vegetables including shredded unripened papaya and tomato. 接下来这道菜是最著名的之一。外国人叫它青木瓜沙拉，但正式名称是“汤姆萨姆”。这是一道辛辣沙拉，由新鲜蔬菜混合制成，包括切丝的未成熟木瓜和番茄。
A:  This is delicious! The combination of sour and spicy is really interesting! I could have this everyday! 太美味了！酸辣的搭配真的很有趣！我每天都想这么吃！
B:  Ok, now forthe last and best dish in my opinion. This is called Pad Thai. It’s stir-fried noodles with eggs, fish sauce, tamarind juice, red chili pepper plus bean sprouts, shrimp and tofu and garnished with crushed peanuts and coriander. It’s practically Thailand’s national dish! 好的，最后也是我认为最好的一道菜。这道叫泰式炒河粉。它是用鸡蛋、鱼露、罗望子汁、红辣椒，再加上豆芽、虾仁和豆腐炒制而成，最后撒上碎花生和香菜作为点缀。这几乎是泰国的国菜！
A:  Wow, this is great! I never knew Thai food was so creative and delicious! 哇，太棒了！我从未想过泰国菜如此富有创意又美味！
B:  Wantsome more? 再来点吗？
A:  I’m stuffed! 我饱了！
