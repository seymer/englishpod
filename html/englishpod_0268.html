The Office - Purchasing Manager (C0268)

A:  Good morning, Angela, how have you been lately?早上好，Angela，最近怎么样？
B:  Morning, Michael. I’ve been very busy lately. One of our other vendors is going out of business and I’ve been searching for a suitable replacement.早啊，Michael。我最近特别忙。我们另一个供应商要倒闭了，我一直在找合适的替代者。
A:  Well, rest assured that you can count on us to be here for the long run sit down. Coffee?放心吧，你可以指望我们会长期合作。坐下吧。喝咖啡吗？
B:  No, thanks. I’ve been trying to cut down on the caffeine .不用了，谢谢。我一直在努力减少咖啡因摄入。
A:  Haha, I could never do that. I’d be a zombie if I didn’t have my morning coffee fix. Let’s get down to business then.哈哈，我做不到。要是没有早晨的咖啡，我就成僵尸了。那咱们直接谈正事吧。
B:  Yes. I’ve come to talk with you about ordering the eight megapixel cameras for our new MePhone. The demand for phone cameras is growing, and Pear has been falling behind in the market.好的。我来和你谈谈订购八百万像素相机的事，用于我们新的 MePhone 手机。手机相机的需求正在增长，Pear 公司一直落后于市场。
A:  That’s great! I’m glad to hear that Pear has finally jumped on the bandwagon. Right now our contract is for the five megapixel cameras. Is Pear still interested in having those?太好了！很高兴听到 Pear 终于赶上了潮流。目前我们的合同是五百万像素的相机。Pear 还对那些感兴趣吗？
B:  No, we’re changing all the cameras to eight megapixels. We were hoping that by making your company our sole supplier for cameras we could negotiate a better deal. 不，我们要把所有相机都换成八百万像素。我们希望把贵公司作为我们唯一的相机供应商，这样能谈下更好的条件。
A:  Surely. Let’s get started by drafting a new contract. 没问题。我们先起草一份新合同吧。
