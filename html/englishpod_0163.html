A:  Well that was an interesting documentary! 那纪录片真有意思！
B:  For sure! I didn't really understand some ofthe technical jargon they used inthe film when they talked about social security in the US. 没错！我在看美国社会保障相关内容时，有些技术术语没太听懂。
A:  Like what? 比如什么？
B:  Well, they mentioned how people put away money in something called a 401K? 嗯，他们提到人们把钱存入一种叫 401(k) 的东西里。
A:  Yeah, I know it sounds weird, but a 401k is a type of retirement plan that allows employees to save and invest for their own retirement Through a you can authorize your employer to deduct a certain amount of money from your paycheck and invest it inthe plan Everyone tries to contribute as much as possible so that when you retire, you can rest peacefully on your nest egg. 对，听起来有点怪，但 401(k) 是一种退休计划，允许员工储蓄和投资以保障自己的退休生活。通过它，你可以授权雇主从工资中扣除一定金额并投入该计划。大家都尽量多存一些，这样退休后就能安心享受积蓄带来的安稳生活。
B:  That's interesting and logical I guess. In my country, we also have to contribute to a government-run retirement fund, but most people don't really trust itso they just invest in properties or things like that. 这挺有意思也挺合理的吧。在我们国家，我们也需要向政府运营的养老基金缴费，但大多数人不太信任它，所以更愿意投资房产之类的东西。
A:  That seems a bit unstable don't you think? 那听起来有点不稳定，你觉得呢？
B:  Yeah, but corrupt governments inthe past have created distrust among banks and financial institutions, so now people prefer to have money hidden in a jar or a piggy bank. 是啊，过去腐败的政府让人们对银行和金融机构失去信任，所以现在人们更倾向于把钱藏在罐子或存钱箱里。
A:  I've been thinking of doing that lately! I don't want some banker to run off with my money! 我最近也在考虑这么做！我可不想让某个银行家卷款跑路！

Supplementary Vocabulary

pension养老金money paid at regular intervals from the government or a previous employer to a person following retirement or to their surviving dependents政府或前雇主定期向退休后的人或其尚存家属支付的钱
disability income insurance伤残收入保险financial assistance to people who acquire a disability so that they can maintain a certain income level为患有残疾的人提供经济援助，使他们能够维持一定的收入水平
survivor benefits遗属抚恤金financial assistance for the families of workers who die为死亡工人家属提供经济援助
unemployment benefits/ compensation失业救济金payments made to an unemployed worker by the goverment or employer during all or part of the period of unemployment政府或雇主在整个或部分失业期间向失业工人支付的款项
defined benefit plan固定收益计划a pension plan that pays a defined pension/benefit, but the amount invested may vary支付固定养老金/福利的养老金计划，但投资金额可能会有所不同
defined contribution plan固定缴款计划a pension plan that has a defined amount to invest, but whose pension/benefit depends on the success of those investments具有明确投资金额的养老金计划，但其养老金/福利取决于这些投资的成功
