Elementary - Foul!(0033)

A:  Has the game started yet?比赛开始了吗？
B:  Yeah, about 5 minutes ago.开始了，大约 5 分钟前。
A:  Who’s winning?谁在赢？
B:  The Bulls, of course!当然是公牛队！
A:  What! That wasn’t a foul! C'mon, ref!什么！那不是犯规啊！拜托了，裁判！
B:  Don't worry, Shaq always screws up free throws.别担心，沙克罚球总是搞砸的。
A:  You were right! He didn't make the shot!你猜对了！他投丢了！
B:  That was a great shot! A three pointer, yeah!那球太漂亮了！三分球，没错！
A:  Did you see that? He traveled and the ref didn't call it!你看见了吗？他走步了，裁判居然没吹！
B:  This ref needs glasses. Hey ref, open your eyes! I can't believe he didn't see that!这裁判得戴眼镜。嘿裁判，睁大眼睛！我真不敢相信他没看见那个！
A:  Okay... end of the first quarter... Alright, I'm gonna make a beer run.好吧……第一节结束了……行吧，我去买瓶啤酒。
