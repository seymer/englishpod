Global View - Health Insurance (C0160)

A:  Hey honey, how was your day? 亲爱的，今天过得怎么样？
B:  It was alright. I ran into Billand we got totalking for a whileHe's in a bit of a jam. 还行。我碰见了比尔，我们聊了一会儿。他最近有点麻烦。
A:  Why? What happened? 怎么了？出什么事了？
B:  Well, his son had an accident and Billdoesn't have health insurance. This really got me thinking, and I wondered if we shouldn't look into a couple of different HMO's. 嗯，他儿子出了事故，可比尔没有医疗保险。这让我想了很多，我在想我们是不是该研究几家不同的 HMO（健康维护组织）。
A:  Yeah, you're right. We aren't getting any younger and our kids are getting older. 没错，你说得对。我们都不是越来越年轻了，孩子们也长大了。
B:  Exactly! I searched onthe web and found a couple of HMO's with low co pays and good coverage. The deductibles are low, too. 太对了！我在网上查了一下，发现几家保费低、保障好的 HMO。免赔额也很低。
A:  Sounds good, although, do you think we can qualify for insurance? Those insurance companies are real pirates when it comes to money. 听起来不错，不过你觉得我们能符合投保资格吗？那些保险公司一谈到钱就真像强盗一样。
B:  Well, we don't have any pre existing illnesses or conditions, so we should be fine. 嗯，我们没有任何既往病史或病症，应该没问题。
A:  I wish our company or country provided us with healthcare. 我真希望我们公司或者国家能给我们提供医疗保健。
B:  Not in a million years! 想都别想！

Supplementary Vocabulary

prescription处方the medicine or remedy ordered by a physician for a patient医生为患者开出的药物或疗法
exclusion除外责任certain services that an insurance company will not cover in the plan保险公司不会在计划中承保的某些服务
healthcare医疗保健the prevention and treatment of illness and the preservation of well-being through the services offered by the health professions通过卫生专业人员提供的服务预防和治疗疾病并保持福祉
premium保险费the amount paid for an insurance policy, often in installments保险单支付的金额，通常分期付款
coinsurance共同保险After the deductible is exceeded, it is when the insured must also pay a percentage of the total cost of services while the insurance company pays the rest. It can be used instead of or in addition to copays.超过免赔额后，被保险人还必须支付服务总费用的一定比例，而保险公司支付其余部分。它可以代替自付额或与自付额一起使用。
