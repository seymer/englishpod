Daily Life - Going To The Gym(0130)

A:  Hey there, you look a little lost. Are you new here?嗨，你看起来有点迷路了。你是新来的吗？
B:  Yeah how'd you know?是啊，你怎么知道？
A:  You can always spot the newbies. I can give you a few pointers if you want. Were you trying to use this machine here?一眼就能看出新人。如果你想的话，我可以给你几点建议。你刚才想用这台机器对吧？
B:  Yeah! I just started my training today and I'm not really sure where to begin.对！我今天刚开始训练，不太确定该从哪里开始。
A:  It's ok, I know how it is. This machine here will work out your upper body, mainly your triceps and biceps. Are you looking to develop strength or muscle tone and definition?没关系，我懂那种感觉。这台机器主要锻炼上半身，特别是肱三头肌和肱二头肌。你是想增力还是塑形？
B:  Well, I don't want to be ripped like you! I just want a good physique with weights and cardio.嗯，我可不想练得像你那样肌肉爆棚！我只想通过力量训练和有氧运动拥有健康的体态。
A:  In that case you want to work with less weight. You can start off by working ten to fifteen reps in four sets. Five kilo weights should be enough. Now it's very important that you stretch before pumping iron or you might pull a muscle.那样的话你需要用较轻的重量。你可以从四组每组十到十五次开始。五公斤的重量应该就够了。现在非常重要的一点是：举铁前一定要拉伸，否则可能会拉伤肌肉。
B:  Got it! Wow is that the weight you are lifting? My goodness that's a lot of weight!明白了！哇，你举的是那个重量吗？天哪，那太重了！
A:  It's not that much. Just watch... I'm ok...没那么重。看好了……我没事的……
