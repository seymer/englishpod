Daily Life - Picking A University (C0294)

A:  I've never heard of AmLion College. Could you... 我从来没听说过美洲狮学院。能给我介绍一下吗？
B:  Of course sir, let me give you a brief overview. AmLion College is located in the center of New York city. The school covers a wide range of academic subjects; and eighty percent of the courses are transferable to other state universities. And, last year AmLion College was ranked number one in terms of graduate employment. 当然，先生。让我给您做个简要介绍。美洲狮学院位于纽约市中心。学校涵盖广泛的学术科目；其中 80% 的课程可转学到其他州立大学。而且，去年美洲狮学院的毕业生就业率排名第一。
A:  Interesting, and what about the tuition fees, then? 挺有意思的，那学费是多少呢？
B:  You'll be looking at somewhere around fifteen thousand US dollars per semester. 每学期大约需要一万五千美元。
A:  Okay, well. 好的，嗯。
B:  And, did I mention our on-campus housing? Students can stay in our newly renovated dorms for as little as three thousand dollars per month! 对了，我说过我们的校内住宿吗？学生可以入住我们新翻修的宿舍，每月低至三千美元！
A:  Sounds good. Well. I'll just grab one of your flyers. 听起来不错。嗯。我先拿一张你们的宣传册吧。
B:  Sir, you got the wrong flyer. Sir, sir! 先生，您拿错宣传册了。先生，先生！
