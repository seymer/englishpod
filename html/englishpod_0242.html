Global View - Learning The Piano (C0242)

Charles:  Hi Cody, how did practicing go this week? 嗨，科迪，这周练习得怎么样？
Cody:  Well I had several tests and an oral presentation this week so I didn't get a chance to memorize the second page, but I think I mastered the tricky section. 嗯，我这周有几场考试和一个口头展示，所以没机会背第二页，不过我觉得我已经掌握了那个难的部分。
Charles:  Great! Warm up with some scales and arpeggios first. Good, good. This week, work on keeping the rhythm steady when you play the last part with the sixteenth note . Now let's take a look at this tricky section. 太好了！先练音阶和分解和弦热身。好，很好。这周重点是在演奏十六分音符的最后一部分时保持节奏稳定。现在我们来看看这个难点段落。
Cody:  Charles? Before I start I was wondering if it was ok if I put a small crescendo in here and then decrescendo back to pianissimo again over here? 查尔斯？在我开始之前我想问问，我在这加一个小渐强，然后再在这里回到弱音，这样行吗？
Charles:  It might work. I'll have to hear it . Show me what you've done. Not bad , not bad . 也许可以。我得先听一听。让我看看你做了什么。不错，不错。
Cody:  It was horrible! I played play it much better at home! 太糟糕了！我在家里弹得好多了！
Charles:  It's just nerves. Just play the right hand for now. One two three four five six, ta ti tri-ple-ti. Good, good. Don't forget the accidentals! The key signature says that note should be a G-sharp but now it's a G-natural. Now add the bass clef. You're going too fast. Remember the tempo for this piece is andante. 只是紧张。现在先只弹右手。一二三四五六，塔提三普莱提。好，很好。别忘了变音记号！调号显示那个音符应该是升 G，但现在却是自然 G。现在加上低音谱号。你弹得太快了。记住这首曲子的速度是行板。
Cody:  Is that better? 这样好些了吗？
Charles:  Yes, much better. Watch where you lift your foot off the pedal. What was that? 是的，好多了。注意抬脚离开踏板的位置。那是什么声音？
Cody:  Sorry! The stretch for that octave is always hard to make. 抱歉！那个八度的跨度总是很难够到。
Charles:  That's ok, keep going, you're moving ahead by leaps and bounds . Watch your dynamics! Keep your elbows lifted. Remember to stroke the keys, don't pound. That's better! Remember that as a pianist or any other musician, your technique will be what separates you from the pack just as much or more so as your musicianship. 没关系，继续练吧，你进步飞快。注意强弱变化！保持手肘抬起。记得要抚摸琴键，不要砸下去。好多了！记住啊，无论是钢琴家还是其他音乐家，你的技巧和你作为音乐家的素养一样重要，甚至更为关键，正是它让你脱颖而出。
