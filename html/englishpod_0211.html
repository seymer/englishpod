The Weekend - Playing Board Games (C0211)

Jim:  Hey- Why did you take that money? You are such a cheater! I should send you to jail! 嘿，你为什么拿走那钱？你真是个骗子！我应该把你送进监狱！
Karen:  I am not cheating. When you pass go, you collect $200, Everyone knows that! 我没作弊。当你经过“开始”格时，你可以领取 200 美元，大家都知道的！
Jim:  Well you can’t just take the money. You have to ask the bank for money. And I’m the banker. 好吧，你不能直接拿钱。你得向银行家要钱。而我是银行家。
Karen:  Banker? 银行家？
Jim:  Yes… 是的……
Karen:  Can I have my $200 please? 请问我可以领我的 200 美元吗？
Jim:  Sure. Here you are, $200, Thank you, please come again! Now it's my turn to roll the dice. 当然可以。给你，200 美元，谢谢，欢迎下次再来！现在轮到我掷骰子了。
