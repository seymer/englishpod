Daily Life - New Furniture (C0220)

A:  How about this floor lamp? 这个落地灯怎么样？
B:  Fine just get it! We have been shopping for furniture for five hours! I'm so tired! 行，就买它吧！我们已经逛家具店五个小时了，累死了！
A:  We still need to find an armoire and a dresser. 我们还得找个衣柜和梳妆台。
B:  Fine! I am going to go home and drop off this nightstand, coffee table and love seat while you look for the rest of the things. 好吧！我要回家把这床头柜、咖啡桌和双人沙发放下，你去把剩下的东西找齐。
A:  Great! Pick me up in about an hour because I think I'll also get a bean bag and a dining set. 太好了！大约一小时后来接我，我想我也许会买个懒人豆袋和一套餐桌椅。
B:  While you are at it can you pick out a nice recliner? I really want one so I can watch TV. 顺便帮我挑个舒适的躺椅吧？我真的想要一个，好让我看电视。
A:  Recliner? In my beautifully decorated living room? I don't think so! 躺椅？在我装修精美的客厅里？我才不要呢！

Key Vocabulary

floor lamp落地灯a tall lamp with a base that stands on the floor一盏高高的灯，底座立在地板上
armoire大衣柜a large wardrobe or cabinet一个大衣柜或橱柜
dresser抽屉柜a set of shelves usually used for holding clothes一组通常用来存放衣服的架子
nightstand床头柜a small table or stand placed at a bedside放在床边的小桌子或架子
love seat双人沙发small sofa that seats two people可以坐两个人的小沙发
recliner躺椅an armchair that can be reclined可以倾斜的扶手椅
while you are at it顺便during the time you are there当你在那里的时候
