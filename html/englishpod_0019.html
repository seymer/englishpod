The Weekend - Christmas Chronicles I(0019)

A:  I hate working on Christmas Eve! Whoa! Get a load of this guy! Come in central, I think we've got ourselves a situation here.我讨厌在平安夜上班！哇哦，看看这家伙！中央台，我觉得我们这儿出事了。
B:  License and registration please. Have you been drinking tonight, sir?请出示驾照和行驶证。今晚喝过酒吗，先生？
A:  I had one or two glasses of eggnog, but nothing else.我只喝了一两杯蛋奶酒，没别的了。
B:  Step out of the vehicle, please. Sir, what do you have in the back?请下车。先生，你车后座装的是什么？
A:  Just a few Christmas gifts, 'tis the season, after all!就是一些圣诞礼物，毕竟现在是过节嘛！
B:  Don't take that tone with me. Do you have an invoice for these items?别跟我摆这个脸色。这些东西有发票吗？
A:  Umm...no...I make these in my workshop in the North Pole!呃……没有……我在北极的工作室自己做的！
B:  You are under arrest, sir. You have the right to remain silent. You better not pout, you better not cry. Anything you say can and will be used against you. You have the right to an attorney; if you cannot afford one, the state will appoint你被捕了，先生。你有权保持沉默。别撇嘴也别哭，你说的话都可以并且会被用来对付你。你有权请律师；如果你请不起，国家会为你指派
A:  You can't take me to jail! What about my sleigh? It's Christmas Eve! I have presents to deliver! Rudolph! Prancer! Dancer! Get help!你不能把我送进监狱！我的雪橇怎么办？今天是平安夜！我有礼物要派送！鲁道夫！普朗瑟！丹瑟！来人帮忙！
