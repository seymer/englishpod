Daily Life - Coins and Money (C0356)

A:  Help me organize these coins. 帮我整理一下这些硬币。
B:  That’s a lot of money! What did you do? Break the piggy bank? 这么多钱！你干嘛了？把存钱罐砸了吧？
A:  Yeah, I’m gonna go to the bank and change it for bills, but first I have to separate them into little piles. 对，我打算去银行把它们换成纸币，但首先得把它们分成小堆。
B:  Ok, I'll find all the quarters and dimes while you sort the nickels and pennies. 好的，我去找所有的 25 分和 10 分硬币，你负责分拣 5 分和 1 分硬币。
A:  Great, then we can add everything up and take it to the bank. 太好了，这样我们就能算出总数然后去银行了。
B:  I found some coins that are not from here. 我发现了一些不是本地的硬币。
A:  Oh yeah, those are from my trip to London. I have a couple of different pence, but in all it won't add up to one pound. 哦对，那些是我去伦敦旅行时带的。我有几种不同的便士，但加起来也不够一英镑。
B:  Are you sure the bank will change these coins for you? 你确定银行会帮你兑换这些硬币吗？
A:  Hopefully! 希望能行！
