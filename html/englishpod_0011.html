Daily Life - New Guy in Town(0011)

A:  Oh, I don't know if you heard, but someone moved into that old house down the road.哦，我不知道你们有没有听说，有人搬进了那条路上的那栋老房子。
B:  Yeah, I know. I met the owner of the house yesterday as he was moving in. His name is Armand.对，我知道。昨天我碰巧遇到了房主，他当时正在搬家。他叫阿曼德。
A:  Really? What's he like? You have to fill me in.真的吗？他是个什么样的人？你得给我讲讲。
B:  Actually, he's a bit strange. I don't know... I've got a bad feeling about him.其实，他有点怪怪的。我不知道……我对他有种不好的预感。
A:  Really? Why?真的吗？为什么？
B:  Well, yesterday I brought over a housewarming gift, but Armand started acting really weird, and then he practically kicked me out! I tried to, sort of, peek into his house, but everything was so dark inside that I couldn't really get a good look. 嗯，昨天我带了一份乔迁礼物过去，但阿曼德开始表现得非常奇怪，然后几乎把我赶出去了！我想稍微往屋里瞅一眼，可里面太黑了，根本看不清。
A:  Well, you'll never guess what I saw this morning. A delivery truck pulled into his driveway, and it dropped off a long, rectangular box. It almost looked like a coffin!好吧，你绝对猜不到我今天早上看到了什么。一辆送货卡车开进了他的车道，放下一个长长的矩形箱子。那简直像个棺材！
B:  You see! Why would he...你看！他怎么会……
C:  Hello ladies...各位女士……
B:  Ah, Armand! You scared the heck out of me! This is my friend Doris.啊，阿曼德！你吓死我了！这位是我的朋友多丽丝。
C:  A pleasure to meet you...If you are not doing anything tonight, I would like to have you both for dinner. I mean...I would like to have you both over for dinner.很高兴见到你们……如果今晚没什么安排的话，我想请你们两个一起吃晚饭。我是说……我想请你们来家里吃晚饭。
