Daily Life - Working Out (C0345)

A:  Do you want to go catch a movie tonight? 今晚想去看电影吗？
B:  I can’t, I have to go tothe gym. 不行，我得去健身房。
A:  Come on! You can go tomorrow, just skip it today. It’s not as if you are gonna get in trouble! 拜托！你可以明天再去，今天跳过算了。又不是会惹上麻烦！
B:  Actually I will! I am working out with a personal trainer that gets on my case if I don’t go. I like it, because it makes me feel more obligated to go and get healthy. 其实我会去的！我找了个私人教练，他要是发现我没去就会找我麻烦。我喜欢这种方式，因为这让我觉得更有责任去锻炼、保持健康。
A:  That’s cool, does your personal trainer basically teach you how to work out? 挺酷的，你的私人教练主要是教你怎么锻炼吗？
B:  Yeah. He makes a work put plan depending onthe areas I want to work on, orthe muscles I want to build. Like for example in order to get better muscle tone in my abs, pecs and biceps, he makes me work out with free weights. Then for my quads, calves and hamstrings, I do leg lifts or squats. 是的。他会根据我想锻炼的部位或者想增强的肌肉制定计划。比如为了增强腹肌、胸肌和二头肌的线条，他会让我用自由重量训练。至于大腿前侧、小腿和三头肌，我就做抬腿或深蹲。
A:  Sounds like you are really getting in shape! 听起来你身材练得真好！
