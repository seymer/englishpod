Global View - Thanksgiving Dinner (C0324)

A:  So what are you doing for Thanksgiving? 感恩节你打算做什么？
B:  Not much really. It’s more of an American tradition, so back home we don’t really celebrate it. In fact, I am not even sure of what exactly is being celebrated! 没什么特别的。这更多是个美国传统，所以在我们老家并不怎么庆祝。事实上，我甚至不太清楚到底在庆祝什么！
A:  Well you know, it’s a time to get together with all your family and be thankful for everything! 嗯，你知道的，这是个和家人团聚、感恩一切的好时机！
B:  Yeah but, how did this holiday come to be? 是啊，但这个节日是怎么来的呢？
A:  Well, the first settlers of Massachusetts arrived there because of religious persecution from England and King James. Once inthe New World, they befriended an native named Squanto, who taught them how to harvest food from the area such as corn. 嗯，马萨诸塞州的第一批定居者因为受到英格兰和詹姆斯国王的宗教迫害而迁居至此。到了新世界后，他们结识了一位名叫斯夸托的土著朋友，他教他们如何采集当地的食物，比如玉米。
B:  Interesting! I am amazed how big and delicious thanksgiving dinners are! 真有意思！我对感恩节大餐的丰盛和美味惊叹不已！
A:  Come to my house for Thanksgiving! We are having turkey, pumpkin pie, mashed potatoes with gravy, and lots of stuffing! 来我家过感恩节吧！我们准备了火鸡、南瓜派、肉汁土豆泥，还有满满的填料！
B:  Count me in! 算我一个！
