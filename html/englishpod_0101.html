The Weekend - 1970's(0101)

A:  Hey man! How's it hanging?嘿，兄弟！最近怎么样？
B:  Hey man! Everything is just groovy baby!嘿，兄弟！一切都棒极了，宝贝！
A:  Did you go to the roller rink on Saturday? I heard it was far out, man!你周六去溜冰场了吗？听说那儿特酷，兄弟！
B:  I wanted to, but I ran into this foxy lady that just moved to my block! I was chatting her up a bit and then we mellowed out at her place.我想去的，但我在路上碰见一位刚搬到我街区的美女。我聊了几句，然后我们就到她家放松了一下。
A:  Right on, right on! Well, Jim went to the rink with Sherry and he said it was dy-no-mite! He was low on bread, but Sherry paid for everything.太棒了，太棒了！吉姆和雪莉去了溜冰场，他说那里简直绝了！他兜里没钱，但雪莉付了所有账。
B:  Gravy! Jim is such a jive turkey man. He is always hitting me up for cash. Anyway, you wanna book and go grab some grub?真爽！吉姆真是个爱占便宜的家伙。他总是找我借钱。对了，你想约个时间出去吃点东西吗？
A:  Yeah man, I'm starving!好啊兄弟，我饿死了！
