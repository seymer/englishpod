Daily Life - Online Dating (C0300)

A:  Do you want to hang out tomorrow? 明天有空出来聚聚吗？
B:  Oh, I can’t. I have a date! 哦，不行。我有约会了！
A:  Really? Wow with who? 真的？哇，和谁啊？
B:  This girl I’ve been chatting with forthe past couple of months. She’s really cool and she’s driving over here this weekend. 是我过去几个月一直在聊天的那个女孩。她特别酷，这周末会开车过来找我。
A:  Wait a minute, you mean you met her online? 等等，你的意思是你们是在网上认识的？
B:  Yeah! I signed up for a website called match. and it is great! You fill in all your details and preferences, like if you are a smoker or if you have any pets. Then you find people that have similar characteristics and you can email them or chat. 对！我在一个叫 Match 的网站注册了，效果超棒！你填完所有个人信息和偏好，比如是否抽烟、有没有宠物之类的。然后系统会帮你找到特征相似的人，你可以发邮件或者聊天。
A:  That is kind of weird! What if she is a psycho or something like that? 那感觉有点怪啊！万一她是疯子之类的怎么办？
B:  It’s the same as meeting people anywhere and dating them! I am just tired of going to bars or being set up for dates by my friends! I think this is a really cool alternative, especially if you are a bit shy. 这和在任何地方认识人、约会没什么区别嘛！我就是厌倦去酒吧或者被朋友安排相亲了！我觉得这是个特别棒的替代方案，尤其是如果你有点害羞的话。
A:  I guess it does seem logical. I’ll have to check it out! 听起来确实挺合理的。我得去看看！
