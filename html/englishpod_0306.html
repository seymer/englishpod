The Weekend - Pick Up Lines (C0306)

A:  Let’s got out tomorrow night. We can go to a bar and try to find you a girlfriend.明天晚上我们出去吧。我们可以去酒吧，帮你找个女朋友。
B:  I don’t think that’s a good idea. I am just not good with approaching someone and starting up a conversation.我觉得这主意不好。我不擅长搭讪和开启对话。
A:  Maybe you just need a few pick up lines,you know, break the ice. 也许你只需要几句“搭讪用语”，你知道的，用来打破僵局。
B:  Pick up lines don’t work!搭讪用语不管用！
A:  Come on! You can just walk up to a girl and say: “If you were a booger I'd pick you first.”拜托！你直接走到一个女孩面前说：“如果你是个鼻屎，我会先选你。”
B:  What? Come on! That’s just lame! No girl would fall for that!什么？拜托！这太烂了！没有哪个女孩会吃这一套！
A:  Fine, then you can say: “So there you are! I've been looking all over for YOU, the woman of my dreams!”好吧，那你可以说：“原来是你啊！我一直在到处找你，我的梦中情人！”
B:  That’s a good one! I think that’s pretty funny.这个不错！我觉得挺有趣的。
A:  Yeah, so you make her laugh, you make a fool of yourself a little bit and then you buy her a drink.对，让她笑一笑，稍微出个丑，然后请她喝杯东西。
B:  Ok, how does this sound: “I was so enchanted by your beauty that I ran into that wall over there. So I am going to need your name and number for insurance purposes.”好的，这句怎么样：“你的美貌让我如此着迷，以至于我撞到了那边的墙。所以我需要你的名字和号码用于保险目的。”
A:  Nice! Let’s go!不错！我们走吧！
