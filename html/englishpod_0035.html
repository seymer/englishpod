Daily Life - He's not a Good Fit(0035)

A:  So, Lauren, I just wanted to talk to you quickly about our new customer support representative, Jason Huntley.所以，劳伦，我想跟你快速聊聊我们的新客服代表杰森·亨特利。
B:  Sure, what's up?好的，怎么了？
A:  Basically, I've got a few concerns about him, and the bottom line is, I don't think he's a good fit for our company.基本上，我对他有些顾虑，归根结底，我觉得他不适合我们公司。
B:  Okay... what makes you say that? I thought you were pleased with his overall performance. Didn't you just tell me last week how impressed you were with his attitude?好吧……你怎么会这么觉得？我以为你对他的整体表现很满意啊。上周你不是还告诉我，对他的态度印象深刻吗？
A:  Yeah, his attitude is great, but he's really unreliable. Sometimes he's really productive, but then other times... take last Tuesday for instance, he was forty-five minutes late for our morning meeting!是啊，他的态度确实不错，但他真的很不可靠。有时候他很高效，但其他时候……比如上星期二，我们的晨会他迟到了四十五分钟！
B:  Well, I'm sure he had a perfectly good reason...嗯，我相信他肯定有正当理由……
A:  But that's not the only thing... you know, he really doesn't have the best work ethic, I'm constantly catching him on MSN and Facebook when he should be talking to clients.但这还不是唯一的问题……你知道的，他的职业道德也不怎么样，我老是发现他在该跟客户沟通的时候，却在 MSN 和 Facebook 上聊天。
B:  Yeah, but come on, Geoff, as if you don't check Facebook at work. Look, you hired this guy, we've invested a lot of time and money in his training, so now it's up to you to coach him. Make it work, Geoff!得了吧，杰夫，你又不是没在工作时间刷 Facebook。听着，是你招的他，我们花了很多时间和金钱培训他，所以现在得靠你来带他。让他适应工作，杰夫！
A:  Make it work, Geoff. You would say that, wouldn't you, he is your cousin; what a jerk, make me hire your stupid, useless, cousin.让他适应工作，杰夫。你会这么说，对吧？毕竟他是你的表亲；真是个混蛋，逼我招你这个愚蠢又无用的表亲。
