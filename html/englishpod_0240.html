Daily Life - Getting A Pet (C0240)

A:  We have been over this a hundred times ! We are not getting a pet! 我们说过一百遍了！我们不养宠物！
B:  Why not? Come on! Just a cute little puppy. or a kitty! 为什么不行？拜托啦！就是一只可爱的小狗狗或者小猫！
A:  Who is going to look after a dog or a cat? 谁来照顾狗或猫呢？
B:  I will! I'll feed it, bathe it and walk it every day! We can get a Labrador or a German Shepard ! 我来！我会每天喂它、给它洗澡、带它散步！我们可以养拉布拉多或者德国牧羊犬！
A:  What if we want to take a vacation ? Who will we leave it with? Plus, our apartment is too small for that breed of dog. 如果我们想度假怎么办？把它托付给谁呢？而且，我们的公寓太小了，不适合那种大型犬。
B:  Ok. How about we get a cat or a ferret! 好吧。那我们养只猫或者雪貂怎么样？
A:  We're planning on having children soon, I don't think those animals are a good idea with a baby in the house. 我们计划很快要生孩子了，我觉得家里有婴儿的话，养这些动物不太合适。
B:  Fine! Let's get a bird then! We can keep it in its cage and teach it to talk! A parrot would be awesome! 行吧！那我们就养只鸟吧！我们可以把它关在笼子里，教它说话！鹦鹉太棒了！
A:  I'll tell you what, I can get you some hamsters and we'll take it from there . 我跟你说，我可以给你买几只仓鼠，我们再从那里开始考虑。
B:  Yay! 万岁！
