Global View - Baby Shower (C0219)

A:  Thank you for organizing this great baby shower for me! I've always been to baby showers but never actually had one held for me! Let's get started! 谢谢你为我举办这么棒的婴儿派对！我一直都参加过别人的婴儿派对，却从未有人专门为我办过！我们开始吧！
B:  Ok, let's start opening some presents! 好，我们先拆开一些礼物！
A:  Oh look! What a great little bib for the baby! This will definitely come in handy! Oh wow, you also got me a stroller! That's so great! Thank you! 哦，看！这是给宝宝的超可爱的小围嘴！这绝对派得上用场！哇，你还送了我一辆婴儿车！太棒了！谢谢你！
B:  This next one is from Betty. 下一个是贝蒂送的。
A:  A highchair and car seat! Wow Betty thank you so much! I really appreciate it! 一把高脚餐椅和一个汽车安全座椅！哇，贝蒂，非常感谢你！我真的很感激！
B:  One more from Carla. 还有一个是卡拉送的。
A:  A playpen and crib! Thanks Carla! This is just what I needed! 一个游戏围栏和一张婴儿床！谢谢卡拉！这正是我需要的！
B:  OK, that's all of them. No more gifts. Now who wants to guess when the baby is due! 好了，礼物都拆完了。没有更多礼物了。现在谁想猜猜宝宝什么时候预产？
A:  Umm. I think my water just broke! Get me to a hospital! 嗯……我觉得我的羊水破了！快带我去医院！

crib婴儿床baby bed with high sides高边婴儿床
