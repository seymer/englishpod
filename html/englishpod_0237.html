Daily Life - Morning Routine (C0237)

Jacob:  Stephanie! Did you just get to school? But you were up and about when I left the dorm this morning! That was about an hour and a half ago. This happens all the time! Why do you always take so long to get ready the morning? Stephanie! 你刚到学校吗？可我今天早上离开宿舍时你已经出门了！那还是大约一个半小时前呢。这种事总是发生！为什么你每天早上准备要花这么长时间？
Stephanie:  It's a skill. What can I say? I don't know why, I just have a long routine. 这是个技巧。我能说什么呢？我也不知道为什么，我的流程就是很长。
Jacob:  Please explain because it makes no sense to me. How can a girl's routine be so complicated? You get up , you shower, you get dressed , you brush your teeth, you're out the door. Half an hour, tops. 请解释一下，因为这对我来说毫无道理。女生的流程怎么能这么复杂？起床、洗澡、穿衣、刷牙，然后出门。顶多半小时。
Stephanie:  Jacob, you have the luxury of having a haircut that rarely needs styling. I don't. I have to set aside about an hour and a half to get ready in the mornings. Every day, I wake up and head straight for the shower. Every second day , I wash my hair. If it's a hair-washing day, I frequently need to wash my hair twice because it gets really oily. Then I usually put in a conditioner and have to rinse that out too. Because my hair is so long, I seldom manage to take a shower in under twenty minutes. Afterwards, I often put on a pot of coffee and get dressed while I wait for it to brew. I take a long time to get dressed in the morning. Every now and then I remember to choose my outfit the night before , but usually I do it in the morning. In all , getting dressed takes about half an hour , at which time my hair is now semi-dry so then I have to style my hair. From time to time I'll put my hair up, but oftentimes I blow-dry it straight. And then, because of the texture of my hair, I regularly have to flat-iron it to keep it from frizzing. That's another twenty minutes or so. After that, I have my daily makeup routine. Jacob，你拥有无需频繁打理的发型，真是令人羡慕。我可没有这待遇。每天早上我得预留一个半小时来准备出门。每天醒来，我直奔浴室。隔天洗一次头发。如果是洗头日，我常常得洗两遍，因为头发特别油。接着我会涂上护发素，还得冲洗干净。由于我的头发很长，很少能在二十分钟内洗完澡。之后，我通常会煮一壶咖啡，一边等它冲泡好，一边穿衣服。早上穿衣花的时间很长。偶尔我会记得前一天晚上选好衣服，但通常都是当天早上才弄。总的来说，穿衣大约要花半小时，这时候我的头发半干了，于是就得开始打理发型。时不时我会把头发盘起来，但更多时候是用吹风机拉直。然后，由于发质原因，我不得不定期用直发夹板夹一下，防止毛躁。那又得花个二十分钟左右。之后，我就开始日常的化妆程序了。
Jacob:  True, I hardly ever see you without your hair done and your makeup on, even when you show up to class in sweatpants. Tell me, how long does it take you to choose that outfit in the morning? 没错，我几乎没见过你不化妆、不整理头发就出门的样子，哪怕你穿运动裤来上课。告诉我，早上挑那身衣服要花多久？
Stephanie:  Not funny. 真没劲。
