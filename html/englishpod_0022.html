The Weekend - Christmas Chronicles II(0022)

A:  Really, gentlemen, you can't take me to jail! Don't you know who I am? Kris Kringle, you know, Papa Noel, Pere Noel, Babbo Natale, sheng dan lao ren!真的，各位先生，你们不能把我送进监狱！难道你们不知道我是谁吗？我是圣诞老公公，也就是圣诞老人、圣尼古拉、巴博·纳塔莱、圣诞老人！
B:  Yeah, Yeah, we've heard that one before, haven't we Joe?是啊，是啊，我们以前听过这个说法了，对吧，乔？
C:  Yeah, last week we booked this guy who claimed to be the tooth fairy! Can you believe that?是啊，上周我们就逮捕了一个自称是牙仙子的人！你相信吗？
A:  It's Christmas Eve and I have all these presents to deliver! Where is your Christmas spirit? What will happen when all the children wake up tomorrow and don't find any gifts in their stockings?今天是平安夜，我还有这么多礼物要送呢！你们的圣诞精神去哪了？明天早上孩子们醒来发现袜子里没有礼物怎么办？
B:  Sorry buddy, you were parked in a no-parking zone, you were speeding, and you have no ID!抱歉朋友，你停在禁止停车区，超速行驶，而且你没有身份证明！
C:  Besides that, even if we let you go now, your sleigh has been impounded and those reindeer were taken to the city zoo.除此之外，就算我们现在放你走，你的雪橇已经被扣押了，那些驯鹿也被送到市动物园去了。
A:  What! This is unbelievable! What's this world coming to? Christmas is ruined!什么！这简直不可思议！这个世界变成什么样了？圣诞节毁了！
C:  What's that up ahead? It looks like... elves!! Elves!! Whoa, they're shooting candy canes! Mayday, Mayday, we are under heavy attack! We need backup!前面那是什么？看起来像是……精灵！！精灵！！哇，他们在发射拐杖糖！救命，救命，我们遭到猛烈攻击！我们需要支援！
