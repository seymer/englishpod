The Weekend - Gardening (C0230)

A:  I've decided to grow my own garden! 我决定自己种花园！
B:  What? You don't know the first thing about gardening! 什么？你连园艺的第一件事都不知道！
A:  On the contrary, I have been reading a lot of books about the subject. 恰恰相反，我读了很多关于这个主题的书。
B:  Oh yeah? Tell me then, smarty pants, how will you go about setting up your garden? 哦是吗？那你倒是说说，聪明鬼，你打算怎么布置你的花园？
A:  Well, first I need to buy some things, such as fertilizer, seeds and tools. 嗯，首先我得买些东西，比如肥料、种子和工具。
B:  What type of tools? 什么类型的工具？
A:  You know, the basics. A rake, shovel, spade and a hoe. 你知道的，基础款。耙子、铲子、锄头和锄头。
B:  Right. Well it seems like you have all your bases covered . What's next? 对。看来你已经万事俱备了。接下来呢？
A:  I'll till the soil and then sow the seeds. I'll then add some fertilizer and voila! Gardening all done! 我会翻土然后播种。接着加些肥料，搞定！园艺全部完成！
B:  Well, good luck with your garden, especially considering we are inthe dry season and it won't rain for the next three months! 祝你的花园好运，尤其是考虑到我们现在处于旱季，未来三个月都不会下雨！
