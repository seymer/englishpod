Daily Life - Hotel Upgrade(0003)

A:  Good afternoon. What can I do for you?下午好，请问有什么可以帮您？
B:  I'd like to check in please. I have a reservation under the name Anthony Roberts.我想办理入住，我的预订是以安东尼·罗伯茨的名字登记的。
A:  All right R.O.B.E.R.T.S... Oh, Mr. Roberts we've been expecting you… and here is your keycard to the presidential suite.好的，R.O.B.E.R.T.S……哦，罗伯茨先生，我们一直在等您……这是您的总统套房房卡。
B:  But there must be some mistake; my reservation was for a standard room.但这肯定有误会吧，我预订的是标准间。
A:  Are you sure? Let me double check .您确定吗？让我再核实一下。
B:  Yeah…Here, this is my confirmation number.是的……这里是我的确认号码。
A:  You're right Mr. Roberts, there seems to be a mix-up, unfortunately we're overbooked at the moment .您说得对，罗伯茨先生，看来确实出了点差错，不过目前我们房间超售了。
B:  So…那……
A:  Not to worry. We're pleased to offer you a complimentary upgrade.别担心，我们很乐意为您提供免费升级。
B:  Presidential suite baby!总统套房走起！
