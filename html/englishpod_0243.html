The Weekend - Talking to a Travel Agent (C0243)

A:  Welcome to Perfect Getaway Tours . How can I help you? 欢迎光临完美度假旅行社，请问有什么可以帮您的？
B:  I would like to plan a surprise getaway for me and my wife. 我想为我和我妻子策划一次惊喜的短途旅行。
A:  Very well, we have a couple of different options such as beaches, the wilderness, the countryside or even going to a spa for the weekend. 好的，我们提供几种不同的选择，比如海滩、荒野、乡村，或者去度周末水疗。
B:  I think something in the countryside would be nice. 我觉得去乡村会不错。
A:  Perfect! This package includes round-trip flights to New Hampshire . A free airport pick- up is included. Our VIP limousine will pick you up and provide you with complimentary champagne and finger foods to soften the thirty-minute ride to the countryside. 太好了！这个套餐包含往返新罕布什尔州的航班。我们还提供免费机场接送服务。我们的 VIP 豪华轿车将接您前往目的地，并为您奉上免费香槟和小吃，让您在三十分钟的旅途中轻松惬意。
B:  Sounds good! What is the hotel that we will be staying at like? 听起来不错！我们要住的酒店是什么样的？
A:  That is the best part. Your hotel is actually an old country villa that has been restored and refurbished to accommodate a maximum of that is guests. You will enjoy an intimate and private time in this very spacious and warm N Included in the price is three meals a day, excluding beverages. You can choose to eat at the fabulous restaurant that offers a stunning view of the lush, green gardens. If you prefer, your own private butler can arrange your meal to be served in your room or outside on our terrace. 这是最棒的部分。您的酒店实际上是一座经过修复和翻新的古老乡村别墅，最多可容纳一定数量的客人（原文此处有缺失，翻译时保留语意连贯）。您将在这宽敞温馨的别墅中享受私密而亲密的时光。价格包含每日三餐，不含饮料。您可以选择在风景绝佳的餐厅用餐，那里拥有郁郁葱葱花园的壮丽景色；或者如果您更喜欢，您的专属管家可以安排您在房间或我们的露台上享用美食。
B:  Wow! This sounds like something my wife would really enjoy! Are there any outdoor activities we can take part in ? 哇！这听起来我妻子一定会非常喜欢！我们可以参加哪些户外活动呢？
A:  Of course! The hotel has a stable with beautiful stallions for a very romantic horseback ride along the country trail. You can also go fishing to the nearby lake or visit the local vineyard. 当然！酒店有一片马厩，养着漂亮的骏马，可以沿着乡间小径进行非常浪漫的骑马之旅。您还可以去附近的湖泊钓鱼，或者参观当地的葡萄园。
B:  I'm sold ! I want to book this trip. I don't care what it costs! Money is no object ! 我心动了！我想预订这次旅行。不管花多少钱我都无所谓！钱不是问题！
