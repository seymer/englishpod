Daily Life - Asking For A Loan (C0165)

B:  Hello Sir may I help you? 您好，有什么可以帮您？
A:  Yes. I would like some information for requesting a loan. 是的，我想咨询一下贷款申请的相关信息。
B:  Very well, here arethe general termsof our loan policies. We pride ourselves in having the lowest interest rate inthe country for personal loans. 好的，这是我们的贷款政策概要。我们在个人贷款利率方面以全国最低利率为傲。
A:  I see. So let me get this straight. If I borrow let's say, ten thousand dollars, how much will I have to pay each month? 我明白了。让我确认一下：如果我借一万美元，每个月需要还多少钱？
B:  It depends on how long you take to pay it back. If we lend you ten thousand dollars at an annual interest rate of ten percent for forty eight months, you would have to pay each month a portion ofthe loan which is called the principal and another small portion ofthe annual interest rate. This of course is considering that you don't default on a payment! 这取决于您还款的期限。如果我们以年利率 10% 借给您一万美元，分 48 个月偿还，您每月需偿还一部分本金和一小部分年度利息。当然，前提是您不会违约！
A:  It sounds good but, there is just one problem. I have a terrible credit score. 听起来不错，但有个问题：我的信用评分很差。
B:  That is a very serious problem you see, the bank must assess your personal information, past loans, assets and any other relevant information such as your credit scorein order to approve your loan. 这是个很严重的问题，您看，银行必须评估您的个人信息、过往贷款记录、资产以及其他相关信息（如信用评分），才能批准您的贷款。
A:  You know what? I don't really need the money. Thanks anyways! 您知道吗？其实我并不需要这笔钱。还是谢谢您了！
