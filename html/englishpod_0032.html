Daily Life - Opening a Bank Account(0032)

A:  Next, please. May I help you, sir?接下来请。先生，有什么可以帮您？
B:  Hello, yes, I'd like to open a bank account.你好，我想开个银行账户。
A:  Certainly, I can can help you with that. What type of account would you like to open? A chequing or a savings account?当然，我可以帮您办理。您想开哪种账户？支票账户还是储蓄账户？
B:  What What features do they offer?它们都提供哪些功能呢？
A:  Well, if you just take a look here, see, with our chequing account, you can have unlimited daily transactions for a small monthly fee, and our savings account has a higher interest rate, but you must carry a minimum balance of $ 10,000 dollars.嗯，您看这里，我们的支票账户每天交易次数不限，只需支付少量月费；而我们的储蓄账户利率更高，但必须保持最低余额 10,000 美元。
B:  I see, well, I think I'm more interested in a chequing account; I like to have easy access to my money.明白了，我觉得我更倾向于支票账户，我喜欢随时能取到钱。
A:  Alright, then, with this chequing account you'll be issued a debit card and a cheque book. Will you require overdraft protection? There is an extra fee for that.好的，那么办理这个支票账户会为您发放借记卡和支票簿。您需要超额透支保护吗？那需要额外收费。
B:  No, that won't be necessary.不用了，不需要。
A:  In that case, I'll get you to fill out this paperwork; I'll need your social insurance number, and two pieces of government ID. If you could just sign here, and here, and here; we'll be all set. Would you like to make a deposit today?既然如此，请填一下这些表格；我需要您的社保号以及两份政府身份证件。您在这里、这里和这里签个字就行，这样就办好了。今天想存入一笔钱吗？
B:  Yes, I'd like to deposit one billion dollars.好的，我想存一亿美元。
