The Weekend - What Do I Wear? (0142)

A:  Honey come on! We are going to be late! Honestly, you take longer getting ready than I do!亲爱的，快点！我们要迟到了！说真的，你打扮的时间比我长多了！
B:  I was drying my hair and ironing my shirt! Can you come here for a sec? I need your help.我在吹头发、熨衬衫呢！你能过来一下吗？我需要你的帮忙。
A:  What is it? Why are all these clothes on the bed?怎么了？为什么床上全是衣服？
B:  I don't know what to wear! Ok, give me your opinion. Do you like the way this looks? The striped short sleeved shirt with this checkered sweater and my lucky sandals. I like the cut and hemline of these shorts so I think I'll wear these as well.我不知道穿什么！好吧，给我出个主意。你觉得这样好看吗？条纹短袖配格子毛衣，还有我最爱的凉鞋。我喜欢这条短裤的剪裁和裙摆，所以我也打算穿它。
A:  Are you joking? What am I going to do with you? We are going to a dinner party not the beach! Wear the shirt with the silk tie I bought you and these corduroy pants. It's chilly outside so you can wear this coat.你在开玩笑吧？我拿你怎么办？我们要去参加晚宴，不是去海滩！穿上我给你买的丝绸领带衬衫，再配上灯芯绒裤。外面冷，你就把这件外套穿上。
B:  Thanks honey! You have such great fashion sense. Now, what am I going to do with my hair?谢谢亲爱的！你的时尚感真棒。那我的头发该怎么办呢？
