Daily Life - Paranoid (C0148)

A:  Dan, Dan dude. You have to come over to my house right now! Dan，Dan 兄弟。你现在必须马上过来！
B:  Is everything Ok? 一切还好吗？
A:  Just get over here! 赶紧过来！
A:  Come in! Quickly! 快进来！快点！
B:  So, since when is your house a bank? 所以，什么时候你家变成银行了？
A:  What do you mean? 你是说真的？
B:  I mean, what's up with the and locks and iron bars on your windows. 我是说，你的窗户上怎么装了那么多锁和铁栏杆。
A:  Security Dan, security! You can never be too safe you know! A lot of sickos out there. Just the other day they caught that peeping tom red handed! Had a high power telescope and binoculars by his window. 安全啊，丹，安全！你永远不能太有安全感，你知道的！外面到处都是变态。就在前几天，他们当场抓住了那个偷窥狂！他窗边放着高倍望远镜和双筒望远镜。
B:  Whats the matter with you? Why are you acting all paranoid? 你到底怎么了？为什么表现得这么多疑？
A:  Paranoid? I'm not paranoid! I'm cautious! You see Dan, we have to be on guard at all time! People just invade your privacy as if they knew you! Telemarketers, solicitors, even your bank! They have way too much information! I like to keep everything on a need to know basis 多疑？我才不多疑！我很谨慎！你看，丹，我们必须时刻警惕！人们好像认识你一样随意侵犯你的隐私！推销员、律师，甚至你的银行！他们掌握的信息太多了！我喜欢把一切都控制在“需知”的基础上
B:  OK, well, what did you want to see me about? 好吧，你想见我有什么事？
A:  You are being watched! Be careful Dan! Be careful! 你被监视了！小心点，丹！小心！

Key Vocabulary

sicko变态A deranged, psychotic, or morbidly obsessed person精神错乱、精神病或病态痴迷的人
red handed当场被抓doing something wrong or illegal做错事或违法的事
invade侵入to affect in an unwanted way以不受欢迎的方式影响
paranoid偏执having an feeling that people will harm you感觉人们会伤害你
need to know basis按需知密situation where you have only the information you need您仅拥有所需信息的情况
