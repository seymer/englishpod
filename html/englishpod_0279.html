Global View - Drugs (C0279)

A:  Hey man, you wanna buy some weed? 嘿兄弟，想买点大麻吗？
B:  Some what? 什么玩意儿？
A:  Weed! You know? Pot, Ganja, Mary Jane some chronic! 大麻！你懂的，就是那啥，高草，玛丽珍，还有那种常备的！
B:  Oh, umm, no thanks. 哦，呃，不用了。
A:  I also have blow if you prefer to do a few lines. 我还有一吹，如果你更喜欢抽几根的话。
B:  No, I am ok, really. 不，我没事，真的。
A:  Come on man! I even got dope and acid! Try some! 拜托啦兄弟！我还有海洛因和 LSD 呢！来点试试！
B:  Do you really have all of these drugs? Where do you get them from? 你真有所有这些毒品？你从哪儿弄来的？
A:  I got my connections! Just tell me what you want and I’ll even give you one ounce for free. 我有路子！只要告诉我你想要什么，我甚至免费给你一盎司。
B:  Sounds good! Let’s see, I want. 听起来不错！让我想想，我想要……
A:  Yeah? 什么？
B:  I want you to put your hands behind your head! You are under arrest! 把双手抱头！你被捕了！
