The Office - Job Interview II(0062)

A:  Thanks for coming in today, did you have any trouble finding us? Please take a seat.今天谢谢你来，路上没遇到什么麻烦吧？请坐。
B:  Thank you.谢谢。
A:  So, let's get started; tell me a bit about your educational background.那我们就开始吧，先跟我说说你的教育背景。
B:  Sure! Well, I graduated with honors from Chesterton University with a major in Business Administration, with a specialization in Information Management, and I minored in psychology. I chose this course of study for two reasons: I wanted to gain some practical, marketable skills, which the information management track provided, and I also feel that interpersonal skills are essential for professional success, hence the minor in psychology.好的！我从切斯特顿大学毕业，成绩优异，主修工商管理，专攻信息管理，辅修心理学。我选择这个专业有两个原因：一是我想获得一些实用的、在市场上受欢迎的技能，而信息管理方向正好能提供这些；二是我觉得人际交往能力对职业成功至关重要，所以选了心理学作为辅修。
A:  Interesting. And, your postgraduate studies?很有意思。那你的研究生学习经历呢？
B:  Well, I am really passionate about consumer behavior, so I pursued a master's in that area. I also strive to keep my professional skills current, so I continuously attend seminars and conferences related management and customer service.我对消费者行为非常感兴趣，因此攻读了一个该领域的硕士学位。同时，我也致力于保持专业技能的与时俱进，所以会持续参加与管理及客户服务相关的研讨会和会议。
A:  Very good. Now, tell me a little bit about your work experience. I see here that you previously worked at Oracle.很好。现在跟我说说你的工作经历吧。我看到你之前在甲骨文公司工作过。
B:  Yeah, I worked as their customer support manager, which brought me a breadth of experience in both client care, and process management. I supervised and coordinated the customer support team as well as implemented new strategies to achieve better customer satisfaction.是的，我在那里担任客户支持经理，这让我积累了广泛的客户关怀和流程管理经验。我还负责监督和指导客户支持团队，并推行新策略以提升客户满意度。
A:  Interesting...很有意思……
B:  Yes, in this position I was able to make some pretty significant contributions to the overall success of the company. With the different initiatives that we implemented, we lowered our churn rate to about five percent, which had a direct impact on revenue.是的，在这个职位上，我为公司的整体成功做出了一些相当显著的贡献。通过实施不同的举措，我们将流失率降低到了约 5%，这直接影响了收入。
