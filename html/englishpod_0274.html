Mr Ford:  You may ask yourself, why is this market so cut-throat? Well the answer is simple. There is a huge untapped potential market out there ,with a huge untapped potential for profit. 你们可能会问自己，为什么这个市场如此残酷？答案很简单：那里存在一个巨大的未被开发的潜在市场，蕴含着巨大的利润潜力。
Mr Ford:  If I bring up the first graph here, it shows the increase in terms of number of computer owners across the globe. 如果我展示这里的第一个图表，它显示了全球电脑拥有量的增长趋势。
Mr Ford:  As you can see in the 1980's computer ownership amounted to around 0.5% of the total world population. Since the 1990's, computer ownership has risen dramatically. 正如大家从 1980 年代的数据可以看出，当时电脑普及率仅占全球总人口的约 0.5%。自 1990 年代以来，电脑拥有量急剧上升。

Mr Ford:  Now of course 60 million is just a drop in the ocean if you compare the total population of China, and this is a key reason why the personal computer market is such a hot market. 当然，如果将中国的总人口作为参照，6000 万不过沧海一粟，这也是个人电脑市场如此炙手可热的关键原因。
