The Weekend - Adventure Sports (C0239)

A:  Welcome to Adventure Tours . How may I help you? 欢迎来到冒险之旅。有什么可以帮您的吗？
B:  I want to book a tour with adventure sports . 我想预订一个包含极限运动的旅行团。
A:  Excellent! Our company has more than ten years of experience in the adventure tourism and sports field . Let me show you some options. This is our most popular choice, our river guides will take you on a whitewater rafting trip followed by a ride in a hot air balloon ! 太好了！我们公司在这个冒险旅游和运动领域拥有超过十年的经验。让我给您展示一些选择。这是我们要最受欢迎的方案，我们的河流向导将带您进行白水漂流，随后乘坐热气球！
B:  I don't really think I'm ready to throw myself down a river full of jagged rocks in a rubber boat or go up in the air in a wicker basket held up by an oversize balloon. What else do you have? 我实在觉得我还不够准备好把自己扔进布满嶙峋岩石的河里坐橡皮艇，或者被一个巨大的气球吊着的柳条篮子里带到空中。你们还有什么别的吗？
A:  Well, in that case, we can take you hang gliding with one of our experienced instructors. It's the closest you can get to flying. 嗯，那样的话，我们可以让您在经验丰富的教练带领下体验滑翔伞。这离飞行最近了。
B:  What? You mean strap myself to a flimsy kite? No thank you! Next! 什么？你是说把自己绑在一个脆弱的风筝上？不用了！下一个！
A:  Mmm. ok. Well, why don't you tell me a little bit more about what you would like? We have everything from mountain biking, to rock climbing to street luge. 嗯，好的。那么，您能多告诉我一些您想要什么吗？我们有从山地自行车到攀岩再到街头滑板车等各种项目。
B:  I'm thinking something exciting but. safer. 我在想一些既刺激又相对安全的项目。
A:  I have the perfect option, this package will take you on a hiking trip through the Himalayas for three days and afterwards there's a dog sledding journey! 我有个完美的方案，这个套餐将带您进行为期三天的喜马拉雅山脉徒步旅行，之后还有一次狗拉雪橇之旅！
B:  That's more like it ! 这就对了！
